### 2.1 G4 Complete Solution (Sakana Fugu Ultra v2)

Sakana Fugu Ultra v2 established the full-Fock continuous-time thermodynamic limit on the periodic CAR algebra without spin-chain or free-fermion approximations:

1. **Exact Cancellation of the One-Point Term:**
   Using the CAR parity-chiral automorphism $\mathscr{C}(c_x) = (-1)^x c_x^\dagger$, which satisfies $\mathscr{C}(n_x - 1/2) = -(n_x - 1/2)$ and $\mathscr{C}(a_x) = -a_x$:
   - For all bulk bonds $x < L-1$, $\mathscr{C}(P_x) = P_x$.
   - For the periodic bond, $\mathscr{C}(c_{L-1}^\dagger c_0) = (-1)^{L-1} c_{L-1} c_0^\dagger = (-1)^L c_0^\dagger c_{L-1} = c_0^\dagger c_{L-1}$ because $L$ is even.
   - Hence $\mathscr{C}(G_L) = G_L$. By trace invariance under $\mathscr{C}$, $\langle a_x \rangle_{L,\theta} = 0$, guaranteeing that no connected subtraction is required.

2. **Absence of Two-Sublattice Distortion:**
   One-site translation acts as $T(G_L) = G_L$ and $T(a_x) = -a_{x+1}$. The two signs cancel in the two-point function $\langle a_0(\tau) a_r(\sigma) \rangle$, proving that the microscopic two-point function depends solely on coordinate separation $r$, with no two-sublattice kernel.

3. **Connected-Interval Linked-Cluster Expansion:**
   Expanding $e^{(1-s)\theta G_L} a_0 e^{s\theta G_L} a_r$ into bond words, any disjoint component containing exactly one $a$-insertion vanishes under $\mathscr{C}$. Thus only words forming a connected bond interval $I$ spanning $0$ and $r$ survive:
   $$|W_s(I; 0, r)| \le \frac{1}{4}\sum_{n \ge m}\frac{(\theta m)^n}{n!} \le \frac{1}{4}\left(\theta e^{1+\theta}\right)^m = \frac{1}{4}\varepsilon(\theta)^m.$$
   At $\theta = 1/192$, $\varepsilon(1/192) < 0.01424$.

4. **Thermodynamic Limit and Spatial Moment Summability:**
   Exterior disconnected bonds resum into partition-function ratios $\rho_L(m) = Z_{L-m-1}^{\rm op}(\theta) / Z_L^{\rm ring}(\theta) \to e^{b(\theta) - (m+1)p(\theta)}$. Truncation at interval size $M$ satisfies:
   $$\sup_{0 \le s \le 1} |k_\theta(s) - k_\theta^{(<M)}(s)| \le \frac{1}{4}\sum_{m \ge M}(m+1)^2 \varepsilon(\theta)^m.$$
   Consequently, all spatial moments are uniformly summable:
   $$\sup_{L, s} \sum_{r \in \mathbb{Z}_L} (1 + d_L(0, r))^q |K_{L,\theta}(s; r)| < \infty \quad (\forall q \ge 0).$$
   The macroscopic limit is strictly:
   $$\lim_{L \to \infty} C_{L,\theta}(\tau, \sigma; F, G) = k_\theta(|\tau - \sigma|)\int_0^1 F(u)G(u)\,du.$$
