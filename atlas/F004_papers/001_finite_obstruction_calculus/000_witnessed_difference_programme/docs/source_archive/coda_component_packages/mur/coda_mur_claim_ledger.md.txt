## 3. Verification Invariants & Defect Policy

1. **Zero Floats Invariant & Exact Rational Domain:** All numerical scalars and defect masses are strictly exact rational numbers (`sympy.Rational` or `fractions.Fraction`). Floats, floating-point approximations, square roots (`sqrt(2)`), and unconstrained algebraic symbols strictly fail validation with `TypeError` or `ValueError` across `coda_mur_core.py` and `cochain_presentation.py`.
2. **Strict Boolean & Callback Typing:** All certificate flags (`commutes`, `chi_certified`) and ledger dimensions (`e`, `i`, `a`, `u`) require `type(v) is bool`. Strings (e.g. `"False"`) and integers are rejected with `TypeError`. Target callback `target_regime.is_auth()` must return strict Python `bool`, and `target_regime.terminalize()` must return `Optional[str]`.
3. **Finite Verification Completeness:** `DescentSquareCertificate.verify()` requires the full carrier and all four maps (`carrier`, `state_map`, `observation_map`, `source_look`, `target_look`) to verify the square. Partial constructions return `False` and are rejected by `is_valid()` rather than falling back to unverified Boolean attestation.
4. **Source Carrier & Commutation Binding:** Requested source IDs must belong to the verified carrier of the certificate, or have dynamic commutation explicitly verified across the square. Requests failing commutation yield `R_notransport`.
5. **Provenance Exception Safety & Action Agreement:** Swallowing of exceptions in provenance evaluation is eliminated; square evaluation errors yield `R_notransport` and action evaluation errors yield `R_substituted_artifact`. When both `FiniteCoreAction` and `certificate.state_map` are supplied, their outputs on the requested source must strictly agree.
6. **5×5 Kind Monoid & Exact Loss-Free Invariant:** Morphism kinds compose via a closed associative total-order monoid: $\mathsf{EXACT} < \mathsf{EMBEDDING} < \mathsf{QUOTIENT} < \mathsf{APPROXIMATION} < \mathsf{UNMEASURED}$. Morphisms of kind `EXACT` or `EMBEDDING` must declare zero loss mass; non-empty loss renders `is_valid() == False`.
7. **Pasting Hygiene, Intermediate Preservation, & Leaf Receipts:** Pasting requires both input legs to be individually valid, verifies agreement of intermediate observation/look maps, verifies intermediate carrier coverage, and retains all leaf leg receipts in `leg_ledgers` associatively across composition. Incompatible defect vector shapes strictly raise `ValueError`.
8. **Context-Bound Quotient Distance Certificates & Snapshots:** `QuotientDistanceCertificate.verify()` enforces 1-column cochain shapes `(m, 1)` and exact rational scalar entries. In `evaluate_transport_authority`, the certificate's cochain and repair map are strictly bound to `certificate.defect_vector` and `certificate.repair_map` (`R_malformed_quotient` / `R_obstruction`). The returned `TransportResult` stores an immutable snapshot of the certificate, isolating it from caller mutations.
9. **Policy Diagnostics:** Residual codes (`DIAGNOSTIC_NO_TRANSPORT = 1`, `DIAGNOSTIC_UNPRICED_LOSS = 3/4`, `DIAGNOSTIC_NO_TERMINAL = 1/2`, `DIAGNOSTIC_TARGET_UNAUTH = 1/4`) are explicit policy diagnostics, not derived mathematical defect bounds.

## 5. Explicit Non-Claims & Theoretical Boundaries

1. **Finite scope:** A commit, test pass, or green gate in this repository does not confer real-world physical authority or establish external operational safety. The gate verifies internal finite consistency only.
2. **Induced Action (L01) Non-Constructive Boundary:** The mathematical construction in `coda_mur_l01_finite_core_action.lean` connects induced action existence to descent-fiber invariance and totality using classical choice (`Classical.choose`) over unrestricted types. It is not an algorithmic finite enumeration procedure. The Python runtime class `FiniteCoreAction` is a lightweight callable check, not an executable realization of the L01 classical construction.
3. **Bicategory Files as Scaffolding:** `coda_mur_t02_t07_instances.lean` is an open `Unit` research placeholder, and `coda_mur_t02_t07_kernel.lean` is an open 3-field record. Neither constitutes a completed proof of a bicategory of regimes or full coherence (associators, unitors, pentagon identity).
4. **Finite Loss Algebra Scoping:** The formal loss theorems establish the four-bit Boolean operation only. Rational addition is the separate paper/Python extension. They do not prove conservation or classify all continuum, topological, or measure-theoretic loss.
5. **No-Third-Source Hypothesis:** Theorem `lossy_no_creation` relies on an explicitly stated hypothesis in `NoCreationContext` (`noThirdAuthoritySource`); it does not mathematically derive exhaustiveness of authority sources from information loss alone.
6. **Certificate Validity vs Relevance:** A mathematically valid quotient distance certificate proves distance for its specific cochain; it is relevant to transport only when both its cochain and repair matrix are bound to the declared transport model.
