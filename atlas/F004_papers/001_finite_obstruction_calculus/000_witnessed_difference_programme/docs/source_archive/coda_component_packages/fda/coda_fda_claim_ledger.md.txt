## 1. Operational Standing & Attestation Framework

Standing in CODA-FDA is defined as an internal dependent pair:
$$\mathsf{Standing}(p) := \Sigma(t:\mathbb{T}) \bigl( \Pi^\sharp(\text{packet}, p) = \mathsf{inl}(t) \wedge \mathsf{IsAuth}(t) \bigr).$$

Standing requires both an exact terminal-branch pipeline execution path and seven derived halt guards. This operational record is definitionally distinct from the regime model predicate $\mathsf{Standing}_R(g, p)$ of Section 7. Soundness (FDA-T7) is proved strictly within the regime model under explicit verifier-soundness and grant-soundness hypotheses; operational standing (FDA-D2) is not connected to regime soundness without a declared regime embedding $R_\Pi$. The policy core consumes caller attestations. The separate execution harness records and checks all finite candidate certificates, stage-boundary skeleton snapshots, declared lift IDs and two-run replay. Neither proves external semantic soundness.

An external linear cochain repair operator $d_0: V_0 \to V_1$ with defect $x \in V_1$ and exact rational $L_1$ quotient distance $\Phi_1([x]) = \min_f \|x + d_0 f\|_{1, w}$ optionally provides a rational scalar to guard $g_3$ (`zero_residual_mass`). That solver measures leftover mass; it does not construct an authority cochain or prove an origin of standing.

## 4. Refusal Taxonomy

The Python companion implements the formal `RefusalCode` enum:

- `RefusalCode.R_nopacket`: Missing or invalid five-field packet (evaluated before guard inspection).
- `RefusalCode.R_candidate`: Terminal failed one or more deterministic halt guards (e.g. $g_1$, $g_3$, $g_4$, $g_5$, $g_6$, $g_7$).
- `RefusalCode.R_unreplayable`: Replay determinism guard $g_2$ failed.
- `RefusalCode.R_contradictory_evidence`: Supplied residual certificate failed verification or distance mismatched evidence residual.
- `RefusalCode.R_obstruction`: Nonzero quotient obstruction.

## 5. Vocabulary Fence & Scope Boundaries

### 5.1 Terminology & Posture Fences

| Term / Posture | Preferred Framing | Prohibited Overclaim |
|---|---|---|
| Standing | Operational witness pair under declared packet and pipeline | Legal, institutional, or cosmic authority |
| Authorization | Stepwise policy evaluation over caller attestations | Autonomous real-world proof of pipeline run |
| Quotient Solver | External scalar calculator measuring leftover mass | Origin-of-standing theorem |
| Verifier | Local check producing evidence or failing into refusal | Inherently sound oracle for real-world truth |

### 5.2 What CODA-FDA Does Not Support

1. **Does Not Prove Universal Operational Authority:** CODA-FDA defines a finite standing constructor under a declared context $S$. It does not establish authority outside this declared finite setting.
2. **Does Not Promote Fluency to Proof:** High logit confidence, benchmark scores, or LLM output fluency cannot satisfy the 5-field packet requirement or the 7 halt guards.
3. **Does Not Mint Global Authority:** No authority tokens, release gates, or external credentials are minted by this package.
4. **Does Not Claim Continuum Verifier Soundness:** Lean 4 formalization models the discrete definitional firewall; it does not claim infinite-dimensional or empirical verifier soundness.

### 5.3 Narrowest Defensible Claim

In any finite evaluation setting $S$ with a declared 5-field descent packet $(K, E, V, \tau, \Lambda)$ and sealed pipeline $\Pi_S^\sharp$, operational standing (`AuthStanding`) is an inhabited dependent pair type if and only if all 7 derived halt guards hold simultaneously and the pipeline yields a terminal outcome in $\operatorname{inl}(\mathbb{T}_S)$.

## 7. Statement-level correspondence and operational limits

- FDA-T2 now concerns absence of a supplied optional input. The historical
  `uniform_non_creation` premise is impossible because an `Empty`-carrier packet
  exists. Neither theorem encodes Python's nonblank-field and unique-ID validation.
- Lean FDA-T5/T6 model one descent and verification. The Python harness enumerates
  a declared candidate tuple and requires every bound certificate to have zero
  distance; this finite extension is tested, not proved by those Lean theorems.
- The execution trace stores canonical immutable values. Skeleton equality is
  measured at stage boundaries under a declared projection; registry completeness
  and callback semantic adequacy remain model assumptions.
- The solver implementation is unweighted exact L1. General positive weights
  in the mathematical notation are not a supported API argument.
