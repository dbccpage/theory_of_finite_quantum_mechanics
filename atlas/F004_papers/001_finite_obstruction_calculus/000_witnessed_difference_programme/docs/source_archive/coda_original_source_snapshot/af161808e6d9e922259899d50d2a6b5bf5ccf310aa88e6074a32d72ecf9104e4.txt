**What this document is.** One carrier for the root arc of the Witnessed

argument they carry between them is one argument: WD-0 argues that nothing is

**What it replaces.** The source papers named in the reading map below. Each is

else**. It resolved no mathematics. No claim was promoted, retired, re-typed, or
absorbed into another claim. No proof obligation was discharged, dropped, or
renumbered. Where sections state overlapping or apparently redundant content,
both statements survive: judging two rows to say the same thing is a
mathematical act, and it was reserved to the operator rather than taken here.
Every claim type — OPEN, CONJECTURE, INTERPRETIVE, STANDARD, DEFINITION —
stands exactly as it stood in its source, and no source's tagging vocabulary
was normalized against another's.

**Identifiers are preserved verbatim.** Every claim, support-lemma, non-claim,
and falsifier id (`WD0-C*`, `WD1-L*`, `WD2-N*`, `WD3-F*`, and their siblings)

### Bare non-sameness cannot equivariantly generate nontrivial structure

### 1. Abstract (target lead sentence)

*Target payoff sentence — to be drafted to this specification:*

> A finite carrier equipped with nothing but equality admits every permutation
> as an automorphism; consequently no point, order, orientation, adjacency,
> clock, scale, nonuniform weight, or observer can be selected from it by any
> parameter-free construction. Structure in such a system is always imported,
> and we classify exactly what the import must supply.

**Drafting constraint (hygiene Part A).** The first line states the result in the
language of finite model theory and permutation groups — not in the language of
obstruction calculus. A reader who has never opened Paper 000 must be able to
evaluate the claim.

### 2. Why a researcher should care

The Anomalon corpus declares a root boundary

$$
\mathrm{Real}_0 \;=\; \mathrm{PrimitiveDifference} \;\sqcup\; \mathrm{Structure},
$$

and has repeatedly aspired to reduce the second summand to the first. This paper
is the honest test of that aspiration. It is also of independent interest to
anyone who proposes a "difference-first," "distinction-first," or
"relation-first" ontology: the symmetry argument applies to all of them.

The practical payoff is a **checklist of injected asymmetries**. Any construction
claiming to derive a world from difference alone must be locatable on that list,
and this paper makes the location mechanical rather than rhetorical.

### 3. Setting

Let $X$ be a finite set with $|X| \geq 3$. Let $\mathcal{L}_{=}$ be the
first-order language whose only nonlogical symbol is equality. The automorphism
group of the structure $(X, =)$ is the full symmetric group

$$
\mathrm{Aut}(X, =) \;=\; \mathrm{Sym}(X).
$$

*Claim type:* `[STANDARD → WDP001-RA0-C1]` — also proved as (Aut) in encyclopedia
`ENC-1217` (PROVED-PROSE); not re-proved as a novel paper result.

**Interpretive fence.** Identifying Primitive Difference with the relation
$x \not\equiv y$ already presupposes the relata $x, y$, a carrier containing
them, and an admissible act of comparison. **ENC-1218** commits Reading A as
operative for §4; Reading B is priced, not settled. That presupposition is the
subject of §7 and is *not* silently granted as a generation story.

### 4. Theorem targets

Stated as targets. **Partial discharge (2026-07-25):** T1–T3 and **T4-weak** are
secured in the encyclopedia under Reading A; full T4 exhaustiveness remains open.

#### T1 — Unary triviality

Every parameter-free $\mathcal{L}_{=}$-definable subset $S \subseteq X$ satisfies
$S = \varnothing$ or $S = X$.

*Claim type:* `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL → WDP001-RA0-C2]` — **ENC-1217** (T1); hypotheses:
finite $X$, $\lvert X\rvert\ge 3$, parameter-free $\mathcal{L}_{=}$, Reading A
(ENC-1218). Lean still OPEN (PL-WDP001-RA0-5).

#### T2 — Binary orbit classification

Every parameter-free $\mathcal{L}_{=}$-definable relation $R \subseteq X \times X$
is a union of the two $\mathrm{Sym}(X)$-orbits on $X \times X$: the diagonal
$\Delta = \{(x,x)\}$ and its complement.

*Claim type:* `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL → WDP001-RA0-C3]` — **ENC-1217** (T2); same
hypotheses as T1.

#### T3 — Non-selection

No parameter-free equivariant construction on $(X, =)$ selects any of:

| Target | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| A distinguished point | Would be a nontrivial unary definable set (T1) |
| A nontrivial partition | Its classes would be a nontrivial definable family |
| A directed adjacency | Would refine the off-diagonal orbit (T2) |
| A linear order | Antisymmetry is not $\mathrm{Sym}(X)$-invariant |
| An orientation | Same |
| A nonuniform weight $w: X \to \mathbb{R}$ | Equivariance forces $w$ constant |
| A clock successor | Would be a directed adjacency |

*Claim type:* `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL → WDP001-RA0-C4]` — **ENC-1219**; conditional on
ENC-1217 and ENC-1218. Lean OPEN (PL-WDP001-RA0-5).

#### T4 — Injection classification

Any construction that *does* select one of the T3 targets has introduced at
least one of: a **parameter**, a **retained mark**, an **asymmetry**, a **random
choice**, an **external witness**, or an **additional structural channel**.

*Claim type:* `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL → WDP001-RA0-C5]` — **ENC-1220** discharges only
**T4-weak** under the declared constructor grammar $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{WD0}}$
(resources P/M/A/R/E/S). Full exhaustiveness outside that grammar remains
**OPEN** (support lemma WD0-L4; residual of PL-WDP001-RA0-4). Do not read T4 as a
universal ontology of all possible metatheories.

T4-weak is the load-bearing *priced* half for the programme inside
$\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{WD0}}$. T1–T3 are the negative half.

### 5. Proof strategy — three independent levels

The paper's methodological claim is that the same statement should be
established three ways, and that agreement between the three is itself evidence.

| Level | Instrument | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Finite group-action argument in prose | Human-checkable; establishes T1–T3 by orbit counting |
| L2 | Lean 4 theorem over finite types and permutation invariance | Mechanized; guards against a definitional slip in L1 |
| L3 | Executable exhaustive checker for small $\lvert X \rvert$ | Empirical; enumerates all candidate structures for $\lvert X \rvert \leq 6$ and confirms non-selection |

**L2 placement.** Per repository convention, any Lean development belongs under
`omega_engine/lean4/` and nowhere else.

**L3 is the hygiene-mandated worked example** (Part A, item 4). It must produce a
number a reader can reproduce.

### 6. Worked example (required, not yet built)

*Specification for the example that must exist before this paper ships:*

Take $\lvert X \rvert = 3$. Enumerate all $2^{9} = 512$ binary relations on $X$.
Exhibit that exactly the $\mathrm{Sym}(3)$-invariant ones are the $2^2 = 4$
unions of $\{\Delta, \Delta^{c}\}$, and that none of them is a linear order, a
successor function, or a nonuniform weight. Report the counts.

This is small enough to print in full and is the paper's reproducibility anchor.

### 7. The typing problem (the honest fork)

The no-go is only as strong as the formalization of "Primitive Difference." Two
readings are available, and the paper must commit to treating both:

**Reading A — relational (operative for §4).** Primitive Difference is formalized
as the pure-equality structure $(X,=)$ / relation $x \not\equiv y$. Relata,
carrier, and comparison already exist; difference has not generated them, and §4
applies directly. *Claim type:* `[DEFINITION → WDP001-RA0-C9]` with interpretive
gloss — **ENC-1218** commits Reading A as the operative host of T1–T4; the
philosophy that this is the only possible ontology is *not* claimed.

**Reading B — event (priced, non-operative for §4).** Primitive Difference as an
untyped primitive occurrence cannot be composed, oriented, repeated, or compared
without added constructors. *Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP001-RA0-C10]` for a full
occurrence calculus; **ENC-1218** prices Reading B as non-operative for the
no-go without inventing that calculus.

Consequently there are exactly two honest root strategies, and the paper's
conclusion is to name which one the corpus takes:

1. **Two-channel root.** Admit Primitive Difference *together with*
   carrier-Structure or exhibited retention — the current Anomalon boundary.

2. **Enriched single primitive.** Define a primitive distinction-event that
   intrinsically has retainable or composable faces. This preserves the rhetoric
   of one primitive, but the retained/composable structure is then *part of the
   definition*, not derived from bare non-sameness.

**The paper must not present strategy 2 as a derivation.** Doing so is the
principal self-deception this outline exists to prevent.

At minimum the drafted paper must situate itself against:

trivial."* The correct response is that their triviality is the point — the

### 9. Falsifiability

> **This paper fails if** a parameter-free, $\mathrm{Sym}(X)$-equivariant
> construction is exhibited that selects any T3 target from $(X, =)$ alone for
> some $\lvert X \rvert \geq 3$; or if the injection classification T4 is shown
> non-exhaustive by a construction whose asymmetry source is not on the list.

### 10. What this paper does NOT claim

* It does **not** claim Primitive Difference is incoherent, useless, or false.
* It does **not** claim Structure is metaphysically prior to difference.
* It does **not** derive time, space, probability, quantum mechanics, or an
  observer. If T1–T4 hold, it **targets** a classification showing none of them
  come for free from bare \((X,=)\); that classification is **not established**
  in this outline.

* It does **not** establish that the two-channel root is *correct* — only that
  bare non-sameness is insufficient, so *some* second channel is required.

* It does **not** prove anything about infinite carriers. $\lvert X \rvert$
  finite is a live hypothesis, not a convenience.

* The result is about *parameter-free equivariant* constructions. Constructions
  with parameters are not refuted; they are **priced**.

### 11. Consequences for the programme

If T1–T4 hold, the programme's root reads:

> Primitive Difference supplies a field of possible distinctions, not a canonical
> world. A world begins when some distinction is **retained as an asymmetric mark**
> or **witnessed relative to a context**.

*Claim type:* `[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA0-C8]` — conditional on T3 (WDP001-RA0-C4) and T4
(WDP001-RA0-C5); the second-channel requirement follows only if both hold.

This converts an apparent weakness into the constitutive mechanism, and hands
WD-1 its exact question: what is the minimal retention constructor?

**Stop condition triggered on success.** Programme stop condition 1 (*root
impossibility*) fires: derivation of nontrivial Structure from bare Primitive
Difference stops here, and every later WD paper must declare its second channel
explicitly.

### 1. Abstract (target lead sentence)

*Target payoff sentence — to be drafted to this specification:*

> Retention is not one notion but five — *occurrence*, *mark*, *persistence*,
> *replay*, and *comparison* — and we separate them by explicit countermodels:
> each holds in a finite structure where the other four hold and it fails.
> Persistence and replay are defined without a time parameter, as invariance
> under a declared disturbance family and as agreement across an unordered
> access family respectively; the familiar temporal readings are strictly
> stronger specializations, and we exhibit the gap.

**Drafting constraint (hygiene Part A).** The first line states the result in the
language of finite structures, endomorphism families, and countermodels — not in
the language of memory, experience, or observation. A reader who has never opened
Paper 000 or WD-0 must be able to evaluate the claim. In particular the abstract
must contain **no** occurrence of "then", "again", "later", "stores", or
"remembers" used as a load-bearing term.

### 2. Why a researcher should care

WD-0's classification T4 asserts that any construction selecting nontrivial
structure from bare non-sameness has injected at least one of: a parameter, a
**retained mark**, an asymmetry, a random choice, an external witness, or an
additional structural channel. Of those six, five are already precise notions in
existing mathematics. "Retained mark" is not. It is the load-bearing entry for
the programme — the one on which the entire route to an observer runs — and it is
currently the least defined term in the corpus.

This paper is the repair. Its payoff is a **constructor with a bill of costs**: a
finite, checkable specification of what must be added to a difference so that it
can be retained and compared, with each added component located on the WD-0 T4
list rather than smuggled.

The independent interest is broader. Any account of identity-through-change —
process algebra, automata memory, information channels, default persistence in
AI planning — assumes some subset of the five notions and rarely says which. The
independence separation is useful whether or not the Anomalon programme is.

### 3. Setting

Throughout, $X$ is a finite carrier as in WD-0 and $D$ is a finite set whose
elements are called **occurrences**. No structure on $D$ is assumed: no order, no
composition, no labelling.

A **medium** is a pair $(M, \bot)$ with $M$ finite and $\bot \in M$ a
distinguished element read as *unmarked*. The distinguished element is an
**import**, priced in §7 and §13; it is not derived.

A **retention structure** is a tuple

$$
\mathsf{Ret} \;=\; \bigl(D,\; M,\; \bot,\; \mu,\; E,\; \{\rho_i\}_{i \in I},\; \kappa \bigr)
$$

with $\mu : D \to M$ the **marking map**, $E \subseteq \mathrm{End}(M)$ a declared
**disturbance family**, $\{\rho_i\}_{i \in I}$ a family of **access maps**
$\rho_i : M \to R$ into an **access medium** $R$ indexed by an unordered set $I$,
and $\kappa : M \times M \to N$ a **co-presentation map** into a comparison
medium $(N, \mathsf{same})$ with $\kappa(m,m) = \mathsf{same}$ for all $m \in M$.

#### 3.1 The five notions

Each is a predicate on $\mathsf{Ret}$ and on an occurrence $d \in D$.

| Id | Notion | Formal clause | What it does **not** supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | Occurrence | $D \neq \varnothing$ | Any medium, any state, any difference outside $D$ |
| N2 | Mark | $\mu(d) \neq \bot$ | Survival, readability, or comparability of $\mu(d)$ |
| N3 | Persistence | weak: $e(\mu(d)) \neq \bot$ for all $e \in E$; strong: $e(\mu(d)) = \mu(d)$ for all $e \in E$ | Access to the mark; any notion of *when* |
| N4 | Replay | $\rho_i(\mu(d)) = \rho_j(\mu(d))$ for all $i, j \in I$, with $\lvert I \rvert \geq 2$ | Survival under $E$; comparison of two distinct marks |
| N5 | Comparison | $\kappa$ is defined on $\mu(D) \times \mu(D)$ and separates: $\kappa(m,m') = \mathsf{same} \iff m = m'$ | Any mark to compare; any persistence of the verdict |

*Claim type (§3.1).* N1–N5 are `[DEFINITION]`s — predicates on $\mathsf{Ret}$,
not results. No clause of §3.1 is asserted proved; their *mutual independence* is
the theorem target T3 (§4), typed `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C4]`. The definitions are stated
as predicates, not as theorems, precisely to avoid the def-as-theorem trap.

**Weak versus strong persistence.** N3 is stated in two grades. The paper must
decide which grade is constitutive for WD-2's O1 observer and justify the choice;
see PL-WDP001-RA1-3. They are not interchangeable — strong persistence is a fixed-point
condition, weak persistence only a non-annihilation condition. *Claim type:*
`[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C12]`.

#### 3.2 The temporal-import fence (central discipline)

The words *persists*, *again*, *then*, *survives*, and *replay* all carry an
ordering in ordinary use. The definitions above are constructed to avoid it:

* **Persistence is not endurance-through-time.** It is invariance of a
  distinction under a *declared, unordered* family $E$ of endomorphisms. Nothing
  in N3 iterates, composes, or sequences.

* **Replay is not repetition.** It is *agreement across access*: the mark yields
  the same value under any member of an unordered index family. Nothing in N4
  says one access precedes another.

The temporal readings are recovered only by specialization: taking
$E = \{\tau^{k} : k \geq 0\}$ for a single generator $\tau$, or taking $I$
linearly ordered. **Each specialization is an additional import and must be
declared.** T4 below is the theorem target that this fence is real and not
merely notational.

**Second fence — no observer.** No clause of §3.1 quantifies over an observer, a
reader, an agent, or a subject. A mark is *a difference in a second carrier*, not
*a state something can read*. Defining it the latter way would presuppose the
observer that WD-2 is charged with constructing, and would trip programme stop
condition 2 (circular observer) before WD-2 begins.

### 4. Theorem targets

Stated as targets. None is proved here.

#### T1 — Retention is not free

No retention structure with $\mu(d) \neq \bot$ is definable from $(X, =)$ alone
by a parameter-free equivariant construction. Immediate corollary of WD-0 T1 and
T3, hence conditional on them.

*Claim type:* `[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA1-C1]` — conditional on WD-0 T1 and T3, neither of
which is itself established.

#### T2 — Medium asymmetry necessity

If $\mathrm{Aut}(M) = \mathrm{Sym}(M)$ — that is, if the medium carries nothing
but equality — then no parameter-free construction selects $\bot$, and N2 is
unstatable. Hence marking requires the medium to be non-homogeneous.

**Consequence (the paper's structural contribution).** "Retained mark" is *not* a
sixth primitive alongside the WD-0 T4 entries. It decomposes as

$$
\text{retained mark} \;=\; \underbrace{(M, \bot)}_{\text{parameter / structural channel}}
\;+\; \underbrace{\mu}_{\text{additional structural channel}}
\;+\; \underbrace{E}_{\text{declared stability regime}},
$$

so WD-0's T4 list is *shortened*, not lengthened, by this paper. If T2 holds, the
programme's second channel is exactly a medium with a distinguished state.

*Claim type:* T2 `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C2]`; the decomposition consequence
`[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C3]`. C3 *shortens* WD-0's T4 list and must be reconciled with WDP001-RA0-C5
rather than presented as independent evidence. See PL-WDP001-RA1-4.

#### T3 — Five-fold independence (main technical content)

For each $k \in \{1, \dots, 5\}$ there is a finite retention structure satisfying
every $N_j$ with $j \neq k$ and failing $N_k$ — modulo those entailments that are
genuinely forced and must be identified rather than assumed. Candidate
countermodels:

| Id | Separates | Sketch |
|---|---|---|
| CM1 | occurrence $\not\Rightarrow$ mark | $D = \{d\}$, $M = \{\bot\}$, $\mu(d) = \bot$. Something occurs; nothing anywhere differs |
| CM2 | mark $\not\Rightarrow$ persistence | $M = \{\bot, m\}$, $\mu(d) = m$, $E = \{e\}$ with $e(m) = \bot$. The declared disturbance annihilates the distinction |
| CM3 | persistence $\not\Rightarrow$ replay | $E$-invariant $m$, but $\lvert I \rvert = 2$ with $\rho_1(m) \neq \rho_2(m)$: an access-dependent read-out |
| CM4 | replay $\not\Rightarrow$ comparison | Marks in fibres admitting no co-presentation: $\kappa$ undefined on $\mu(D) \times \mu(D)$, or non-separating |
| CM5 | comparison $\not\Rightarrow$ persistence | $\kappa$ separating on all of $M$, but $E$ annihilates every mark: a comparator with nothing to compare |

The forced-entailment question is open and is *not* to be settled by intuition.
Plausibly $N2 \Rightarrow N1$ (a mark is a mark *of* an occurrence); the paper
must prove or refute each of the remaining candidate implications and publish the
resulting **independence lattice** over the five notions.

*Claim type:* T3 (five-fold independence) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C4]`; the forced-entailment
lattice `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C5]` — it must be *computed* (PL-WDP001-RA1-9), not assumed; the
suspected $N2 \Rightarrow N1$ `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C6]`, currently intuition only. See
PL-WDP001-RA1-5.

#### T4 — Order non-import, and strictness of the temporal specialization

Two halves, both required:

1. There exist models of N3 and N4 in which no component carries a definable
   partial order, total order, or successor function. Hence the definitions do
   not presuppose time.

2. The temporal specializations ($E$ cyclically generated; $I$ linearly ordered)
   are **strictly** stronger: exhibit a structure satisfying N3 and N4 that fails
   the corresponding temporal reading.

Half 2 is what makes half 1 non-vacuous. If half 2 fails — if every model of N3
and N4 turns out to carry a definable order — that is a substantive negative
result: time is *not* eliminable from retention, and the programme must import
and price it explicitly at WD-1 rather than at WD-5.

*Claim type:* half 1 `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C7]`; half 2 `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C8]` — **if half 2
fails, half 1 is vacuous** and the negative result (time non-eliminable) stands
in its place. See PL-WDP001-RA1-6.

#### T5 — Comparison localizes its cost in co-presence

WD-0 T2 gives that the diagonal $\Delta$ and its complement are the only
parameter-free definable binary relations on $(X, =)$. Hence the *sameness test*
is free; what is not free is putting two marks into one medium so the test
applies. The theorem target is that the entire cost of N5 sits in the existence
of $\kappa$ on $\mu(D) \times \mu(D)$, not in the separation property.

*Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C9]` — uses WD-0 T2 (itself ``OPEN``, WDP001-RA0-C3) and is
conditional on it. See PL-WDP001-RA1-5.

#### T6 — Non-circularity

Every clause of §3.1 is expressible in a language whose nonlogical symbols name
only $D$, $M$, $\bot$, $\mu$, $E$, $\{\rho_i\}$, $\kappa$, $\mathsf{same}$. No
clause quantifies over observers, agents, or readers. This is a syntactic check
and must be mechanized, not asserted.

*Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP001-RA1-C10]` — must be a mechanized syntactic check
(PL-WDP001-RA1-2), not an assertion.

### 6. Worked example (required, not yet built)

*Specification for the example that must exist before this paper ships:*

Fix $\lvert D \rvert = 2$, $\lvert M \rvert \leq 3$ with $\bot$ distinguished,

Enumerate every such structure. For each, evaluate the five predicates N1–N5 and

1. The population of each of the 32 cells.
2. Which cells are **empty** — these are the forced entailments, and they are

### 7. The typing problem (the honest fork)

The constructor is only as honest as its answer to: *where does the mark live?*
Two readings are available, and the paper must commit to treating both. This is
the WD-1 instance of WD-0 §7 and inherits its warning verbatim.

**Reading A — extrinsic mark.** The mark is a state of a medium $(M, \bot)$
distinct from the occurrence. Then retention is openly two-channel: $D$ and $M$
are both given, and $\bot$ is a declared parameter. Costs are visible and
locatable on the WD-0 T4 list. §4 applies directly. *Claim type:* `[SCOPE FENCE]`
— the honest two-channel setting §4 assumes; it derives nothing, it declares the
imports.

**Reading B — intrinsic mark.** The occurrence itself has a retainable face; no
second carrier is introduced. This preserves the rhetoric of a single primitive,
but the retainable face is then *part of the definition of occurrence*, not a
consequence of it. Reading B is WD-0 §7 strategy 2 wearing new clothes.
*Claim type:* `[INTERPRETIVE → WDP001-RA1-C13]` — a reading that *names* retention; it
must be carried as a definitional import, never as a derivation.

**The paper must not present Reading B as a derivation of retention.** An
occurrence defined to be retainable does not explain retention; it names it. This
is the principal self-deception this outline exists to prevent, and it is the
same one WD-0 flagged. If the corpus takes Reading B, the claim ledger must carry
it as a definitional import, not a theorem.

**A third temptation, to be refused explicitly.** Defining the mark as "a state
some system can read" makes N2 depend on an observer and collapses WD-1 into
WD-2. It also makes the programme circular at exactly the point stop condition 2
guards. The paper must state this refusal in the body, not only in the ledger.
*Claim type:* `[INTERPRETIVE → WDP001-RA1-C14]` — argued, not proved; its formal residue
is T6 (WDP001-RA1-C10).

### 8. Related work (to be written — nearest neighbours)

At minimum the drafted paper must situate itself against the following. Each entry
states the fit *and* the delta; entries with no genuine delta do not belong.

* **Myhill–Nerode and automata state minimization.** The canonical account of
  minimal memory: states are equivalence classes under a distinguishability
  relation, and the minimal automaton is the quotient. This is the closest
  existing answer to "what is the least that must be retained." The delta is
  decisive: Nerode's relation is defined over *words* — an ordered input — so the
  construction presupposes exactly the sequence order §3.2 refuses. WD-1 is, at
  best, the order-free fragment of that idea, and the paper must say so rather
  than claim novelty over minimization. `[STANDARD]` typing for the classical
  statement.

* **Process calculi and bisimulation (Park, Milner).** The standard formal
  treatment of identity-through-change. The delta: bisimulation is defined over a
  labelled transition system, which is a directed adjacency with labels —
  precisely a T3 target that WD-0 says cannot be had for free. Bisimulation is
  therefore a *consumer* of the retention constructor, not a supplier of it. The
  paper should position it downstream, not as prior art that settles the
  question.

* **Barwise–Seligman information flow / channel theory.** A classification is a
  triple (tokens, types, support relation), and the marking map $\mu : D \to M$
  is a classification in that sense. This is the nearest existing formalism to
  §3, and the paper must credit it plainly. The delta: channel theory takes the
  type set as given and does not ask whether the type set can be homogeneous —
  the T2 medium-asymmetry question is not posed there.

* **Trace semantics.** Included as an *anti-neighbour*. A trace is a sequence;
  adopting trace semantics would import a clock in the first definition. The
  paper cites it to mark the boundary it is refusing to cross, and should say
  which results become available if the import is later accepted and priced.

* **The frame problem (McCarthy–Hayes) and default persistence.** The classical
  observation that "nothing changes unless something changes it" must be
  *assumed*, not derived, and that the assumption is expensive. N3's declared
  disturbance family $E$ is a way of paying that price explicitly rather than by
  default rule. This is a genuine philosophical neighbour and probably the
  closest one outside mathematics.

* **Anomalon WD-0** (`000_wd0_primitive_difference_symmetry_no_go`) — supplies
  T1, T2, T5, and the injection classification this paper refines.

*Claim type (§8 neighbours).* The Myhill–Nerode minimal-automaton statement is
`[STANDARD → WDP001-RA1-C15]`, bisimulation-over-an-LTS is `[STANDARD → WDP001-RA1-C16]`, and
the frame-problem observation is `[STANDARD → WDP001-RA1-C17]` — each cited, never
claimed as a result of this paper. Reading $E$ as the *explicit pricing* of
default persistence is `[INTERPRETIVE]`, not part of the cited results.

**Anticipated referee objection, to be answered in-paper:** *"A retention
structure is just a Moore machine, or just a classification with extra fields."*
The response must not be a denial. The structure *is* weaker than a Moore machine
— deliberately, because a machine's transition function is the ordering this
paper declines to assume. The contribution is the independence separation (T3),
the order-freedom result (T4), and the decomposition of WD-0's "retained mark"
(T2), none of which follow from having written down a familiar-looking tuple.

### 10. What this paper does NOT claim

* It does **not** construct an observer, a detector, or any level of the §5
  observer hierarchy. That is WD-2's charge, and pre-empting it would be circular.

* It does **not** derive time, and does not claim time is unnecessary. It claims
  only that the definitions of §3.1 do not presuppose it — and T4 half 2 is what
  makes that claim checkable rather than verbal.

* It does **not** claim the retention constructor is unique. Other constructors
  may exist; T7 is minimality relative to a *declared* demand set only.

* It does **not** claim marks are physical, material, causal, or energetic. $M$ is
  a finite set with a distinguished element and nothing more.

* It does **not** claim the medium $(M, \bot)$ is derived. It is imported, and §7
  and §13 are where the import is priced.

* It does **not** establish that retention is *sufficient* for anything. It aims
  to establish what retention *is*, and what it costs.

* It does **not** claim that the five notions exhaust retention. Five is a
  proposal; a sixth notion not reducible to these would refine, not refute, the
  paper — but the paper must say so rather than imply exhaustiveness.
  (Exhaustiveness of the five is typed `[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA1-C18]`, not a result.)

### 13. Genesis-bias audit (mandatory before drafting)

Per the programme charter and the repository's Genesis Bias doctrine, this paper
must answer, for its own construction:

* Where did the medium $M$ come from, and what makes it a *second* carrier rather
  than a relabelling of $X$?

* What distinguishes $\bot$? A distinguished element is precisely a WD-0
  parameter; the paper must say so and not treat "unmarked" as a natural default.

* Who declares the disturbance family $E$, and on what authority? A persistence
  claim relative to an $E$ chosen to make it come out true is empty.

* What supplies the index set $I$ with $\lvert I \rvert \geq 2$? Two distinct
  indices *are* a primitive difference; N4 therefore consumes the very thing the
  programme starts from, and must declare it.

* What licenses writing $\mu : D \to M$ at all? Functions, products, and
  endomorphism monoids are structure in the metatheory. As in WD-0 §13, a
  retention constructor built *inside* a structured metatheory does not establish
  that retention is available *to* an unstructured one.

* Why is "unmarked" prior to "marked" rather than the converse? The asymmetry of
  the medium is imported; the direction of that asymmetry is a second, separate
  import, and the paper must not let the word "unmarked" carry it silently.

The last two questions are not rhetorical, and neither is answered by the
mathematics of §4. They are the boundary of what this paper can honestly claim,
and they are now tracked as obligation **PL-WDP001-RA1-11** (§12) rather than left as
prose — parallel to WD-0's PL-WDP001-RA0-8.

and WD-1 supplies a retention constructor. WD-2 is the first paper that attempts
to build an *observer* on top of them. If observer genesis cannot be typed
honestly here, programme stop condition 2 (*circular observer*) fires and the
route from difference to a witnessed world is cut at its second joint.

**For foundations of measurement.** A large literature relativizes states,
probabilities, or facts to an observer while leaving the observer itself
primitive. This paper does the complementary and less-attempted job: it asks
what a system must already have before the word "observer" is licensed at all,
and it separates the operational levels that are earnable from the levels that
are not.

**For anyone worried about smuggling.** The paper's most portable contribution
is a discipline: **witness is not observer** (§4). Conflating a finite
differentiating receipt with a conscious subject is the standard way that
consciousness gets imported into ordinary measurement talk without anyone
noticing the import. §4 makes the separation formal, and every later section is
required to respect it.

The practical payoff is an **admission ledger**: a component-by-component
verdict on which parts of the observer packet are generated from retained
distinction and which are admitted as structure. Any construction claiming
observer genesis must be locatable on that ledger.

### 3. Setting — the observer packet

Let $D$ be a finite alphabet of **presented distinctions**, supplied by WD-1's
retention constructor and not manufactured here. Let $D^{*}$ be the finite
sequences over $D$; a sequence is a **presentation**.

**Definition (target).** An *observer packet* is the tuple

$$
\mathcal{O} \;=\; (M,\; U,\; p,\; \rho,\; \mathcal{G},\; T)
$$

with components:

| Component | Type (target) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| $M$ | finite set with distinguished $m_{0} \in M$ | retained state / memory |
| $U$ | $U : M \times D \to M$ | updates retained state from a presented distinction |
| $p$ | $p : M \to R$, $R$ finite | profile / quotient map: what the observer can tell apart |
| $\rho$ | $\rho : C \times D^{*} \to D^{*}$, $C$ finite | declared family of context actions |
| $\mathcal{G}$ | finite set of admissible report expressions | the observer's finite report grammar |
| $T$ | $T : \mathcal{G} \to \mathsf{Term}$ | terminal routing rule |

with terminal alphabet

$$
\mathsf{Term} \;=\; \{\,\mathsf{Proved},\; \mathsf{Refuted},\; \mathsf{Unresolved},\; \mathsf{Outside},\; \mathsf{Refused}\,\}.
$$

The **history update** is the free extension $U^{*} : M \times D^{*} \to M$,
defined by $U^{*}(m, \varepsilon) = m$ and $U^{*}(m, wd) = U(U^{*}(m,w), d)$.
The **induced presentation profile** is

$$
\hat{p} \;=\; p \circ U^{*}(m_{0}, -) \;:\; D^{*} \longrightarrow R .
$$

*Claim type (§3).* The observer packet is a `[DEFINITION → WDP001-RA2-C1]` — a tuple
over finite data with an initial state and a free-monoid history extension, not a
result. It is stated as a definition, never as a derived object, precisely
because writing it down presupposes the Structure the typing fence prices. The
history update $U^{*}$ and induced profile $\hat{p}$ are notation, not claims. The
two fences below are `[SCOPE FENCE]`s, not results.

**Typing fence (the paper must not let this pass).** Writing the packet down
already presupposes finite sets, a product, function spaces, a free monoid on
$D$, and a distinguished initial state $m_{0}$. Every one of those is Structure
in the sense of WD-0. The packet is therefore *not* a root construction, and §8
is the section that pays for it. Nothing in §5 may be read as deriving the
packet; §5 derives only *properties of packets*, conditional on the packet being
granted.

**Scope fence (hierarchy levels).** The programme charter defines a five-level
hierarchy O0 (detector) / O1 (retaining observer) / O2 (contextual observer) /
O3 (self-witnessing agent) / O4 (conscious subject). **This paper treats O1 and
O2 only.** O3 and O4 are the subject of WD-5
(`004_wd4_self_witnessing_and_the_awareness_boundary`) and are not addressed,
approximated, or partially established here. Any sentence in the drafted paper
that could be read as bearing on O3 or O4 is a defect to be removed, not a
result to be qualified.

### 4. Witness is not observer

This section is stated before the theorem targets because every target depends
on the separation holding.

**Definition (target).** For a profile $p$ and a context action $\rho$, the
finite tuple $(x, y, c)$ satisfying

$$
p(x) \;=\; p(y), \qquad p(\rho(c)x) \;\neq\; p(\rho(c)y)
$$

is a **context witness**: a receipt of non-congruence. It records that a
declared representation identified two cases which a declared context later
separates.

*Claim type (§4 definition).* The context witness is a `[DEFINITION]` — a finite
tuple checkable by four profile evaluations, not a theorem. The three properties
below are the reading the definition is intended to carry and must be *argued*,
not assumed.

Three properties are load-bearing and must be argued explicitly in the drafted
paper rather than assumed:

1. **A witness is finite data.** It is a tuple, checkable by evaluating $p$ four
   times. It carries no persistence, no agency, no self-model, and no awareness.

2. **A witness need not be conscious and need not be a person.** Nothing in the
   definition quantifies over subjects. A thermostat, a hash collision under a
   later-applied salt, or a compiler pass that distinguishes two programs it had
   previously identified all instantiate the form.

3. **An observer is the system; a witness is a receipt inside it.** The observer
   is the packet maintaining profile, contexts, reports, and retention. The
   witness is a finite differentiating datum that the packet's own components
   make well-typed.

**Why this is the paper's safety rail.** If witness and observer are
identified, then every non-congruence receipt anywhere in mathematics silently
posits a subject, and "measurement" acquires phenomenal content for free. The
separation is what prevents consciousness being smuggled into ordinary
measurement. The drafted paper must foreground this — it is not a caveat
appended to a definition.

*Claim type (§4 separation).* That a context witness is not conscious,
intentional, or personal is `[INTERPRETIVE → WDP001-RA2-C16]` — a definitional reading
the separation protects, not a theorem the mathematics forces.

**Typing fork (to be settled, not finessed).** The charter's witness equation is
stated for a profile $p$ applied directly to the compared objects. The packet of
§3 offers two candidate carriers for that role:

* **Fork W1 — state-level.** $x, y$ range over retained states in $M$ and $p$ is
  the packet's profile. Contexts must then act on $M$, requiring a component
  $\rho_{M} : C \times M \to M$ that §3 does not supply.

* **Fork W2 — presentation-level.** $x, y$ range over presentations in $D^{*}$
  and the witness uses $\hat{p}$, with $\rho$ acting on $D^{*}$ as declared.

W2 matches the packet as typed; W1 matches the charter's phrasing. The two are
*not* interchangeable, and the paper must commit to one and prove the comparison
map to the other, or explicitly record the mismatch as an open obligation. This
is the WD-2 analogue of WD-0's Reading A / Reading B fork, and it must not be
resolved by silent notation.

*Claim type (§4 fork).* The non-interchangeability of Fork W1 and Fork W2 is
`[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C20]`: the paper owes either the comparison map or an explicit
record of the mismatch (PL-WDP001-RA2-1). It is stated as an open obligation, not
finessed by notation.

### 5. Theorem targets

Stated as targets. None is proved here.

#### T1 — Packet well-formedness and component independence

The six components are independent: for each component $K$, there is a packet
pair agreeing on all components except $K$ whose O1/O2 classification differs.
Consequently no component of §3 is redundant, and no smaller tuple supports the
§6 classification.

*Failure mode to test first:* $\mathcal{G}$ and $T$ may be jointly eliminable
for O1/O2 classification, being needed only for the WD-3 interface. If so, T1
must be weakened to a four-component core with $(\mathcal{G}, T)$ declared as
interface rather than as classification data. **This weakening is the expected
outcome and the paper should be structured to survive it.**

*Claim type:* T1 (component independence) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C2]` — **expected to weaken
to a four-component core**; see PL-WDP001-RA2-3.

#### T2 — O1 criterion (retention)

$\mathcal{O}$ is a **retaining observer (O1)** if and only if the induced
presentation profile is non-constant:

$$
\exists\, w_{1}, w_{2} \in D^{*} \;:\; \hat{p}(w_{1}) \;\neq\; \hat{p}(w_{2}).
$$

Equivalently, some presented distinction changes a retained state in a way the
profile can still see. A packet failing this is at most O0: it maps presented
differences to reports, but no mark changes what it can report.

**Corollary target (O0/O1 boundary).** Memory size alone does not confer O1.
A packet with $\lvert M \rvert$ arbitrarily large but $U$ constant in its second
argument, or with $p$ constant, is O0.

*Claim type:* T2 (O1 criterion) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C3]` — **expected `[STANDARD]`** once
located in the Moore-machine / coalgebra literature, hence to be cited rather than
claimed (PL-WDP001-RA2-4); the O0/O1-boundary corollary `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C4]`, witnessed by
§7 E3.

#### T3 — O2 criterion (context)

$\mathcal{O}$ is a **contextual observer (O2)** if and only if the set of
context witnesses of §4 is nonempty — that is, iff $\hat{p}$ (Fork W2) fails to
be a congruence for the declared family $\rho$.

This target is the reason §4 precedes §5: it makes O2 status *exactly* the
existence of a receipt, which is a decidable finite question once $C$, $D$, and
the packet are fixed, and which quantifies over no subject.

**Corollary target (O1 $\Rightarrow$ O2 fails).** O1 does not imply O2. A packet
whose profile is already a congruence for every declared context retains marks
but exposes no previously-identified pair, and is O1 but not O2. The paper must
exhibit such a packet in §7.

*Claim type:* T3 (O2 criterion) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C5]` — depends on the PL-WDP001-RA2-1 fork
being settled (PL-WDP001-RA2-4); the O1$\not\Rightarrow$O2 corollary `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C6]`,
witnessed by §7 E1.

#### T4 — Passivity (the WD-2 analogue of Paper 000 Lemma O1)

Paper 000 §1.7 Lemma O1 (*Observer Passivity / No-Creation*,
`[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]`, Lean-backed at
`PaperFOC000/L000_05_ObserverPassivity.lean`; master-index handle
`L000_05_ObserverPassivity`) establishes for a
repair-compatible quotient observer $\mathcal{O} : Q^{1} \to Q^{1}_{\mathcal{O}}$
with induced quotient norm that

$$
\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{O}(q)) \;\leq\; \Phi(q),
$$

so a passive quotient observer may **erase or identify** obstruction but cannot
**create** it. Note the corrected reading recorded in the corpus: this is a
*no-creation* bound and places no constraint on erasure — a totally collapsing
observer is still passive.

*Claim type (import).* Paper 000 Lemma O1 (observer passivity / no-creation) is
`[STANDARD → WDP001-RA2-C7]` — **imported** from Paper 000 §1.7 at its published
`[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]` status (Lean `PaperFOC000/L000_05_ObserverPassivity.lean`);
it is cited, not re-established here, and it does **not** constrain erasure.

**WD-2 target.** The same no-creation conclusion survives the addition of
retention. Adding $M$, $U$, and history to a quotient observer does not let the
packet manufacture positive core obstruction that was not present in what was
presented.

*Claim type (target).* That no-creation survives the addition of retention
$(M, U)$ to a quotient observer is `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C8]` — the transport out of the
normed linear setting is unproved (PL-WDP001-RA2-5).

**Consequences to be drawn if T4 holds.**

* O1 and O2 status are properties of *what was presented together with the
  declared components*, not of the observer's internal elaboration. No amount of
  memory or bookkeeping upgrades an O0 packet on a presentation that carries no
  distinction.

* A witness cannot be self-generated. The receipt of §4 is exposed, not
  produced. This is what makes T3 a genesis criterion rather than a definition
  the observer can satisfy by fiat.

*Claim type (consequences).* That O1/O2 status is a joint property of what was
presented and the declared components (not of internal elaboration) is
`[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA2-C19]`, and that a witness is exposed, never self-generated, is
`[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA2-C9]` — both follow **only if** T4 (WDP001-RA2-C8) holds in the
norm-free form; until then they are conditional, not established.

**Anticipated difficulty.** Paper 000's passivity is stated for a *linear*
quotient of a normed cochain space; the WD-2 packet's $U$ is a set-level update
with no linear structure. Transporting the inequality therefore requires either
(a) a norm-free reformulation of no-creation in terms of profile fibres, or
(b) an explicit linearization with its import priced. Route (a) is preferred;
route (b) must be typed as an import if taken.

#### T5 — Observer morphisms

Define a morphism $\phi : \mathcal{O} \to \mathcal{O}'$ as a family of maps
$(\phi_{M}, \phi_{R}, \phi_{C}, \phi_{\mathcal{G}})$ commuting with update,
profile, context action, grammar, and terminal routing — in particular

$$
\phi_{M}\bigl(U(m,d)\bigr) \;=\; U'\bigl(\phi_{M}(m), d\bigr),
\qquad
p' \circ \phi_{M} \;=\; \phi_{R} \circ p .
$$

**T5a.** Observer packets and these morphisms form a category $\mathsf{Obs}$.

**T5b (morphism-level passivity).** Along a *coarsening* morphism (one whose
$\phi_{R}$ is non-injective), O2 status can be lost but not gained: witnesses
may be erased, never created. This is the packet-level counterpart of Paper 000
Lemma O2 (*Witness Erasure Bounds*), under which $\mathcal{O}(q_{w}) = 0$ makes
$w$ not a witness relative to $\mathcal{O}$.

**T5c.** O1 status behaves likewise: coarsening can collapse a retaining
observer to a detector, and no coarsening turns a detector into a retainer.

T5b and T5c together are the statement that the hierarchy is **monotone along
refinement** — which is the property WD-3 needs in order to compare presentations
from several observers.

*Claim type:* T5a (the category $\mathsf{Obs}$) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C10]` — **expected
`[STANDARD]`** (Chu spaces / classifications / coalgebra homomorphisms); T5b
(coarsening erases but cannot create O2) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C11]` — the packet-level
analogue of Paper 000 Lemma O2, itself imported, and to be checked against
Barwise–Seligman infomorphisms before claiming; T5c (coarsening collapses O1, no
coarsening raises O0) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C12]`. See PL-WDP001-RA2-6.

#### T6 — The admission ledger (load-bearing)

For each of the six components, a verdict:

| Component | Target verdict | Justification owed |
|---|---|---|
| $M$ | **admitted** | A carrier of retained states is Structure; WD-0 T3 forbids selecting it from difference |
| $U$ | **partly generated** | The *existence* of an effect of a mark is WD-1's constructor; the *function type* is admitted |
| $p$ | **admitted (choice)** | Which quotient is taken is a selection with no equivariant justification |
| $\rho$ | **admitted (declaration)** | The context family is declared; the paper must not present it as discovered |
| $\mathcal{G}$ | **admitted** | A finite grammar is imported syntax |
| $T$ | **admitted** | Terminal routing is a governance convention, not a theorem |

T6 is the analogue of WD-0's T4: it converts a negative result into a positive
classification. **The paper's contribution is the exactness of this table, not
the derivation of an observer.** If the table can be shortened by a genuine
derivation of any row, that is a stronger result and should replace the row; if
it must be lengthened, the paper is still correct and the programme is more
expensive than hoped.

*Claim type:* T6 (the admission ledger classifies each component as generated or
admitted) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C13]` — **exhaustiveness against WD-0 T4 is the hard part**
and cannot exceed the strength of WD-0's own WD0-L4 (PL-WDP001-RA2-7).

#### T7 — Non-circularity (no-go form)

If the definition of the first context witness quantifies over observer-relative
equivalence — that is, if the equivalence used to state $p(x) = p(y)$ is itself
supplied by an already-constituted observer — then no observer genesis has
occurred and programme stop condition 2 fires.

**Target.** Exhibit a definition of the witness set that is stated purely in
terms of $(D, C, \rho)$ and a declared $p$, with no appeal to a prior observer,
and prove that the §5 criteria are invariant under the choice of any equivalent
such presentation.

T7 is the paper's primary falsifier and is treated as a theorem target, not as a
disclaimer.

*Claim type:* T7 (non-circularity of the witness definition) `[OPEN → WDP001-RA2-C14]` —
its failure fires programme stop condition 2. The obligation is a proof of
*absence* over an unfixed notion of "is an observer" (support lemma WD2-L5, the
paper's real research risk); see PL-WDP001-RA2-8.

### 6. Proof strategy — three independent levels

The methodological claim, inherited from WD-0, is that the same statements
should be established three ways, and that agreement between the three is itself
evidence.

| Level | Instrument | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Finite state-machine / congruence argument in prose | Human-checkable; establishes T2, T3, T5 by direct construction |
| L2 | Lean 4 development over finite types: packet, $U^{*}$, witness predicate, morphism laws | Mechanized; guards against a definitional slip in the O1/O2 criteria |
| L3 | Executable exhaustive enumerator over small packets | Empirical; classifies every packet under a size bound and confirms the criteria and the corollaries |

**L2 placement.** Per repository convention, any Lean development belongs under
`omega_engine/lean4/` and nowhere else.

**L2 caution.** Mechanizing T2 and T3 is expected to be routine; mechanizing T4
is not, because the passivity import from Paper 000 is stated in a normed linear
setting (§5, T4). The Lean development must not silently restate T4 in whatever
form is easiest to prove. If the norm-free reformulation is not available, T4
stays ``OPEN`` and the Lean file must say so.

**L3 is the hygiene-mandated worked example** (Part A, item 4). It must produce
numbers a reader can reproduce.

### 7. Worked example (required, not yet built)

*Specification for the examples that must exist before this paper ships.*

Fix $\lvert D \rvert = 2$, $\lvert C \rvert = 1$, and enumerate all packets with
$\lvert M \rvert \leq 3$ and $\lvert R \rvert \leq 3$ over a fixed
$\rho$. Report, as a reproducible table, the counts of packets classified O0,
O1-but-not-O2, and O2. Then exhibit by hand:

**E1 — smallest O1 packet.** $M = \{m_{0}, m_{1}\}$, $D = \{a, b\}$, with
$U(m, a) = m_{0}$, $U(m, b) = m_{1}$, and $p = \mathrm{id}$. Here
$\hat{p}(a) \neq \hat{p}(b)$, so T2 is satisfied. Verify no context witness
exists for the declared $\rho$, establishing the T3 corollary that O1 does not
imply O2.

**E2 — smallest O2 packet.** A packet whose profile identifies two presentations
that the single declared context separates; print the witness tuple $(x, y, c)$
in full and verify all four profile evaluations.

**E3 — no-go case: large memory, no observer.** A packet with $\lvert M \rvert$
large and $U(m, d) = U(m, d')$ for all $d, d'$ — the update ignores what is
presented. Verify it is O0 for every $p$, every $\rho$, every $\mathcal{G}$, and
every $T$. This is the example that refutes "more memory means more observer"
and it should be the one quoted in the abstract's supporting sentence.

**E4 — no-go case: fine memory, blind profile.** $U$ injective in its second
argument but $p$ constant. O0 again, by a different mechanism than E3. The pair
E3/E4 shows the two independent ways O1 fails, which is the empirical content of
T1's component-independence claim.

These are small enough to print in full and are the paper's reproducibility
anchor.

### 8. The admission problem (the honest fork)

**This is the paper's organising tension, not a caveat.**

The packet of §3 is built from sets, products, functions, a free monoid, and an
initial state. Every one of those is Structure. By WD-0's T3 none of them is
selectable from bare non-sameness, and by WD-0's T4 each therefore represents an
injection that must appear on a ledger. It follows immediately that

> **WD-2 is not a root construction.**

*Claim type (§8 reading).* "WD-2 is not a root construction" is
`[INTERPRETIVE → WDP001-RA2-C15]` — it follows from WD-0 T3 applied to the §3 typing
fence, but the word "root" is the reading, not something the mathematics forces.
The strength of this reading is itself fenced by PL-WDP001-RA2-12 (§13).

The research task is consequently not "derive the observer" but "determine
exactly which components can be generated from retained distinction, and which
must be admitted" — the T6 table. Three postures are available and the paper
must name the one it takes.

**Posture 1 — full admission (honest, weak).** Declare all six components as
imports and prove only the classification theorems T2, T3, T5. The paper then
contributes criteria and a category, and claims no genesis at all. This is the
safe fallback and is a publishable result.

**Posture 2 — partial generation (the target).** Show that $U$'s effect and some
of $p$'s fibre structure follow from WD-1's retention constructor, and admit the
rest. The paper then contributes a *reduced* import list, and the size of the
reduction is the measurable contribution.

**Posture 3 — enriched primitive (forbidden as stated).** Define retained
distinction so that it intrinsically carries memory, comparison, and context.
This preserves the rhetoric of genesis while placing the observer inside the
definition of its own precondition.

**The paper must not present Posture 3 as a derivation.** It is the exact
analogue of WD-0's §7 strategy 2, and it is the principal self-deception this
outline exists to prevent. If the drafted paper's definitions can be
re-described as Posture 3, the correct response is to relabel the paper, not to
defend the framing.

*Claim type (§8 postures).* Posture 1 and Posture 2 are `[SCOPE FENCE]`s naming
which import list the paper commits to; Posture 3 is a `[SCOPE FENCE]` marking a
**forbidden** framing, not a claim. Which posture the drafted paper takes is a
declaration, never a derived result.

### 9. Related work (to be written — nearest neighbours)

At minimum the drafted paper must situate itself against the following. The
positioning claim is uniform: **the neighbours listed under (a) relativize to an
observer without deriving one; the neighbours under (b) supply machinery this
paper reuses.**

**(a) Observer-relative formalisms — positioning neighbours, not precedents.**

* **Relational Quantum Mechanics** (Rovelli, `https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002`).
  States and physical variables are relative to a physical system taken as
  observer. The observer is *posited* as any physical system; the quantum
  formalism is retained rather than derived from the relativization. RQM is
  therefore a positioning neighbour: it shares the relativizing move and shares
  none of this paper's genesis question.

* **QBism** (Fuchs, Mermin, Schack, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5253`).
  Quantum states are an agent's personal degrees of belief; probabilities are
  agent-relative. Again the agent is primitive and the quantum formalism —
  Hilbert space, the Born rule as a normative constraint — is retained. No
  observer or agent is constructed.

**The paper must state this explicitly and not overclaim priority.** RQM and
QBism make states or probabilities observer- or agent-relative *while retaining
the quantum formalism*. They do not derive observers. They are neighbours in
posture and are **not** precedents for the genesis question, and this paper is
not an improvement on them — it is asking a different question, at a level below
where they begin.

*Claim type (§9a).* The non-claim of priority over, or improvement on, RQM or
QBism is `[INTERPRETIVE]` positioning keyed to non-claim WD2-N10; RQM and QBism
are cited as posture neighbours, not as sources of results this paper uses.

**(b) Machinery neighbours — likely sources of `[STANDARD]` rows.**

* **Chu spaces and interface/Chu-style dualities.** A two-sided object of
  points and states with an evaluation map is structurally close to the
  $(M, p, R)$ fragment of the packet. Expect the category-theoretic content of
  T5a to be standard here; cite rather than claim.

* **Barwise–Seligman information flow.** Classifications (tokens, types,
  a support relation) and infomorphisms are a direct precedent for the observer
  morphisms of T5 and for the token/type separation that §4's witness/observer
  distinction reproduces. The drafted paper must check whether T5b is already
  available as an infomorphism result.

* **Epistemic logic and agent models** (Kripke models, interpreted systems).
  These formalize what an agent knows given an indistinguishability relation.
  The relation is given, not generated — which is precisely the WD-2 gap. Useful
  for vocabulary and for the O1/O2 distinction's relation to knowledge
  operators; not a source of genesis results.

* **Coalgebraic state-based systems** (Moore and Mealy machines, bisimulation,
  final coalgebras). The packet's $(M, U, p)$ fragment is a Moore machine, and
  T2 is a statement about the reachable behaviour map. Expect T2, T5a, and the
  observational-equivalence content of T5c to be `[STANDARD]` in this setting.

* **Anomalon Paper 000** — Lemma O1 (observer passivity / no-creation), Lemma O2
  (witness erasure), the context-witness definition, and the lift-contract
  grammar. Imported with its `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]` status intact.

* **Anomalon Paper 000b** (*The Observer Object*) — the corpus's existing
  consolidation of finite evaluation structures; the drafted paper must
  reconcile the §3 packet against it rather than introduce a rival object.

* **Anomalon WD-0 and WD-1** — the injection classification and the retention
  constructor this paper consumes.

*Claim type (§9b).* The category-theoretic content of T5a in the Chu-space
setting, and the Moore-machine / coalgebra content of T2 and the
observational-equivalence part of T5c, are **expected `[STANDARD]`** (keyed to
WDP001-RA2-C10, WDP001-RA2-C3, WDP001-RA2-C12) — to be cited in that literature rather than claimed as
results of this paper; T5b is to be checked against Barwise–Seligman
infomorphisms before it is claimed (WDP001-RA2-C11). The Paper 000, 000b, WD-0, and WD-1
entries are imported at their published status (Paper 000's lemmas
`[STANDARD → WDP001-RA2-C7]`), not re-established here.

**Anticipated referee objection, to be answered in-paper:** *"Your observer is
just a Moore machine with a quotient, and your witness is just a failure of
congruence."* The correct response is that this is exactly the claim. The
contribution is not new machinery; it is (i) the T6 admission ledger's
exactness, (ii) the proof that this machinery earns O1 and O2 and *no more*, and
(iii) the §4 separation that stops the machinery from being narrated as a
subject. The paper must be structured so that a reader who grants the machinery
as standard on first reading still finds the contribution intact.

### 11. What this paper does NOT claim

$$
\mathrm{Fact}(W_{\mathcal{O}})
\;=\;
\bigl\{\,
\phi \subseteq \operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}}
\;:\;
\phi \text{ is } \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}\text{-stable and }
\mathcal{G}_{\mathcal{O}}\text{-expressible}

Different observers may have different quotients and therefore genuinely

$$

*Claim type (§3.2).* $\mathrm{Fact}(W_{\mathcal{O}})$ is a `[DEFINITION]`
(provisional, PL-WDP001-RA3-1). The inherited passivity bound
$\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{O}(q)) \le \Phi(q)$ is `[STANDARD → WDP001-RA3-C1]` —

Target: $\phi$ is stable across $\mathfrak{O}$ iff

**This is the paper's most dangerous target** — see §9 and the fence in §9.2.

*Claim type (T3).* The $k$-redundancy characterization of stability

### 5. The disagreement taxonomy (required deliverable)

When $\mathcal{O}_A$ and $\mathcal{O}_B$ report incompatibly, the drafted paper
must classify the cause. Five classes are proposed; exhaustiveness is **not**
assumed.

| Id | Class | Signature | Diagnostic receipt |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Profile / quotient divergence** | $\ker p_A \neq \ker p_B$; each report is correct in its own world | A pair identified by one profile and separated by the other |
| D2 | **Context divergence** | $\mathcal{C}_A \neq \mathcal{C}_B$; disagreement disappears on the common context sub-family | The context $c$ present in one admissible set and absent from the other |
| D3 | **Grammar divergence** | $\mathcal{G}_A$ and $\mathcal{G}_B$ are not intertranslatable; the reports are not comparable propositions at all | A failure of the declared translation on a specific report token |
| D4 | **Genuine witnessed non-congruence** | A context witness $(x,y,c)$ survives both profiles | The finite triple with $p(x)=p(y)$, $p(\rho(c)x)\neq p(\rho(c)y)$ |
| D5 | **Evaluator defect** | The evaluators do not factorize across the declared boundary | The LGGR residual $\Delta_E \neq 0$ (Papers 020 / 023 / 025) |

Two obligations attach to this table and neither is optional.

1. **Decidability.** Target: the class is decidable from receipts, and the five
   classes are mutually exclusive. Both halves are `[CONJECTURE]`. D2 and D3 in
   particular are suspected to overlap, since an inadmissible context and an
   inexpressible report may be the same phenomenon under two descriptions.

2. **D5 is imported, not derived.** D5 is the LGGR evaluator gluing defect. This
   paper cites it. If the drafted paper finds itself proving a composition
   theorem for $\Delta_E$, it has left its own subject and must stop — that
   result already exists at Paper 020 under hypotheses G1–G3, with G1 itself a
   declared, not-yet-proven EPK hypothesis.

*Claim type (§5).* The taxonomy table D1–D5 is a `[DEFINITION]` — a proposed
classification whose exhaustiveness is **not** assumed. That D1–D5 is exhaustive
is `[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA3-C11]`; that the five classes are mutually exclusive and
decidable from receipts is `[CONJECTURE → WDP001-RA3-C12]` (the D2/D3 overlap is already
suspected; PL-WDP001-RA3-5, support lemma WD3-L5). Class D5 (evaluator defect

### 8. The no-view-from-nowhere fence (the honest fork)

Every construction in §4 requires either a declared common refinement
$\mathcal{O}_{AB}$ (T1), a declared coverage (T2), a declared independent
sub-family (T3), or a declared class $\mathfrak{O}$ (T4). None of these is
derived, and the paper must not pretend otherwise. Three readings are available
and the paper must commit:

**Reading A — declared core.** Admit a common core $Q^1$ of which all observers
are quotients. Then Paper 000 O3 applies directly and the descent problem is
comparatively easy. **The cost is that the core is a view from nowhere admitted
by hypothesis**, and every "objectivity" result is then objectivity relative to a
posited absolute presentation. This is honest only if said out loud.

**Reading B — declared coverage, no core.** Admit no common core; admit instead a
coverage on $\mathsf{ObsPkt}$ and ask whether $\mathsf{Fact}$ descends. Objectivity
becomes a genuinely relational property, and the hard work moves into showing the
coverage is not secretly a core in disguise.

**Reading C — no common refinement.** Some observer families admit no common
refinement at all. Then the honest terminal is `OpenObstruction`, and the correct
report is that the observers present incommensurable worlds — not that one is
mistaken.

**The paper must not present Reading A as Reading B.** Declaring a core and then
announcing that objectivity has been derived relationally is the principal
self-deception this outline exists to prevent, and it is the exact analogue of
WD-0's warning against presenting an enriched primitive as a derivation.

*Claim type (§8).* Which of Reading A (declared core), Reading B (declared
coverage, no core), or Reading C (no common refinement) the drafted paper commits
to is a **declaration**, not a derived result; it is owed by PL-WDP001-RA3-2. The
overarching reading "objectivity is a theorem about compatible witnesses, never a
view from nowhere" is `[INTERPRETIVE → WDP001-RA3-C15]` (the mathematics permits Reading

#### T3 — Rank-truncation no-go

> Let $1 \le r < n = \dim_k N$. Then the rank class $\mathcal A^{\le r}$ has no
> initial object.

*Proof strategy.* Let $q : N \to U$ be a candidate with $\dim U \le r$. Existence
of a factorization $L q = \mathcal O$ forces $\ker q \subseteq \ker \mathcal O$.
Since $\dim \ker q \ge n - r \ge 1$, choose $0 \ne x \in \ker q$ and a functional
$\phi \in N^{*}$ with $\phi(x) \ne 0$. Then $\phi : N \to k$ lies in
$\mathcal A^{\le r}$ because $1 \le r$, and $\ker q \not\subseteq \ker \phi$, so
$\phi$ does not factor through $q$. Hence $q$ is not initial. $\square$

This is the paper's candidate **no-go**, and per the programme charter a proved
no-go is a successful outcome. Its scope must be stated exactly: it says that a
*bandwidth-limited* observer class admits no universal member, not that
observation is impossible.

*Claim type (T3).* The rank-class no-go (for $1 \le r < n$, $\mathcal A^{\le r}$
has no initial object) is `[OPEN → WDP001-RA4-C4]`; the functional-separation strategy
above must be written out (support lemma WD4-L2), and its edgewise generalization

#### 6.1 The model

The transverse-field Ising ring on $L$ sites, $L \in \{4, 6\}$:

$$
\mathcal H = \bigotimes_{v \in \Lambda} \mathbb C^2,
\qquad
\Lambda = \mathbb Z / L\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H = -J \sum_{v \in \Lambda} \sigma^z_v \sigma^z_{v+1} \; - \; h \sum_{v \in \Lambda} \sigma^x_v ,
$$

with $J, h$ fixed rational numbers reported in the paper. The generator is the
Liouvillian $A(\rho) = -i[H, \rho]$, acting on the real vector space of traceless
Hermitian operators. The manifold $M$ is the set of full-rank product states, and
$\Pi$ is the mean-field retraction of T7. $\Pi$ is smooth (multilinear in $\rho$)
and satisfies $\Pi|_M = \operatorname{id}_M$, so §3.1 applies.

**Non-collapse precondition.** The chosen base point $m$ must satisfy
$n_m \ne 0$; this excludes $J = 0$ and excludes product eigenstates. The paper
must report the chosen $m$ and the computed $\lVert n_m \rVert$ in a declared norm.

**Anti-tuning obligation (binding, not advisory).** The failure mode to guard
against is not that the computation is hard — at $L = 6$, $R = 2$ it is small —
but that the declaration is tuned until the verdict is pretty. The paper
therefore **binds itself** to the following procedure, and a certificate produced
in any other order is not admissible under PL-WDP001-RA4-7:

1. Fix $(L, R, J, h, m)$, the differentials $(d_0, d_1)$, **and the norms** — the
   norm on each stalk $W_\sigma$, the induced product norm on each $C^p$, and the
   inner product used by the least-norm solve — **before** running level L3.

2. Publish the §6.5 prediction first.
3. Report whichever of the three verdicts comes out.

Naming the norms is a *second* declaration on top of the observation system, and
it must be named and priced rather than absorbed: Paper 001h §2 explicitly
declines to assume an inner product, so a least-norm solve does not inherit one.
The three-way verdict itself is **independent** of all three choices — the gates
$d_1 d_0 = 0$, $d_1\omega = 0$, $\omega \in \operatorname{im} d_0$, and
$\dim H^1$ are norm-free linear algebra — so the norms may be reported for
magnitude only and may never be what decides the verdict.

#### 6.2 The carrier

The Vietoris–Rips complex $\mathrm{VR}_R(\Lambda, d)$ of the ring metric $d$ at
declared scale $R$: cells are subsets $\sigma \subseteq \Lambda$ of diameter
$\le R$. This family is closed under faces, which is what makes the coboundary
well-typed — a nearest-neighbour bond graph alone is **not** closed under the
faces of its triples and cannot carry $d_1$.

**Cell census, stated explicitly because it is easy to get wrong.** At $L = 6$,
$R = 2$ the ring metric is $d(u,v) = \min(\lvert u - v\rvert, 6 - \lvert u - v\rvert)$,
so a subset has diameter $\le 2$ exactly when it contains no antipodal pair
($\{0,3\}$, $\{1,4\}$, $\{2,5\}$). This gives $6$ vertices; $12$ edges (the six
pairs at distance $1$ and the six at distance $2$); and **eight** 2-cells — of
the $\binom{6}{3} = 20$ triples, the $3 \times 4 = 12$ containing an antipodal
pair are excluded, leaving the six *consecutive* triples $\{v, v+1, v+2\}$ **and
the two alternating triples $\{0,2,4\}$ and $\{1,3,5\}$, whose pairwise ring
distances are all $2 \le R$**. The alternating triples are the ones a reader
drops; they are cells, and omitting them under-counts $\dim C^2$.

Stalks are the connected-correlation spaces $W_\sigma$ of traceless Hermitian
operators on $\bigotimes_{v \in \sigma} \mathcal H_v$, so $\dim_{\mathbb R} W_\sigma = 4^{\lvert\sigma\rvert} - 1$,
with co-restriction maps $\iota_{\sigma \subseteq \tau} : X \mapsto X \otimes I$.
This is a **cellular sheaf** on $\mathrm{VR}_R$, not a constant coefficient system.

#### 6.3 The complex

$$
C^p := \prod_{\substack{\sigma \in \mathrm{VR}_R \\ \lvert\sigma\rvert = p+1}} W_\sigma ,
$$

$$
(d_0 s)_{\{u,v\}} = \iota(s_v) - \iota(s_u),
\qquad
(d_1 c)_{\{u,v,w\}} = \iota(c_{\{v,w\}}) - \iota(c_{\{u,w\}}) + \iota(c_{\{u,v\}}),
$$

for $u < v < w$ in a declared total order on $\Lambda$. The chain law

$$
(d_1 d_0 s)_{\{u,v,w\}} = (s_w - s_v) - (s_w - s_u) + (s_v - s_u) = 0
$$

is expected to hold because the co-restrictions compose strictly, discharging the
hypothesis of Paper 001h Proposition 4.2 — **but it must be verified numerically
as well as symbolically**, since the tensor-order convention is a place where a
sign or an ordering slip would be invisible in prose.

Observation maps: $\mathcal O_{\sigma,m} := $ the T7a projection $N \to \mathcal K_\sigma$
followed by the inclusion $\mathcal K_\sigma \hookrightarrow W_\sigma$.

#### 6.4 What must be computed and reported

| Quantity | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| $\dim C^0, \dim C^1, \dim C^2$; cell counts per degree | Reproducibility anchor. For $L = 6$, $R = 2$: $18$, $12 \times 15 = 180$, $8 \times 63 = 504$ (the eight 2-cells are enumerated in §6.2) |
| $\operatorname{rank} d_0$, $\operatorname{rank} d_1$, and $\lVert d_1 d_0 \rVert$ | Verifies the complex before any class is named |
| $\lVert n_m \rVert$ and $\lVert \omega^{\mathfrak O}(m) \rVert$ | Non-collapse evidence (001h §8.1 item 5) |
| $\lVert d_1 \omega^{\mathfrak O}(m) \rVert$ | The closure gate |
| Residual of the least-norm solve $d_0 s = \omega^{\mathfrak O}(m)$ | The exactness gate; report the witness $s$ or certify non-membership |
| $\dim H^1 = \dim \ker d_1 - \operatorname{rank} d_0$ | The class is meaningless without this |

The verdict is then exactly one of three, and **all three are publishable**:

1. $d_1\omega = 0$ and $\omega \in \operatorname{im} d_0$ — an **exactness
   certificate**, with witness $s$ printed;

2. $d_1\omega = 0$ and $\omega \notin \operatorname{im} d_0$ — a
   **non-exactness certificate**, with the class and $\dim H^1$ printed;

3. $d_1\omega \ne 0$ — an **active closure-failure certificate**: no $H^1$ class
   exists in this complex, which is Paper 001h's own falsifier fired honestly.

**Topological triviality proves nothing here.** Because the stalks vary, $H^1$ of
this cellular sheaf is not determined by the homotopy type of $\mathrm{VR}_R$;
the ring-versus-interval intuition ($S^1$ has a loop, an interval does not) is a
heuristic for *constant* coefficients only. The paper must compute, and must
explicitly refuse the citation shortcut.

*Claim type (§6).* Everything in §6 is a certificate **specification**, not a
computed result. That the declared cellular sheaf on $\mathrm{VR}_R(\Lambda, d)$

#### 6.5 Predicted difficulty, recorded in advance

At $R = 1$ the observation family sees only pair cells, and Paper 001h §6.1's
even-parity example transports to this lattice: a state with vanishing pair
cumulants and a nonzero triple cumulant defeats the non-collapse test. The
outline therefore **predicts** that $R = 1$ will fail Paper 001h's PL-001H-7 and
that $R \ge 2$ will be required. Recording the prediction before computing is
what makes the eventual number evidence rather than decoration.

**What $R = 1$ degenerates to, stated so the prediction is not misread.** On
either ring ($L = 4$ or $L = 6$), no triple has diameter $\le 1$ — that would
require three mutually adjacent sites, and neither ring contains a triangle. So
at $R = 1$ the carrier has **no 2-cells at all**: $C^2 = 0$ and $d_1 = 0$
identically, every cochain is trivially closed, and verdict form 3 of §6.4 (an
**active closure-failure certificate**) is *structurally unreachable* at $R = 1$.
The predicted $R = 1$ failure is therefore a **non-collapse failure only** — the
observation family cannot see the obstruction — and must not be reported, or
read, as a closure verdict. A vanishing $\lVert d_1\omega^{\mathfrak O}(m)\rVert$
at $R = 1$ is a statement about the empty cell set, not evidence of closure.

**Truncation.** Cells above radius $R$ are omitted. Bounding the omitted
contribution is Paper 001h's PL-001H-5; a Lieb–Robinson bound for $H$ is the
expected instrument, giving short-time control on correlations beyond radius $R$.
That import is `[STANDARD]` as a statement about the model and **not** standard
as a statement about this cell complex; the binding is an obligation, not a
citation.

### 5. Proof strategy — three independent levels

The methodological claim, inherited from WD-0, is that the same statement should
be established three ways and that agreement between the three is itself
evidence.

| L1 | Algebraic identity chase in prose, over additive groups only | Human-checkable; establishes T3, T4, T5 as *identities*, not estimates |
| L2 | Lean 4 development over finite additive groups, extending 001a's `TripleRestriction`/`QuadRestriction` skeleton with a duration-action structure | Mechanized; guards against a sign slip in L1, which is the characteristic failure mode of mixed-differential arguments |

**L2 placement.** Per repository convention, any Lean development belongs under

#### 6.1 Spatial fixture — the $4$-chain

Take $P=\{a<b<c<d\}$, the $4$-element chain, with scalar fibers
$F(x)=\mathbb R$. The comparable-pair restriction data is then $6$ scalars
$\rho_{xy}$ (one for each of $(a,b),(a,c),(a,d),(b,c),(b,d),(c,d)$), the strict
triples number $4$, and the quadruples number $1$. Index-set sizes:

$$
\lvert K_{\bullet}\rvert=(4,6,4,1).
$$

Restrict each scalar to $\{-1,0,1\}$ and enumerate all

$$
3^{6}=729
$$

restriction systems. For each, compute the $4$ curvatures
$K_{z,y,x}=\rho_{xy}\rho_{yz}-\rho_{xz}$ and the single Bianchi value
$d_{2}K$ from 001a Theorem 3.4. **Report the partition of the $729$ into:**
flat ($K\equiv0$), curved-with-Bianchi ($K\not\equiv0$, $d_{2}K\equiv0$), and
curved-without-Bianchi ($d_{2}K\not\equiv0$). The three counts must sum to $729$.

**$d_{2}K$ is a function of the section, and the quantifier must be declared.**
001a Theorem 3.4 gives
$(d_{2}K(s))_{d,c,b,a}=K_{c,b,a}(r_{d,c}s(d))-K_{c,b,a}(s(c))$, which depends on
$s$; "$d_{2}K\equiv0$" is therefore not a property of a restriction system until
one says *for which sections*. The checker must read it as **for every section
$s\in C^{0}$** — the same universal quantifier 001a uses in Theorem 3.2 and
Corollary 3.3 — and must say so in its output. Under that reading the classes are
disjoint and exhaustive (every flat system satisfies the Bianchi condition
vacuously), so the three counts do sum to $729$ as claimed. A checker that
silently evaluates $d_{2}K$ at one convenient section is measuring something else
and its partition is not the partition specified here.

**Reach caveat — the $4$-chain's Bianchi test sees one triple, not four.** On the
$4$-chain there is exactly one comparable quadruple $a<b<c<d$, and in the scalar
case Theorem 3.4's right-hand side is
$\kappa_{c,b,a}\bigl(\rho_{cd}\,s(d)-s(c)\bigr)$: the curvatures of the other
three triples $\{a,b,d\}$, $\{a,c,d\}$, $\{b,c,d\}$ **cancel identically** out of
$d_{2}K$. So the Bianchi test on this fixture is a test on the bottom triple
$a<b<c$ alone, and a system may be curved on the other three triples while
passing it. This does not affect T6's necessity half — a $3$-chain has no
quadruple at all — but it does bound what the fixture certifies, and the draft
must not report "Bianchi holds" as though all four curvatures had been tested.
Whether a larger $P$ is needed to exercise the Bianchi condition on every triple
is part of `PL-WDP001-RA5-8`.

The third count is the paper's headline number. If it is nonzero, the curved
alternative of §7 is *itself* refuted on those systems, and the honest verdict
descends to $A_\infty$-style higher coherence or to nothing. **The count is not
stated here**: producing it is `PL-WDP001-RA5-9`'s job, and an outline that quotes a
number it has not computed is exactly the defect this pipeline exists to catch.

By T6 this is the smallest fixture that can distinguish bicomplex from curved:
a $3$-chain has no comparable quadruple, so its Bianchi test is vacuous and it
cannot tell a curved-but-closed system from a curved-and-open one.

### 7. The three honest outcomes (the structural fork)

The bicomplex is one of three possible verdicts, and the corpus has so far
written as though it were the only one.

**Outcome A — Bicomplex.** All three identities hold. Then, **in the
anticommuting convention declared in §3.4**, the total differential
$D=d_{\mathrm{sp}}+d_{\mathrm{hist}}$ satisfies $D^{2}=0$, the total complex
exists, and the two filtration spectral sequences become available. (If instead
the mixed law is stated in commuting form, the total differential is
$D=d_{\mathrm{sp}}+\tilde d_{\mathrm{hist}}$ with the Koszul twist
$\tilde d_{\mathrm{hist}}=(-1)^{p}d_{\mathrm{hist}}$. The two presentations are
equivalent — that equivalence is `PL-WDP001-RA5-2` — but the twist belongs to exactly
one of them, and applying it to an already-anticommuting pair reintroduces the
cross term. This is the sign slip §5 names as the characteristic failure mode of
mixed-differential arguments, and it is why L2 exists.)
*Expected to hold only on the intersection of the spatial flat locus, the
history-flat locus, and $\Lambda\equiv0$* — three independent conditions, each
already known or expected to fail generically.

**Outcome B — Curved complex.** Each square equals its own curvature,

$$
d_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}=K\cdot(-),
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{hist}}^{2}=\mathcal H\cdot(-),
$$

with the mixed law holding. This is the shape of a **curved dg-structure**, with
$K$ and $\mathcal H$ as curvature elements in degree $2$ of their respective
axes; the $A_\infty$ reading places them as the $m_{0}$ term and demands the
Stasheff identities in place of $d^{2}=0$.

> **Outcome B is not a soft landing, and the draft must not sell it as one.**
> A curved dg-structure has **no cohomology**: if $d^{2}\ne0$ then
> $\operatorname{im}d\not\subseteq\ker d$, the quotient $\ker d/\operatorname{im}d$
> is not defined, and every invariant the bicomplex was wanted for — including
> both spectral sequences — is unavailable. Outcome B costs the entire
> cohomological apparatus and returns a bookkeeping structure in exchange.

Moreover Outcome B has its own admission gate. A curved dg-algebra requires the
**Bianchi condition** $d(\text{curvature})=0$. On the spatial axis that is
exactly 001a Theorem 3.4's $d_{2}K$, and 001a states plainly that $d_{2}K$ is
controlled by lower curvature applied to the top-edge defect and is *not
automatically zero*. So Outcome B is conditional on a vanishing that the parent
paper explicitly declines to assert. §6.1's third count decides it on the
fixture.

**Outcome C — Neither.** Some Bianchi condition fails, or the mixed law fails.
Then there is no bicomplex and no curved dg-structure, and the honest
continuation is either an $A_\infty$/homotopy-coherent structure with an infinite
tower of higher operations — each of which must be *constructed and typed*, not
gestured at — or the conclusion that spatial and history defects do not assemble
and must be carried as two separate diagnostics.

**Outcome C is a legitimate terminal for this paper.** If it is the verdict, WD-5
ships as a refutation of 001H Architecture 7.2, WD-5 receives no history
differential, and the programme records the obstruction rather than routing
around it. The draft must not treat Outcome C as a failure to be avoided by
weakening the hypotheses until something closes.

**The self-deception this outline exists to prevent** is the mirror of WD-0's:
there, presenting an enriched primitive as a derivation; here, presenting a
weakened structure as the discovered one. Concretely — declaring
$\Lambda\equiv0$, or restricting to the flat locus, and then reporting that the
bicomplex exists. It exists *there*; the question was whether it exists.

*Claim type (§7).* Outcomes A, B, and C are ``OPEN`` as verdicts — the paper
predicts but does not establish which obtains. That a curved dg-structure **has
no cohomology** is `[STANDARD → WDP001-RA5-C14]`, elementary and cited to Positselski,
not a WD-5 finding. That Outcome B carries its own Bianchi admission gate is
`[OPEN → WDP001-RA5-C13]`: 001a states $d_{2}K$ is not automatically zero, and the
consequence *for Outcome B* is WD-5's own inference from that statement. The
closing self-deception paragraph is `[INTERPRETIVE]` — a methodological reading,
which the mathematics does not force.

**Cochain-realization standing (read this before any $d$, $H$, or "exact"
below).** Nothing in this paper establishes that $C^{\bullet,\bullet}$ is a
complex. T3 and T4 are ``OPEN`` and both are *expected refuted*, so until they
are settled the words **exact**, **closed**, **coboundary**, **cocycle**,
**$H^{n}$**, **page**, and **spectral sequence** have no referent here and are
used only as **targets** or as descriptions of what would become available under
Outcome A. $K$ is 001a's curvature *cochain*, not a class; $\mathcal K_{\Pi,A}$
is 001M's *coefficient*, fenced by 001M-G3 from being a cochain at all; and
placing either in a bidegree does not promote it. The bigraded carrier is a
**declared product of stalks**, which is all 001H ever built.

### 13. Genesis-bias audit (mandatory before drafting)

Per the programme charter and the repository's Genesis Bias doctrine, this paper
must answer, for its own construction:

* Where did the finite poset $P$ come from, and what makes its elements
  *regions* rather than unrelated indices? 001a declares it and does not
  construct it; WD-5 inherits the admission and its cost.

* Where did the duration monoid $D$ come from? A history axis presupposes
  durations that compose, which presupposes an additive structure on time that
  is imported, not derived.

* What supplies the **orientation** of the history axis? $d_{\mathrm{hist}}$
  raises $q$; the direction of that raising is a declared asymmetry, and WD-0's
  T4 injection classification requires it to be located on that list.

* What licenses the ambient additive-group structure in which "composite minus
  direct" is even expressible? Subtraction is not free; both $K$ and
  $\mathcal H$ are differences, and a carrier without subtraction has neither.

* **What licenses the bigrading itself?** Writing $C^{p,q}$ presupposes that the
  index category factors as spatial $\times$ history. If the true index category
  does not so factor, there is no bigrading, no bicomplex question, and §4 is
  malformed rather than false. This is the deepest genesis bias in the paper and
  it is *inherited from 001H unexamined*. The draft must either exhibit the
  factorisation or state that it is assumed.

* What supplies the Banach fibers, the bounded generator, and the $C^{3}$
  retraction? All are continuum antecedents imported from 001a and 001M, priced
  there, and re-priced here. "Finite" in this paper applies to the index sets,
  never to the fibers.

The fifth question is not rhetorical. A bicomplex proved *inside* an index
category that was assumed to factor does not establish that spatial and history
degrees are separable in whatever the carrier is meant to represent. The paper
must state this boundary explicitly rather than let it pass.
