> This proposal repairs a type error in WD-5. O0 is a degenerate base case, not
> a member of a containment chain, and O1/O2/O3 become comparable only after
> reachability and observable-compatibility witnesses are declared.

1. Payoff


``OPEN`` The intended result is a non-vacuous three-level operational chain

\[
\mathrm{O3}^{\star}\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{O2}^{\star}\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{O1},
\]

with O0 stated separately. The stars denote load-bearing compatibility
hypotheses, not editorial decoration. The paper must also give finite separating
packets or stop calling the predicates a hierarchy.

### Why a researcher should care

Capability ladders are attractive and dangerous. If adjacent levels inspect
different observables, an implication between their labels says nothing. If a
degenerate no-retention case is conjoined with retained observation, the ladder
becomes empty. WD-4 turns WD-5's self-diagnosed countermodels into a small,
checkable repair packet instead of letting later consciousness or cosmology
claims inherit an invalid hierarchy.

3. Frozen packet and reachability


`[DEFINITION]` Begin with WD-5's finite packet

\[
(S,\Sigma,u,r,s_0,\Pi,p,\Lambda,C,\rho)
\]

and, for O3, its declared receipt data
\((R,k,\mathrm{rec},\pi,\kappa,p_{\mathrm{self}})\).

`[DEFINITION]` The reachable set is

\[
## \operatorname{Reach}(s_0)

\{u_w(s_0):w\in\Sigma^\ast\}.
\]

All repaired witness predicates below quantify over reachable states and require
their context images to be reachable. This condition is declared; it is not
inferred from the existence of \(\rho\).

4. O0 is outside the chain


`[DEFINITION]` Keep the two WD-5 readings separate:

* \(\mathrm{O0}_{\mathrm{struct}}\): \(|S|=1\);
* \(\mathrm{O0}_{\mathrm{beh}}\): every reachable state has the same report at
  each input.

`[PROVEN]` The structural condition implies the behavioural condition.

*Proof.* On a singleton carrier any two reachable states are equal, so their
reports agree at every input. \(\square\)

`[WORKED EXAMPLE]` The converse fails for a two-state packet with a constant
report.

`[SCOPE FENCE]` Neither O0 predicate is below O1 in a containment hierarchy.
O1 asserts report-visible retained variation; O0 asserts its structural or
behavioural degeneracy. WD-4 adopts WD-5's R3 repair and places O0 outside the
chain.

5. Repaired operational predicates


### O1 — report-visible retention

`[DEFINITION]` O1 holds when there exist reachable states \(a,b\) and an input
\(\sigma\) with

\[
r(a,\sigma)\ne r(b,\sigma).
\]

This is WD-5's Myhill–Nerode-index condition in its finite witness form.

### O2-star — reachable contextual witnessing with report refinement

`[DEFINITION]` The report **refines** \(p\) on reachable states when

\[
p(a)\ne p(b)
\Longrightarrow
\exists\sigma\in\Sigma,\;
r(a,\sigma)\ne r(b,\sigma).
\]

`[DEFINITION]` O2-star holds when report refinement is declared and there are
\(x,y,c\) such that \(x,y,\rho(c)x,\rho(c)y\) are reachable and

\[
p(x)=p(y),
\qquad
p(\rho(c)x)\ne p(\rho(c)y).
\]

### O3-star — self-witnessing packet with a common observable

`[DEFINITION]` O3-star requires WD-5's A1–A4 and all of:

1. A4's witness and both context images are reachable;
2. \(\ker p=\ker p_{\mathrm{self}}\) on the reachable carrier and the declared
   context images;

3. the report refines \(p\) on the reachable carrier.

`[SCOPE FENCE]` These conditions repair predicate transport. They do not prove
that A1–A4 are an adequate or unique formalization of self-witnessing.

6. Theorem targets


### WD4-T1 — repaired containment

``OPEN`` Every O3-star packet is O2-star, and every O2-star packet is O1.
The intended proof transports A4 across kernel equality, then transports the
resulting \(p\)-difference across report refinement.

### WD4-T2 — non-emptiness

``OPEN`` Complete WD-5's six-state A1–A4 witness to the full frozen packet and
verify every O3-star condition. If no such completion exists, weaken or reject
O3-star rather than retaining an empty level.

### WD4-T3 — strict separation

``OPEN`` Exhibit:

1. an O1 packet that is not O2-star;
2. an O2-star packet that is not O3-star.

Strictness means proper containment after T1 is established, not merely two
unrelated labels with separating examples.

### WD4-T4 — blind-spot inheritance

`[STANDARD + citation]` WD-5's T6a candidate is a one-line consequence
of A4 once the packet is well typed. WD-4 does not claim it as new. The
reachability-restricted T6b remains WD-5's open obligation.

7. Worked compatibility example


`[WORKED EXAMPLE]` Let

\[
S=\{x,y,z\},\quad p(x)=p(y)=0,\quad p(z)=1,
\]

and let one context satisfy

\[
\rho(c)x=x,\qquad \rho(c)y=z,\qquad \rho(c)z=z.
\]

Take \(\Sigma=\{a,b\}\), \(s_0=x\), and

\[
u_a(x)=y,\qquad u_b(x)=z,\qquad
u_\sigma(y)=y,\qquad u_\sigma(z)=z
\quad(\sigma\in\Sigma).
\]

Thus \(x,y,z\) are all reachable. With
\(r_{\mathrm{flat}}(s,\sigma)=0\), the contextual witness exists but O1 fails:
this is WD-5's countermodel. With
\(r_{\mathrm{ref}}(s,\sigma)=p(s)\), report refinement holds, the images
\(x,z\) are report-distinct, and the same reachable witness supplies O2-star
and O1. The example shows exactly which compatibility condition performs the
repair; it does not establish O3-star.

8. Falsifiability


WD4-T1 fails if a fully typed O3-star packet is not O2-star or a fully typed
O2-star packet is not O1. The proposed hierarchy fails as useful mathematics if
either starred class is empty, or if no finite proper-containment witnesses
exist. The interpretation fails if A1–A4 can be shown irrelevant to a defended
operational meaning of self-witnessing; that would not prove or refute
consciousness.
