3. Setting


### 3.1 The observer-relative world

Carried forward unchanged from WD-3. For an observer $\mathcal{O}$ with profile
map $p_{\mathcal{O}}$,

$$
W_{\mathcal{O}}
\;=\;
\bigl(
\operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}},\;
C_{\mathcal{O}},\;
H_{\mathcal{O}},\;
\Vdash_{\mathcal{O}},\;
\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}
\bigr),
$$

where $\operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}}$ is the presented image, $C_{\mathcal{O}}$
the admissible contexts, $H_{\mathcal{O}}$ the retained histories (supplied by
the WD-1 retention constructor), $\Vdash_{\mathcal{O}}$ the witness relation, and
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}$ the report grammar.

**None of the five components is derived in this paper.** Each is imported from a
named prior layer, and §7 records what happens to WD-7 if that layer fails.

### 3.2 The claim grammar

$\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}$ is a declared formal language whose atoms are report
classes in $\operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}}$, context actions in
$C_{\mathcal{O}}$, and finite retained histories in $H_{\mathcal{O}}$. Write
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ for its **well-typed finite claims**:
claims of bounded syntactic depth over finitely many atoms.

The restriction to $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ is a hypothesis of
every theorem below, not a convenience. Dropping it is the fastest route to a
false theorem; see §9.1.

### 3.3 The status set

$$
\mathrm{Status}
\;=\;
\{\texttt{Proved},\;\texttt{Refuted},\;\texttt{Underdetermined},\;
\texttt{OutsideProfile},\;\texttt{Refused}\}.
$$

Each status is **witness-typed**: it is not a label but a pair (label, finite
receipt of the label's own type).

| Status | Required receipt | Informal reading |
|---|---|---|
| `Proved` | A finite derivation from the declared evidence base | The evidence settles it affirmatively |
| `Refuted` | A finite counter-witness in $\Vdash_{\mathcal{O}}$ | The evidence settles it negatively |
| `Underdetermined` | Two admissible extensions of the evidence base disagreeing on the claim | Expressible, witnessable in principle, not settled by what is retained |
| `OutsideProfile` | A proof that no context action in $C_{\mathcal{O}}$ separates the claim's truth conditions | Expressible, **not** witnessable by this observer |
| `Refused` | A typed obstruction receipt (ill-typed, unbounded, or gate-blocked) | The claim does not enter the evaluation at all |

**The distinction between `Underdetermined` and `OutsideProfile` is the paper's
technical core.** The first is ignorance inside the profile; the second is a
boundary of the profile. Collapsing them makes T1 true and worthless.

### 3.4 Evaluation

The declared evaluation and terminal system is a map

$$
\mathrm{Ev}_{\mathcal{O}} \colon \mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}^{\mathrm{fin}}
\longrightarrow \mathrm{Status},
$$

specified by the paper, not assumed to exist. T1 asserts totality and
single-valuedness of a *specific* $\mathrm{Ev}_{\mathcal{O}}$; it does not assert
that every conceivable evaluation is total.

**Interpretive fence.** `[INTERPRETIVE]` — `WDP007-C17`, non-claims `WD7-N1`–`WD7-N3`.
Writing $\operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}}$ presupposes a

universe", nor that it is exhausted by any $W_{\mathcal{O}}$, nor that it exists

5. Proof strategy — three independent levels


The methodological claim is the same as WD-0's: establish the statement three
ways, and treat agreement between the three as evidence.

|---|---|---|
| L1 | Prose proof over a finitely presented $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}$ with an explicit evaluation algorithm | Human-checkable; establishes T1, T2, T5 by structural induction on claim depth |

### 8.1 The boundary token, and what it does not license

The phrase "we cannot witness an outside" is a statement about
$\Vdash_{\mathcal{O}}$ and $C_{\mathcal{O}}$. It licenses **neither** of:

1. "There is an outside." — This asserts a witnessed content the observer does
   not have.

2. "There is no outside." — This treats unwitnessed as nonexistent, which is
   programme stop condition 7 and a category error.

The correct status is the explicit boundary token `OutsideProfile` (equivalently
`Unwitnessed`), and T4 is the **conjecture** that this token cannot be discharged
in either direction from inside the system. *Corrected this pass:* "the theorem".
T4 is `[CONJECTURE]` (`WDP007-C8`) with no proof and no strategy (§5); this section

two positions below are separated by a conjecture, not by a result.**

Two honest positions are therefore available, and the paper must name which it
takes:

* **Position A — boundary-token realism about the method.** The system reports
  `OutsideProfile` and stops. Nothing further is claimed, and the paper's

* **Position B — grammar extension.** A larger domain is *constructed and
  witnessed*, and completeness is re-proved over the larger grammar. This is

**The paper must not present Position B as achieved by Position A.** Doing so is

### 9.1 Gödel incompleteness — the careful part

This is where a crank overreaches, so the paper must be pedantic.

Relative completeness as stated in T1 is **not** in tension with the first
incompleteness theorem, for a specific and checkable reason: T1 asserts that a
routing procedure assigns exactly one *status* to every claim, where the status
set includes `Underdetermined`, `OutsideProfile`, and `Refused`. It does **not**
assert that $\mathrm{Ev}_{\mathcal{O}}$ decides truth.

The honest fence, which must be printed:

> If $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}$ interprets a sufficiently strong arithmetic and
> $\mathrm{Ev}_{\mathcal{O}}$ is effective, then the set of claims receiving
> `Proved` is recursively enumerable and therefore cannot coincide with the set
> of arithmetical truths. Relative completeness is consistent with this only
> because `Underdetermined` is a permitted output. **T1 must never be read as
> "every claim is decided."** It is read as "every claim is *routed*, with a
> receipt."

The paper should additionally state whether the intended
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}$ interprets arithmetic at all. For the §6 packet it
does not — the grammar is finite and depth-bounded — which is why the packet is
a legitimate demonstration and not an evasion.
