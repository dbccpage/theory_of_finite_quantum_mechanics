2. Why a researcher should care


The corpus has, for several papers, been accumulating reasons to move from states
to *histories*: composition curvature (001a), the projection-to-cochain adapter
with its spatial/history bigrading (001h), the exact semigroup defect (001m), and
WD-5's history differential. Each of these says that a retained representation is
not closed, and each of them points in the same direction.

Pointing is not arriving. The temptation at this exact juncture is to write down

3. The motivating result, and exactly what it does not supply


Paper 001m proves the following. Let $X$ be a Banach space, $A \in \mathcal{L}(X)$,
$T_t = e^{tA}$, let $\Pi$ be a local $C^{3}$ retraction onto a split embedded
submanifold $M \subset X$, and set $S_t = \Pi T_t|_{M}$. Then at $m \in M$, for
sufficiently small $t, s \geq 0$,

$$
S_{t+s}(m) - S_t\bigl(S_s(m)\bigr)
\;=\; ts\,\mathcal{K}_{\Pi,A}(m) \;+\; O\bigl(ts(t+s)\bigr).
$$

The coefficient $\mathcal{K}_{\Pi,A}(m)$ is tangent, and $\mathcal{K}_{\Pi,A}(m) \neq 0$
forces $S_{t+s}(m) \neq S_t(S_s(m))$ for all small positive $t, s$.

*Claim type:* `[STANDARD → WDP005-C3]` — Paper 001m Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.2,
cited verbatim and **not reproved here**. **Typing note (repaired 2026-07-20).**
At source both carry 001m's own type `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]` — *proved there from
the hypotheses displayed in the statement*, namely $A \in \mathcal L(X)$ bounded,
$\Pi \in C^{3}$, and $0 \le t,s \le \varepsilon$. `[STANDARD]` here is a statement
about WD-5's *relation* to the result — external input, cited and not reproved,
carrying no WD-5 proof obligation — and **not** a promotion to unconditional
standing: the hypotheses travel with it, as does 001m's bounded-generator
`[SCOPE FENCE / OBSTRUCTION]` below. WD-5's closed type set has no
`[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]` slot, so the conditionality is carried in the annotation
and in ledger row `WDP005-C3` rather than in the tag. The defect is 001m's
$\mathfrak M_{t,s}(m) := S_{t+s}(m) - S_t(S_s(m))$; 001m states the remainder as
the explicit bound $\lVert R_{t,s}(m)\rVert \le C\,ts(t+s)$, of which the
$O(ts(t+s))$ above is the standard abbreviation.

> **Inherited scope fence — bounded generator.** Paper 001m carries a

4. Setting


Let $G = (\mathsf{S}, E)$ be a finite directed graph on a finite state set
$\mathsf{S}$. For $a, b \in \mathsf{S}$ let $\Omega(a,b)$ denote the finite set of
directed paths $a \to b$ of bounded length, and let $\Omega = \bigsqcup_{a,b}\Omega(a,b)$.
Concatenation

$$
\ast : \Omega(a,b) \times \Omega(b,c) \longrightarrow \Omega(a,c)
$$

is associative with identities the empty paths $\mathrm{id}_a$; that is, the
**unbounded** path set $\Omega^{\mathrm{all}} = \bigsqcup_{a,b}\Omega^{\mathrm{all}}(a,b)$
is the free category on $G$, and the length-bounded $\Omega$ fixed in the
declaration below is that free category **truncated** — a *partial* structure,
**not itself a category**, unless composition is restricted or $G$ is acyclic
(`WDP005-C15`, falsifier `WD5-F6`).

*Claim type:* `[STANDARD → WDP005-C1]` — elementary, **and only for the unbounded
path set**: it is the *all*-paths statement that is standard. Truncation is
``OPEN``; see `WDP005-C15`/`WD5-F6`. Cite, do not claim. *(Repaired 2026-07-20: the
row previously asserted the free-category property of the **bounded-length**
paths, which §4's own finiteness declaration contradicts — concatenating two
length-$L$ paths gives length $2L$.)*

> **Finiteness declaration (added 2026-07-20; the hypothesis the whole paper
> rests on).** Fix once and for all a **length bound** $L \in \mathbb N$ and set
> $\Omega := \bigsqcup_{a,b} \Omega_{\le L}(a,b)$, the paths of edge-length at
> most $L$. Then $\lvert\Omega\rvert \le \lvert\mathsf S\rvert \cdot
> \sum_{n=0}^{L} \lvert E\rvert^{\,n} < \infty$, so **every $\bigoplus$ in this
> paper is a finite indexed sum in the algebraic sense: an $L$-fold iterate of the
> binary $\oplus$, requiring no order of summation, no summability hypothesis, and
> no completeness of $R$.** Truncation is *not* a convenience: dropping it makes
> $\Omega(a,b)$ infinite whenever $G$ has a directed cycle, and every sum in §5
> step 4, §6 T1, and §6 T3 then becomes an object this paper has not defined.
> **The bound $L$, its uniformity across $(a,b)$, and whether the truncated
> $\Omega$ is closed under $\ast$ are all undischarged** — concatenating two
> length-$L$ paths gives length $2L$, so $\Omega_{\le L}$ is a *partial* category,
> not the free one, unless the composition law is restricted or $G$ is acyclic.
> That reconciliation is `PL-WDP005-1`, and it gates the meaning of $\ast$ in §5
> step 3. *Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP005-C15]`.

An **admissible history** is a path passing the EPK evaluator gate

$$
Z_\theta(b \mid a) \;=\; \sum_{\gamma \in \Omega(a,b)}

\qquad \mathcal{A}[\gamma] \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0},\ \beta \geq 0,

a $\mathbf{Kl}(W)$-morphism valued in the nonnegative reals. **This is a

**Not `[STANDARD]`:** `ENC-0019` is `kind: definition`, `status: DEFINED`, with

A **weight system** is a map $w : \Omega \to R$ into a semiring
$(R, \oplus, \otimes, 0_R, 1_R)$, optionally equipped with an involutive
antiautomorphism $(-)^{\dagger} : R \to R$.

Call $R$ **zerosumfree** (equivalently *antinegative*, *positive*) if

$$
x \oplus y = 0_R \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 0_R \ \text{and}\ y = 0_R .
$$

The Boolean semiring, the tropical semiring $(\mathbb{R}\cup\{+\infty\}, \min, +)$,
and $(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}, +, \times)$ are zerosumfree. No ring with $1_R \neq 0_R$ is.

*Claim type:* `[DEFINITION]` for zerosumfreeness; the four examples are
`[STANDARD]` and were re-derived in the 2026-07-20 producer pass rather than
transcribed. Boolean: $x \vee y = 0 \Rightarrow x = y = 0$ (all four cases).
Tropical: $0_R = +\infty$, $1_R = 0$, and $\min(x,y) = +\infty \Rightarrow x = y =
+\infty$. Nonnegative reals: $x + y = 0$ with $x,y \ge 0 \Rightarrow x = y = 0$.
Ring with $1_R \neq 0_R$: $1_R \oplus (-1_R) = 0_R$ while $1_R \neq 0_R$ and
$-1_R \neq 0_R$ (since $-1_R = 0_R$ would give $1_R = 0_R$) — so **the failure of
zerosumfreeness in a ring is witnessed by a single explicit pair, not by an
appeal to genericity.** That witness is the entire content of §9 Route B.

**Interpretive fence.** Writing $\gamma \ast \gamma'$ already presupposes a
carrier, an adjacency, a direction on each edge, and a sequential order on the
steps of a history. WD-0 established that none of these is selectable from bare
non-sameness. They are therefore **imports**, priced in §15, and they are *not*
silently granted as free structure anywhere in §6–§8.

### T1 — Representation theorem (semiring-valued finite history theorem)

Let $w : \Omega \to R$ satisfy multiplicativity under concatenation (step 3),
$w(\mathrm{id}_a) = 1_R$, and additivity under coarse-graining (step 4). Then $w$
is determined by the edge-weight matrix $M \in R^{\mathsf{S}\times\mathsf{S}}$,
$M_{ab} = w(a \to b)$, and the coarse weight of the $n$-step histories from $a$ to
$b$ is the matrix power

$$
W_n(b \mid a) \;=\; \bigl(M^{\otimes n}\bigr)_{ab},
\qquad
Z(b \mid a) \;=\; \Bigl(\bigoplus_{n \geq 0} M^{\otimes n}\Bigr)_{ab} = (M^{\ast})_{ab}
$$

whenever the Kleene star $M^{\ast}$ exists in $R$. Equivalently: compositional
weightings of finite histories are exactly the $R$-recognizable series on $G$.

**This is the first rigorous target of the paper, and it is a semiring-valued
finite history theorem — not a quantum path integral claim.** It is expected to
be `[STANDARD]` in weighted-automata theory; the contribution is the transport
and the typing, not the theorem.

*Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP005-C4]`, expected `[STANDARD]` once located; `PL-WDP005-2`.

> **Two different objects, and the paper must not conflate them (added
> 2026-07-20).** $W_n$ and the **truncated** weight
> $Z_{\le L}(b\mid a) = \bigl(\bigoplus_{n=0}^{L} M^{\otimes n}\bigr)_{ab}$ are
> finite $\oplus$-iterates and are exactly the objects §4 defines. The
> **starred** $Z = \bigl(\bigoplus_{n\ge0} M^{\otimes n}\bigr)_{ab} = (M^{\ast})_{ab}$
> displayed above is **not** that object: $\bigoplus_{n \ge 0}$ is an infinite
> indexed sum, which a bare semiring cannot evaluate. It acquires a meaning only
> under one of two *separately declared and separately priced* hypotheses:
>
> 1. **Nilpotence.** $M^{\otimes n} = 0$ for all $n > N$ — equivalently $G$ is
>    acyclic — in which case $\bigoplus_{n\ge0}$ collapses to the finite
>    $\bigoplus_{n=0}^{N}$ and **no hypothesis on $R$ is needed at all**. This is
>    the honest finite route, and it is the case the §8 fixture realizes.
> 2. **A star structure on $R$.** $R$ is a Conway / complete / $\omega$-complete
>    star-semiring carrying a primitive $(-)^{\ast}$ satisfying
>    $x^{\ast} = 1_R \oplus x\otimes x^{\ast}$. This is an **additional algebraic
>    import**, and in the archetypal analytic instance $R = (\mathbb R_{\ge0},+,\times)$
>    it is a *convergence* hypothesis — $(1-x)^{-1}$ for $x<1$ — i.e. exactly the
>    analysis the finite programme has not earned.
>
> The clause "whenever the Kleene star exists in $R$" names the gap but does not
> discharge it, and **which of the two routes T1 is stated over changes what T1
> says.** Under §4's length bound the paper is committed to route 1 or to the
> truncated sum; the starred form must therefore be either (a) restricted to
> acyclic $G$, (b) restated as $Z_{\le L}$, or (c) promoted to a *second*,

### T2 — Obstruction action and its cohomological criterion

Define a local history functional $\mathcal{A} : \Omega \to (\mathbb{R}, +)$ by
summing, along a history, local contributions drawn from the projection defect

**(a) Additivity.** $\mathcal{A}[\gamma \ast \gamma'] = \mathcal{A}[\gamma] + \mathcal{A}[\gamma']$,

$$
[\mathcal{A}] \neq 0 \ \text{in}\ H^{1}_{\mathrm{hist}}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{the weighting is genuinely history-dependent.}
$$

T2(b) is what converts 001m's motivation into a statement with a criterion

### T5 — Phase-insufficiency no-go (foregrounded)

Let $R$ be zerosumfree and let $\gamma_1, \gamma_2$ be histories with
$w(\gamma_1), w(\gamma_2) \neq 0_R$. Then

$$
w(\gamma_1) \oplus w(\gamma_2) \;\neq\; 0_R .
$$

Consequently no coarse-graining of nonzero-weight alternatives can vanish: **a
zerosumfree weight system admits no cancellation, hence no destructive
interference.**

*Claim type:* `[OPEN → WDP005-C9]`, `PL-WDP005-7`. The two-history form displayed is
immediate from the definition; the form actually needed downstream is the
**finite** extension — $\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k} x_i = 0_R$ with some $x_i \neq 0_R$ is
impossible — which is an induction on $k$ and is finite because $\mathcal C$ is
(§4). Support lemma `WD5-L5`. No infinite coarse-graining is claimed and none
would be meaningful here.

**Corollary (the $\ell^{1}$ tally cannot interfere).** The obstruction tally that
sums nonnegative magnitudes of defects is valued in $(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0},+,\times)$,

cancellation, hence no **destructive** interference in this paper's sense
(vanishing of the total weight)*, at any $\beta$, for any action, on any finite
path system. It says nothing about `ENC-1135`'s $\lvert\sum_s c_s\rvert^{2}$
notion, which WD-5 has no functional to express (`WDP005-C19`).

9. The honest fork: where cancellation could enter


If T5 holds, exactly three routes remain open, and the paper's job is to name
them rather than to choose among them:

**Route A — accept F1–F3.** The history weighting stays zerosumfree. Then the
corpus has a classical, optimization-theoretic, or stochastic histories calculus.
This is a coherent and defensible outcome. It is also the outcome under which
programme stop condition 3 (*classical shadow*) becomes live for WD-6.

**Route B — import a signed or complex carrier.** Move to F4. Cancellation
becomes available immediately, but the additive inverses were *declared*, not
derived. If the declaration is equivalent to complex Hilbert structure, phase, or
Born probabilities, programme stop condition 4 (*hidden Hilbert import*) fires and
any subsequent result is a reconstruction *relative to that axiom*.

**Route C — construct signs from the obstruction structure.** Attempt to derive an
orientation on defects such that the tally is naturally signed rather than
$\ell^{1}$. This is the only route that would be a derivation rather than an
import, and it is the one the corpus would most like to be true. **It is
therefore the one most in need of adversarial review.** The paper must state
plainly that Route C is unproven, and the ledger must carry it as a conjecture
(`WDP005-C10`) with an explicit possibility of being false.

> **Route C must confront the corpus's nearest apparent counterexample — and it
> is not one (added 2026-07-20 repair pass).** Paper 025 §4 defines a **signed**
> evaluator residual on the *same* $\mathbf 1_{\mathrm{EvalPass}}$ gate that §4
> imports: $\Delta_E = E(\gamma_2 \star \gamma_1) - E(\gamma_2)E(\gamma_1)$, with
> $\Delta_E \in \{-1,0,+1\}$ for binary evaluators and the safety-critical case
> $\Delta_E = -1$. A reader will reasonably ask whether this is Route C already
> executed: a sign, on obstruction data, from the declared gate.
>
> **It is not, and the paper must say why.** The sign enters through the
> **subtraction**, and subtraction is an **imported additive-inverse carrier**:
> $\Delta_E$ is valued in $\mathbb Z$, not in the $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$ /
> $\mathrm{Kl}(W)$ carrier the gate itself lives in. Paper 025 §13.3 draws exactly
> this conclusion — because $\Delta_E$ can be negative and $\mathrm{Kl}(W)$ is
> nonnegative, the honest object is a **signed loss ledger**, not a nonnegative
> $2$-cell — and `ENC-0811` mechanizes the obstruction ($d<0 \Rightarrow
> \neg\exists\,n:\mathbb N,\ (n:\mathbb Z)=d$). So $\Delta_E$ is evidence **for
> `WD5-L6` and for Route B**, not for Route C: it shows the corpus *buying* signs
> by moving carrier, precisely the purchase Route C proposes to avoid. A Route C
> construction that reaches for $\Delta_E$ has taken Route B and mislabelled it —
> **the exact failure this section exists to prevent.**
>
> **And what Route B buys is already mechanized (added 2026-07-20 cycle-2 repair
> pass).** Once the signed carrier is granted, the corpus *immediately* exhibits
> the cancellation Route A cannot have: `ENC-0812` (*EPK Aggregate Cancellation
> Does Not Imply Pointwise Zero*, `status: MECHANIZED`, Paper 025 §4 Lemma 4.3,
> Lean module `PaperAE006.L025_07_AggregateCancellation`) gives signed evaluator
> defects $d_{\mathrm{Neg}} = -1$, $d_{\mathrm{Pos}} = +1$ summing to $0$ **with
> both nonzero** — a vanishing aggregate over nonzero summands, on the same
> evaluator data, and the exact configuration T5 forbids in a zerosumfree carrier.
> The A/B fork is therefore **not** a choice between a proved side and a
> speculative one: **both sides are already mechanized in the corpus** —
> `ENC-0811` that the nonnegative carrier cannot hold the signed defect, `ENC-0812`
> that the signed carrier cancels — and only **Route C is genuinely open.** WD-5
> claims novelty for neither side of the fork (`WDP005-C18`); its contribution at §9
> is the *typing* of the fork, not either branch. Note also the moral `ENC-0812`
> attaches to its own witness — an aggregate zero is not a pointwise no-defect
> certificate — which is the **converse warning to T5**: T5 says a zerosumfree
> carrier cannot reach $0_R$ from nonzero summands; `ENC-0812` says that in a
> carrier that can, reaching $0$ tells you nothing about the summands. A draft that
> reads T5's no-go as an endorsement of aggregate readouts has inverted both.

*Claim types:* Route C `[CONJECTURE → WDP005-C10]`, `PL-WDP005-7`; the enumeration of
the three routes as exhaustive-given-T5 `[INTERPRETIVE]`, non-claim `WD5-N10`.
**Route B's witness is one line** (§4: $1_R \oplus (-1_R) = 0_R$ in any ring with
$1_R \neq 0_R$), which is exactly why Route B is cheap and why presenting it as
Route C would be the paper's characteristic failure. Note also that the corpus
has *already taken* Route B at $\delta 8$ (Paper 058 §4/§5.4), so Route B is not
hypothetical — what remains genuinely open is only Route C.

**The paper must not present Route B as Route C.** Doing so is the principal
self-deception this outline exists to prevent, and it is the exact analogue of
WD-0's "enriched single primitive" trap.

the history sum give quantum mechanics?"; it must ask:

> Which additional principle moves the weight system from the zerosumfree families
> F1–F3 into F4 or F5, and what does that principle cost?

are *exactly* the T4 families, so that any **destructive**-interference claim must

`WDP005-C19`'s.

Three programme stop conditions become simultaneously armed:

- **Stop condition 5 (phase loss)** — fired by T5 itself. **Destructive**
  interference — in WD-5's only sense, vanishing of the total weight of a
  coarse-graining class — requires a new carrier; the finite obstruction tally
  will not produce it. The qualifier is load-bearing and may not be dropped: T5
  establishes no-cancellation, and WD-5 declares no readout functional, so it says
  nothing about `ENC-1135`'s $\lvert\sum_s c_s\rvert^{2}$ notion (`WDP005-C19`).

- **Stop condition 4 (hidden Hilbert import)** — live on Route B. If the

This is a good outcome for the programme. A classification plus a no-go is a
firmer foundation than an unearned derivation, and it makes WD-6's success
condition checkable in advance.

15. Genesis-bias audit (mandatory before drafting)


Per the programme charter and the repository's Genesis Bias doctrine, this paper
must answer, for its own construction:

- **Where does the ordering on histories come from?** This is the sharpest
  question this paper faces. Step 3 — "sequential composition multiplies weights"
  — presupposes that the steps of a history are *sequentially ordered*, that
  $\gamma \ast \gamma'$ is distinguishable from $\gamma' \ast \gamma$, and that
  "before" and "after" are already available. WD-0's T3 proved that no linear
  order, orientation, directed adjacency, or clock successor is selectable from
  bare non-sameness. The order is therefore an **import**, and the paper must
  locate it on WD-0's T4 injection list by name and say which item it is. A
  histories calculus that quietly helps itself to time has assumed the very thing
  the programme set out to construct.

- Where does the directed graph $G$ come from, and what makes an edge directed?
- What makes two histories *alternatives* — coarse-grainable together — rather
  than unrelated occurrences? Step 4 presupposes a notion of "same endpoints,
  different route" that is not given by the carrier.

- What licenses a semiring at all? $\oplus$ and $\otimes$ are **two distinct
  declared operations**. Nothing in the difference calculus supplies one, let
  alone two, let alone the distributivity between them. Distributivity is the
  hinge on which T1 turns and it is assumed, not derived.

- What supplies the finiteness of $\mathsf{S}$ and the length truncation on $\Omega$?
- What licenses $\beta \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ and the real exponential in
  $e^{-\beta\mathcal{A}}$? This presupposes an ordered field, a scale, and a metric
  magnitude — and the charter's typing discipline states explicitly that a
  primitive distinction is not yet a metric magnitude.

- What licenses the ambient combinatorics in which $M^{\ast}$ is formed?

- What licenses the **finiteness** the title leans on? The length bound $L$ is
  declared, not derived; §4 says so. And "finite" applies to the index sets only:
  **four of the five T4 families are infinite, all four of continuum cardinality —
  F2's $\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$, F3's $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$, F4's
  $\mathbb R,\mathbb C,\mathbb H$, F5's $\mathbb Q_p$ — and only F1's two-element
  Boolean semiring is finite**; F4 and F5 are additionally analytic completions.
  A "finite witness-history integral" valued in any of F2–F5 is finite in its
  *index* and continuum in its *coefficients*, and the draft must say so in the
  abstract. *(Repaired 2026-07-20: this row previously named only F4 and F5,
  which misplaces the continuum line at F3|F4 — it sits at F1|F2, and the EPK's
  own $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$ is already past it.)*

The last two are not rhetorical. A classification proved *inside* a structured
metatheory, using a real-valued scale parameter, does not establish a
classification *for* structure or a derivation of magnitude. The paper must state
this boundary explicitly rather than let it pass.

This boundary — *a weight-system classification reached inside a metatheory that
was already equipped with two distributing operations, a real scale, and ambient
finite combinatorics does not establish a classification of weightings for
structure, nor a derivation of magnitude* — is now tracked as obligation
**`PL-WDP005-11`** (§14) with boundary fence **`WD5-L8`** (Claim Ledger §2a) and
non-claim **`WD5-N11`**, rather than left as prose alone; parallel to WD-0's
PL-WD0-8, WD-1's PL-WD1-11, WD-2's PL-WD2-12, WD-3's PL-WD3-11, WD-4's PL-WD4-11,
and WD-5's PL-WDP005-13. The audit's other rows — the origin of the ordering on
histories and of the directed graph $G$, what makes two histories *alternatives*,
what licenses the two semiring operations and the distributivity between them,
what supplies finiteness and the length truncation, and what licenses $\beta$ and
the real exponential — are the same boundary applied to the individual imports,
and each is priced in `PL-WDP005-1` and `PL-WDP005-10` rather than derived. The fence is
**standing**: discharging `PL-WDP005-11` either exhibits the accounting, which
narrows what the fence excludes, or states the boundary in the paper's own voice.
Neither outcome retires it.
