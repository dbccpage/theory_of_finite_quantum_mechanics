## The entrance

- Pre-Genesis separates supplied marks, adopted rules and retained events.
- Genesis supplies a particular admission and continuation account.
- S(n) tracks presentation stages under that account.
- MSS-N names proposed mathematical capabilities and their representation questions.
- This paper makes report sufficiency, recovery and repair into separate typed contracts.
- WDP develops those contracts on declared cochain, observer and quantum-capable carriers.

These steps form the intended reading order. A stage name does not itself supply
the data or theorem required by the next construction.

## Toward finite QM

The next route is §§17.9–17.11 of the reference monograph:

- A rational-linear complex structure satisfying its square and adjoint laws
  supports the Gaussian-rational carrier and Hermitianization.
- State and effect factors, completeness and a supplied trace readout yield
  exact nonnegative normalized probabilities for the certificate class.
- Complete process families supply branch probabilities and sequential or
  tensor composition under the declared carrier assumptions.

The monograph records nonselection examples alongside these constructions.
The reading route therefore carries explicit additional data at each step.
This paper's proofs and runtime replay do not constitute a fresh verification
of every monograph theorem or implementation.
