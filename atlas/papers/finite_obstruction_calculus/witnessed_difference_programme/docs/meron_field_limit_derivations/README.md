## Open derivation targets supported here

1. Prove or refute the missing uniform spatial and time-dependent correlation
   estimates needed for a joint $(L,M)$ limit.
2. Supply cumulant bounds and a declared topology before asserting a Gaussian
   spacetime field.
3. Establish the required all-$M$ spectral statement, or show precisely why
   the fixed-carrier theorem avoids needing it.
4. Construct native physical observation and repair adapters rather than
   identifying an algebraic quotient with an available operation.
5. Repair Chapter 23's toric-code kernel, restricted-error rank and adaptivity
   claims under explicit detector and noise hypotheses.
