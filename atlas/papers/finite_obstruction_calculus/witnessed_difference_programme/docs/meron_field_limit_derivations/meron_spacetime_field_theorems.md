# Scope of the proposed Meron spacetime limits

The earlier claims of a completed Gaussian spacetime field theorem and a
joint discrete-to-continuum limit are withdrawn. The
[retained synthesis](../source_archive/superseded_derivations_20261008/meron_spacetime_synthesis.md)
contains the reviewed historical text; its theorem labels and closure claims
must not be used as established results.

The proposed problem concerns the periodic, full-Fock Meron chain and a
spacetime field obtained by spatial averaging and time smearing. A proof needs
uniform bounds with the stated volume and time-discretization quantifiers,
convergence of the covariance, and conditions that give a positive probability
law and tightness in the declared function space. These obligations are not
settled by a leading-order Fourier expansion or by vanishing formal cumulants.

The maintained [finite covariance result](../meron_euclidean_covariance_positivity.md)
and [conditional volume-limit result](../meron_euclidean_thermodynamic_limit.md)
have explicit hypotheses. The latter's later positivity-window argument is
separate from the unsupported estimates in the retained synthesis.
The [time-dependent analyticity note](../meron_time_dependent_analyticity.md)
states its own uniform assumptions and conditional cumulant estimate.

The exact [Gate E calculation](code/gate_e_exact_counterexample.py) supplies
one negative quadratic-form witness at $L=4$, $M=1$, $r=1/10$. It rules out
positive semidefiniteness of that finite response matrix. It supplies no
all-discretizations claim and no theorem about extinction in a limiting field.

See the [current status table](g3_g4_research_dossier_and_next_targets.md) and
[source errata](../source_errata_20261008.md) for the remaining obligations and
the failures in the older arguments. The archived synthesis remains unchanged;
this note adds no derivation.
