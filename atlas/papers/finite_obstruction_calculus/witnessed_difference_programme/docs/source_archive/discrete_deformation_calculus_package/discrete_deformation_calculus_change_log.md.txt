### Mathematical corrections

- §23.1 state machine: the classes were swapped (`ActiveClosureFailure` tagged
  $\Phi_1 > 0$, `ClosedResidual` tagged $\Gamma_2 > 0$), and `ExactClass` was not
  a canonical class. Replaced by the FOC classes and the driver of §12.
- E is the exact part, not the co-exact part (DDC-E-01). The sub-paper's
  "residual invariance" became the gauge-invariance theorem DDC-E-03. The
  idempotence claim is restated at the defect level (DDC-E-05).
- R handles any finite graph, with one coordinate per independent cycle
  (DDC-R-06), and the Hodge split has three parts, not two (DDC-R-01).
- I chains every letter, I included; the sub-paper's letter set omitted it.
- Λ states the Law 4 trigger. DDC-TAPE-09 is the restriction theorem: when
  restriction along a complex extension commutes with $d_0$, exactness of the
  lifted residual implies exactness before the lift. The tape law for Λ does
  not add that check.
- M: one closed refusal vocabulary. The ungrounded locus is an intake refusal,
  not an M reason (DDC-M-02).
- T states the nine-conjunct `CanContinue_n` of the engine; an out-of-budget
  open obstruction routes to M instead of a terminal verdict; every terminal
  receipt records the unlicensed authority terminal (GTLA-T9).
- Demo 5's lawful tape ends in T (`D·I·T`). Demo 2's `C·R·M·T` is an unlawful
  falsifier (`T` cannot seal $\Gamma_2 > 0$), and Demo 4's `C·M·T` is unlawful
  under a blind detector.
- The eight candidate laws are restated as step and control laws that refine
  the engine's `op_verification::verify_step`; Law 7 now requires `E` to
  discharge from an exact class, and Law 8 makes `C` monotone.
