## Status vocabulary

- **Definition:** notation or a declared model.
- **Finite theorem:** proved for the stated finite setting, in Lean (`lean4/`)
  or in the text, and checked by a test.
- **Conditional theorem:** proved only under the listed hypotheses.
- **Computed fixture:** exact output for explicitly supplied data.
- **Model consequence:** follows inside a declared engineering model or assumption.
- **Positioning:** comparison with prior work; not a result.
- **Scope:** a boundary on interpretation.
- **Open problem:** stated, not proved; no evidence is claimed.

Lean declarations prove only their typed statements over abstract state models.
Every Lean result uses no axiom outside {`propext`, `Quot.sound`} (`lean4/axiom_report.txt`).
Tests live in `code/test_oe_prime_algorithms.py`.
