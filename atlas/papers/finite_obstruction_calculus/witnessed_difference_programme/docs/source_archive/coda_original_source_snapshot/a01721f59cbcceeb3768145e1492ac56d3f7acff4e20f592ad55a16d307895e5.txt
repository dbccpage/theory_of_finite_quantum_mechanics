## Programme Abstract & Executive Vision

The **Witnessed Difference Programme** investigates how retained distinctions become mathematical representations, and how failed representations are revised without laundering unstated assumptions. Beginning from the primitive distinction of a witnessed irreflexive relation ($a \ne b$), the programme constructs a finite mathematical ladder spanning cochains, temporal succession, observation apparatuses, history integrals, and cosmological models.

This Master Monograph consolidates the seven constituent research papers of the programme into a single, unified volume. Each part represents an operational rung of the descent:

1. **Part 0 (Charter & Symmetries)**: Proves that bare finite symmetric sets admit no parameter-free selection of points, subsets, or orientations without global fixed points.
2. **Part I (Cochain Systems)**: Bridges primitive distinctions into discrete chain complexes $C^0 \to C^1 \to C^2$ and $L_1$ defect geometry.
3. **Part II (Awareness Boundary)**: Formalizes the operational observer hierarchy and establishes an epistemic firewall stopping short of subjective phenomenology.
4. **Part III (Succession & Duration)**: Lifts retained distinctions to temporal succession, establishing duration as an additive cochain rather than an ambient continuous clock.
5. **Part IV (Observers & Observation)**: Constructs the finite observer object $\mathcal{O}$ and formalizes the five-stage ladder from raw interaction to verified $E$-observation.
6. **Part V (History Integrals)**: Investigates compositional path weighting across finite evaluated path kernels (EPK).
7. **Part VI (Quantum Reconstruction)**: Tests whether quantum phase and amplitude structures can be selected over classical stochastic alternatives under finite operational constraints.
8. **Part VII (Cosmogenesis)**: Synthesizes the full arc into observer-indexed relative cosmogenesis, defining completeness relative to admitted witness capacity.

## Part 0: Programme Charter & Finite Symmetry Obstruction

*Source Module:* [`witnessed_difference_programme.md`](witnessed_difference_programme.md) (Foundational Charter)  
*Claim Family:* `WD0-ARCH-*`, `WD0-SYM-*`, `WD0-CODE-*`, `WD0-NC-*`  

### 0.1 The Programme Boundary

The programme organizes inquiry along the representation-revision cycle:

$$
\text{distinction} \to \text{retention} \to \text{representation} \to \text{projection} \to \text{residual} \to \text{witness} \to \text{refinement} \to \text{new representation}
$$

This sequence establishes strict typing boundaries:

- A distinction is not yet a metric magnitude.
- A residual is not automatically a cochain.
- A nonzero quotient is not automatically a closure defect.
- A witness is not an administrative authority.
- An operational observer is not a conscious subject.

### 0.2 Finite Relabelling Model

Let $X$ be a finite carrier set and $G = \operatorname{Sym}(X)$ its full symmetric group. A declared target $Y(X)$ is a set equipped with an induced $G$-action. A selection $y_X \in Y(X)$ is **parameter-free and relabelling-natural** if:

$$
\sigma \cdot y_X = y_X \qquad \text{for every } \sigma \in \operatorname{Sym}(X).
$$

### 0.3 Core Theorems

#### Theorem WD0-SYM-01 (Finite Equivariant Selection Obstruction)

Let $X$ be finite and $Y(X)$ a $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$-set. A parameter-free relabelling-natural selection of an element of $Y(X)$ exists if and only if the global fixed-point set $Y(X)^{\operatorname{Sym}(X)}$ is nonempty.

#### Corollary WD0-SYM-02 (No Distinguished Point)

For $|X| \ge 2$, the natural action of $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ on $X$ has no global fixed point. Pure equality cannot select a distinguished element.

#### Corollary WD0-SYM-03 (No Nontrivial Invariant Subset)

For nonempty finite $X$, the only subsets fixed by all permutations are $\varnothing$ and $X$. No parameter-free rule selects a proper nonempty subset.

#### Corollary WD0-SYM-04 (No Invariant Orientation or Strict Order)

For $|X| \ge 2$, neither oriented pairs $(x,y)$ with $x \ne y$ nor strict total orders on $X$ possess a global fixed point.

#### Proposition WD0-SYM-05 (Classification of Invariant Binary Relations)

For $|X| \ge 2$, under the diagonal action on $X^2$, there are exactly two orbits: the diagonal $\Delta_X$ and its complement $\Delta_X^c$. Consequently, the only permutation-invariant binary relations are $\varnothing$, $\Delta_X$, $\Delta_X^c$, and $X^2$.

## Programme Stop Conditions & Non-Claims

The programme recognizes the following formal stop conditions:

1. An observer or coordinate anchor is invoked without explicit admission.
2. An iteration index is described as physical time without a calibrated cochain metric.
3. A normalization trick is sold as a derivation of quantum amplitudes.
4. An autopoietic boundary is conflated with phenomenal awareness.
5. An evaluation acceptance is equated with institutional authority.
6. A classical stochastic model is presented as a quantum reconstruction without proving non-commutative contextuality.

This monograph establishes finite operational models and rigorous obstructions within their stated assumptions. It does not prove unconstrained universal physics, subjective consciousness, or automatic authority.
