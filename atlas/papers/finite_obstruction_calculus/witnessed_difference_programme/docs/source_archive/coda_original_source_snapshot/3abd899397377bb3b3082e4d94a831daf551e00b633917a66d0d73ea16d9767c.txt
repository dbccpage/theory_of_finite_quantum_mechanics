Lead result (field language)

**[DEFINITION]** Measurement, in the CODA term register, is a disciplined observation that also produces a typed value or tally relative to a declared scale or partition — so the output is comparable under that scale, not merely a retained mark that something happened.

Why a researcher should care

Physics, statistics, AI evaluation, and instrumentation all use “measurement” as if the word already carried a shared finite structure. In practice it often collapses three different moves: (i) an interaction occurred, (ii) a residual was retained, (iii) a number (or discrete tally) was assigned against a scale. Separating **observation** (ladder of registration → witness → authority-standing) from **measurement** (observation plus typed scale-output) blocks a common smuggle: treating any retained mark as if it already licensed numerical residual talk or QM-style postulates. The gloss is deliberately thin so it can be stress-tested against the observation chain without claiming a full measurement theory.

Dependency note

**[SCOPE FENCE]** This note depends on the CODA observation ladder (interaction → registration → witness → authority-standing relative to evaluator $E$). Measurement is defined only after an $E$-observation is available as a checkable fact-of-record. If the observation paper’s formal object or ladder changes, this note must be rechecked and, if needed, re-aligned before any stronger claim or deposit.

Working gloss

**[DEFINITION]** **Measurement** (candidate structural reading): a disciplined **observation** that also produces a typed value / tally relative to a declared **scale** or **partition**.

**[SCOPE FENCE]** All measurements (in this sense) are observations; not all observations are measurements.

```text
observation  ⊇  measurement
observation  =  interaction + registration + witness + authority-standing (relative to E)
measurement  =  observation + scale/partition + typed tally/value
```

```text
retained mark ≠ observation
observation ≠ measurement
measurement ≠ authority
measurement ≠ quantum collapse
measurement ≠ consciousness
```

Relation to the observation chain

The controlling observation chain (see [observation](../011_calculus_of_observation/terms/observation.md) and the CODA observation paper) is:

```text
interaction → registration → witness → authority → observation
```

**[INTERPRETIVE]** Measurement sits **after** that ladder (or as a refinement of the last step): the observation is already available as a checkable fact-of-record under evaluator $E$; measurement further exports a value in a declared codomain (numeric or discrete partition labels).

**[DEFINITION]** **MEAS-D3.** Candidate measurement ingredients form a definitional packet shape (not a theorem package):

1. An $E$-observation $O$ of interaction $I$ (per the observation gloss / paper).  
2. A declared **scale** $S$ or **partition** $\Pi$ of the relevant residual/carrier window.  
3. A **typed map** $m: \text{support}(O) \to V_S$ producing value $v \in V_S$ (tally, bin label, rational, etc.).  
4. Re-access of $(O,v,S)$ under the same evaluator window that licensed $O$.

**[OPERATIONAL RULE]** **MEAS-R1.** Treat a structure as a measurement in this register only when all four ingredients are exhibited. Without (2)–(3), the structure may still be an observation; it is not a measurement here.

Worked example (toy, finite)

**[WORKED EXAMPLE]** Carrier $C$ has three bins $\{B_0,B_1,B_2\}$. An interaction leaves a retained residual on $C$ that a witness re-compares and an evaluator $E$ accepts as observation $O$. Partition $\Pi = \{B_0,B_1,B_2\}$. The measurement map returns the bin label $v = B_1$.  

| Piece | Instance |
|-------|----------|
| Observation | $O$ accepted under $E$ |
| Scale / partition | $\Pi$ with three named bins |
| Typed value | $v = B_1 \in \Pi$ |
| Non-measurement control | Same $O$ with no $\Pi$ and no $v$ — still an observation, not a measurement here |

This example is **definitional scaffolding**, not a physical experiment and not a QM pointer-state model. It exhibits the contrast: the same accepted observation without a declared partition/value remains an observation, not a measurement.

Falsifiability

**[SCOPE FENCE]** This gloss fails (must be repaired or withdrawn) if:

1. A structure is called a “measurement” here while lacking a declared scale/partition and typed value, **or**  
2. A structure is called a “measurement” here while failing the observation ladder (e.g. registered but unwitnessed residual treated as measured), **or**  
3. The gloss is used to smuggle collapse postulates, consciousness, or continuum-as-given ontology.

What this does not claim

**[SCOPE FENCE]**

* Quantum measurement postulates, collapse, or pointer-state uniqueness  
* That instruments are conscious or that observers “create” reality  
* That every scientific use of the English word “measurement” already matches this schema  
* A full theory of error bars, calibration, units, or SI metrology  
* Continuum measures, probability measures, or Lebesgue structure “for free”  
* That a typed tally is authority, truth, or promotion (`AuthorityEffect::None`)  
* That measurement invents difference (PrimitiveDifference remains prior)

Open questions (honest, not theorems)

Open research questions are tracked as **MEAS-Q1–Q4** in `measurement_claim_ledger.md`. Summary only (no open tags in body):

1. Minimal scale / partition import: when is a binary yes/no partition enough?  
2. Relation to registration of **numeric** residual vs discrete bin labels.  
3. How far to stay from QM “measurement problem” language in later term papers.  
4. Whether measurement always factors as observation + map, or whether some regimes collapse the steps (if so, price the collapse explicitly).
