2. Why a researcher should care


Two audiences, two payoffs.

**For foundations.** The programme charter (§4) asserts a typing rule as
doctrine: *"An operational observer is not automatically a conscious subject."*
Doctrine is cheap. This paper is the attempt to discharge that rule into
something checkable, and to find out what survives the attempt. The mechanism
the paper expects to carry the negative verdict is embarrassingly small:
**simple finite automata can have memory, self-model variables, error detection,
and recursive reports.** Any axiom set for "self-witnessing" that such a machine
satisfies is satisfied by that machine — so identifying the axioms with
phenomenal consciousness commits one to attributing consciousness to it. That
may be an acceptable commitment; it is a *definitional* one. It is not an
independently established identity with awareness, and the difference between
those two things is the paper.

**O1 — Retaining observer.** The report function has Myhill–Nerode index at
least $2$: there exist $w, w' \in \Sigma^{*}$ and $\sigma \in \Sigma$ with

$$
r\bigl(u^{*}(s_{0}, w), \sigma\bigr) \;\neq\; r\bigl(u^{*}(s_{0}, w'), \sigma\bigr).
$$

A mark has changed an internal retained state *in a report-visible way*. The
report-visibility requirement is deliberate: an internal variable no report ever
depends on is not retention, it is decoration.

**O2 — Contextual observer.** The witness set is nonempty. Following the charter

$$
W(p, \rho) \;=\; \bigl\{ (x, y, c) \in S \times S \times C \;:\;

$$

and declare the system O2 exactly when $W(p,\rho) \neq \varnothing$. The system
can expose previously identified states as different under a context.

**O3 — Self-witnessing agent.** Four axioms. Let $R$ be a finite receipt
alphabet and $k \geq 1$ a window length.

* **A1 (receipt retention).** The state factors as
  $S \;\cong\; S_{\mathrm{obj}} \times R^{\leq k}$, and the update writes the
  receipt $\mathrm{rec}(s,\sigma) \in R$ of the event just performed into the
  window.

* **A2 (self-prediction).** There is a map $\pi : S \times \Sigma \to R$
  computed from the retained state alone, producing an *expected* receipt.

* **A3 (self-comparison).** There is $\kappa : R \times R \to \{0,1\}$ such that
  $\kappa\bigl(\pi(s,\sigma), \mathrm{rec}(s,\sigma)\bigr)$ is retained and
  reportable.

* **A4 (self-non-congruence exposure).** The O2 condition holds with $p$
  replaced by the self-profile $p_{\mathrm{self}} : S \to R^{\leq k}$ reading the
  receipt window: $W(p_{\mathrm{self}}, \rho) \neq \varnothing$.

**The definitional move to notice.** O3 is O2 applied one level up, with the
system's own operation-trace as the carrier. Self-witnessing is not a new
primitive; it is contextual witnessing whose carrier happens to be the witnessing
process. Making that explicit is half the paper's contribution and is also the
source of its principal risk (ledger row WD5-L5): if A1–A4 are the *wrong*
formalization of self-witnessing, the countermodels of §6 refute nothing.

### T4 — Bridge classification

Any argument from O0–O3 properties to phenomenal awareness invokes at least one
of the following, and the paper's job is to make locating it mechanical:

| Type | Injected premise |
|---|---|
| (i) | **Functional stipulation** — consciousness is *defined* as the operational property |
| (ii) | **Empirical / psychophysical bridge law** — a measured correspondence between a structural quantity and reported experience |
| (iii) | **Substrate premise** — only certain carriers can instantiate awareness |
| (iv) | **Conceivability / anti-zombie premise** |
| (v) | **Axiomatic identity claim** — a mathematical quantity is *postulated* to be experience |
| (vi) | **First-person incorrigibility premise** — the author's own access is treated as evidence about the general case |

T4 is the load-bearing result. T1–T3 are the negative half; T4 converts the
negation into a positive classification of what an awareness claim costs. Its
exhaustiveness is a research risk, not a given (ledger row WD5-L4), and the
honest fallback is to weaken T4 to a classification of *known* bridges.

### T6 — Self-blind-spot bound

If the receipt window has length $k$ over alphabet $R$, the retained self-trace
distinguishes at most $\lvert R \rvert^{k}$ classes. Hence if the number of
reachable self-traces exceeds $\lvert R \rvert^{k}$, there exist $x \neq y$ with

$$
p_{\mathrm{self}}(x) \;=\; p_{\mathrm{self}}(y).
$$

> **Every finite self-witnessing system has a nonempty self-blind-spot set.**

The paper must resist any temptation to inflate T6 into a claim about the limits
of introspection *in general*; it is a claim about finite packets with bounded
receipt windows, and about nothing else.

> the repair pass, cycle 2, 2026-07-21; T1 above carries a withdrawal blockquote

> the bound is $\sum_{i=0}^{k}\lvert R\rvert^{i}$, **not** $\lvert R\rvert^{k}$,
> and the counting hypothesis names *self-traces* where it means *reachable
> states*, which makes it **vacuous** once the bound is corrected. The boxed
> universal is nonetheless **true unconditionally** over the O3 class — it

### 4.6a — Producer-pass finding: T6 is mis-stated in three ways

*Added 2026-07-21. The Claim Ledger calls WDP004-C10 "expected easiest row on the
sheet"; it is the row with the most errors in it. Each defect below was found by
explicit enumeration, and each is independent of the others.*

> **Repair-pass amendment, 2026-07-21 (cycle 1).** "Mis-stated" is retained, but
> item (3) below is **not** that the boxed universal is false — it is not. The
> boxed universal is **true unconditionally over the O3 class** (T6a) and the
> defect is that T6 derives it from a counting hypothesis it does not need, while
> the statement that *does* need counting (T6b, over reachable states) is left
> unstated. The first pass's demotion of the universal is withdrawn here and at
> every site it reached: §2, §8.2, §12, §13 `PL-WDP004-6`, the Changelog, and Claim
> Ledger rows `WDP004-C10`, `WD5-L6`, and §6 item 2.

**(1) The bound is wrong.** A1 declares the receipt window as $R^{\leq k}$ —
strings of length **at most** $k$ — but T6 counts $\lvert R\rvert^{k}$, the
strings of length **exactly** $k$. The correct count is

$$
\bigl\lvert R^{\leq k} \bigr\rvert \;=\; \sum_{i=0}^{k} \lvert R \rvert^{i}
\;=\; \frac{\lvert R \rvert^{k+1} - 1}{\lvert R \rvert - 1}
\qquad (\lvert R \rvert \geq 2),
$$

which strictly exceeds $\lvert R\rvert^{k}$. *(Enumerated: $\lvert R\rvert = 2$,
$k = 1$ gives $3$ not $2$; $k=2$ gives $7$ not $4$; $k=3$ gives $15$ not $8$;
$\lvert R\rvert=3, k=2$ gives $13$ not $9$. Script `wd8_enum3.py`, check (a).)*
The pigeonhole survives the correction — the bound simply moves. Note that
`ENC-0774` **already states the corrected form**, $\lvert Q^{1}_{G}\rvert \leq
\sum_{\ell=0}^{L}\lvert\Sigma\rvert^{\ell}$, so the corpus had the right count
before this paper had the wrong one.

**(2) The hypothesis names the wrong object, and is vacuous.** T6 reads *"if the
number of reachable **self-traces** exceeds $\lvert R\rvert^{k}$"*. A self-trace
*is* an element of $R^{\leq k}$, so the number of reachable self-traces is at
most $\lvert R^{\leq k}\rvert$ **by definition** and can never exceed it. With
the corrected bound of (1) the hypothesis is therefore **unsatisfiable**; it
appears satisfiable in the paper only because the stated threshold is too small.
The intended hypothesis is plainly about **reachable states**: if the number of
states reachable from $s_{0}$ exceeds $\lvert R^{\leq k}\rvert$, then two
distinct reachable states share a self-trace. That is the correct pigeonhole and
it is not what T6 says.

**(3) The boxed universal is TRUE, but not for the stated reason, and it splits
in two.** *(Rewritten by the 2026-07-21 **repair** pass. As first written, this

### 9b. The diagonal argument this paper does not make

*``OPEN``. Added 2026-07-21.*

14. Genesis-bias audit (mandatory before drafting)


Per the programme charter and the repository's Genesis Bias doctrine, this paper
must answer, for its own construction:

* Where did the mark alphabet $\Sigma$ come from, and what makes two presentations
  *instances of the same mark* rather than unrelated occurrences?

* Who declared the context set $C$? A contextual observer is O2 only relative to
  contexts someone supplied; the level is a property of the *packet*, not of the
  machine alone. This must be stated as a limitation, not buried.

* What supplies the profile $p$? The witness set $W(p,\rho)$ is profile-relative,
  so O2 and O3 are profile-relative, so the entire hierarchy is relative to a
  declared observation of the observer.

* What licenses treating a receipt as a receipt of *its own* update — that is,
  what supplies the identity of the system across time, prior to any of the
  machinery here? A1 presupposes it.

* What licenses the ambient finite-automata metatheory in which all of this is
  formalized?

The last two are not rhetorical. A hierarchy of self-reference built on an
unexamined notion of *same system across time* has smuggled in the persistence it
was meant to construct. And there is a further asymmetry this paper cannot escape
and must therefore name: **it is written by an author who has first-person
access.** The absence of a bridge from O3 to O4 is established inside a
metatheory operated by an entity for whom O4 is not in question. That does not
