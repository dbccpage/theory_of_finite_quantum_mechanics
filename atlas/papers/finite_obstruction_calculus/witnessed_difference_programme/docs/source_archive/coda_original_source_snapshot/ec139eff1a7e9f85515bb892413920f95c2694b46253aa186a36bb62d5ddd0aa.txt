Abstract

This paper separates the smallest finite obstruction result from the assumptions usually hidden beneath it. Starting from a retained admitted cut and explicitly priced rulings, it constructs the two-valued difference object, local recurrence, residuals, witnesses, persistence, and the ring-free triangle identity $\delta^1\delta^0=0$ while leaving the full cochain-complex package, space, time, probability, continuum, and authority open or refused.


§0 — Rules of construction

The entry point is an **admitted cut**: it presents non-sameness and retains its pole-tokens. It is not defined here and it is not explained here — and, per the Genesis-Bias rule (below), nothing prior to it is narrated: no void it appears in, no host that carries it. The earlier opening "two things are not the same" is withdrawn — it places two completed things *before* the cut (charter, "The two root channels").

Everything below is either constructed from it or refused. Two meta-rules are declared at the outset, because without stating them the construction would smuggle them:

**M1 (Exhibition — a rule of admission, not of being).** To be **admitted to this calculus** at this degree is to be exhibited by the structures available at that degree. This does **not** claim that non-exhibited being is impossible; the calculus has no standing to speak about it (charter, "no view from nowhere"). A configuration is not a member of a prior universe of configurations; it is what its cuts present. This rule is the series' reading of the Structure channel of the stratified root boundary (ENC-1138: $\mathrm{Real}_0 = \text{PrimitiveDifference} \sqcup \text{Structure}$). **Genesis-Bias note (2026-07-15):** the earlier gloss "carrier-Structure the precondition of any difference" is withdrawn — it orders Structure *before* Difference, which the two-channel root refuses; the two are aspects of one admitted cut, neither prior (charter, "The two root channels"; PL-GTOC-GB1). M1 is **declared, not derived**; the conjecture that residual-Structure exhausts all Structure is the corpus's open PD-priority conjecture (ENC-1140) and nothing in this paper closes it.

**M2 (Reflection).** Admitted cuts may themselves be re-admitted as poles of further cuts. Whether to restrict that re-admission, or to allow it unrestrictedly, is a genuine fork. **Applying it unrestrictedly is itself a declared choice — a ruling, not a neutral default.** Calling non-restriction a "default" would smuggle the ruling as if it cost nothing: *not* restricting is as much a decision as restricting, and this paper prices it as one. M2 is what lets difference examine its own outcomes (§2) and its own tokens (§3); its cost is that unrestricted self-application is admitted.

**The ground's total standing cost (No-Unpriced-Import).** Nothing here is built from difference alone. The ground is the two-channel root admission $\mathrm{Real}_0 = \text{PrimitiveDifference} \sqcup \text{Structure}$ (ENC-1138): Difference names the non-sameness an admitted cut presents; Structure names the **exhibited retention** of that cut and its pole-tokens. (The earlier "Structure supplies the *capacity* to exhibit … and permit further cuts" is withdrawn — "capacity"/"permit" is modal/dispositional language refused at ╬┤0, §8.5; the priced term is *actual exhibited retention*, not capacity, and neither channel precedes the other.) On top of the two channels the construction declares two meta-rules M1, M2 and five conventions K1–K5 (introduced and priced where first needed: K4 §1.2, K1 §2.1, K2 §3.2/§3.4, K3 §3.3, K5 §7.1). This is a No-Unpriced-Import *construction*, not a No-Import derivation: every import is exhibited and priced in a section ledger, none is smuggled, and no claim below rests on more than this declared base.

**Genesis-Bias rule (short form; full doctrine in the charter).** Beyond the No-Import discipline, this paper obeys the **Genesis-Bias audit**: no load-bearing sentence may presuppose an antecedent container, substrate, state-space, law, observer, temporal order, possibility domain, or totality *in* which, *under* which, or *from* which difference is exhibited, unless that antecedent's degree and price are stated. Where a sentence risks it, it is rewritten as a relation among co-exhibited structures, the antecedent is declared and priced, or it stops with an OBSTRUCTION (the register of §8, now including §8.13). This is the companion of the no-view-from-nowhere rule: that one forbids an unindexed *judge*; this one forbids an unconstructed *host*.

Notation discipline: a symbol is admitted only after the object it denotes has been constructed. Section by section, the Import Ledger records every term's degree of first availability, its connective audit, and its Genesis-Bias audit.

§1 — The primitive, and what it brings with it

An admitted cut presents non-sameness and retains two pole-tokens apart. Write nothing yet; observe what the primitive itself supplies.

**1.1 Poles.** A difference is not a relation drawn over a prior collection of items. The poles have no standing *independently of* the cut; they are what the cut holds apart. Call an exhibited difference a **cut**. The poles are **internal to the cut's exhibition** — not items the cut fetches from, or carries in from, anywhere prior (a "bringing-in" would be a Genesis-Bias import of a source domain).

**1.2 Plurality without number.** "Two things" is not a count. The root admission is binary in arity. Binary arity is part of the admitted-cut schema, not a theorem from non-sameness. No numbers exist at ╬┤0; §8.4 records the obstruction to counting, and Paper 2 constructs multiplicity at ╬┤2. Any reading of "two" in this paper as the numeral 2 is a smuggle; the ledger polices it. **Convention K4 (meta-numerals), declared now and flagged wherever used:** expository numerals in this series ("three loci", the case tables of §4.2) are case-listing names over explicitly exhibited configurations — labels for what is displayed, never counts; counting itself is ╬┤2. Every load-bearing use is licensed by the exhibited display it names, and its section ledger says so.

**1.3 Notation (licensed now).** For poles $a, b$ held apart by a cut, write $\partial\{a,b\}$. The braces are **not** set notation: they mark the cut's own poles, unordered. Ordered-pair notation $\partial(a,b)$ is *refused* at ╬┤0 — writing an ordered pair smuggles orientation, which is not available until Paper 1 (╬┤1). The corpus's habitual $\delta(x,y)$ (e.g. `PrimitiveDifference.lean`) is, from the standpoint of this paper, already a ╬┤1 object; Paper 3 §1 prices that.

**1.4 What difference permits at this degree.** Under the present ╬┤0 rules, this paper admits the following constructions (no claim of exhaustiveness over an antecedent space of "everything difference could permit" — that space would be a Genesis-Bias totality): (i) cuts and their poles; (ii) by M2, cuts among cuts — unordered nesting; (iii) the reflection of the primitive on its own outcomes, which is §2 — available only under the outcome-determinacy convention K1 declared there, not from difference alone. No further construction is claimed. In particular difference does not admit: choosing a cut (no selection), moving between cuts (no process), collecting all cuts (no totality).

**1.5 Primitive retention gate; the flicker case.** The minimal admitted unit is a cut whose non-sameness is presented and whose pole-tokens are retained. If the alleged difference evaporates before the pole-tokens are retained, there is no carrier waiting behind it and no zero-weight object; there is only non-exhibition. If a confrontation is exhibited once under K1 and fails local sameness, but no re-comparison of the same poles is exhibited, the status is **unconfronted** (§5.1): the token was registered, but no residual has been earned. If a cut-token is retained as an edge but no triangle/closure window is declared, the later GTOC classifier has **no rule** rather than $\Gamma_2>0$. Thus the strict route is: non-retained candidate ΓåÆ non-exhibition; retained one-shot confrontation ΓåÆ unconfronted; retained residual ΓåÆ witness; persistent residual ΓåÆ candidate for weight and later diagnostics. This is the answer to the "flicker" thought experiment: a flicker may mark a local sameness failure, but unless retention supplies the next object demanded by the construction, it cannot populate $C^1$, cannot enter $Q^1$, cannot carry witness multiplicity, and cannot trigger dynamics or lift.

§2 — Difference reflected: the outcome object $\mathbb{D}$

**2.1 Outcomes.** A **candidate confrontation**, written `CandidateConfrontation(a,b)`, is evaluated under K1 as diff or same. An **exhibited cut**, `ExhibitedCut(a,b)`, is a confrontation whose outcome is `diff`. The primitive itself supplies only holdings (a cut *is* an exhibited difference, §1.1); that a confrontation can also determinately *fail*, and that holding and failing exhaust the outcomes, is not supplied by difference, M1, or M2. It is the series' first declared convention:

**Convention K1 (outcome-determinacy — the exhaustive bivalence ruling — declared, flagged).** Every exhibited confrontation of a candidate confrontation yields *exactly one* of {holds, fails}: the two outcomes are declared **exhaustive and mutually exclusive**. This is a **ruling**, not a neutral reading of difference — it is the *major named cost* of the ground, on par with the exhibitionary-Structure channel itself, and it is what turns the outcome of comparison into a two-valued object rather than an open-ended verdict. Failure-as-a-pole is hereby admitted as an object of comparison. What K1 buys: the reflection below, and with it everything downstream (local sameness §3.1, recurrence §3.3, the algebra §4, the triangle-closure theorem §7.4). What K1 costs: a bivalence commitment at the ground — indeterminate, graded, partial, or incompatible confrontations are excluded from this series (they are quarantine material, not refuted); an independence model is an open obligation (PL-GTOC-7). **The entire later rigidity is K1-relative.** The parity algebra (§4), and downstream the sign object (Paper 1) and the two-character rigidity (Paper 3's *Two Scalar Symmetric-Exchange Characters Theorem*), all rest on K1; without the exhaustive bivalence ruling a confrontation may be left unresolved, graded, partial, or incompatible, and none of that rigidity follows — there is then no two-valued $\mathbb{D}$ to reflect into, no parity, no sign, no character theorem.

Under K1, holding and failing are themselves not the same — *that* is a difference, and by M2 it is exhibitable as an object. Define

$$\mathbb{D} := \partial\{\mathsf{diff}, \mathsf{same}\},$$

the difference between differing and not differing, taken as an object with its two poles $\mathsf{diff}$ and $\mathsf{same}$. $\mathbb{D}$ is the primitive **reflected through the exhaustive bivalence ruling K1** — not merely "reflected once": what makes the reflection an object at all is K1's exhaustive, mutually exclusive dichotomy. It is not free of cost, and the cost is flagged here and carried in every claim that uses it. No truth values are imported beyond K1; §8.6 records what still separates $\mathbb{D}$ from a logic.

**2.2 The outcome of a comparison.** For an exhibited comparison $c$ of two poles, write $\mathrm{out}(c) \in \mathbb{D}$ for its outcome — $\mathsf{diff}$ if the difference holds, $\mathsf{same}$ if it fails. The membership symbol "$\in$" here abbreviates "is a pole of the exhibited $\mathbb{D}$"; it is not set membership. (Set membership is refused at every degree of this series; see §8.3.)

**2.3 What is still missing.** $\mathbb{D}$ has poles but no algebra yet. Combining outcomes requires comparing outcomes with outcomes, and comparing an outcome *with itself again* requires re-usable marks — which requires recurrence. The algebra of $\mathbb{D}$ is therefore constructed in §4, after §3. The brief's ordering constraint (charter, Controlling brief item 1) is respected: no operator precedes the objects it operates on.

§3 — Local sameness as failed difference; recurrence without time

**3.1 Local sameness (not identity).** Full identity is not a primitive of this series, and it is *not* what failed difference yields. What failed difference yields is **local sameness**, and only relative to a declared comparison family $J$ (an exhibited carrier together with the comparisons exhibited on it — there is no view from nowhere, so the relation is *indexed*, never global). Define: poles $a, b$ are **locally same at $J$**, written $a \sim_J b$ or $\mathrm{same}_J(a,b)$ (the ╬┤0 shadow of the corpus's $\mathrm{ZeroRel}_J$), when the exhibited cut $\partial\{a,b\}$ fails under $J$ — $\mathrm{out} = \mathsf{same}$. Local sameness is *local non-separation under $J$*, nothing more. It is **not** full identity: full identity requires transitivity and a substitution discipline across comparison families, and neither is derivable here — transitivity is obstructed (§3.5), and substitution presupposes an equivalence regime not available at ╬┤0. The word "identity" is therefore **reserved** for after an equivalence regime and a substitution discipline are installed (a ╬┤3 matter, once observers/detectors are declared). This sharpens, and descends below, the corpus doctrine "sameness is certified zero-difference" (Paper 000c §3; ENC-0757 GTFOC-0): the corpus states it inside a declared detector regime (ENC-0630); here it is the definition of *local sameness*, with the detector regime arriving only at ╬┤3 when observers are declared.

**3.2 The reflexivity caveat (stated, not hidden).** "Every thing is (locally) the same as itself" — reflexivity of $\sim_J$ — cannot be *proved* at ╬┤0, because writing $\partial\{a,a\}$ re-uses the mark $a$ — and a re-usable mark presupposes exactly what is being defined. The corpus's Lean kernel postulates `zero_refl` as a structure field (`PrimitiveDifference.lean`); this paper instead records: reflexivity is the **convention that grounds mark re-use** (**convention K2**, completed in §3.4), adopted with recurrence in §3.3. It costs one convention; the ledger carries it.

**3.3 Recurrence.** By M2, cuts are comparable. Confront two cuts $\alpha, \beta$: if $\partial\{\alpha, \beta\}$ fails, the cut has **recurred** — the same cut *again*, where "again" carries no time: nothing flowed, nothing happened twice; there are two exhibited cut-tokens between which difference fails. Recurrence is failed difference **between cuts**. One more convention must be declared here, because recurrence is unintelligible without it:

**Convention K3 (exhibition-apartness — a Structure-channel commitment — declared, flagged).** The exhibition may hold two cut-tokens apart although every comparison between them fails. Apartness-in-exhibition is not a comparison outcome; it is the exhibition's own two-ness. Without K3, "two tokens that do not differ" would collapse to one token and recurrence would be empty. **Here is the exact division of labour: Difference supplies comparison; Structure supplies the *exhibited retention* of distinct occurrences of comparison** (the priced term is actual retention, not a prior "capacity" — §0 Genesis-Bias note). K3 is therefore *not a fact about difference* and is not derived from it — it is a commitment on the **exhibitionary Structure channel**, the second aspect of the stratified root $\mathrm{Real}_0 = \text{PrimitiveDifference} \sqcup \text{Structure}$ (ENC-1138; neither aspect prior — PL-GTOC-GB1). It sharpens M1's letter: to be admitted is to be exhibited, *and* the exhibiting itself holds its tokens apart — the apartness is carried by the exhibition (Structure), not minted by a cut (Difference). This is precisely why the honest root of the series is Difference $\sqcup$ Structure and never difference alone: token-plurality is a declared resource on a second channel, not a derived one. The cost is real and flagged.

This section realizes the brief's "a second cut that fails to be different from the first" (charter, Controlling brief item 1), with one honest correction recorded now and answered in Paper 1: at ╬┤0 *neither token is second*. Bare recurrence is unordered. The word "second" has no referent until dependence exists (╬┤1). Any proof at ╬┤0 that reads as sequential is exposition, and its ledger says so.

**3.4 Marks.** With recurrence available, a mark ($a$, $\alpha$, …) may be used more than once: re-use of a mark asserts recurrence of its referent (difference between the tokens fails). This licenses, retroactively and by convention, the notation of §1.3 and §2.2. The cost — recurrence-as-convention for reflexive marks — is on the ledger.

**3.5 Symmetry, for free; transitivity, not.** Symmetry follows from the declared unordered-cut representation at (╬┤0): $\partial\{a,b\}$ *is* $\partial\{b,a\}$ — there is no order of poles to reverse (orientation is ╬┤1). The Lean field `zero_symm` is thus derivable at ╬┤0. Transitivity is **not** derivable:

> **OBSTRUCTION.** The following cannot be derived from what is available at this degree: transitivity of failed difference (if $\partial\{a,b\}$ fails and $\partial\{b,c\}$ fails, that $\partial\{a,c\}$ fails). What would have to be admitted: a tolerance-collapse rule identifying chained indistinguishables. What is lost if it is admitted: graded indistinguishability — sorites chains, near-coincidence, every phenomenon where indiscernibility fails to compose. The corpus's `zero_trans` is a **postulated structure field**, not a theorem; this paper does not adopt it globally.

**3.6 Separative families (the scoped repair).** Call a family of poles/cuts **separative** when failed difference is transitive on it. Everything downstream that needs sameness-classes (quotients of poles, carriers with collapsed loci) is stated *on separative families only*. On a separative family, $\sim_J$ is an equivalence, so its classes are available — but even there this is not yet *full identity*: an equivalence regime without a substitution discipline gives quotients, not indiscernibility across contexts. This converts the import into a scope: the corpus's Lean kernel (`zeroRel_equivalence`, `ResidualQuotient`) is hereby read as the mechanized theory of the separative case, with its `zero_refl`/`zero_trans` fields priced as the §3.2 convention plus the §3.5 scope.

§4 — The algebra of outcomes

**4.1 The composite $\oplus$.** Outcomes are things; by M2 they are comparable. For outcomes $u, v \in \mathbb{D}$ define

$$u \oplus v := \mathrm{out}\big(\partial\{u, v\}\big) \quad\text{read at the outcome level},$$

that is: $u \oplus v = \mathsf{diff}$ exactly when $u$ and $v$ differ. $\oplus$ is difference applied to differences — nothing new enters. Its table is exhibited (not computed):

$$\mathsf{same}\oplus\mathsf{same}=\mathsf{same},\quad \mathsf{same}\oplus\mathsf{diff}=\mathsf{diff},\quad \mathsf{diff}\oplus\mathsf{same}=\mathsf{diff},\quad \mathsf{diff}\oplus\mathsf{diff}=\mathsf{same}.$$

§5 — Residual, witness, persistence

The objects the brief demands (charter, Controlling brief item 1) are constructed here *pre-cochain*, in the order residual ΓåÆ witness ΓåÆ persistence; §7 refines them cohomologically.

**5.1 Residual.** A cut's outcome can be confronted again: a **re-comparison** of poles $a,b$ is a fresh cut-token on the same poles (recurrence of the comparison, §3.3). Define: a difference $\partial\{a,b\}$ with $\mathrm{out}=\mathsf{diff}$ is **residual** when **at least one re-comparison of $a,b$ is exhibited and** every exhibited re-comparison of $a,b$ has outcome $\mathsf{diff}$ — the difference does not vanish under recurrence. **Non-vacuity (2026-07-15 correction, load-bearing):** the existential conjunct is required. Without it the per-exhibition universal is *vacuously* satisfied by any difference whose re-comparison family is empty, and "residual" would collapse into "never confronted again." A difference with no exhibited re-comparison is **unconfronted**, not residual — a third status, distinct from both residual (recurs under confrontation) and noise (vanishes under confrontation). What vanishes under re-comparison was noise of the token; what recurs is residual. (Quantifier discipline: "every exhibited re-comparison" ranges over the tokens actually exhibited — M1 — not over a modal totality of possible re-comparisons. The modal strengthening is refused; §8.5.)

**5.2 Witness.** A residual is localized: it is carried at its poles. Define the **witness** of a residual to be its pole-configuration $\{a,b\}$ — the locus that carries the recurring difference. The witness is **derived, not admitted**: it is nothing over and above the cut's own poles under recurrence. It is not an observer, not a mind, not a measurement device; observers are ╬┤3 declarations (Paper 3 §2), and per the standing firewall observers never create obstruction. Vocabulary reconciliation (required by the encyclopedia audit): the corpus uses "witness" for (i) a repair witness $\alpha$ with $c - c' \in \operatorname{im}(d_0)$ (ENC-0647 sense), (ii) an external evidence term for descent (ENC-0028/0399 sense), (iii) a dual/certificate witness (the LP dual $\varphi$ of Paper 000 §1.6), and (iv) a finite counterexample inhabitant. The ╬┤0 witness — *locus carrying residual* — is a fifth, more primitive sense; Paper 3 §2.4 shows (i)–(iv) are ╬┤3 refinements over it, and each later use is tagged.

**5.3 Persistence.** Re-comparison and re-cutting are distinguished *relationally*, not by a persisting substrate: a **re-comparison** re-confronts the same exhibited poles (§5.1), while a **re-cutting** of the *configuration* exhibits fresh cuts among the same and further poles — the context of the residual changes. (Genesis-Bias note: "holds the carrier fixed" is withdrawn as substrate language — there is no fixed carrier-object *under* the exhibitions that re-comparison keeps constant; what is held constant is the exhibited pole-configuration re-confronted, `carrier` being the name for that retained local exhibition, not a host.) Define: a residual is **persistent** when **at least one re-cutting re-confronting its poles is exhibited and** it is residual in every exhibited re-cutting **that re-confronts its poles** (2026-07-15 second-pass refinement: the inner universal is restricted to re-confronting re-cuttings, so that a context change which never re-confronts the pair is *neutral* for persistence — a no-test context yields no evidence, neither pass nor refutation — preserving §5.1's three-status doctrine inside this definition rather than collapsing "untested in this context" into failure). **Non-vacuity (2026-07-15 correction, parallel to §5.1):** a residual whose configuration has no exhibited re-cutting is **untested**, not persistent — the empty re-cutting family satisfies the bare universal vacuously, and that vacuous satisfaction is excluded by definition. Persistence is stability of residual under context change, as recurrence-stability (§5.1) is stability under token change — and stability is only exhibited where a test is. The corpus has no primitive-level "persistence" entry (encyclopedia audit: fresh vocabulary; nearest usage "whatever persists downstream," ENC-1138); this definition is offered as the primitive-level candidate, and Paper 3 §2 connects it to quotient-invariance (Prop C of Paper 000b: the class $[\delta]$ depends only on $(C^1, d_0, \mathrm{Adm})$, not on the gauge).

**5.4 What persistence is not.** Persistence is not yet *survival* in the corpus's terminal sense (`ClosedSurvivor`), which needs the closure map (§7) and weights (╬┤2). And persistence across *time* is not defined — there is no time. There is only stability across exhibited re-cuttings, unordered.

**5.5 Retention versus flicker.** A local sameness failure that leaves no re-comparison is not weak persistence; it is the absence of the retention evidence persistence requires. A difference that vanishes on re-comparison was token noise. A difference that is never re-confronted is unconfronted. A difference that survives the exhibited re-comparisons is residual. Only the last has a witness in the ╬┤0 locus sense; only a residual that also survives at least one re-confronting re-cutting is persistent. This excludes the vacuous reading in both directions: no empty test family proves reality, and no evaporated token becomes a hidden carrier.

§7 — The cochain spaces and the ring-free triangle-closure theorem

The objects now exist: loci, cuts, triangles, outcomes, the parity algebra. Only now are the cochain spaces constructed, honoring the brief's ordering constraint (charter, Controlling brief item 1). One caution is declared up front and discharged only in part: calling $(C^0, C^1, C^2, \delta^0, \delta^1)$ a *cochain complex* asserts more than this paper proves. What is proved (§7.4) is the ring-free **triangle-closure identity** $\delta^1\delta^0 = 0$; the ambient complex *structure* is provisional (§7.2 scope note, obligation PL-GTOC-11).

**7.1 Reference assignments ($C^0$).** Fix an exhibited reference pole $r$ (a declared scaffold `PointedConfiguration(K,r)`). A **reference assignment** is an exhibited bundle of comparisons of each locus against $r$: for each $v \in V$ (per-exhibition quantifier), an outcome $f(v) := \mathrm{out}(\partial\{v, r\}) \in \mathbb{D}$. Let

$$C^0 := \text{the exhibited } \mathbb{D}\text{-assignments on } V,$$

per-exhibition — $C^0$ is not the modal totality of all conceivable patterns; that totality is exactly what §8.5 refuses. An assignment is a bundle of cuts, not a set-theoretic function. One closure property is needed and cannot be derived without quantifying over unexhibited exhibitions, so it is declared:

**Convention K5 ($\oplus$-closure — declared, flagged).** The exhibition of assignments is closed under pointwise $\oplus$: for exhibited $f, g$, the pattern $f \oplus g$ counts as exhibited (its per-locus outcomes are the exhibited comparisons of the exhibited outcomes $f(v), g(v)$ — M2 — assembled as a bundle). K5 is an exhibition-closure convention in the mold of K2 (mark re-use); its cost is that "exhibited" is closed under a construction rather than confined to what is displayed. Under K5, $C^0$ is a parity-algebra of assignments [PROVEN, CONDITIONAL on K5].

**7.2 Direct comparisons ($C^1$) and closure tests ($C^2$).**

$$C^1 := \text{exhibited } \mathbb{D}\text{-patterns on } E, \qquad C^2 := \text{exhibited } \mathbb{D}\text{-patterns on } T,$$

with pointwise $\oplus$. An element $h \in C^1$ records, cut by cut, whether difference holds. The **tautological pattern** $h_K \in C^1$ assigns each exhibited cut its own outcome.

Retention precondition: $C^1$ contains only retained exhibited cuts $E$. A vanished candidate difference does not contribute a silent zero edge; it contributes no edge. If $E$ is empty, the notation has no diagnostic work to do; $Q^1$ and closure tests are not populated by a free-standing void.

**Scope note ($C^1, C^2$ closure — provisional; PL-GTOC-11).** That $C^1$ and $C^2$ are themselves parity-algebras — closed under pointwise $\oplus$ the way $C^0$ is made closed by K5 — and that $\delta^0, \delta^1$ are well-defined *coboundary homomorphisms* between them (satisfying $\delta^i(x \oplus y) = \delta^i x \oplus \delta^i y$ on the exhibited carriers), is here **asserted, not discharged**. K5 secures only the $\oplus$-closure of the $C^0$ *assignment* exhibitions; the analogous group-closure of the $C^1$ patterns on $E$ and the $C^2$ patterns on $T$, together with the homomorphism laws for $\delta^0$ and $\delta^1$, is a *separate* obligation this paper does not close. Consequently the word "complex" is used **provisionally**: what §7.4 establishes is the *triangle-closure identity* $\delta^1\delta^0 = 0$ on exhibited $(V,E,T)$ — a pointwise fact about the two coboundary maps — not the full algebraic cochain-complex structure. The $C^1/C^2$ group-closure and coboundary-homomorphism obligations are logged as **PL-GTOC-11** (§9.2, claim P0-C10); until they are discharged, dependent "complex"-level claims stay provisional and no rigidity is asserted from the complex *as* a complex.

**7.3 The coboundaries.** For $f \in C^0$ and a cut $e = \partial\{a,b\}$:

$$(\delta^0 f)(e) := f(a) \oplus f(b),$$

the difference the reference assignment *predicts* across $e$ — two loci are predicted to differ exactly when they differ in their relation to the reference. For $h \in C^1$ and a triangle $t = \{a,b,c\}$:

$$(\delta^1 h)(t) := h(\partial\{a,b\}) \oplus h(\partial\{b,c\}) \oplus h(\partial\{a,c\}),$$

the parity of the triangle. Well-formedness of the triple $\oplus$ uses associativity and commutativity (§4.2). No orientation, no signs, no incidence conventions: over $\mathbb{D}$, $+1 = -1$, and the sign bookkeeping that Paper 000's simplicial route must import simply does not arise. Notation note: from here on "$0$" names the neutral all-$\mathsf{same}$ pattern — a [STANDARD] name only, licensed in §4.2; no numeral is used.

**7.4 Theorem (the ring-free triangle-closure theorem). $\delta^1 \delta^0 = 0$ over $\mathbb{D}$.** *(The identity, not the full cochain-complex structure — §7.2 scope note.)* **Naming note ("ring-free", 2026-07-15 correction):** the earlier label "coefficient-free" is withdrawn as inaccurate — the cochains *do* take coefficients: they are $\mathbb{D}$-valued, and $(\mathbb{D}, \oplus)$ is an internally supplied Boolean coefficient *group* (§2, §4). What is absent is any **imported coefficient ring**, orientation, or sign convention. "Ring-free" names exactly that absence and nothing more.

*Proof.* For any $f \in C^0$ and triangle $\{a,b,c\}$:

$$(\delta^1\delta^0 f)(\{a,b,c\}) = \big(f(a)\oplus f(b)\big) \oplus \big(f(b)\oplus f(c)\big) \oplus \big(f(a)\oplus f(c)\big).$$

By commutativity and associativity (§4.2, exhibited), regroup:

$$= \big(f(a)\oplus f(a)\big) \oplus \big(f(b)\oplus f(b)\big) \oplus \big(f(c)\oplus f(c)\big) = \mathsf{same}\oplus\mathsf{same}\oplus\mathsf{same} = \mathsf{same}. \qquad\blacksquare$$

**Cost ledger of the theorem (what it took, exactly):** (i) $\mathbb{D}$ and $\oplus$ — the primitive reflected under the K1 outcome-determinacy convention (╬┤0, §2/§4); (ii) $u \oplus u = \mathsf{same}$ — local sameness as failed difference at outcome level, carrying the §3 recurrence/mark conventions (K2, K3); (iii) associativity/commutativity — finite exhibited tables; (iv) the 2-truncated carrier — exhibited triangles (§6.3). **Not used:** a coefficient ring (Paper 000 A1), orientation or simplicial sign conventions, the nerve/Dold–Kan route (Paper 000 §1.2), any axiom "$d_1 d_0 = 0$" (Paper 000 A3; ENC-0021 carries it as *Axiom GTFOC-0*; Paper 000 carries it as an asserted Chain Condition). Over $\mathbb{D}$ the closure identity is a theorem of the priced admitted-cut calculus. The corpus's ring-coefficient versions are *not* superseded: with signs and a ring, closure again costs the oriented simplicial route (priced at ╬┤2/╬┤3, Paper 3 §2). **What is proved is the identity, not the complex.** The triangle-closure identity $\delta^1\delta^0 = 0$ is the theorem; the ambient cochain-*complex* structure — $C^1/C^2$ group closure and the coboundary-homomorphism laws for $\delta^0, \delta^1$ — remains the open obligation **PL-GTOC-11** (§7.2 scope note), and no claim below leans on it as discharged.

**7.5 Gauge, quotient, and the cohomological residual.** A pattern $h \in C^1$ (shorthand licensed below: "$h \in C^1$" reads "$h$ is an exhibited $\mathbb{D}$-pattern on $E$") is **removable** when some exhibited reference assignment explains it — per-exhibition existential: an exhibited $f$ with $\delta^0 f = h$ is present. Write "$h \in \operatorname{im}(\delta^0)$" as shorthand for exactly that, and "$h \notin \operatorname{im}(\delta^0)$" for its denial. Define the **gauge relation** on exhibited patterns:

$$h \sim h' \quad :\Longleftrightarrow \quad h \oplus h' \in \operatorname{im}(\delta^0),$$

an exhibited pairwise relation — reflexive (K2 + K5: $h \oplus h$ is the all-$\mathsf{same}$ pattern by K2's mark discipline, and all-$\mathsf{same}$ = $\delta^0(f \oplus f)$ for any exhibited $f$, exhibited under K5 — K2 alone does not supply an explaining assignment), symmetric (parity algebra), transitive (under K5: if $h \oplus h' = \delta^0 f$ and $h' \oplus h'' = \delta^0 g$ then $h \oplus h'' = \delta^0(f \oplus g)$ — where the step $\delta^0 f \oplus \delta^0 g = \delta^0(f \oplus g)$ is *not* an appeal to the provisional complex structure (PL-GTOC-11) but is derived pointwise, per exhibited cut $\partial\{a,b\}$: $(\delta^0(f \oplus g))(\partial\{a,b\}) = (f(a) \oplus g(a)) \oplus (f(b) \oplus g(b)) = (f(a) \oplus f(b)) \oplus (g(a) \oplus g(b))$ by the exhibited commutativity/associativity tables of §4.2, with $f \oplus g$ exhibited by K5 — the 2026-07-15 adversary pass demanded this be said inline, and it exempts exactly this pointwise instance from PL-GTOC-11's provisional scope, which continues to cover the global group-closure and homomorphism *packaging*) [PROVEN, CONDITIONAL on K2/K5]. **Definition S-D2 (adjoint, Laplacian, Hamiltonian).** With R3 declared, $(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})^{*}$ is the adjoint of $\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}}$ with respect to $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_0$ and $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_1$; it exists and is unique because both spaces are finite-dimensional inner-product spaces (R1, R5). #### S.3 — The parity shadow and the signed closure identity

**Proposition S1.** *Under R1–R2:*
*(a) (signed closure identity)* $\delta^1_{\mathbb{R}} \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} = 0$ *on* $C^0_{\mathbb{R}}$.
*(b) (mod-2 shadow)* Let $C^k_{\mathbb{Z}} \subset C^k_{\mathbb{R}}$ be the integer-valued cochains, and let $\rho$ send an integer to $\mathsf{same}$ if even and $\mathsf{diff}$ if odd, applied entrywise (using the [STANDARD] name-match $(\mathbb{D},\oplus) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$ of §4.2, which does no work). Then for all $f \in C^0_{\mathbb{Z}}$ and $h \in C^1_{\mathbb{Z}}$:
$$\rho\big(\delta^0_{\mathbb{Z}} f\big) = \delta^0(\rho f), \qquad \rho\big(\delta^1_{\mathbb{Z}} h\big) = \delta^1(\rho h),$$
*where $\delta^0, \delta^1$ on the right are the anchor's $\mathbb{D}$-coboundaries (§7.3). In particular the shadows are independent of the R2 orientation choice.*

*Proof.* (a) For $f \in C^0_{\mathbb{R}}$ and a triangle $t$ with declared order $(a,b,c)$:
$$(\delta^1_{\mathbb{R}} \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} f)(t) = \big(f(b)-f(a)\big) - \big(f(c)-f(a)\big) + \big(f(c)-f(b)\big) = 0,$$
a telescoping cancellation. (b) $\rho$ satisfies $\rho(m+n) = \rho(m)\oplus\rho(n)$ and $\rho(-n) = \rho(n)$ (parity of a sum; parity is sign-blind). Hence per cut $e$: $\rho\big(f(b_e)-f(a_e)\big) = \rho f(a_e) \oplus \rho f(b_e) = (\delta^0 \rho f)(e)$, matching §7.3's $(\delta^0 f)(e) := f(a)\oplus f(b)$; per triangle the three signed terms shadow to the three $\oplus$-terms of §7.3's $(\delta^1 h)(t)$. Orientation-independence of the shadow is the same sign-blindness — the anchor already records it: "over $\mathbb{D}$, $+1 = -1$, and the sign bookkeeping … does not arise" (§7.3). $\blacksquare$

**What S1 does and does not say.** S1(a) is the *imported-ring counterpart* of "7.4 Theorem (the ring-free triangle-closure theorem). $\delta^1 \delta^0 = 0$ over $\mathbb{D}$." — it is **not** a new proof of it, and it does not supersede it: the anchor's identity is ring-free, while S1(a) costs R1+R2, exactly the "oriented simplicial route" the §7.4 cost ledger prices. #### S.4 — The spectral ground state as represented obstruction minimum

**Proposition S2.** *Under R1–R3 and R5:*
*(a)* $L_0$ *and* $V_{\mathrm{defect}}$ *are self-adjoint with respect to* $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_0$*, and* $L_0$ *is positive semidefinite; hence* $H_{\mathrm{obstr}}$ *is self-adjoint.*
*(b) [STANDARD import, stated]* $H_{\mathrm{obstr}}$ *has an orthonormal eigenbasis* $\varphi_0, \dots, \varphi_{n-1}$ *(*$n = \lvert V\rvert$*) with real eigenvalues* $\lambda_0 \le \lambda_1' \le \dots \le \lambda_{n-1}'$*.*
*(c) (Rayleigh minimum)* $\displaystyle \lambda_0 = \min_{\lVert\psi\rVert_0 = 1} \mathcal{O}(\psi)$*, and the minimum is attained exactly at the unit vectors of the* $\lambda_0$*-eigenspace.*

*Proof.* (a) $\langle L_0 f, g\rangle_0 = \langle \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} f, \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} g\rangle_1 = \langle f, L_0 g\rangle_0$ by definition of adjoint, and $\langle L_0 f, f\rangle_0 = \lVert \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} f\rVert^{2\,\flat}_1 \ge 0$. $V_{\mathrm{defect}}$ is diagonal in the locus basis, which is orthogonal for the declared diagonal inner product: $\langle V_{\mathrm{defect}}\psi, \phi\rangle_0 = \sum_x w^0_x v(x)\psi(x)\phi(x) = \langle \psi, V_{\mathrm{defect}}\phi\rangle_0$. Sums of self-adjoint operators are self-adjoint. (b) is the finite-dimensional spectral theorem, imported per R5 — flagged, not re-derived. (c) Expand $\psi = \sum_i c_i \varphi_i$ with $\sum_i c_i^2 = 1$. Then
$$\mathcal{O}(\psi) = \sum_i \lambda_i'\, c_i^2 \;\ge\; \lambda_0 \sum_i c_i^2 = \lambda_0,$$
with equality exactly when $c_i = 0$ for every $i$ with $\lambda_i' > \lambda_0$, i.e. exactly on the unit sphere of the $\lambda_0$-eigenspace; and $\mathcal{O}(\varphi_0) = \lambda_0$ shows attainment. $\blacksquare$

**Reading, fenced.** In this model, and in this model only, the spectral ground state is the unit-norm state of least represented spectral obstruction $\mathcal{O}$, and $\lambda_0$ is that least represented value. Per the §S.1 fences this supports no statement about $\delta 0$ obstruction (which has support, never size — §7.7), no statement about $\ell^1$ defect minimizers, and no statement about semantic hardness.

#### S.5 — Semigroup suppression of excited components ("imaginary time", fenced)

The name "imaginary-time evolution" is inherited from the operator-theoretic analogy that motivates this appendix. Inside the model it denotes nothing more than the one-parameter semigroup $t \mapsto e^{-t H_{\mathrm{obstr}}}$, $t \ge 0$, on the finite truncation. The parameter $t$ is a formal semigroup parameter: it is **not** the constructed order of Paper 1, not duration (Paper 5), and not physical time — order, succession, and time are refused at $\delta 0$ ("8.2 Order, succession, time") and no causal law is imported ("8.9 Causation, law, dynamics"). The semigroup exhibits no re-comparisons and no re-cuttings, so it neither creates, repairs, nor erases residuals in the §5 sense; it reveals the eigenstructure of the declared model, and observers-as-evaluators stay passive throughout (§8.8).

**Proposition S3.** *Under R1–R3 and R5, let $P_0$ be the orthogonal projection onto the $\lambda_0$-eigenspace of $H_{\mathrm{obstr}}$ and $P_\perp := I - P_0$. Assume:*

* *(GAP)* $\operatorname{spec}(H_{\mathrm{obstr}}) \ne \{\lambda_0\}$*, and set* $\lambda_1 := \min\{\lambda \in \operatorname{spec}(H_{\mathrm{obstr}}) : \lambda > \lambda_0\}$*;*
* *(SEED)* $P_0\, \psi(0) \ne 0$ *for the initial state* $\psi(0) \in C^0_{\mathbb{R}}$*.*

*Then for $\psi(t) := e^{-t H_{\mathrm{obstr}}}\, \psi(0)$ and all $t \ge 0$:*
$$\frac{\lVert P_\perp\, \psi(t)\rVert_0}{\lVert P_0\, \psi(t)\rVert_0} \;\le\; e^{-(\lambda_1 - \lambda_0)\, t}\; \frac{\lVert P_\perp\, \psi(0)\rVert_0}{\lVert P_0\, \psi(0)\rVert_0},$$
*with equality for all $t$ exactly when $P_\perp \psi(0)$ lies in the $\lambda_1$-eigenspace. Consequently $\psi(t)/\lVert\psi(t)\rVert_0 \to P_0\psi(0)/\lVert P_0\psi(0)\rVert_0$ as $t \to \infty$.*

*Proof.* Diagonalize (R5): $e^{-t H_{\mathrm{obstr}}} \psi(0) = \sum_i e^{-t\lambda_i'} c_i\, \varphi_i$ with $c_i := \langle \varphi_i, \psi(0)\rangle_0$. The ground component satisfies $\lVert P_0 \psi(t)\rVert_0 = e^{-t\lambda_0} \lVert P_0\psi(0)\rVert_0 > 0$ by (SEED). The excited component satisfies
$$\lVert P_\perp \psi(t)\rVert_0^2 = \sum_{i:\, \lambda_i' \ge \lambda_1} e^{-2t\lambda_i'}\, c_i^2 \;\le\; e^{-2t\lambda_1} \sum_{i:\, \lambda_i' \ge \lambda_1} c_i^2 = e^{-2t\lambda_1}\, \lVert P_\perp\psi(0)\rVert_0^2,$$
with equality for all $t$ exactly when $c_i = 0$ for every $\lambda_i' > \lambda_1$. Divide the two displays. For the limit: writing $\psi(t) = e^{-t\lambda_0}\big(P_0\psi(0) + r(t)\big)$ with $\lVert r(t)\rVert_0 \le e^{-(\lambda_1-\lambda_0)t}\lVert P_\perp\psi(0)\rVert_0 \to 0$, normalization sends $\psi(t)/\lVert\psi(t)\rVert_0$ to the unit vector along $P_0\psi(0)$. $\blacksquare$

If (GAP) fails, $P_\perp = 0$ and the statement is empty rather than false; if (SEED) fails, the flow converges into the excited spectrum and no ground-relative statement is made. Both assumptions are declared, not hidden. Which exhibited carriers and potentials satisfy (GAP) is open (OB-7).

#### S.6 — Gauge shadow: local resolution of the removable component only

The anchor's gauge relation ("7.5 Gauge, quotient, and the cohomological residual") reads $h \sim h' :\Longleftrightarrow h \oplus h' \in \operatorname{im}(\delta^0)$. Its real shadow under R1–R3 is $h \sim_{\mathbb{R}} h' :\Longleftrightarrow h - h' \in \operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$.

**Proposition S4.** *Under R1–R3 and R5, on $C^1_{\mathbb{R}}$ with the declared inner product:*

*Proof.* (a) $h \perp \operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$ holds exactly when $\langle \delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} f, h\rangle_1 = \langle f, (\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})^{*} h\rangle_0 = 0$ for all $f$, i.e. exactly when $(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})^{*} h = 0$; finite-dimensional orthogonal complementation gives the direct sum. (b) Write $h = h_g + h_r$ per (a); every member of the class of $h$ has the same $h_r$ (members differ by elements of $\operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$), and $\lVert h\rVert^2 = \lVert h_g\rVert^2 + \lVert h_r\rVert^2$ is minimized in the class exactly at $h_g = 0$, i.e. at $h_r$ itself. (c) $L_1^{\mathrm{down}}$ vanishes on $\ker((\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})^{*})$, so the flow is the identity there. On $\operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$: if $L_0 v = \mu v$ with $\mu \ne 0$, then $L_1^{\mathrm{down}}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} v) = \mu\, (\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} v)$ and $\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}} v \ne 0$ (else $\mu v = (\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})^*0 = 0$); since $\operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$ is spanned by such images, the nonzero spectra of $L_1^{\mathrm{down}}$ and $L_0$ coincide, every eigenvalue of $L_1^{\mathrm{down}}$ on $\operatorname{im}(\delta^0_{\mathbb{R}})$ is $\ge \mu_1$, and diagonalizing on that invariant subspace bounds the contraction by $e^{-t\mu_1}$. $\blacksquare$
