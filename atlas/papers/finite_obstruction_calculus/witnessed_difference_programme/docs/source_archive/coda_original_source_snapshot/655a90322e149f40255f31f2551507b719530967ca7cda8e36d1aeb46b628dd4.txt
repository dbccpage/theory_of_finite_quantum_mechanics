## Paper 4 — Duration as an Additive Cochain

GTOC Foundations — Physics-Contact Extension. Weighted Succession, Exactness, Coupled Clocks, and Continuum Debt

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Version:** v0.1.3 (Python-Q residual-classification evidence binding) · **Series:** GTOC Foundations (see ../001_coda/gtoc/00_series_charter.md)

**Requires:** Papers 0–3: retained cuts and recurrence (╬┤0), packet-indexed succession and constitutive order (╬┤1), finite tally and scalar construction (╬┤2), declarations and carrier scope (╬┤3).

**Main claim:** duration is not primitive and is not identified with bare step count. On an admitted finite succession carrier, a duration field is a declared additive edge valuation carried in an ordered abelian group, with an edgewise positivity receipt

$$
\tau:E_{\mathrm{succ}}(P)\longrightarrow G,
\qquad
\tau(e)\in G_{\ge 0}\ \text{ for every admitted edge } e,
$$

where the coefficient packet supplies $G$, its positive cone $G_{\ge0}$, finite addition, equality, and order receipts. The carrier is the group $G$, not the cone: $G_{\ge0}$ is not closed under the subtraction that residuals require, and "1-cochain" is licensed only once the declared cochain contract is exhibited (§3.2). For an explicitly factorized finite chain packet $X$, duration is the sum of $\tau$ over its exhibited leaf-edge **occurrence** family, which is multiplicity-preserving by construction rather than identity-keyed. Unit step-tally is the special gauge $\tau_1(e)=1$. Endpoint-only duration is earned by an exactness certificate $\tau=d_0T$. Residuals are classified, not lumped: **exact** earns endpoint-potential duration; **closed nonexact** witnesses path dependence somewhere in the admitted carrier via an exhibited global period; **active closure failure** exhibits a *local* duration-composition defect; **undetermined** means the packet lacks the receipts to classify. A local defect does not by itself establish a global path-dependence class.

**Non-claim:** no passage, presentness, simultaneity, physical proper time, preferred clock, universal unit, continuum, dynamics, or quantum time is derived.
**Authority:** none. A duration or clock receipt licenses nothing.
**State note:** all constructions inherit Paper 1’s M3, K11, and admissible-$\Sigma$ conditions and Paper 2’s conditional scalar package. No full cochain-complex authority is presumed beyond each explicitly supplied finite carrier and incidence receipt.
**Retention inheritance note:** an evaporated ╬┤0 flicker supplies no handle, edge receipt, interval, duration, or clock reading. Duration is formed only from retained, co-exhibited succession receipts.

Abstract

This paper constructs finite duration as an additive edge valuation on a declared succession carrier, carried in an ordered abelian group with an edgewise positivity receipt. It proves scoped additivity and exactness-based endpoint dependence, separates local-face closure from global periods to classify duration receipts as exact, closed-nonexact, active closure failure, or undetermined, defines coupled clock packets with a typed adequacy outcome, and itemizes the topology, refinement, convergence, and completeness debts that block continuous time and $\exp(tH)$.


§1 — The question and its price

Succession provides an asymmetric relation but no magnitude. A magnitude requires additional data. This paper separates five objects that ordinary language often conflates:

1. a finite succession carrier;
2. an interval packet with explicit endpoints and factorization receipts;
3. a duration edge valuation $\tau$ assigning a weight to each admitted succession edge (a 1-cochain once the §3.2 contract is exhibited);
4. an exactness or residual receipt classifying endpoint dependence;
5. a coupled clock packet comparing one exhibited change against another.

Nothing in this list passes, flows, or becomes present. The construction is tenseless.

§2 — Interval packets without universal token identity

**2.1 Cut handles.** A cut is referred to inside one packet by a **CutHandle**

$$
h=(\mathrm{artifact\_ref},\mathrm{exhibition\_ref},\mathrm{local\_handle}).
$$

Handle equality is a packet validation fact. It is not metaphysical identity across all exhibitions. Cross-packet transport requires a separate representation or transport receipt.

**2.2 Endpoint receipts.** An **EndpointReceipt** binds two CutHandles to one carrier reference and records which is the source and which is the target. It passes only when both handles occur in that carrier and the packet exhibits a K11 witness from source to target.

**2.3 FiniteChainPacket.** A finite chain packet contains:

| Field | Required receipt |
|---|---|
| carrier reference | hash-bound admitted succession carrier |
| cut-handle case table | finite co-exhibited handles, not a universal set |
| immediate-edge receipts | one $\succ_{\Sigma}$ receipt for each displayed leaf edge |
| endpoint receipt | source and target handles on this carrier |
| linearity receipt | stated over **occurrences** (§3.2), not edge identities: exactly one displayed incoming occurrence and exactly one displayed outgoing occurrence at every internal handle; the source handle has no displayed incoming occurrence and exactly one outgoing; the target handle has exactly one incoming and no outgoing; every occurrence belongs to the same connected factorization; no occurrence appears in two leaf positions unless separately tokenized; and the projected leaf occurrences agree with the endpoint and factorization receipts. The earlier "one admitted continuation at every internal handle" is withdrawn as too weak — it admitted branching and disconnected edge families as chains |
| factorization receipts | explicit binary decompositions of the interval into subinterval packets |
| leaf-edge projection receipt | every K11 constructor term used for the interval projects to the displayed leaf-edge family |
| provenance receipt | Witness-Robust or Curated status inherited from each succession edge |

A FiniteChainPacket is therefore more than an asserted walk. The host may not infer missing intermediates, reorder handles, or search an ambient graph to manufacture a chain.

**2.4 Interval extraction.** The interval $[\alpha,\beta]_X$ is the subpacket named by an explicit factorization receipt inside $X$. If a factorization

$$
X_{\alpha\gamma}\cong X_{\alpha\beta}\star X_{\beta\gamma}
$$

is not exhibited, neither subinterval nor additivity at $\beta$ is available. Interval extraction never relies on an unrecorded list position or a hidden traversal.

**2.5 Incomparability.** If no EndpointReceipt and factorized FiniteChainPacket relate two handles, this paper assigns no duration to the pair. It does not infer simultaneity and does not assert that some unknown metric separation exists.

§3 — Duration fields

**3.1 Coefficient packet.** Fix a declared coefficient packet $(G,+,0,\leq,G_{\ge0})$ with machine-checkable finite addition, equality, order, and positive-cone laws. No real-number completeness is used.

**3.2 Duration edge valuation, and when it is a cochain (2026-07-18 review repair).** A duration field on carrier $P$ is first a declared **additive duration edge valuation**

$$
\tau:E_{\mathrm{succ}}(P)\longrightarrow G,
\qquad
\tau(e)\in G_{\ge0}\ \text{ for every admitted edge } e .
$$

> **Why the codomain is $G$ and not $G_{\ge0}$.** The positive cone $G_{\ge0}$ is a cone, **not** a group: it is not closed under the subtraction that coboundaries and signed path differences require, and $d_1\tau$ in §4.2 is literally a difference of two boundary sums. Writing $\tau:E\to G_{\ge0}$ and simultaneously calling it a 1-cochain conflates the **carrier** with the **restriction**. The corrected reading separates three things: the cochain carrier $G$; a **positivity receipt** certifying $\tau(e)\in G_{\ge0}$ edgewise; and the subtraction in $G$ that residuals need. Earlier text writing $\tau:E_{\mathrm{succ}}(P)\to G_{\ge0}$ as the definition of the cochain is withdrawn.

The valuation is called a **1-cochain**, $\tau\in C^1(P;G)$, only once the declared cochain contract is exhibited for the packet; until then "additive duration edge valuation" is the accurate name. This matches the header state note: no unrestricted cochain-complex authority is presumed.

**Edge occurrences, not edge identities.** Duration is summed over an exhibited family of **occurrences**, not of distinct edge handles. Each occurrence is a token

$$
\mathsf{EdgeOccurrence}=(\textit{occurrence\_handle},\ \textit{succession\_edge\_handle},\ \textit{source\_handle},\ \textit{target\_handle},\ \textit{provenance}),
$$

and $L(X)$ is the exhibited collection of occurrences of $X$. This is **structural, defensive typing**: an identity-keyed family would silently collapse any repetition of a succession-edge handle and under-count the duration. Note that intra-chain repetition is *already* excluded here — §2's linearity receipt forces exactly one incoming and one outgoing occurrence at each internal handle, and M3 acyclicity forbids it independently — so no claim in this paper depends on that case being realizable. The typing keeps unit tally, disjoint factorization, reassociation, duration sums, and replay determinism sound against carriers that relax those receipts, rather than repairing a live defect. Where $\#L(X)$ is used, the packet must carry a `TallyWitness` for the occurrence count.

For a factorized chain packet $X_{\alpha\beta}$ with exhibited leaf-edge occurrence family $L(X_{\alpha\beta})$, define

$$
## \operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X_{\alpha\beta})
\\sum_{o\\in L(X_{\\alpha\\beta})}\\tau(\\textit{succession\\_edge\\_handle}(o)).
$$

The finite sum is evaluated only over the displayed case table. A zero value is allowed by the coefficient packet; it does not erase the distinct endpoint handles or their succession receipts.

**3.3 Unit-tally gauge.** The former definition by step count is retained as one special choice:

$$
\tau_1(e)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau_1}(X)=\#L(X).
$$

Calling this “the” duration would silently set every local unit equal. V1 therefore reports it as **UnitTallyGauge**, never as an unqualified metric.

**3.4 Additivity theorem.** Suppose the packet exhibits

$$
X_{\alpha\gamma}\cong X_{\alpha\beta}\star X_{\beta\gamma}
$$

and the factorization receipt proves that the leaf-edge **occurrence** family of $X_{\alpha\gamma}$ is the disjoint exhibited union of the two subfamilies,

$$
L(X_{\alpha\gamma})\;\cong\;L(X_{\alpha\beta})\,\sqcup\,L(X_{\beta\gamma}),
$$

as a decomposition of occurrences (§3.2) rather than of edge identities. Defensively typed, consistent with §3.2: were a shared edge handle to appear in both factors it would have to be carried as two occurrences rather than identified — though §2's linearity receipt and M3 acyclicity already exclude that case on any carrier admitted here. Then

$$
## \operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X_{\alpha\gamma})
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X_{\alpha\beta})
+
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X_{\beta\gamma}).
$$

This follows from finite additivity in the coefficient packet. Without the factorization and disjointness receipts, the equation is not evaluated.

**3.5 K11 reassociation invariance.** Duration is evaluated on the projected leaf-edge family, not on the syntax tree of a K11 witness. Two differently bracketed K11 terms receive the same duration only when a normalization or reassociation receipt proves that both project to the same leaf-edge case table. Associativity of $+$ then equates the sums. The paper does not silently assume that all K11 witness terms are definitionally equal.

**3.6 Ratios, and a fence about density (narrowed 2026-07-18).** A ratio of two nonzero durations uses Paper 2's scalar package and inherits its conditional status; plain finite addition and order comparison do not require that ratio construction.

> **SCOPE FENCE — density is a property of the coefficient object, not a result of this construction.** The admitted coefficient object may contain a dense scalar order. **No density of realized durations, realized ratios, or clock readings follows**, and none is claimed: a fixed finite carrier exhibits finitely many chains and therefore finitely many realized values. The earlier phrasing — that "candidate ratio values inherit that density" — is **withdrawn**: "candidate ratio value" was never a defined object of this paper, and the sentence read as a theorem about the duration construction when it is at most an observation about the scalar package. Nothing here bears on the continuum, which remains `OracleNeeded` at G7 (§5.2).

§4 — Exactness, residuals, and clocks

**4.1 Path comparisons and admitted 2-cells (boundary contract added 2026-07-18).** Two objects must be kept apart. Comparing two common-endpoint chains is *not* the same as exhibiting an admitted 2-cell with an oriented boundary, and only the latter can define $d_1$.

A **`PathComparisonReceipt`** is the weaker object: two factorized `FiniteChainPacket`s $X$ and $Y$ sharing a source `CutHandle` $\alpha$ and target `CutHandle` $\omega$, together with $\tau$ evaluated on the leaf-edge occurrences of both. It compares two path sums and nothing more. **Not every common-endpoint path pair is thereby a declared 2-cell.**

A **`DurationComparisonCell`** is an admitted **2-cell** — a generator of the chain group $C_2$, not an element of a cochain group — and must additionally carry an oriented boundary:

$$
\mathsf{DurationComparisonCell}=
\begin{cases}
\textit{positive\_chain},\ \textit{negative\_chain} & \text{the two factorized chains}\\
\textit{shared\_source},\ \textit{shared\_target} & \text{common endpoint handles}\\
\textit{positive\_orientation\_receipt} & \text{which chain is } X^{+}\\
\textit{boundary\_receipt} & \partial_2 \text{ of the cell, as an oriented 1-chain}\\
\textit{carrier\_binding} & \text{the declared face family this cell belongs to}
\end{cases}
$$

Only with the orientation and boundary receipts — making it an admitted **2-cell**, a generator of the chain group $C_2$ rather than an element of a cochain group — is the evaluation

$$
(d_1\tau)(F)\;=\;\operatorname{Dur}_\tau(X_F^{+})-\operatorname{Dur}_\tau(X_F^{-})
$$

well defined — and note that this is a subtraction in $G$, which is why §3.2 carries the cochain in $G$ rather than in the cone $G_{\ge0}$. The diamond test below is exhibited as a `PathComparisonReceipt`; it becomes a `DurationComparisonCell` only when the orientation, boundary, and carrier-binding receipts are supplied.

**4.1a Worked reproducible example — the diamond tests the unit gauge, not every duration.** Exhibit a `PathComparisonReceipt` (§4.1 — *not* a `DurationComparisonCell`: the diamond needs no orientation, boundary, or carrier-binding receipt to do its work, and claiming one would be the very conflation §4.1 separates) with five packet-local handles with succession edges
$$
\alpha\to\beta\to\omega,
\qquad
\alpha\to\zeta\to\eta\to\omega,
$$
each edge carrying an admissible $\Sigma$ receipt and the whole carrier carrying M3/K11 receipts. The two factorized chain packets share endpoint handles $(\alpha,\omega)$.

For $\tau_1(e)=1$,
$$
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau_1}(X_{\alpha\omega})=2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau_1}(Y_{\alpha\omega})=3.
$$
Thus the unit-tally gauge is not endpoint-only on this carrier. But the same comparison admits lawful nonunit weights: assign $(1,2)$ to the upper edges and $(1,1,1)$ to the lower edges. Both sums are $3$. The comparison therefore refutes universal pair-independence of unit tally; it does not prove that every lawful duration field is path-dependent, and — carrying no boundary receipt — it computes no $d_1\tau$ and classifies nothing.

**4.2 Duration residual (classification repaired 2026-07-18).** Closure and nonexactness must be evaluated against **two different objects**, and the previous three-class table conflated them.

> **The defect in the earlier table.** It read "closed nonexact" as: $d_1\tau=0$ everywhere locally, *but* an exhibited `DurationComparisonCell` witnesses $\Delta\operatorname{Dur}\ne0$. Since $d_1\tau$ is *defined* as the boundary difference of such a cell (§4.1), a cell with nonzero difference **is** a face on which $d_1\tau\ne0$ — which the same table classifies as **open**. The two rows therefore overlapped, and any packet meeting the closed-nonexact condition met the open condition as well. The repair is to fix which family closure is checked on, and to require a genuinely *global* witness for nonexactness.

**Declared local face family.** The packet declares a finite family $F_{\mathrm{local}}$ of admitted 2-cells (§4.1). Closure is the audit

$$
(d_1\tau)(f)=0\qquad\text{for every } f\in F_{\mathrm{local}} .
$$

**Notation for this subsection (added 2026-07-19; these symbols are declared, not imported).** All four are packet-local finite objects on the declared carrier — no ambient chain complex is presumed:

| Symbol | Reading on this carrier |
|---|---|
| $\partial_1$ | the boundary map sending a displayed edge occurrence to (target $-$ source) of its handles; a **1-chain** is a finite formal $\mathbb{Z}$-combination of occurrences |
| $\partial_2$ | the boundary map sending an admitted 2-cell $F\in F_{\mathrm{local}}$ to the oriented 1-chain $X_F^{+}-X_F^{-}$ recorded by its `boundary_receipt` (§4.1) |
| $\ker\partial_1$ | 1-chains whose endpoints cancel — the **cycles** of the carrier |
| $\operatorname{im}\partial_2$ | 1-chains generated by the *declared* faces — the **boundaries**. These are cycles only where the contract supplies $\partial_1\circ\partial_2=0$, the dual of the identity the OBSTRUCTION below declines to presume; absent it, a boundary need not lie in $\ker\partial_1$ |
| $\\langle\\tau,z\\rangle$ | evaluation of the duration valuation on a 1-chain, $\\sum_i n_i\\,\\tau(\\textit{succession\\_edge\\_handle}(o_i))$, taken in $G$ (which is why §3.2 carries $\\tau$ in the group) |

**Global period witness.** Nonexactness requires a cycle that the declared faces do not generate:

$$
z\in\ker\partial_1,\qquad z\notin\operatorname{im}\partial_2,\qquad \langle\tau,z\rangle\ne0 .
$$

A common-endpoint difference *inside* $\operatorname{im}\partial_2$ is not a period — it is a face, and it belongs to the closure audit. With these separated, the classification is coherent:

| Class | Machine condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Exact** | a displayed potential $T$ satisfies $\tau=d_0T$, **and** either no declared face fails closure or the $d_1\circ d_0=0$ contract is exhibited | endpoint-only duration is earned on the scoped carrier |
| **ClosedNonExact** | $(d_1\tau)(f)=0$ for all $f\in F_{\mathrm{local}}$ **and** a nontrivial global period $z$ is exhibited with $\langle\tau,z\rangle\ne0$ | local audits close, yet a global cycle not generated by the declared faces obstructs any endpoint potential; the nonexactness is *witnessed*, not inferred |
| **ActiveClosureFailure** | $(d_1\tau)(f)\ne0$ for at least one $f\in F_{\mathrm{local}}$, **and** either no potential receipt is displayed or the $d_1\circ d_0=0$ contract is exhibited | a *localized* duration-composition defect is exhibited |
| **ExactnessUndetermined** | **no other class's machine condition is met**, **or** the packet exhibits conflicting potential and face receipts absent the $d_1\circ d_0=0$ contract | the packet cannot classify; the host reports the weaker available receipt |

> **A missing exactness receipt is never a proof of nonexactness.** Absence of a displayed potential, *with no exhibited period witness*, routes to `ExactnessUndetermined` and never to `ClosedNonExact`. The qualifier is load-bearing: a `ClosedNonExact` packet has no displayed potential by design, so the unqualified reading would make that class unreachable in exactly its canonical case. The quotient conclusion $[\tau]\ne0$ is drawn only when the finite cochain contract, the quotient contract, the declared face family, **and** an exhibited global period witness are all present. The former class name *open* is renamed `ActiveClosureFailure` because "open" was doing double duty for "a local face fails to close" and "we do not know", which are different terminals.

> **OBSTRUCTION — what the cochain contract buys is that a *bare* potential receipt suffices for `Exact`; the four classes are disjoint either way.** *(Restated 2026-07-19 after a third audit iteration.)*
>
> **Disjointness — all six pairs, and exhaustiveness (completed 2026-07-19 under an operator budget extension).** `ExactnessUndetermined`'s first disjunct is **residual**: it fires exactly when no other class's machine condition is met. That single change makes both properties provable together, where the earlier "the required receipts are incomplete" phrasing could not — read broadly it overlapped three pairs in *canonical* cases, read narrowly it made classification depend on which class the host attempted.
>
> **The residual definition is well-founded.** `Exact`, `ClosedNonExact`, and `ActiveClosureFailure` each state their machine conditions **without reference to** `ExactnessUndetermined`, so \"no other class's machine condition is met\" is not circular. This is what licenses the complementation argument below; were any of the three to refer back to `ExactnessUndetermined`, the construction would collapse.
>
> **EU's two disjuncts overlap, and that is deliberate.** Whenever the second fires — a potential plus a failing face, absent the contract — all three other classes are excluded, so the residual fires too: the second disjunct is *subsumed* by the first. It is retained because it names the canonical undetermined case explicitly rather than leaving it implicit in a complement. Overlap *within* one class is a union, not a classification defect; a later editor should not \"simplify\" by deleting either.
>
> **Exhaustiveness** is then immediate: `ExactnessUndetermined` is by definition the complement of the other three, so every packet lands in at least one class.
>
> **The six pairs.** Among the three substantive classes:
> `Exact` $\cap$ `ActiveClosureFailure` $=\varnothing$ — ACF exhibits a failing face, which kills `Exact`'s first disjunct, so `Exact` would need the contract; but contract plus $\tau=d_0T$ gives $(d_1\tau)(f)=(d_1\circ d_0)T(f)=0$ on every face, contradiction.
> `Exact` $\cap$ `ClosedNonExact` $=\varnothing$ **unconditionally** — for $z\in\ker\partial_1$, $\langle d_0T,z\rangle=\langle T,\partial_1 z\rangle=0$ by adjointness of $d_0$ to $\partial_1$ alone, $\partial_2$ never entering, so no exact field admits a nonzero period.
> `ClosedNonExact` $\cap$ `ActiveClosureFailure` $=\varnothing$ by construction — closure holds on every declared face in the first and fails on some declared face in the second.
>
> **The three pairs against `ExactnessUndetermined`** now split by disjunct. Against the **first** (residual) disjunct, `Exact` $\cap$ `EU`, `ClosedNonExact` $\cap$ `EU`, and `ActiveClosureFailure` $\cap$ `EU` are all empty **by construction**: that disjunct fires only when no other class's condition is met, so it cannot co-fire with any of them. Against the **second** disjunct — a packet holding both a potential and a failing face, absent the contract — `Exact` is excluded because its own second conjunct then fails, `ActiveClosureFailure` by the matching guard in its second conjunct, and `ClosedNonExact` because a failing declared face contradicts its closure requirement. Six of six.
>
> *Why the residual reading is the honest one.* A `ClosedNonExact` packet has **no displayed potential by design** — the period witness is precisely what shows none exists — so under the old "required receipts are incomplete" phrasing it satisfied EU as well, and the same held for an `ActiveClosureFailure` packet with no potential. Those were canonical cases, not edge cases. Making the disjunct residual removes the overlap without weakening any other class.
>
> **What is genuinely obstructed.** Not disjointness — the *strength of a potential receipt*. With $d_1\circ d_0=0$ supplied by the declared cochain contract, $\tau=d_0T$ **alone** certifies `Exact`. The header state note declines full cochain-complex authority, so **this paper does not presume $d_1\circ d_0=0$**; absent it a potential receipt must be accompanied by a clean face audit, and a packet displaying a potential *and* a failing face routes to `ExactnessUndetermined`. **Registered as PL-GTOC-35** (`proof_ledgers/proof_ledger_20260719.md`).
>
> *What the earlier wording got wrong, recorded rather than silently swapped.* It said the `Exact` / `ActiveClosureFailure` exclusion "is not free". Under the current narrowed conditions that exclusion is **definitional** — `Exact`'s own second conjunct supplies the contract or rules out a failing face — so the claim described a superseded version of this table. It also pointed at the wrong pair: the genuine overlap was `ActiveClosureFailure` $\cap$ `ExactnessUndetermined`, and it went undisclosed. This is the **third iteration of one defect** — the original three-class table overlapped; the first repair relocated the overlap to `Exact`/`ExactnessUndetermined`; the second relocated it to `ActiveClosureFailure`/`ExactnessUndetermined`. The guards are now carried by **both** classes that can see a conflicting receipt. **A fourth iteration was nevertheless found** (2026-07-19, terminal adversary cycle): the defect did not relocate to another class pair but into `ExactnessUndetermined`'s first disjunct, the one clause never given a machine condition. Under its broad reading three pairs overlap in *canonical* cases — a `ClosedNonExact` packet has no displayed potential **by design**, since the period witness is what shows none exists, so it satisfies "the required potential receipt is incomplete". Under a narrow reading disjointness holds but classification depends on which class the host attempts, which a machine condition cannot do. **This *was* registered OPEN below and is discharged in the note that follows**: at the time of writing the adversary budget for this paper was exhausted, and applying an unaudited fix to machinery that had failed four audits is exactly what produced the previous three iterations. The operator subsequently extended the budget for one pass, and the repair was applied and independently verified.

> **DISCHARGED 2026-07-19 (operator budget extension) — the classification is now proved disjoint and exhaustive together.** The terminal adversary cycle established that §4.2 could not support both properties as written and specified the repair; it was carried here unapplied while the adversary budget was exhausted, and the operator subsequently extended the budget for exactly this pass. Applied: `ExactnessUndetermined`'s first disjunct is now **residual**, and the two missing pair arguments (`Exact`$\cap$`ExactnessUndetermined`, `ClosedNonExact`$\cap$`ExactnessUndetermined`) are supplied above, alongside the third EU pair, giving six of six. No change was needed to `Exact`, `ClosedNonExact`, `ActiveClosureFailure`, or PL-GTOC-35, and no new mathematics was introduced — the repair is a definitional tightening. **This was the fourth iteration of one defect and the first pass to close it rather than relocate it**; the difference is that the residual disjunct removes the unconditioned clause the defect kept migrating into, instead of adding another guard beside it.

**4.2a Python-Q executable evidence for the classification (PR #1345; finite and partial).** Merged [PR #1345](https://github.com/dbccpage/anomalon/pull/1345) binds an exact-rational Python implementation to this four-way decision surface. On four explicitly displayed finite $\mathbb Q$-linear carriers, `THE_OBSTRUCTION_CRUCIBLE.py` plus `duration_profile.py` reproduces `Exact`, `ClosedNonExact`, `ActiveClosureFailure`, and `ExactnessUndetermined`. The `ClosedNonExact` fixture carries a separately typed period $z$ with

$$

**4.3 Exact duration theorem.** If a potential receipt exhibits $T$ with
$$
\tau(e)=T(\operatorname{target}e)-T(\operatorname{source}e)
$$
for every displayed succession edge, then every factorized chain sum telescopes:
$$
\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X_{\alpha\beta})=T(\beta)-T(\alpha).
$$
Hence all admitted chain packets with the same endpoint handles receive the same value. This paper names this outcome **exactness-certified endpoint dependence** (formerly termed "packet-local absolute duration"). It is strictly relative to admitted paths in the fixed carrier, and is nothing more than a discrete telescoping sum. It is not absolute across every exhibition, nor does it imply a universal physical frame.

**Corollary (monotone potential).** If in addition the positivity receipt of §3.2 holds — $\tau(e)\in G_{\ge0}$ for every admitted edge — then $\tau=d_0T$ gives $T(\operatorname{target}e)-T(\operatorname{source}e)\ge 0$, so **$T$ is monotone non-decreasing along every admitted succession edge**, and hence along every admitted chain by §3.4. This is a consequence of positivity plus exactness, not of exactness alone; it licenses no ordering between handles that no admitted chain connects.

**Converse (rooted, edge-compatibility form; economized 2026-07-18).** On a finite rooted connected carrier it suffices to exhibit:

1. one root-to-handle chain packet for **every** handle $h$ (root coverage);
2. $T(h)$ defined as the duration of that *selected* chain; and
3. for **every** admitted edge $u\to v$, a comparison receipt proving $T(v)=T(u)+\tau(u\to v)$.

Then $\tau=d_0T$. Step 3 is per-edge compatibility with the constructed potential — it does **not** require comparing every pair of alternative paths, which the earlier formulation ("a receipt that all alternative admitted paths have equal duration") demanded and which is unnecessary: a finite packet needs only enough coverage to certify each edge against the selected chains. Without root coverage and per-edge compatibility receipts, "path-independent implies exact" is not claimed.

> **Endpoint-only duration is not primitive. On a fixed admitted carrier, it is earned by an exactness certificate.** "Absolute duration" survives only as an external gloss that this paper explicitly rejects.

**4.4 Coupled clock packet.** A clock is not merely another unrelated tally. A **ClockPacket** contains:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|

| calibration receipt | declares the value or ratio assigned to one coupled comparison |
| readout rule | finite sum or conditional ratio computation |
| coverage receipt | identifies exactly which target intervals are measured |
| stability receipt | states which conservative re-exhibitions preserve the calibration |

| **nonzero-denominator receipt** | any *ratio* readout requires the reference duration to be nonzero in $G$; a zero reference issues no ratio |
| **unit/dimension compatibility receipt** | target and reference values are comparable only under a declared conversion or unit contract; without it the readout is not formed |
| **coupling functionality receipt** | the coupling maps each target interval in scope to exactly one reference interval; a target silently mapping to several incompatible reference intervals is refused unless the packet explicitly declares a relation-valued readout |

\mathsf{ClockAdequacy}=

Only `ValidReading` licenses a duration comparison, and it licenses it *within the declared coverage and calibration scope only*. Every other outcome is a refusal that names its own cause; none of them is a reading with a caveat.

> A clock does not measure time from outside the world. It compares one exhibited change against another.

§5 — Boundaries and contact points

**5.1 Passage and presentness.** A precedence structure does not pass. A duration does not elapse. Nothing in succession or duration constitutes passage, and no handle is selected as present. Passage and presentness remain unconstructed.

**5.2 Continuous duration.** Completeness is necessary for the intended real-valued continuum construction but is not the only debt. A continuum packet would need, at minimum:

1. a topology or convergence structure;
2. a directed refinement system for finite chain packets;
3. embeddings or comparison maps between refinements;
4. convergence receipts for duration sums;
5. compatibility of addition and order with limits;
6. representation-transport and gauge-stability receipts;
7. a finite-shadow certificate binding every continuum claim back to checked finite packets;
8. the required completeness principle.

Until all of these are admitted and verified, continuous duration is **OracleNeeded at G7**, not latent inside dense scalar values and not recovered from completeness alone. [Paper 9 — Space and Extension](../008_space_and_extension/008_space_and_extension.md) constructs only the finite part of items (1)–(3): a topology from an explicitly supplied finite order (its §2), monotone refinement maps with derived continuity, and their continuity receipts. It does **not** supply cofinality, convergence, completeness, or the bridge from finite DDA/CODA-A replay to a semigroup or $\exp(tH)$ — Paper 9's own P9-C8 records ─îech comparison, cofinality, convergence, and continuum promotion as `[OPEN / OracleNeeded]`, and its refinement boundary states plainly that one map is not a directed system, a directed family is not cofinality, cofinality is not convergence, and convergence is not completeness. The QM audit's M11 gap remains open.

**5.3 Zeno.** At ╬┤4 there is no refinement order, no same-interval transport across refinements, and no limit object. **The classical *infinite-refinement* Zeno problem** therefore cannot yet be formulated in this language, because no infinite refinement or limit object has been admitted; it is not solved or dissolved. The narrower reading matters: a *finite* chain of decreasing durations is already perfectly formulable here — it is an ordinary admitted chain packet — and exhibits no regress. What is unavailable is the infinite subdivision the classical argument requires. A later Q-gated packet may reopen it by supplying refinement, transport, convergence, and limit receipts. Atomicity is claimed only for an edge inside one fixed FiniteChainPacket.

**5.4 CODA-A contact.** Finite CODA-A path sums may use $\operatorname{Dur}_{\tau}(X)$ as a grading of an exhibited chain packet. The special unit-tally grading is only one case. Any expression involving a real parameter $t$, a continuum semigroup, or a continuum path integral inherits §5.2’s OracleNeeded boundary.

**5.5 Physical proper time.** There is a fenced structural resonance: active-closure-failure or closed-nonexact duration fields can distinguish admitted paths with common endpoints, while exact fields cannot. No Lorentzian metric, signature, invariant interval, dynamics, or empirical law is present. The paper does not claim that path dependence is universal or that relativistic proper time has been derived.

**5.6 Simultaneity and units.** Incomparability is not simultaneity. A unit is part of the coefficient or calibration packet, not a universal scale. Neither a global slice nor seconds are obtained.

**5.7 The Lynds boundary: interval readings without an instant or chronon ontology.** [INTERPRETIVE] Peter Lynds's 2003 paper treats time operationally as a relative interval reading indicated by a clock, denies a literal physical flow, privileges relative order over reversible direction, and rejects placing the universe in an external time. That comparison sharpens this paper's clock boundary: a `ClockPacket` reading is the output of an exhibited coupling and calibration over a finite interval packet, not a measurement made from an external temporal viewpoint.

[SCOPE FENCE] The agreement does not license Lynds's stronger claim that every physical magnitude must be ontically indeterminate. An exact coordinate value is compatible with nonzero mathematical change - for example, $x(t)=t$ is exact at every real argument while $dx/dt=1$. This is a countermodel to *exact point value implies stasis*, not a physical ontology. Conversely, the present paper does not claim that clock readings necessarily have non-singleton physical support unless a resolution model is separately supplied.

A finite succession edge is an incidence receipt, not a chronon. Its duration value may be zero or nonunit under the admitted coefficient packet, and no indivisible universal time quantum is asserted. Finiteness therefore neither proves discrete physical time nor rules out a later continuum lift. The topology, refinement, transport, convergence, finite-shadow, and completeness debts of §5.2 remain open; Zeno remains neither solved nor dissolved.
