**3. Keep stalled states inside the mathematical losing region.**

The producer constructs

```python

```

and then requires closure inside `U`.

That breaks the simple losing game

$$
s\xrightarrow{1}d,\qquad \mathrm{Adm}(d)=\varnothing.
$$

The only successor of \(s\) is losing, but has been removed from its trap. The `WIN` branch encounters the same defect when certifying its losing complement, causing `StopIteration`.

Use the entire losing region:

$$
U=L.
$$

For each non-stalled \(b\in L\), certify

$$
\forall a\in\mathrm{Adm}(b),\qquad
\phi(b,a)\in\mathrm{Succ}(b,a)\cap L.
$$

At dead ends, this condition is vacuous. You can still report `Stalled` with higher priority. **Reporting precedence should not change the set used in the proof.**

**5. Goal-resolvable does not imply that immediate emission is optimal.**

§S.1 concludes

$$

$$

Generally, successful immediate emission only proves

$$
V(b)\le
\min_{r\text{ valid at }b}c(b,\operatorname{emit}(r)).
$$

Another valid emission might be cheaper. A preliminary action might also lead to a cheaper successful emission.

Equality needs additional assumptions about available actions and costs. Your examples can declare those assumptions, but the general definition cannot infer them.
