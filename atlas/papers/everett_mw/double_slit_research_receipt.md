## 2. Independent literature checked

- **Jaeger, Shimony, Vaidman, Phys. Rev. A 51, 54 (1995); Englert, Phys. Rev. Lett. 77, 2154 (1996).** Confirmed via F. De Zela's comment (arXiv:1305.0861): inequality D² + V² ≤ 1, first derived by Jaeger–Shimony–Vaidman and independently by Englert; De Zela repairs a step in Englert's proof without changing the result. INSPIRE record 439199 did not render (client-side app), so the bibliographic data come from the comment's reference list. `check_math.py` confirms the pure-marker equality V² + D² = 1 with V = |⟨d_L|d_R⟩| and D the trace distance of the two marker states.
- **Steane, "A quantum computer only needs one universe", arXiv:quant-ph/0003084v3, DOI 10.1016/S1355-2198(03)00038-8.** Abstract and key sentences read from the source. His argument: quantum superposition does not let a computer "perform many computations simultaneously" except in a "highly qualified and to some extent misleading sense"; the essential element is entanglement, not superposition; Shor's algorithm works through interferometric cancellation that requires the terms to form one coherent system; "The impression of vast parallel computation ... is a false impression engendered by an imperfect mathematical notation." The paper's one-sentence summary matches this. Journal volume and pages (34, 469–478, 2003) are as given in the task brief; the arXiv page shows only the DOI.
- **Everett, Rev. Mod. Phys. 29, 454 (1957).** Standard reference; QM_094 records a DOI check of its metadata on 2026-07-17.
- **Deutsch, The Fabric of Reality (1997).** The Shor's-algorithm passage was found verbatim in a public quotation list (Goodreads, quote 7175129) and, in part, in a 1999 mailing-list post that gives "p 217"; the printed book was not available in this environment. The verified text reads: "The argument of Chapter 2, applied to any interference phenomenon destroys the classical idea that there is only one universe. Logically, the possibility of complex quantum computations adds nothing to a case that is already unanswerable. But it does add psychological impact. ... When Shor's algorithm has factorized a number, using 10^500 or so times the computational resources that can be seen to be present, where was the number factorized? There are only about 10^80 atoms in the entire visible universe ..." Two consequences: (a) the phrase quoted in QM_091 Appendix A (D5) matches this text, so that part of its open quotation obligation can be discharged against the same source; (b) Deutsch himself says the computational argument adds only "psychological impact" to a case he regards as settled by interference, so the paper presents the interference argument as his real case and the Shor challenge as rhetorical reinforcement. Chapter numbers (2 "Shadows", 9 "Quantum Computers") are from the book's table of contents as known to the reviewer; the passage itself cites "the argument of Chapter 2".
- **Scully, Englert, Walther, Nature 351, 111 (1991).** Micromaser which-way detectors that leave the atom's centre-of-mass motion essentially unchanged. The "no momentum kick" reading was contested (Storey, Tan, Collett, Walls 1994) and defended (Englert, Scully, Walther 1995); the paper's wording follows the authors' claim, and the overlap accounting itself does not depend on it.
- **Kim, Yu, Kulik, Shih, Scully, Phys. Rev. Lett. 84, 1 (2000)** (delayed-choice eraser): the unconditioned signal shows no fringes; fringes appear only in idler-sorted subsets, with the two subsets shifted by half a period. `check_math.py` reproduces the bookkeeping. Not cited in the paper for space.

## 3. Adversarial review: corrections made before writing

1. **QM_091 Appendix A ("finiteness contradiction", `D1 ∧ D5(literal) ⊢ ⊥`).** The derivation reads "finitely realizable physical system" as "realizable with the resources present in one universe". Deutsch denies exactly that premise: for him the multiverse is the physical system. The argument therefore begs the question and would not survive being put to Deutsch. The paper does not use it; horn (ii) of the appendix (the 10^500 is the size of the description, not a count of substrates) is the defensible point and is what Steane argues. Recommend retagging the appendix claim from `[PROVEN — textual/logical, conditional]` to `[INTERPRETIVE]` with the single-universe premise stated, and discharging the open quotation-verification obligation.
2. **`papers/foundations/theory_of_finite_quantum_foundations.md` §7.1** calls it "a foundational result" that the standard interpretations share identical predictions. That is true by definition for interpretations that leave the formalism untouched and false for objective-collapse models, as QM_090's own Reading B notes. Not a result; not used.
3. **QM_094 reduced-state formula.** The cited Lean closure theorem (`ds_qm094_everett_reduced_state_overlap_isometric_refinement_and_collapse_refutation`) is a literal integer identity, not the formula. The formula is a correct written proof under imported finite Hilbert/tensor/partial-trace structure (claim rows QCE-C1, QCE-C2 `[PROVEN, CONDITIONAL]`), and its content is textbook quantum mechanics. The paper cites the physics literature for it and the repository only for the kernel-checked example.
4. **Born rule.** Disclosed as a supplied input. The repository's own evidence: WDP §9.2 (normalization does not select q = 2), `FQ-GAP-BORN-UNIQUENESS-GLEASON` `CONDITIONAL`, Q03 ladder (Born readout supplied). No sentence of the paper presents it as derived from difference.
5. **Decoherence.** QM_043 is `IMPORTED_ASSUMPTION`; the paper makes no claim that decoherence is derived from primitive difference. Decoherence is presented as the overlap going to zero in the unconditioned screen distribution, and explicitly distinguished from collapse (the combined state stays coherent; QM_094 Proposition 7.2 says the same).
6. **Deutsch, fair attribution.** The paper attributes to Deutsch (a) the interference-based argument for parallel universes, which he calls "already unanswerable", and (b) the Shor's-algorithm challenge, quoted from the verified passage and described, as he describes it, as adding psychological impact. Everett is described only as holding that every term is equally real (his 1957 footnote), not as using the word "worlds". The Many-Worlds paragraph says the reading "is coherent and is held by serious physicists" and that "none of this disproves many worlds".
7. **No internal vocabulary in public text.** The paper and replies contain none of: WDP, LGGR, L1, L2, CODA, Genesis, packet, terminal, authority, fiber, quotient. The report-fiber idea appears only in plain language.

**Examiner checklist.** (1) Standard interference mathematics reproduced: yes, verified numerically. (2) Probabilities, amplitudes, phases, detector states distinguished: yes. (3) Premises explicit: amplitudes, squared-size rule, combined state, overlap. (4) Which-path explained without consciousness: yes. (5) Decoherence vs collapse: yes (two cautions paragraph). (6) No claim that terms are demonstrated worlds: correct. (7) No claim that Many-Worlds is disproved: correct, stated twice. (8) Deutsch fairly represented: yes, both arguments named. (9) Internal formal result vs physical result: the repository is cited only for a kernel-checked algebraic example, with its imports named. (10) Readable without physics training: the only symbols are a, b, |·|², Re, Ψ, ⟨d_L|d_R⟩, each introduced in words.

## 4. Residual scientific obligations

The squared-size probability rule, the complex amplitudes, the tensor-product combined state and the partial trace are all supplied inputs in the audited sources, so the paper can report what the formalism predicts but cannot claim that any of it follows from primitive difference. The reduced-state/overlap formula in QM_094 has written conditional proofs but no general Lean statement, and `DS-QM094` stays `COMPUTATIONALLY_CHECKED` until QC-EVE-PL1/PL2 are formalized. The repository has no theorem that excludes Many-Worlds, only the non-entailment of QM_094 Proposition 7.1 (relabelling terms as worlds adds no prediction), so no stronger public claim is licensed. QM_091 Appendix A needs the retagging and quotation check described above before it can be cited anywhere public. No audited source bridges the report-fiber criterion to physical decoherence, so the paper keeps that idea at the level of plain-language bookkeeping rather than a derivation.

## 5. PR #497 review corrections

The public note now describes environmental decoherence as effectively irreversible when control of the distributed records is impractical. It no longer claims that the overlap must be exactly zero forever. Coherent reversal before detection differs from sorting recorded hits or discarding and resetting a detector. The classroom version carries the same distinction.

The companion math note now includes path priors in visibility and distinguishability, and an explicit rational unitary dilation of its dephasing channel. The seven tests in `test_measurement_boundaries.py` check these boundaries with fringe scans, weighted trace distances, exact rational matrix arithmetic, and finite record/reversal examples. The `everett-checks` workflow runs them alongside the existing scripts. These are computational checks, not new Lean proofs or collapse evidence.
