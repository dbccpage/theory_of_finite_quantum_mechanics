2. John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, and Jeffrey D. Ullman.
   *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*.

These references place partition refinement and minimal quotients in their
classical setting. The paper supplies its own proofs for the declared finite
Moore-machine model; no unavailable repository module is a premise.
