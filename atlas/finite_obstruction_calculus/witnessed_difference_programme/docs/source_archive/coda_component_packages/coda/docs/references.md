# References — CODA Monograph

Bibliographic register of foundational literature, formalization environments, and companion monographs for the Calculus of Obstruction, Descent, and Authority (CODA).

1. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *CODA: The Calculus of Obstruction, Descent, and Authority*. CODA Monograph Series, Version 2.0.0. Deposit candidate; DOI not yet assigned.
   - Supports: Unified foundation, image-factorization criteria, cochain quotient diagnostics, and the distinction between evidence and authority.

2. Mac Lane, S. (1998). *Categories for the Working Mathematician*. 2nd ed. Graduate Texts in Mathematics, vol. 5. Springer-Verlag, New York, NY. ISBN: 978-0-387-98403-2. DOI: 10.1007/978-1-4757-4721-8.
   - Supports: Universal factorizations, quotient categories, functorial preservation, and limits/colimits underlying Section 2 and Section 9.

3. Mac Lane, S. (1963). *Homology*. Die Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften, Band 114. Springer-Verlag, Berlin, Heidelberg. ISBN: 978-3-540-58662-3. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-642-62029-4.
   - Supports: Cochain complexes, coboundary operators, nilpotence condition d₁ ∘ d₀ = 0, cocycles and quotient cohomology underlying Section 4.

4. Rockafellar, R. T. (1970). *Convex Analysis*. Princeton Mathematical Series, vol. 28. Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ. ISBN: 978-0-691-01586-6. DOI: 10.1515/9781400873173.
   - Supports: Compact sublevel slice attainment for convex functions (§27), subgradient calculus, and linear programming duality underlying Theorem CODA-05.

5. Schrijver, A. (1986). *Theory of Linear and Integer Programming*. Wiley-Interscience Series in Discrete Mathematics and Optimization. John Wiley & Sons, Chichester. ISBN: 978-0-471-90854-8.
   - Supports: Farkas' lemma, polyhedral separation, strong LP duality, and exact rational arithmetic solvability of linear programs underlying the exact solvers in code/coda_core.py.

6. Rudin, W. (1991). *Functional Analysis*. 2nd ed. International Series in Pure and Applied Mathematics. McGraw-Hill, New York, NY. ISBN: 978-0-07-054236-5.
   - Supports: Quotient spaces by closed linear subspaces, quotient seminorm properties, and attainment in finite dimensions underlying Section 4.

7. Steenrod, N. E. (1951). *The Topology of Fibre Bundles*. Princeton Mathematical Series, vol. 14. Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ. ISBN: 978-0-691-00548-5.
   - Supports: Classical foundations of obstruction cocycles and extension theory, providing topological motivation for discrete obstruction diagnostics.

8. de Moura, L., & Ullrich, S. (2021). The Lean 4 Theorem Prover and Programming Language. In *Automated Deduction – CADE 28*, edited by A. Platzer and G. Sutcliffe, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 12699, pp. 625–635. Springer, Cham. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-030-79876-5_37.
   - Supports: Interactive theorem prover architecture, dependent type theory, elaboration kernel, and Lake build system used for the formal verification in lean4/.

9. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *CODA System Foundations and Architecture*. Sealed Derivation v1 (commit 3100c4e, cut 2026-08-17). URL: <https://github.com/dbccpage/19L57G1/tree/sealed-derivation-v1>.
   - Supports: Repository of Record baseline establishing pipeline invariants and exact rational constraints.

10. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *The Omega Tower: Role Non-Impersonation and Authority Preservation in Agentic AI* (Paper 004). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/records/20710065>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.4 (CODA-22; Role Non-Impersonation Invariant), category separation of proposals, evaluations, and authorizations, and restriction of solver agents from issuing self-authorized execution grants.

11. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *The Authority Gap: Epistemic Verification Does Not Entail Normative Authorization* (Paper 005). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/records/20684965>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.5 (CODA-23; Authority Gap Invariant), establishing that vanishing mathematical residuals or valid dual witness certificates do not entail administrative or operational entitlement to execute state changes.

12. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *The Authority Firewall: A Machine-Checked Separation of Evidence from Authority in Finite Systems* (Paper 027). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/records/20439167>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.3 (CODA-21; Coproduct Separation Invariant), the coproduct decomposition \(\mathbb{T} := \mathbb{T}_{\mathsf{auth}} \amalg \mathbb{T}_{\mathrm{res}} \amalg \mathbb{F}_{\mathrm{term}}\), and the seven FDA halt guards (g₁ accepted, g₂ deterministic, g₃ zero_residual_mass, g₄ skeleton_preserving, g₅ exhaustive, g₆ no_pending_lift, g₇ no_refusal) required for admission to \(\mathbb{T}_{\mathsf{auth}}\).

13. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *Finite Authority and Non-Terminal Computation* (Paper 028). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/records/20725629>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.6 (CODA-24; Reasoning Authority Non-Generation Invariant), domain-preserving non-terminal computation limits, and bounded budget requirements (\(T < T_{\max}\)) preventing token synthesis.

14. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *The Anomalon Boundary: Primitive Difference, Structured Residuals, and Persistent Defect Registries* (Paper 029). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/records/20710065>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.7 (CODA-25; Anomalon Boundary Invariant), partition of primitive ontology \(\mathrm{Real}_0 = \text{Diff} \cup \text{Struct}\), and retention of non-zero optimal quotient defects in append-only fingerprint registries.

15. Carroll, J. H. (2026). *Authority Firewall and Verification Limits: Undecidability, Self-Reference, and Bounded Halting in Finite Formal Systems* (Paper 030). F004 Finite Obstruction Monograph Series. Zenodo. DOI/Record: <https://zenodo.org/uploads/22884718>.
    - Supports: Policy 8.8 (CODA-26; Verification Limits Invariant), halting closed into \(\mathbb{F}_{\mathrm{term}}(\text{refusal})\) with explicit scope documentation upon encountering undecidable or ungrounded self-referential authorization envelopes.
