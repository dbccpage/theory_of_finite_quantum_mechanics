## Formal boundary and runtime repair notes

- `Coda.CSQ.image_factor_unique` proves equality of two factors satisfying recovery and equal-value invariance on the explicit-witness image carrier.
- Python domain/state/profile mutations are checked afresh on each solve. A successful lookup certifies the observed finite pairs only; callables must be deterministic and non-mutating for use as mathematical maps.
- `ImagePoint` requires a bound profile map and revalidates its witness; `evaluate_factored` requires a supplied factor and checks agreement at that witness.
- All four admission flags require actual booleans. Empty evaluation domains are refused by runtime policy even though empty-domain mathematical implications can be vacuous.
- BRIDGE-004/005 remain predicate definitions and discrete convenience adapters. They do not establish exponential or Hamilton–Jacobi generator theorems.
