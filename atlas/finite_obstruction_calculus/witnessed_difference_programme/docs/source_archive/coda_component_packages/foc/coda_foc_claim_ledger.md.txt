## 1. Algebraic & Discrete Cochain Foundation

The mathematical substrate is a finite cochain complex of finite-dimensional real spaces (with exact rational representations in software):
$$C^0 \xrightarrow{d_0} C^1 \xrightarrow{d_1} C^2 \quad \text{with} \quad d_1 \circ d_0 = 0,$$
augmented by an auxiliary stage-2 linear repair space $U^2 \xrightarrow{a_2} C^2$, and strictly positive observer coordinate weight vectors $w_1 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^{\dim C^1}$ and $w_2 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^{\dim C^2}$.

Linear repair subspaces are defined by exact linear images:
$$M_1 := \operatorname{im}(d_0) \subseteq C^1, \qquad M_2 := \operatorname{im}(a_2) \subseteq C^2.$$
Quotient spaces and weighted $\ell_1$ quotient seminorms:
$$Q^1 := C^1 / M_1, \qquad Q^2 := C^2 / M_2,$$
$$\Phi_1([x]) := \min_{f \in C^0} \|x + d_0 f\|_{1, w_1}, \qquad \Gamma_2([d_1 x]) := \min_{u \in U^2} \|d_1 x + a_2 u\|_{1, w_2}.$$

The stage-2 defect evaluation is invariant under all stage-1 repairs: $d_1(x + d_0 f) = d_1 x$ since $d_1 d_0 = 0$.

## 3. Detailed Specifications with Hypotheses and Bounds

### 3.1 Mathematical Complex & Boundary Bounds

#### FOC-D1: Valid Packet Contract

- **Hypotheses:** Real vector spaces $C^0, C^1, C^2, U^2$ with finite integer dimensions $n_0, n_1, n_2, m_2 \ge 0$. Matrices $d_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_0}$, $d_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_2 \times n_1}$, $a_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_2 \times m_2}$. Weight vectors $w_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1}$, $w_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{n_2}$.
- **Conditions:**
  1. Dimension compatibility: $\operatorname{cols}(d_0) = n_0$, $\operatorname{rows}(d_0) = n_1 = \operatorname{cols}(d_1)$, $\operatorname{rows}(d_1) = n_2 = \operatorname{rows}(a_2)$, $\operatorname{cols}(a_2) = m_2$.
  2. Coboundary nilpotence: $\|d_1 d_0\|_\infty = 0$ (exact algebraic zero).
  3. Weight strict positivity: $\min_i w_{1, i} > 0$ and $\min_j w_{2, j} > 0$.
- **Refusal Behavior:** Any packet failing these conditions is rejected with `ValueError` or `TypeError` prior to optimization.

#### FOC-T1 & FOC-T2: Quotient Norm Attainment and LP Duality

- **Hypotheses:** Subspace $M = \operatorname{im}(A)$ where $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$, weight vector $w \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^n$, input vector $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
- **Primal Problem:** $\min_{y \in \mathbb{R}^m, r^+, r^- \ge 0} \sum_{i=1}^n w_i (r_i^+ + r_i^-)$ subject to $x + A y = r^+ - r^-$.
- **Dual Problem:** $\max_{z \in \mathbb{R}^n} z^T x$ subject to $A^T z = 0$ and $-w_i \le z_i \le w_i$ for all $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$.
- **Bounds:**
  - Primal objective $\|x + A y\|_{1, w} \ge 0$.
  - Dual box constraint: $|z_i| \le w_i$ for every $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$, equivalently $\max_i \frac{|z_i|}{w_i} \le 1$.
  - Exact zero duality gap: $\sum_{i=1}^n w_i \vert r_i^* \vert - z^{*T} x = 0$.
- **Full-Subspace Requirement:** Annihilation $A^T z = 0$ applies to the full span $\operatorname{im}(A)$, not a proper generating subset.

#### FOC-T3: Exhaustive Diagnostic Trichotomy

- **Hypotheses:** Valid packet $P$, defect vector $x \in C^1$.
- **Partition:**
  1. `ExactRepair`: $\Phi_1([x]) = 0$. Implies $x \in \operatorname{im}(d_0)$, whence $d_1 x = 0 \in \operatorname{im}(a_2)$, so $\Gamma_2([d_1 x]) = 0$.
  2. `CoherentResidual`: $\Phi_1([x]) > 0$ and $\Gamma_2([d_1 x]) = 0$. The defect is non-exact at stage 1, but its boundary defect vanishes in the stage-2 quotient.
  3. `NonclosedDefect`: $\Phi_1([x]) > 0$ and $\Gamma_2([d_1 x]) > 0$. The defect is obstructed at both stage 1 and stage 2.
- **Exclusivity & Totality:** Disjoint union $\mathbb{R}^{n_1} = \mathcal{S}_1 \sqcup \mathcal{S}_2 \sqcup \mathcal{S}_3$. The Lean sources mechanize the abstract decidable partition and a separation kernel conditional on primitives P1–P4. The numerical quotient construction and its trichotomy are proved in prose and exercised by exact Python examples.

#### FOC-T4: Direct-Sum Additivity

- **Hypotheses:** Packets $P = (C, d, a, w)$ and $P' = (C', d', a', w')$. Direct sum packet $P \oplus P'$ with block maps:
  $$d_0^{\oplus} = \begin{pmatrix} d_0 & 0 \\ 0 & d_0' \end{pmatrix}, \quad d_1^{\oplus} = \begin{pmatrix} d_1 & 0 \\ 0 & d_1' \end{pmatrix}, \quad a_2^{\oplus} = \begin{pmatrix} a_2 & 0 \\ 0 & a_2' \end{pmatrix}, \quad w_1^{\oplus} = (w_1, w_1').$$
- **Additivity Bound:**
  $$\Phi_{1, P \oplus P'}\left(\begin{bmatrix} x \\ x' \end{bmatrix}\right) = \Phi_{1, P}([x]) + \Phi_{1, P'}([x']).$$

#### FOC-T5: Quotient Lipschitz Bounds under Transport

- **Hypotheses:** Packets $P, P'$ and linear maps $T_0: C^0 \to C'^0$, $T_1: C^1 \to C'^1$, $T_2: C^2 \to C'^2$, $S_2: U^2 \to U'^2$ satisfying:
  $$T_1 d_0 = d_0' T_0, \qquad T_2 d_1 = d_1' T_1, \qquad T_2 a_2 = a_2' S_2.$$
- **Operator Norms:**
  $$L_1 := \|T_1\|_{1, w_1 \to w_1'} = \max_{j} \frac{1}{w_{1, j}} \sum_{i} w_{1, i}' \vert (T_1)_{i, j} \vert, \qquad L_2 := \|T_2\|_{1, w_2 \to w_2'}.$$
- **Precise Bounds:**
  $$\Phi_1'([T_1 x]) \le L_1 \Phi_1([x]), \qquad \Gamma_2'([T_2 d_1 x]) \le L_2 \Gamma_2([d_1 x]).$$

## 5. Vocabulary Fence & Scope Boundaries

### 5.1 Terminology & Posture Fences

| Term / Posture | Preferred Framing | Prohibited Overclaim |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic | Mathematical partition into disjoint sectors (`ExactRepair`, `CoherentResidual`, `NonclosedDefect`) | Action selection or remediation command |
| Dual Witness | Exact bounded functional annihilating repair subspace with zero duality gap | Infinite-dimensional functional or continuous oracle |
| Quotient Seminorm | Attained polyhedral distance $\Phi_1([x]) = \min_f \|x + d_0 f\|_{1, w}$ | Least-squares or heuristic surrogate |
| Coherent Residual | Boundary defect vanishing in second stage ($d_1 x \in \operatorname{im} a_2$) | Terminal stabilization or survivor promotion |

### 5.2 What CODA-FOC Does Not Support

1. **Does Not Support $L_2$ Least-Squares Surrogates:** Euclidean projections yield distinct repair points and fail polyhedral polar cone box bounds.
2. **Simplex LP Duality Not Mechanized in Lean:** Polyhedral duality on raw matrices is a Python computational certificate, not a Lean formal proof.
3. **Does Not Mint Operational Authority:** Defect diagnosis classifies mathematical obstruction; it confers no sovereign authority, execution grant, or publication standing.
4. **Does Not Conflate Diagnosis with Stabilization:** A `CoherentResidual` diagnosis does not constitute stabilization. Promotion to `StabilizedSurvivor` requires a complete external certificate matching registry, observer, budget, moves, and evaluation digests.

### 5.3 Narrowest Defensible Claim

For any finite cochain complex $C^0 \xrightarrow{d_0} C^1 \xrightarrow{d_1} C^2$ with $d_1 d_0 = 0$ and strictly positive coordinate weights, the state space $C^1$ admits an exhaustive and mutually exclusive 3-way partition into `ExactRepair`, `CoherentResidual`, and `NonclosedDefect`. When the differentials $d_0, d_1, a_2$, weights $w_1, w_2$, and defect vectors $x$ have rational coordinates in $\mathbb{Q}$, the quotient distances, optimal repairs, and dual certificates are decided with exact zero duality gap over exact rational arithmetic.
