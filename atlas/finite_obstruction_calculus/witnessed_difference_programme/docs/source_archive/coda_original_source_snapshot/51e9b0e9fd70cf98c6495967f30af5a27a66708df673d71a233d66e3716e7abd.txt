2a. Standing boundary fence (not a support lemma)
-------------------------------------------------

**WD6-L9 — metatheoretic boundary.** No reading of this paper may claim more than
*a class-relative no-go holding inside a metatheory that was already equipped with
the structure it reasons over*. Every gate in §5, every target in §6, and both
verdict rules in §7 are formulated **after** a finite-dimensional real vector space
has been granted, after an ordered field has been granted, after **convexity** —
and with it a shared randomness source and an agent free to consult it — has been
granted, after **outcome probabilities stable under repetition** have been granted,
which presupposes re-preparable systems, a clock, and a cross-trial identity
criterion, all three **refused at WD-0**; after a **composition primitive** making
"two systems" meaningful has been granted (§4.2); and after the ambient set theory
and finite-dimensional linear algebra in which $\Omega_A$, $\otimes_{\min}$, and
$\check{H}^1$ are formed have been granted. Nothing in the difference calculus
supplies any one of these. A negative verdict reached inside that metatheory is a
statement about *what is available in it*. **It is not a no-go for structure, it is
not a no-go about reality, and it does not establish that the GPT class is the
right arena** — §4.5 states the second half of that for the class specifically.

**Why this is not carried in the support-lemma table above.** It has no upgrade
path, so it is not a support lemma: it is not a mathematical statement whose proof
would promote a claim row, but a standing restriction on how *every* row may be
read, and it binds whether or not anything is proved. Discharging **`PL-WDP006-13`**
(paper §14) does not retire the fence — that obligation either exhibits the
accounting, which narrows what the fence excludes, or reformulates the verdict to
state the boundary explicitly, which puts the fence in the paper's own voice.
Neither outcome removes it. Its permanent home as a non-claim is **`WD6-N19`**, and
the matching claim row is **`WDP006-C26`**.
