2a. Standing boundary fence (not a support lemma)
-------------------------------------------------

**WD5-L8 — metatheoretic boundary.** No reading of this paper may claim more than
*a weight-system classification and a no-go holding inside a metatheory that was
already equipped with the structure being classified over*. Every target in §6 is
formulated after a semiring has been granted — two distinct operations $\oplus$
and $\otimes$ **and the distributivity between them**, which is the hinge on
which T1 turns — after a real scale parameter $\beta$ and the real exponential
have been granted, and after the ambient finite combinatorics in which
$M^{\otimes n}$ and $M^{\ast}$ are formed have been granted. Nothing in the
difference calculus supplies one such operation, let alone two, let alone their
distributivity. A classification reached inside that metatheory is a
classification of *weightings available in it*; it is not a classification for
structure, and it is not a derivation of magnitude.

**Why this is not carried in the support-lemma table above.** It has no upgrade
path, so it is not a support lemma: it is not a mathematical statement whose proof
would promote a claim row, but a standing restriction on how *every* row may be
read, and it binds whether or not anything is proved. Discharging obligation
**PL-WDP005-11** (paper §14) does not retire the fence — PL-WDP005-11 either exhibits
the accounting, which narrows what the fence excludes, or states the boundary
explicitly, which puts the fence in the paper's own voice. Neither outcome removes
it. **`PL-WDP005-10` does not discharge it either:** auditing §4 and §5's imports
against WD-0's T4 injection list *prices* each import by locating it on a list,
which is a different act from establishing that a verdict reached inside the
resulting metatheory is a verdict about what the metatheory represents. Its
permanent home as a non-claim is **WD5-N11**, and the matching claim row is
**WDP005-C17**. This is the paper's deepest honesty risk, and it is a boundary risk
rather than a mathematical gap; parallel to WD-0's PL-WD0-8, WD-1's PL-WD1-11,
WD-2's PL-WD2-12, WD-3's PL-WD3-11, WD-4's PL-WD4-11 (fence WD4-L9), and WD-5's
PL-WDP005-13 (fence WD5-L9), each of which has the same standing.
