# Nonclaims

AuthorityEffect: None.

- The Algorithm Prime Triad does not prove that discrete computational loops
  exhaust physical or metaphysical reasoning, nor does it make claims about
  general artificial intelligence, universal truth, or consciousness.
- The two-phase admission protocol and continuation predicates are operational
  decision procedures within the declared discrete setting; they do not establish
  philosophical infallibility.
- The Lean 4 formalization mechanizes typed properties of the abstract model
  in `lean4/Prime/Basic.lean` under axioms {propext, Quot.sound}; it does not
  certify external unmechanized software or hardware environments.
- The six anti-cheat halting laws enforce structural and budget-bounded termination;
  they do not guarantee that every terminating problem instance resolves to a zero residual.
- Grounding digest integrity assumes the standard collision resistance of SHA-256.
