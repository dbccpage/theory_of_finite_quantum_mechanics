# Discrete Deformation Calculus

A Finite Trace Calculus for Obstruction Handling — version 1.0.3, 2026-10-03

## Abstract

Discrete Deformation Calculus classifies finite obstruction-handling steps
using eight letters. A declared finite rational cochain complex provides two
diagnostics: Φ₁ is weighted ℓ¹ distance to the image of the first coboundary;
Γ₂ is the ℓ¹ norm of the face-closure defect. Positive weights and the chain
law distinguish exact, closed non-exact and open residuals.

## Why This Matters

An exact repair, a retained residual, a change of carrier and a refusal have
different evidential meanings. The trace calculus records that distinction
and makes the assumptions behind each transition inspectable.

## Core Theorems

Written finite-dimensional arguments establish the Hodge decomposition and
attainment of the graph quotient distance at a spanning-forest potential.
For the abstract transition system, Lean establishes replay composition,
terminal and refusal absorption, and that diagnostic decrease requires a
descent or lift. CREDIT is lawful when all six of its step laws hold; exact
start and booked descent are consequences, not a sufficient test by themselves.

## Formal Verification and Computational Artifacts

Core Lean v4.34.0 checks 61 named theorems with a theorem-level axiom census.
The exact rational Python companion supplies primal and dual certificates,
adversarial regression tests, measured-carrier replay and two executable
notebooks. A trusted external head and length distinguish historical tape
integrity from unanchored internal hash consistency.

## Scope Boundaries and Non-Claims

The Lean model has finite control and unbounded abstract data. Supplied
diagnostics, detector adequacy and witness booleans are model hypotheses;
they do not authenticate operational evidence. Rational linear optimization,
cryptographic security and deployed-runtime equivalence are not mechanized.
General adequacy, trace equivalence and minimal lifts remain open.
