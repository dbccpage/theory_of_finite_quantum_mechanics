# Claim ledger — CODA

**Version:** 2.0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-28


This is the active ledger for [coda.md](coda.md). The archived design ledger,
foundational ledger, and discipline ledger remain source records. Their
claim IDs are reconciled individually below. No historical theorem label or
build report is silently promoted into current local evidence.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PROVED_LOCAL` | The stated scoped result has a prose proof and named local Lean evidence. |
| `PROVED_PROSE` | The statement has a proof in the manuscript; the general statement is not mechanized locally. |
| `FINITE_COMPUTATION` | The supplied finite instance is independently executable; it does not establish unbounded generality. |
| `DECLARED_POLICY` | An adopted rule or organizational choice; any formal result proves only properties of the declared model. |
| `HISTORICAL_REPORT` | A dated source assertion, with no present replay or status refresh implied. |
| `CITED_BOUNDARY` | A fixed external source is identified; the local build does not audit or import it. |
| `OPEN` | A construction or claim remains unestablished in this package. |

`PROVED_LOCAL` does not mean that every interpretive sentence surrounding a
lemma is mechanized. The formal coverage and exact axiom dependencies below
are part of each claim's scope. Current claims are CODA-01 through CODA-26.

## Withdrawal and promotion rules

The source text “a count of one is a choice already spent upstream” is not
adopted as a general theorem: a singleton may follow from a proved constraint.
The source's identification-cost conjecture is not called a theorem here.
The historical permission diagram's unqualified “neither can select” is
replaced by the stated model and policy, without extending a symmetry
theorem beyond its regime. A post-search norm error is not automatically a
surviving obstruction, and a faithful-functor label is withdrawn for the
specific point-span candidate.

A later promotion must name the exact changed statement, assumptions,
source, proof or experiment, and remaining scope. It must update the paper,
this ledger, the machine index, and the changelog together. Old claims remain
recoverable through their original files and manifest hashes.
