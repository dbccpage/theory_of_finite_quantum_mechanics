## 1. Summary of Typed Status Codes

- `DEFINITION / IMPLEMENTATION`: named data or finite operations, without a general categorical theorem.
- `PROVED-LEAN`: the exact named declaration and its stated hypotheses; current replay results belong in the verification receipt.
- `PROVED-PROSE`: a written argument for the stated result, not an executable example.
- `CITED`: an external companion statement; its proof and build are not supplied by this package.
- `HISTORICAL`: a dated reference or provenance report, not a new verification result.
- `POLICY`: a declared evaluation or authorization rule.
- `CHECKED-FINITE`: finite computational evidence only; the exact command and revision belong in the verification receipt.
- `OPEN`: an unproved construction or unsupported stronger interpretation.
