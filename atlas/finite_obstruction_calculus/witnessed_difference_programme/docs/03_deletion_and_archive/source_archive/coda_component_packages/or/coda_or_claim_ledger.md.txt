## 3. Edge Cases, Exact Rational Arithmetic & Invariants

1. **Zero Floats Invariant:**
   All numerical scalars, matrix entries in differentials $d_0, d_1$, raw defect vectors, and quotient distances are represented strictly as exact rational numbers (`sympy.Rational` / `fractions.Fraction`). Floats, booleans, and symbolic variables in matrix positions strictly raise `TypeError` (enforced via `_validate_exact_rational` in `coda_or_core.py`).
2. **Target Binding Invariants:**
   - $\text{sieve.target} = \text{object.id}$
   - $\forall u \in \text{sieve.arrows},\quad u.\text{target} = \text{sieve.target}$
   Mismatches between arrow target and sieve target, or sieve target and object ID, strictly raise `ValueError` at construction.
3. **Defensive Immutability:**
   - `EnrichedEnvironment.d0` and `d1` store immutable snapshots (`sympy.ImmutableMatrix`), preventing post-construction mutation of differential matrices or complex nilpotence.
   - `DiagramDefectResult` stores immutable matrix copies of `raw_defect`, `boundary_image`, and `repair_2cell`. Modifying the caller's input after classification does not alter the stored result.
4. **Indeterminate Closure Defense:**
   In `classify_diagram_square`, if $d_1 \delta$ cannot be determined to be zero or non-zero (`d1_delta.is_zero_matrix is None`), the function raises `ValueError` rather than defaulting to an open defect.
5. **Protected Skeleton Discipline:**
   `ProtectedSkeleton.elements` is normalized to an immutable tuple of non-empty strings. Unledgered mutation or mutation with an empty string `loss_ledger=""` raises `SilentMutationRefusal`.
6. **Standing Policy Refinement:**
   - Lean `HasAuthority`: requires at least one supporting arrow, local terminalizer, descent triplet, and object/sieve/all-arrow target binding.
   - Python default evaluator: additionally enforces `require_all_sources=True` (every listed arrow must carry standing). For well-formed nonblank names and weight declarations, `require_all_sources=False` uses the same support, local flags, and target-binding conditions. No full Python/Lean equivalence theorem is claimed.
7. **Scope:**
   No operational authority is conferred unconditionally across all code, tests, and documentation.

Target-binding regressions: `CodaOr.old_flags_admit_mismatched_targets`, `CodaOr.mismatched_targets_have_no_authority`, and `CodaOr.bound_target_example_has_authority` in `lean4/L02SupportSieve.lean`.
