**Abstract.**

Across this corpus, a finite defect is never measured in the abstract: it is measured *relative to a declared evaluation structure* — what is compared, which repairs are admissible, what gauge measures the leftover, and what terminal status follows. That structure has been written three different ways: as the prose "observer object" of the general-media exposition, as the triple $(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J})$ fixed in Paper 008, and as the "observer class $M$ / observer-visible quotient $Q^1_O$" language of the controlling spine. This paper consolidates them into one finite object, the **observer object** $\mathcal{O}=(K,F,R,\delta,d_0,d_1,J,M,\tau)$, and pins down its three load-bearing facts. (i) The observer-relative obstruction $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta])=\inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta-d_0 f)$ is well-defined on the quotient $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}=C^1/\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$. (ii) Paper 000/008's observer triple $(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J})$ assembles into the packet — its aggregation law $\mathcal{A}$ and per-edge gauge $\mathcal{J}$ folding into the packet's single gauge $J$, and its transform class into $M$ — with the carrier and admissible repair class declared separately; so this is consolidation, not a competing definition. (iii) The residual class is gauge-independent while its magnitude is gauge-fixed; the worked $2$-vs-$\sqrt2$ instance lives in Paper 000a and is cited, not re-derived. The paper closes with the two-role typing (observer = evaluator, never proposer) and the terminalizer, the points at which observer theory hands off to the authority layer.

**Why a researcher should care.** Anyone who reports "the model says," "the data shows," or "the measurement gives X" has already chosen an observer — a carrier, a comparison, a repair budget, a gauge, and a terminal rule — and usually left it undeclared. Undeclared, the same defect can be reported as two different numbers, or a proposal can be smuggled out as an authorized judgment. This paper gives the corpus one place to point at when it says "relative to the declared observer," so that downstream papers (conditional lift descent, density/Born lifts, vector and quantum gauges) inherit a single definition instead of re-improvising one. The practical payoff is narrow and concrete: it makes "under what observer?" a checkable question with a finite answer.

**Falsifiability.** This frame fails as a faithful consolidation for the checked scope if the Paper 000/008 observer triple or the Paper 000a gauge examples cannot be written as instances of $\mathcal O=(K,F,R,\delta,d_0,d_1,J,M,\tau)$. The broader Papers 005, 009–020 reconciliation was PL-000b-1, discharged at the encyclopedia level on 2026-06-20 (ENC-0517, conditional; §11); it is recorded, not re-derived, here.

**Notation.** $C^k(P)$, $d_0$, $d_1$ (with $d_1 d_0=0$), the quotient $Q^1$, the obstruction magnitude $\Phi$, the closure diagnostic $\Gamma_2$, the terminalizer $\tau$, and $\Pi^\sharp$/`IsAuth` follow Paper 000 and the canonical spine; they are recalled on first use here. The plain-language inventory of the same object (carrier, gauge, quotient, residual, closure, terminal status) is the LinkedIn master essay *"What the Hell Is an Observer?"*; this paper is its formal anchor.

2. The Observer Object

----------------------

> **Definition 2.1 (Observer object).** `[DEFINITION]`
> An **observer object** over a finite carrier is a nine-tuple
> $$
> \mathcal{O}=(K,\;F,\;R,\;\delta,\;d_0,\;d_1,\;J,\;M,\;\tau),
> $$
> with the following components.
>
> | Symbol | Name | Role |
> |---|---|---|
> | $K$ | carrier | a finite set of sites with an incidence structure (a finite directed graph $P=(V,E)$, or a finite cell complex); $K=P$. |
> | $F$ | local-state assignment | assigns to each site $v$ a finite-dimensional stalk $F(v)$ (e.g. $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{R}^k$, $\operatorname{Herm}(H_v)$). Determines $C^0(P)=\prod_v F(v)$ and the edge groups $C^1,C^2$. |
> | $R$ | comparison | for each edge $e=(a\to b)$ a comparison/restriction map $r_{b,a}$ declaring which local states are comparable. |
> | $\delta$ | defect map | turns a section $s$ and observed edge field into the edge cochain $\delta_e\in C^1(P)$ (the comparison failure on $e$). |
> | $d_0$ | repair coboundary | $d_0:C^0\to C^1$; $\operatorname{im}(d_0)$ is the space of defects produced by admissible local reference changes (gauge shift / calibration). |
> | $d_1$ | closure | optional $d_1:C^1\to C^2$ with $d_1 d_0=0$; tests higher-order (loop/face) closure. |
> | $J$ | gauge | a norm/functional on $C^1$ measuring residual defect (the observer-selected magnitude). |
> | $M$ | transform class | the compatible transformations that preserve the evaluation (the redundancy/symmetry class declared lawful). |
> | $\tau$ | terminalizer | a rule assigning a terminal status to the measured residual and its diagnostics. |
>
> A subspace $\mathrm{Adm}\subseteq C^0(P)$ (the **admissible repair class**, written $\mathrm{Adm}(P)$ or $A(P)$ in consumers) is declared; we take $\mathrm{Adm}$ to be a linear subspace, as in Papers 008–009. Write $d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}}$ for the restriction.

The packet records exactly the data needed to turn a local disagreement into an authorized evaluation, and nothing more. The first six components $(K,F,R,\delta,d_0,d_1)$ are the **carrier context** (what is compared and what counts as repairable); $J$ measures; $M$ declares sameness; $\tau$ terminalizes. The plain-language inventory of these nine is the body of the master essay; this is its typed form.

3. Observer-Relative Obstruction

--------------------------------

> **Definition 3.1 ($\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$, $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$, $\Gamma_2$, $R_{cl}$).** `[DEFINITION]`
> Given $\mathcal{O}$ and an observed defect $\delta\in C^1(P)$:
> $$
> \Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta]) \;=\; \inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}} J\!\left(\delta-d_0 f\right),
> \qquad
> Q^1_{\mathcal{O}} \;=\; C^1(P)\,/\,\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}}).
> $$
> When $d_1$ is declared (so $d_1 d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}}=0$), the **closure diagnostic** is
> $$
> \Gamma_2([\delta]) \;=\; J_2\!\left(d_1 \delta\right),
> $$
> which is independent of representative because $d_1(\delta+d_0 f)=d_1\delta$ for every $f\in\mathrm{Adm}$; equivalently, at a minimizer $h=\delta-d_0 f^\ast$ (Proposition A2), $\Gamma_2([\delta])=J_2(d_1 h)$. The **closure ratio** $R_{cl}=\Gamma_2/\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$ is defined only when $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta])>0$. Here $J_2$ is the declared $C^2$ gauge.

> **Proposition A1 (Quotient well-definedness).** `[PROVEN]`
> If $\mathrm{Adm}\subseteq C^0(P)$ is a linear subspace and $J$ is *any* function on $C^1$, then $\delta\mapsto\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta])=\inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta-d_0 f)$ is constant on cosets of $\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$ — hence descends to a well-defined function on $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$ — whenever the infimum is defined.
>
> *Proof.* Let $\delta'=\delta+d_0 g$ with $g\in\mathrm{Adm}$. Because $\mathrm{Adm}$ is a linear subspace, $f\mapsto f-g$ is a bijection of $\mathrm{Adm}$ onto itself, so
> $$
> \Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta'])=\inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta+d_0 g-d_0 f)
> =\inf_{f'\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta-d_0 f')=\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta]),
> $$
> using the substitution $f'=f-g$. No norm or attainment property of $J$ is used. $\square$

If a future consumer uses a non-linear admissible repair class, the quotient $Q^1_{\mathcal O}=C^1/\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$ and Proposition A1 do not automatically apply; that consumer must supply its own equivalence relation and well-definedness proof.

> **Proposition A2 (Finite norm attainment).** `[PROVEN]`
> If in addition $C^1(P)$ is finite-dimensional and $J$ is a norm, then the infimum $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta])=\inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta-d_0 f)$ is attained at some $f^\ast$. If $J$ is strictly convex, the post-repair residual $h=\delta-d_0 f^\ast$ is well-defined (unique).
>
> *Proof.* The feasible set $\{\delta-d_0 f:f\in\mathrm{Adm}\}$ is a finite-dimensional affine subspace, hence closed; a norm attains its infimum over a nonempty closed set, so $f^\ast$ exists. If $J$ is strictly convex, the minimizer $d_0 f^\ast$ is unique, so any two minimizers $f^\ast, f^{\ast\prime}$ satisfy $f^\ast - f^{\ast\prime} \in \ker(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$, meaning $h$ is unique. $\square$

This keeps the norm/attainment assumptions out of the general observer-object definition: Definition 2.1 allows $J$ to be a norm *or* a functional, so the quotient fact (A1) holds in full generality, while attainment (A2) is invoked only where a norm is declared.

This is the finite, observer-relative version of the Paper 000 statement that $\Phi$ depends only on the cohomology class. Here the class is the *observer-visible* quotient $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$: what survives the declared repair budget $\mathrm{Adm}$, which need not be all of $C^0$.

4. Reconciliation: The Observer Triple Assembles Into the Packet

----------------------------------------------------------------

Paper 000 already names a coarser observer object — the **observer triple**
$$
\mathcal{O}_3=(\mathcal{A},\,\mathcal{M},\,\mathcal{J}),
$$
where $\mathcal{A}$ is the **aggregation law** combining per-edge defect magnitudes (Aczél form $\mathcal{A}(\{a_e\})=g^{-1}(\sum_e g(a_e))$, the plain sum for $\ell^1$), $\mathcal{J}$ is the **per-edge gauge** $j_e$, and $\mathcal{M}$ is the **compatible transformation (symmetry) class**. Paper 008 (*Conditional Lift Descent for Finite Observer-Relative Obstruction Systems*) fixes this triple — its §1.1 writes $\mathcal{O}=(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J})$ with $J_{\mathcal{O}}=\|\cdot\|_1$ — and declares the carrier $(P,s,\omega)$, the admissible repair class $\mathrm{Adm}(P)$, and the coboundary $d_0$ **separately** in the same setup. The controlling spine likewise speaks of a "declared observer class $M$," an "observer-visible quotient $Q^1_O$," and "replayable observer-packet fields." We show these data assemble into one packet — so this paper consolidates rather than competes.

> **Proposition B (Triple-to-packet reconciliation).** `[PROVEN — definitional specialization]`
> The Paper 000 / Paper 008 observer triple $\mathcal{O}_3=(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J})$, together with the separately declared carrier $(P,s,\omega)$, admissible repair class $\mathrm{Adm}(P)$, and coboundary $d_0$, assembles into an observer object $\mathcal{O}=(K,F,R,\delta,d_0,d_1,J,M,\tau)$ under:
>
> - $M=\mathcal{M}$ (transform class identical);
> - $J$ = the **two-level gauge** built from the triple, $J(h)=\mathcal{A}\big(\{\mathcal{J}(h_e)\}_{e}\big)$ — so the triple's $\mathcal{A}$ (aggregation) and $\mathcal{J}$ (per-edge gauge) are the two factors of the packet's single gauge $J$; for Paper 008's choice $g=\mathrm{id},\ j_e=|\cdot|$ this is $J=\|\cdot\|_1$;
> - $K=P$; $(F,R,\delta)$ the declared local-state / comparison / defect data; $d_0$ the declared coboundary, $d_1$ the optional $2$-cell; $\mathrm{Adm}=\mathrm{Adm}(P)$; and $\tau$ the terminal tags (refusal, budget-unknown, lift-incompleteness, licensed lift).
>
> Under these identifications the packet's $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$ (Definition 3.1) coincides term-for-term with Paper 008's $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}(P,\omega;\mathrm{Adm})=J_{\mathcal{O}}(\omega-d_0 f^\ast)$.
>
> *Proof.* Definition-matching. The only non-trivial identification is the gauge: Paper 000's admissible global diagnostic has the Aczél form $J_E(x)=g^{-1}\!\big(\sum_e g(j_e(x_e))\big)=\mathcal{A}(\{\mathcal{J}(x_e)\})$, which is exactly the packet's $J$; Paper 008 specializes $g=\mathrm{id}$, $j_e=|\cdot|$, giving $J=\|\cdot\|_1$. The residual $h=\omega-d_0 f^\ast$ with $f^\ast\in\arg\min_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\omega-d_0 f)$ is Definition 3.1 verbatim once $\delta:=\omega$. The remaining components are exactly the carrier, repair class, and terminal tags Paper 008 declares around the triple. $\square$

**What Proposition B does and does not say.** It is an *assembly*, not a forgetful projection onto a sub-triple. In particular $\mathcal{A}$ is an **aggregation law, not the admissible repair class** — Paper 008 declares $\mathrm{Adm}(P)$ as a *separate* object, so the naive reading "$\mathcal{A}=\mathrm{Adm}$" is false. The packet (i) **folds** the triple's two measurement slots — aggregation $\mathcal{A}$ and per-edge gauge $\mathcal{J}$ — into its single gauge $J$, and (ii) **promotes** the carrier, repair class, closure $d_1$, and terminalizer $\tau$ — left separate or implicit in the triple presentation — to named first-class components. The relation is therefore many-to-one in the other direction too: the triple alone does not determine the packet. Per the corpus reconciliation discipline, this section **adds** a disambiguation; it does not rewrite the observer-triple definition in Paper 000 or Paper 008, which remain canonical for their own scope. The same reading aligns the spine: its "observer class $M$" is the packet's $M$, its "$Q^1_O$" is $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$, and its "observer-packet fields" are the nine components.

5. Same Data, Different Observer

--------------------------------

> **Proposition C (Class is gauge-independent; magnitude is gauge-fixed).** `[PROVEN]`
> Fix a carrier $P$ and a repair coboundary $d_0$ with admissible class $\mathrm{Adm}$. The quotient $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}=C^1/\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$, and hence the residual class $[\delta]$ of any $\delta\in C^1$, depend only on $(C^1,d_0,\mathrm{Adm})$ and **not** on the gauge $J$. The magnitude $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}([\delta])=\inf_{f\in\mathrm{Adm}}J(\delta-d_0f)$ depends on $J$. Consequently two observers that differ only in $J$ assign the same class but, in general, different magnitudes to the same $\delta$.
>
> *Proof.* $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$ is the cokernel of $d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}}$, a construction in which $J$ does not appear; thus $[\delta]$ is gauge-independent. The magnitude is the $J$-distance from $\delta$ to $\operatorname{im}(d_0|_{\mathrm{Adm}})$, determined by $J$: if $J_1\le J_2$ pointwise then the corresponding distances satisfy the same inequality, but for unrelated gauges the magnitudes may differ without either dominating the other. $\square$

**Worked instance (reproducible; imported).** `[STANDARD — imported, Paper 000a]` On the $3$-cycle with edge targets $\omega_{01}=(1,0),\ \omega_{12}=(0,1),\ \omega_{20}=(0,0)$ the cycle circulation is $c=(1,1)$, a fixed element of $Q^1_{\mathcal{O}}$ regardless of gauge. The coordinate-channel observer ($J=\ell^1$) reports $\Phi=|c|_1=2$; the Euclidean vector observer ($J=\ell^1(E;\ell^2)$) reports $\Phi=|c|_2=\sqrt2$. The two packets are identical except in their $J$; the full development, the dual-witness theory, and the self-contained Python replay (`2.000000` vs `1.414214`) are in Paper 000a, *When Obstruction Is Not Scalar*. This is the smallest instance of Proposition C and is the recommended reproducibility entry point.

Proposition C is the structural content of "observer-relative": not "anything goes," but *the class is intrinsic, the number is declared.* A reported magnitude without a declared $J$ is therefore underspecified, not wrong.

6. Two-Role Typing: Observer Is an Evaluator, Never a Proposer

--------------------------------------------------------------

> **Definition 6.1 (Role typing).** `[DEFINITION; firewall imported]`
> A **proposer** emits candidate sections, edge fields, repairs, or lifts; it has no authority. An **observer** $\mathcal{O}$ evaluates: it measures $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$, $\Gamma_2$, and applies $\tau$. The two roles are typed distinct and must not be collapsed: the system that proposes a value must not be the one that terminalizes it.

This is the obstruction-calculus statement of the proposal/authority firewall. It is imported, not established here: the dependent-record firewall is canonical spine §II.2.2 (*Two-Role Typing*), and the machine-checked separation of evidence from authority is Paper 024 (*The Authority Firewall*). The packet merely *locates* the firewall — it is the boundary between $(\,K,F,R,\delta,d_0,d_1,J\,)$ (evidence: what was compared and measured) and $(\,M,\tau\,)$ together with the descent layer (authority: what is allowed to count). `[STANDARD — imported]`

7. The Terminalizer

-------------------

> **Definition 7.1 (Terminalizer).** `[DEFINITION]`
> $\tau_{\mathcal{O}}([h],\,\Phi_{\mathcal{O}},\,\Gamma_2)\;\longrightarrow\;\textsf{TerminalTag}$
> assigns a terminal status — e.g. *accept*, *reject*, *refuse*, *escalate*, *unresolved*, *authorized*, *not-authorized* — to a measured residual and its diagnostics.

**Measurement is not authorization.** A positive $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$ records that the declared observer sees irreducible residual after exhausting the declared repair class; it does not, by itself, license any action. What follows — repair, refusal, escalation to a richer carrier (a *lift*, in the sense of Paper 008), or acceptance as tolerable — is supplied by $\tau$, and terminal *authority* is conferred only through the finite-descent gate ($\Pi^\sharp$ together with `IsAuth`) of the CODA-FDA layer, never by $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}$ alone. `[STANDARD — imported, CODA-FDA]` This is the point at which observer theory hands off to authority theory; the packet defines the handoff interface, not the authority verdict.
