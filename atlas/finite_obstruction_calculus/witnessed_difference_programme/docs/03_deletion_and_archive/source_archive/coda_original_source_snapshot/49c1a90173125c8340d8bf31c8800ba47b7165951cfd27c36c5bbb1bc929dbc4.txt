2a. Standing boundary fence (not a support lemma)
-------------------------------------------------

**WD7-L9 — metatheoretic boundary.** *Entered 2026-07-21.* No reading of this
paper may claim more than *a relative-completeness verdict holding inside a
metatheory that was already equipped with the structure it reasons over*. Every
target in §4 and the §12 programme consequence are formulated **after** a formal
language and a notion of well-typedness have been granted; after a **depth bound**
on $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{O}}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ has been granted, which the
difference calculus does not supply; after a **domain for $p_{\mathcal{O}}$** has
been granted, which $\operatorname{im} p_{\mathcal{O}}$ presupposes and which §3.4
explicitly declines to characterize; after an **index set $I$** and a licence to
form the nerve of $\{\mathcal{O}_i\}_{i\in I}$ have been granted; and after the
ambient combinatorics in which *"exactly one status"* and $H^{1}$ are formed have
been granted. Nothing in the difference calculus supplies any one of these. **A
verdict reached inside that metatheory is a statement about what is available in
it.**

**The self-application twist, which is specific to this paper.** WD-7's thesis is
that completeness is only ever relative to a **declared** grammar. Its own
theorems are proved in a metatheory whose grammar is **not declared and not
indexed**, so the paper's central claim, applied to the paper, says its own
completeness result is complete only relative to an undeclared grammar. Second:
T3 forms $H^{1}$ of the nerve of an observer family, which treats $I$ as a
completed index set and the family as a diagram — **a vantage over all observers
at once, used to argue that every claim is observer-relative.** Non-claim
`WD7-N10` correctly refuses to call the family a cover of anything, and no such
vantage is *used* as a premise; but a paper whose thesis is observer-relativity
owes the accounting in its own voice. This has no analogue in the sibling papers.

**Why this is not carried in the support-lemma table above.** It has no upgrade
path, so it is not a support lemma: it is not a mathematical statement whose
proof would promote a claim row, but a standing restriction on how *every* row
may be read, and it binds whether or not anything is proved. Discharging
**`PL-WDP007-11`** (paper §14a) does not retire the fence — that obligation either
exhibits the accounting, which narrows what the fence excludes, or reformulates
the §4/§12 verdict to state the boundary explicitly, which puts the fence in the
paper's own voice. Neither outcome removes it. Its permanent home as a non-claim
is **`WD7-N14`**, and the matching claim row is **`WDP007-C20`**. **All three
pre-existed this pass; what was missing was the id.**
