# Physical reading

Status: OPEN

Revision: 8b9e7d3704723a80f4927bd24375c55f0d63632e

This note is interpretation. It is not the proof and it is not the finite check.

The system is a ring of spinless fermions with interaction equal to hopping, U=t, on the full Fock space of an even periodic chain. Imaginary-time weights are the original signed meron histories. A sign can come from the fermion hop around the ring. Those weights are not a probability law and not a complex Gaussian path integral. Stripping the hop signs would be a different physical ensemble. Replacing the ring by an unrestricted spin chain would also be a different carrier: the two fermion parity sectors have different boundary twists, and counting both as full spin traces double-counts the model.

A spacetime response, if one were later derived for this same ensemble, would say how staggered occupation at one site and one imaginary-time fraction covaries with staggered occupation at another site and another time fraction, after the ring grows and the time mesh is refined together. No such kernel is identified. There is no propagation speed and no interacting continuum action to assign to the histories.

What is identified is narrower. Inside the written high-temperature disk, a single static spatial profile of the staggered density has a Gaussian source response on the circle. The coefficient chi(theta) is a static susceptibility. The window in which that coefficient stays between 1/8 and 3/8 is a statement about the static number, not a regime of spacetime correlations. The random field built from circle characters lives on the spatial circle alone.

The fixed-length integrated pairing is a global, spatially summed, imaginary-time average of the staggered density with itself on one finite ring, after the time step is removed. It is not the equal-time square of that density, and it is not a continuum kernel on a torus. The canonical split between 48/19 and 149/57 only marks that the time label belongs to the observable. It is not evidence that a spacetime field has been constructed.

The small ring at q=3 with two time repetitions sits outside the high-temperature disk. Its exact finite moments remain properties of that ring. They do not license a continuum reading.

The finite identity in the proof note is a calculation on one even ring: the ordered CAR product equals the signed occupation-history sum, and both sides keep q^{-LM/4}. The mixed numerator at two distinct half-steps is a derivative of that finite partition. That equality assigns no field, no kernel, and no joint limit. The finite check is a separate file. This paragraph is only the reading of that equality.

No operator-valued quantum field is claimed, and the signed histories are not treated as probability measures. This reading assigns no operational authority.
