# 05. Lessons for running symbol trials

**AuthorityEffect:** None.

Each lesson below is taken from the design or the analysis, with the line that
states it. They are working practice from three runs, not tested rules.

1. **Constrain epistemology, not mathematics.** Tell the model what it may
   assume and what it must separate, not what the answer should look like.
   Writing the expected output into the prompt is the over-specification that
   spoiled the earlier Pontryagin run ([v0 3 to 5](sources/design_derivation_v0.md#L3)).
2. **Hide the comparison targets.** Keep the theory you are testing for out of
   the prompt ([v0 7 to 16](sources/design_derivation_v0.md#L7)).
3. **Pre-register the branches, and count "no move" as a result**
   ([v0 173 to 207](sources/design_derivation_v0.md#L173)).
4. **No packet wrapper during discovery**
   ([v0 104 to 136](sources/design_derivation_v0.md#L104)).
5. **Send controls raw.** A code fence changes the reading
   ([v1 358 to 386](sources/analysis_derivation_v1.md#L358)).
6. **One step per turn.** Do not bundle `<a>` with the collision question
   ([v1 388 to 404](sources/analysis_derivation_v1.md#L388)).
7. **Audit at two levels.** Separate the licensed metalanguage (LEVEL M) from
   the internal regime (LEVEL S), or the auditor will flag ordinary words
   ([T2 571 to 598](sources/trial_2_transcript.md#L571)).
8. **Give auditors sufficient state evidence.** Supply the adopted history or
   a summary that preserves the question being checked. A view that loses the
   relevant state cannot support correct reconstruction on every history
   (chapter 03, result 6).
9. **A proposal is not a state change.** Mark adoption explicitly; the replay
   wrapper's rule is "Do not treat an assistant proposal as adopted unless a
   later user turn explicitly adopts or designates the resulting state"
   ([FR 10](sources/full_replay_transcript.md#L10)).
10. **Keep mention, supply, designation, admission, retention and operation
    apart.** The trials failed exactly where these were merged
    ([T2 1248 to 1262](sources/trial_2_transcript.md#L1248)).
11. **Record the model.** The design asked for the model identifier, settings
    and date ([v0 414](sources/design_derivation_v0.md#L414)); the record lacks
    them, which limits what the trials can support.
