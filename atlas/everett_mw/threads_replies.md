## Reply 2 — an intuition worth remembering (407 characters)

The idea worth keeping: each slit gets a number, a and b. The chance of a hit is |a+b|², not |a|²+|b|². The extra piece, 2·Re(a*b), is the interference. A detector that records the path ties each route to a different detector state, and that piece gets multiplied by their overlap, which is 0 once the records can be told apart. No mind needed, no simulation needed, no other worlds needed. Just the record.
