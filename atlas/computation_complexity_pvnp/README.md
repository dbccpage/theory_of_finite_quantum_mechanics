# Computation, complexity and P versus NP

AuthorityEffect: None. Canonical home of the merged computation and complexity sources.

Start with [the compact derivation](from_first_mark_to_quantum_computing.md).
It connects the existing `COMP-C1`–`COMP-C8` records to P, NP, verification,
quantum information and uniform quantum circuits. P versus NP remains an open
question; it is a branch of this map, not a required solution on the route to QM.

The detailed [computation proofs](computation_proofs.md) retain the machine
simulation, computability, finite fault-tolerance models and authorization
results. The [complexity research note](p_vs_np_and_eth_from_genesis.md)
retains the colouring, signs, counting and presentation arguments. Its reported
ETH material is explicitly unverified and is not a premise of the new route.
The exact [small-instance checks](checks/complexity_checks.py) test finite
examples; their success does not decide P versus NP or ETH.

[stage 29](../../dag/derivations/29_computation/), with quantum information in
[stage 13](../../dag/derivations/13_finite_qm_information/quantum_information.md).
The [full reading spine](../../dag/derivations/first_mark_to_quantum_gravity.md)
locates this branch relative to the primitive cut and quantum-gravity targets.
