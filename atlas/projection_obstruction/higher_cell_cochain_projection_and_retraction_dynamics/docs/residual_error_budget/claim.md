# Residual uncertainty and correction budgets

The manuscript derives conditional analytic consequences of supplied physical and error-enclosure assumptions. The checker validates exact scalar arithmetic, including zero-rate windows and zero guaranteed margins. It does not compute the physical residual, establish a remainder bound, validate an integrator, or mechanize the continuous argument.

The narrow claim is that the recorded fractional inputs yield the recorded budget and interval outputs. The next implementation obligation is an independently checked enclosure of every actual-defect, integration and jump contribution.
