1/3 Send one particle along two possible routes and bring the routes together. With either route open, a detector at one exit can click. Open both, and that exit can go quiet. What reaches it by one route cancels what reaches it by the other.

2/3 Give each route a distinct mark in a detector. Now that exit can click again. Read the mark and you get a definite answer about the route. In the equations, each possible route is paired with its own detector record.

3/3 Many-worlds takes that whole pairing literally. When you read the detector, one version of you remembers the first mark and another remembers the second. Each sees one result. Both histories continue in the same quantum state.
