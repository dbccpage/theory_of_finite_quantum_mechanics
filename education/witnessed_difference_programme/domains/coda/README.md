## What Is CODA?

CODA studies what a declared representation can preserve, what its admitted repairs can change, and what evidence supports a subsequent decision. Its starting point is a **witnessed irreflexive relation**; observation, algebra, repair families, costs, and authorization are additional data.

The mathematical core consists of:
- An **image-factorization criterion** (when a query factors through an observation)
- **Collision obstructions** (when it does not)
- Laws for **scoped repair menus** (exhaustion, ties, extension)
- A **finite-dimensional repair quotient** with three-way trichotomy (FOC)
- Elementary **transport and gluing** results (MUR, OR)
- Explicit **authority separation** between evidence and grants (FDA, GTLA)

## Eight Components & GTLA Authority Architecture

| Component | Scope | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **FOC** — Finite Obstruction Calculus | Quotient class, diagnostic, checked certificate | Low defect does not supply permission. |
| **FDA** — Finite Descent Authority | Scoped evaluation and admission conditions | Soundness and grantor standing remain assumptions. |
| **MUR** — Meta-Unifying Re-Presentation | Commuting comparisons and loss records | Transport need not be invertible. |
| **OR** — Omniversal Re-Presentation | Global section or gluing obstruction | Local solvability alone is insufficient. |
| **CS** — Continuum Shadow | Query-relative finite information | A family name supplies no limit theorem. |
| **Q** — Continuum | Continuum model and scoped queries | No quantum or ontology theorem from the name. |
| **CS_Q** — Bridge | Factor on realized image, or a collision | Image factor is not a total inverse. |
| **A** — Anomalon | Residual classification and next obligation | Remainder supplies neither origin nor authority. |
| **GTLA** | Overarching authority architecture | Records how evidence and grants relate. |

## Claim Hygiene & What CODA Does Not Claim

1. **No derivation** of Hilbert space, the Born rule, a physical clock, or an exhaustive classification of mathematical structures.
2. **No closure** of the sealed derivation's residual register.
3. **No inference** of intrinsic origin for every new question.
4. **No promotion** of finite agreement into semantic closure.
5. **No unconditional authorization rule** — evidence alone does not confer standing.
