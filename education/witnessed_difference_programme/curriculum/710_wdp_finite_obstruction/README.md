# Track 710: WDP Finite Obstruction Calculus & Foundational Bridges

**AuthorityEffect:** None.

## Focus
This track covers the mathematical core of the Witnessed Difference Programme:
- Discrete complexes and 1-cochains ($d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$)
- Linear sensing, dual witnesses, and exact Farkas obstruction
- Moore behavioral quotients and finite-state automata
- The 11 Foundational Derivation Bridges connecting Genesis to physical observation
- Sharp observation budgets and priced, calibrated and joint-support recovery (edition 2.4.1 Appendices T, X, Y)
