# Lesson 700.04: The Five-Part Monograph Progression

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 700 (WDP Overview)  
**Topic:** Progression across Parts I to V of the Witnessed Difference Monograph

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Trace the rigorous, non-circular mathematical journey across the 22 chapters of the Witnessed Difference Programme, understanding how physical measurement emerges from finite distinction without continuum assumptions.

## 2. Key Concepts
1. **Part I (Ch 1–5):** Distinction, Witness, and Obstruction. From the admitted cut to degree-1 obstruction calculus ($d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$) and exact $\ell_1$ quotients.
2. **Part II (Ch 6–9):** Order, Quantity, and Finite Probability. Strict partial orders, duration as state-count differences, and discrete multiplicities without Kolmogorov continuum measures.
3. **Part III (Ch 10–15):** Observers and Measurement. Passivity of linear quotient observers, context witnesses, and calibration without observer collapse.
4. **Part IV (Ch 16–19):** Histories and the Quantum Boundary. Finite path summations, discrete deformation calculus, and the firewall separating classical contextuality from quantum assertions.
5. **Part V (Ch 20–22):** Scope and Open Horizon. Profile-relative completeness, terminal routing, and the sovereign difference grounding.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- At which chapter does the boundary between discrete cochains and contextual observers occur?
- Why is probability introduced strictly as exact rational multiplicity rather than real-valued measure?
