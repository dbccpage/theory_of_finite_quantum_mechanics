## 3. Typed Residual Classification

A residual is what remains under a declared closure attempt $\Lambda$:
- **Absorption:** Proved to be absorbed by the closure.
- **Typed Remainder:** Classified residual with explicit unresolved debt.
- **Refusal:** Reason to stop attempting.
- **Unresolved Attempt:** Unverified claim that every repair was tried.

A "surviving anomalon" requires explicit closure, observer, and nonvanishing hypotheses.
