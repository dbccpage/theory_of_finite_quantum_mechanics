**§4 minimal report carrier.** $h \sim_Q h' \iff \forall q \in Q,\ \mathrm{Answer}(h,q) = \mathrm{Answer}(h',q)$. Then $H/{\sim_Q}$ is the coarsest history quotient preserving all declared answers. "The answer rule is an input to this construction, not something the quotient derives."
