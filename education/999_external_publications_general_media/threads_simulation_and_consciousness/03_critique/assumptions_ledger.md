**Five erasures: what survives the noun removal?**

Superradiance (A24–A47): Replace “water”, “microtubule”, “mind” with generic charged two-level emitters. The Maxwell–Bloch-type equations and conditional sech pulse survive; no biological site or memory content is derived. The two-level reduction, coherent coupling, rotating-wave and dipole approximations remain real hypotheses with obligations.

Holography (A48–A52): Replace “memory” with arbitrary pattern H and “brain” with sheet S. The central import is |φ|² ∝ |r+o|² (A51). Algebraic interference terms follow after that assumption; the existence of the object wave o and physical encoding are not produced.

Capacity (§3): Keep only C = 4n_r d A / λ³. For supplied n_r, d, A, λ, arithmetic survives. “Human memory capacity” does not: no observed biological write/store/read channel is linked to this formula. The synaptic comparator assumes 1 bit/synapse.

Control (A53–A91; Fig. 6): Rename “neocortex”, “cerebrospinal fluid”, “dura”, “skull” to layers 1–4. The initialized, driven, damped nonlinear field PDE and the ~21% simulated density depression survive. The target was supplied and an input constructed to produce it. No subject or memory is measured.

Quantum mind (§5): Delete the biological/mind labels. No derived variable in §2–4 provides a test for consciousness, an observer boundary, a memory identity, or phenomenal experience. The proposal is therefore an additional interpretation, not a deduction from its equations.

**Mathematical defect and quantumness checks**

Appendix A constraint: (A1–A7) imposes A⁰=0 and ∇·A=0; its own Gauss equation (A7) yields 0 = J₀ = -e|φ|², incompatible with nonzero normalized charged field as written. This is an issue in the superradiance branch, not a direct disproof of the separate Appendix C simulation. A claimed transverse-field approximation must be stated, with its omitted interactions bounded.

Appendix C: The 2PI/quantum formulation appears at A53–A68. Higher-order and connected-field contributions are then neglected or replaced by local effective mass/damping (A69–A75), leading to classical-form mean-field PDEs A76–A91. Do not conclude “no quantum mechanics anywhere”; conclude the numerical result alone does not identify an effect impossible in a classical driven-field system. Demand a distinguishable quantum-only witness.

No-go needed: Quantify what a purely classical comparator cannot reproduce. Suggested controls: same initial/boundary conditions and targets with classical Maxwell–Bloch or dissipative wave/transport dynamics; vary e, M², γ, v, γ₂; compare output and an operational memory retrieval task.

Capacity arithmetic: The paper rounds to 2.5 PB and 30 TB; plugging its nominal inputs gives ~2.6 PB and ~31.25 TB depending on units and rounding. This is not itself a mathematical defect.

Provenance discipline: A theoretical simulation is a witnessed computation, not a witnessed biological event. Mark all results as supplied / derived / simulated / observed / interpretive separately.

**Minimal falsifiable asks**

1. Which parameter values are measured in living relevant tissue rather than selected?
2. Which observation uniquely identifies collective superradiance, rather than generic coherent emission?
3. What biological operation writes, retains, retrieves, and decodes a particular memory?
4. Which effect cannot be reproduced by a classical model under the same conditions?
5. What derivation maps a measured field difference to a conscious experience?

Verdict: The stripped paper is not mathematically meaningless. It retains several respectable conditional models and a numerical control experiment. What vanishes under controlled relabeling is the evidential bridge from those models to the biological/consciousness interpretation.
