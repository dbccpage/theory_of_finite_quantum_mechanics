# code: continuum checks on the paper

The checks here use the paper's own equations: full calculus, with fields on the continuum.

| Script | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `adversarial_review_checks.py` | 1a: Appendix C keeps Gauss's law in the pure Maxwell part, and M², γ and v break it. 1b: the e/m misprint in (A76)/(A78). 1c: the initial data and the uniform background. 1d: the targets (10)–(11) are curl-free. 2: Landauer numbers and the energy barrier needed for one-year retention. 3: capacity arithmetic. |
| `critique_checks.py` | Replays [critique_wdp_po.md](../critique_wdp_po.md). 1: A⁰ = 0 with ∇·A = 0 freezes the charge density through (A7), so the two-level coherence R and the pulse of (A38) vanish; the Appendix B solution (A49) keeps ∇·A = 0 only as a static medium with the density gradient perpendicular to the polarization. 2: (A36)–(A38) with the loss term (A42) integrate to the sech pulse (A45) only as L₃ → 0. 3: (A63) with Γ₂ = D¹¹ = 0 is exactly the Madelung pair (A69)–(A70); the V² sign printed in (A70) differs from (A81)/(A87). 4: Bohm term against the A² term in A₀ at the Figure 6 edge. 5: the screening parameter e²\|φ\|²/(M²m) = 0.045 of (A49), bounding the image term. 6: the 21 nm waveguide cutoff read as a conducting pipe and as a dielectric rod (V ≈ 0.1). 7: emission is sideways and axially polarized under (A13). 8: photons per second from 10⁻¹³ W against 2.5 PB. 9: the lattice unit a_s = 1/m under two readings of m. 10: Projection Obstruction Theorem 5.1 on a two-mode toy with the paper's coupling structure (ν = 2g\|β\|Δx; full incident norm with on-site terms; the bound inside its window; D ≈ νt only at short times, periodic after; the regression test with coupling 10⁻⁷ at t = 2π that the two-local norm fails; the coherent-state mean-field run against the floor D_e/3). 11: the 3.9 gain, water per λ³, 4 meV against k_BT. Every numbered claim is an `expect()`; any failure lists itself at the end and exits 1. `--demo-failure` adds one expectation that is false on purpose. |
| `retest_checks.py` | R1: reruns the Section 4 / Appendix C simulation at the paper's parameters and measures the Gauss-law residual against the density change (numerical, floating point). R2: the Appendix A transition current is not divergence-free. R3: equal reports with Bayes factor 2. R4: a finite observer with \|P\| = \|X\| separates every state. Used in `../retest_log.md`. |

Run with `python -I code/adversarial_review_checks.py` and `python -I code/critique_checks.py` from the folder above. Both need sympy; the second also needs numpy, exits 0 only when every expectation holds, and `python -I code/critique_checks.py --demo-failure` shows the failing path (exit 1). `python code/retest_checks.py [t_end] [input_max]` also needs numpy; the default run, to m x0 = 40, takes about 30 minutes.
