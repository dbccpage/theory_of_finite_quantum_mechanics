Rhymed 3, the video game on her own turf (464 characters)

Pro gamer tip: a game can skip three different chores.
Drawing the pixels. Running the physics. Remembering what's behind the doors.
Minecraft never builds the chunks you skip, yet the seed already fixes where every diamond lies.
So which chore does the universe skip? And what record would show it to our eyes?
And who decides which chore gets skipped? In a real game, the devs, in the code.
Point me to that line in the universe's source and I'll happily reload.
