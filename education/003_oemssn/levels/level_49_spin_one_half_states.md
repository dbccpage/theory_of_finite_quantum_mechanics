  - **Declared Basis:** The computational qubit basis $\{|0\rangle, |1\rangle\}$ mapped explicitly to the spin-$1/2$ projection eigenbasis $\{|\!\uparrow_z\rangle, |\!\downarrow_z\rangle\}$.

- **Distinction Between Qubit Vectors and Spin Dynamics:**

### 2. Wire Packet Grammar & Mathematical Carrier

The wire packet explicitly declares the two-level basis identifier, spin quantization axis, Gaussian rational amplitudes, and the exact normalization receipt:

```text
<qubit_basis spin_quantization_axis amplitude_up amplitude_down norm_receipt>
```

- `qubit_basis`: Basis identifier (e.g., `spin_z_basis`, `computational_basis`).
- `spin_quantization_axis`: Unit spatial direction vector (e.g., `(0, 0, 1)` for $\hat{z}$).
- `amplitude_up`: Gaussian rational pair $c_\uparrow = (a_\uparrow, b_\uparrow) \in \mathbb{Q}(i)$.
- `amplitude_down`: Gaussian rational pair $c_\downarrow = (a_\downarrow, b_\downarrow) \in \mathbb{Q}(i)$.
- `norm_receipt`: Exact rational evaluation of $|c_\uparrow|^2 + |c_\downarrow|^2$.
