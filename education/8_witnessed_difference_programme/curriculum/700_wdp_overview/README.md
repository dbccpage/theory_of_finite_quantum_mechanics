## Focus
This track introduces the philosophy, nonclaims, and architectural structure of the Witnessed Difference Programme:
- Chapter 1: The Admitted Cut and Difference Foundations
- Chapter 2: The Witness Function and Outcome Monad
- Monograph Architecture: Parts I–V
- Live Claim Ledger: 127 positive claims, 27 nonclaims

1. `lesson_700_01_monograph_architecture`: Foundations, five-part division, and claim ledger governance.

3. `lesson_700_03_claim_ledger_governance`: Strict claim classification, claim hygiene rules, and non-authority fences.
4. `lesson_700_04_five_part_monograph_progression`: The 22-chapter progression across Parts I through V without continuum postulates.
