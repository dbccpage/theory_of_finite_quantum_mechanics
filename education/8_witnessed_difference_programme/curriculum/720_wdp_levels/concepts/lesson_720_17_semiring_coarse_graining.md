# Lesson 720.17: Level 17 Quantum Reconstruction as an Audit Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 17: Quantum Reconstruction as an Audit

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Frame quantum reconstruction as an operational audit, checking finite operational axioms W1–W6 against classical non-negative bounds.

## 2. Invariants
- Operational Audit: Quantum formalism treated as an audit layer over finite statistics, not an ontology.
- Axioms W1-W6: Finite state spaces, convex operational test sets, no-signaling, probability matching.
- Classical Corner: Non-negative weights force permutation dynamics.
- PR Box Boundary: Popescu-Rohrlich non-local correlations audit boundary.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- Why is quantum reconstruction described as an 'audit' rather than a physical derivation?
- What happens to quantum state space when weights are constrained to non-negative reals?
