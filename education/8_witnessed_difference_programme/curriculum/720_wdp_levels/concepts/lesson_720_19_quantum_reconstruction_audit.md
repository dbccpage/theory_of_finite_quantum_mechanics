# Lesson 720.19: Level 19 Relative Completeness for Observer-Indexed Presentations Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 19: Relative Completeness for Observer Presentations

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Formulate relative completeness for observer-indexed presentations using a 5-valued query status algebra, eliminating root observer assumptions.

## 2. Invariants
- Five-Valued Status Algebra: Status in {Valid, Invalid, Refused, Pending, Incomplete}.
- Relative Completeness: Presentation complete relative to Observer O_i profile.
- No Root Observer: Refusal to assume a universal master observer.
- Query Resolution: Queries evaluated strictly within local context profile.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- What are the 5 values in the status algebra for observer queries?
- Why is relative completeness preferable to absolute ontological completeness?
