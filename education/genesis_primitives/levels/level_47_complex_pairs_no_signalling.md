# Level 47: Exact supplied complex arithmetic

AuthorityEffect: None. Teaching milestone; no identity with an S(n) stage or MSS index.

**Carrier:** Python Q(i) rational pairs; separately Lean Gaussian integers Z[i].

**Supplied assumptions:** Pair arithmetic and the quadratic valuation are supplied.

**Statement:** (a,b)(c,d)=(ac−bd,ad+bc), conjugation sends (a,b) to (a,−b). For rational pairs a²+b² is a nonnegative rational; for integer pairs it is a nonnegative integer. Division requires (c,d)≠(0,0). The source studies second-order interference and vanishing third-order quadratic inclusion-exclusion on these declared valuations, with separate finite cyclotomic computations. This construction does not uniquely select quantum theory.
