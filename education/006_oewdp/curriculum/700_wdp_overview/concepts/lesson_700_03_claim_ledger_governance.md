# Lesson 700.03: Claim Ledger Governance and Claim Hygiene

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 700 (WDP Overview)  
**Topic:** Claim Classification, Claim Hygiene Rules, and Non-Authority Fences

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Understand the strict governance rules that control how claims are admitted, typed, and referenced across the WDP. Learn the claim hygiene rules that forbid unearned universal claims, speculative theorems, and hype language.

## 2. Key Concepts
1. **Typed Claim Register:** Every mathematical statement is categorized: `DEFINITION`, `FINITE_PROPOSITION`, `CONDITIONAL_RESULT`, `TYPING_PROPOSITION`, `NONCLAIM`, or `SYNTHESIS_CONCLUSION`.
2. **Claim Hygiene Discipline:** Forbidden hype words ("proves", "guarantees", "universal", "obviously") are systematically replaced with narrow, defensible statements ("supports in this setting", "checked under declared assumptions").
3. **Four-Point Claim Summary:** Every summarized artifact must declare:
   - What it supports
   - What it does not support
   - The narrowest defensible claim
   - The next test needed

## 3. Check for Understanding
- Why are toy setting tests forbidden from being described as "universal mathematical proofs"?
- What is the operational distinction between a definition and a finite proposition?
