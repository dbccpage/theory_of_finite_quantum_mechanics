# Lesson 700.01: WDP Monograph Architecture and Claim Ledger

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 700 (WDP Overview)  
**Topic:** Monograph Architecture, Epistemic Discipline, and Claim Ledger

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Understand how the Witnessed Difference Programme constructs discrete mathematical structures starting from an exhibited difference inside an admitted finite structural package $\mathfrak{C} = (X, E, \tau, \mathcal{K})$, where Primitive Difference and Structure are declared co-inputs (`WDP-CH00-N01`, `0_wdp.md:125–158`), without smuggling in continuous space, unmodeled observers, or quantum postulates.

## 2. Key Concepts
1. **The Admitted Cut:** The admitted package $\mathfrak{C} = (X, E, \tau, \mathcal{K})$ containing carrier tokens $X$, incidences $E$, witness roles $\tau$, and admissible comparisons $\mathcal{K}$ (`WDP-CH01-D01`). A bipartition $(L, R)$ may serve as an elementary illustrative example, but does not replace the canonical structural tuple.
2. **The 5 Parts of the Monograph:**
   - Part I: Difference, witness, and finite obstruction (Chapters 1–5)
   - Part II: Order, quantity, and finite probability (Chapters 6–9)
   - Part III: Observer, observation, and measurement (Chapters 10–15)
   - Part IV: Histories and the quantum boundary (Chapters 16–19)
   - Part V: Relative scope and the open programme (Chapters 20–22)
3. **The Single Live Claim Ledger:** 154 rows consisting of 27 definitions, 82 finite propositions, 15 conditional results, 2 typing propositions, 27 nonclaims, and 1 synthesis conclusion (`WDP-CH22-C01`). Evidence kind is kept strictly separate from claim status (P for prose, C for executable fixture, L:exact for direct Lean mechanization at stated scope, L:partial for partial milestones, R for external references, N for native crate validation).
