# Lesson 720.08: Level 08 Probability Scope Fences Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 08: Probability Scope Fences

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Enforce strict scope fences separating five distinct probability notions: counting, frequency, propensity, Bayesian belief, and Born weights.

## 2. Invariants
- Five-Fold Separation: Counting, frequency, propensity, belief, and Born weight are kept strictly distinct.
- Normalization Fence: Summing to 1 does not select or imply the Born rule.
- No Coercion: Probability measures cannot be automatically coerced across scope boundaries.
- Type Integrity: Probabilities carry their interpretation scope as part of their formal type.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- Why is normalizing a measure to 1 insufficient to claim a quantum Born rule?
- How do scope fences prevent epistemic category errors in finite probability models?
