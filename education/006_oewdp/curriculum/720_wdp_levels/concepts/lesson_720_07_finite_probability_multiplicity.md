# Lesson 720.07: Level 07 Multiplicity, Additive Weight, and Finite Probability Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 07: Multiplicity, Additive Weight, and Finite Probability

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Derive finite probability distributions from discrete orbit multiplicities and additive weights, establishing 'matching before number'.

## 2. Invariants
- Matching Before Number: Equicardinality and bijection established prior to numerical counting.
- Additive Weight: Weight function w: X -> Q_+ on finite outcome space.
- Pushforward Probability: P(A) = sum_{x in A} w(x) / sum_{y in X} w(y).
- Orbit Bijection: Symmetry group actions preserve finite weight multiplicities.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- Why must discrete matching precede numerical probability assignment?
- How does pushforward probability preserve exact rational ratios on finite partitions?
