# Lesson 720.12: Level 12 Measurement as a Typed Extension of Observation Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 12: Measurement as a Typed Extension of Observation

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Extend observation into measurement by constructing typed measurement packets (measurand, indication, result, calibration recurrence, traceability).

## 2. Invariants
- Measurement Packet: M = (Measurand, Indication, Value, CalibrationRecurrence, Traceability).
- Typed Value: Values carry physical units and exact rational calibration uncertainty.
- Calibration Recurrence: Periodic validation against certified reference standards.
- Context Dependency: Measurement results explicitly record measurement context.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- How does measurement extend observation by adding calibration and traceability?
- Why must calibration recurrence be recorded in the measurement packet?
