# Lesson 720.15: Level 15 Spatial/History Diagnostics and the Curved-Complex Fork Walkthrough

**AuthorityEffect:** None.  
**Track:** 720 (WDP Derivation Levels)  
**Topic:** Level 15: Spatial/History Diagnostics and the Curved-Complex Fork

## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Formalize spatial curvature diagnostics on simplicial complexes, analyzing chain contractions and truncation leakage on curved complex forks.

## 2. Invariants
- Simplicial Curvature: Angle deficit or deficit metric K(v) on 2-simplices sharing vertex v.
- Curved-Complex Fork: Choice between flat cochains and curved metric connection carriers.
- Chain Contraction: Homotopy operator h: C_k -> C_{k+1} satisfying d h + h d = Id - pi.
- Truncation Leakage: Bounds on boundary defect leakage under complex truncation.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- How is discrete curvature calculated from angle deficits on a 2-complex?
- What role does a chain contraction play in bounding truncation leakage?
