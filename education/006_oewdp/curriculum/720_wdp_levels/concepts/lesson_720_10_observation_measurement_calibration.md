## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Examine intersubjective presentations across multiple observers, establishing sheaf-like descent conditions for local presentation compatibility.

## 2. Invariants
- Intersubjective Sheaf: Observers O_i with local presentations U_i matching on overlaps U_i ^ U_j.
- Descent Receipt: Certified verification that local observations glue into a global presentation.
- Redundancy Elimination: Duplicate observations identified and quotiented.
- No Central Observer: Intersubjectivity derived from gluing compatibility, not a privileged root observer.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- What descent condition ensures that two observers agree on shared complex loci?
- Why is a global observer unnecessary when intersubjective gluing holds?
