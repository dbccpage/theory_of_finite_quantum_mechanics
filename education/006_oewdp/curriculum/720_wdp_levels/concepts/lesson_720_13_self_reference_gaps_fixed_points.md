- Awareness Firewall: Strict type barrier between self-presentation and external observation.
- Fixed Point Bounds: Self-referential queries resolve to bounded diagonal fixed points or typed refusals.
- Reflexive Provenance: Self-reports carry explicit self-reference tags.

## 3. Check for Understanding
