# Genesis primitives: audited educational models

AuthorityEffect: None. **Correction, 2026-10-01:** this directory is a selected
educational implementation, not a complete or faithful extraction of Genesis.
The previous blanket verification, physical-emergence and historical-pin
attribution claims are withdrawn at that scope. The
[adversarial audit](../adversarial_verification/README.md) records the original
assertions, exact source populations, countermodels, corrections and open work.

The original file population has 52 files, 22 Python implementation modules,
309 exported symbols and 19 Lean family modules. The source attribution is
per notion: event signatures, supplied declarations, metatheoretic apparatus,
derived definitions within declared models and optional domain realizations.
These are different roles, not 309 newly admitted object-language primitives.

## Source boundary

Genesis starts with one opaque whole `<>`; visible glyphs do not supply a
pair, relation, order or transition rule. Subsequent native supplied wholes
are `<a>` and `<ab>`. The `<ara>/<arb>` interpretation belongs to later
research. A witnessed irreflexive relation separates its endpoints after the
relation, witness and law have been supplied; those ingredients do not follow
from typography.

The historical tag `sealed-derivation-v1` peels to
`3100c4ed5ce70d2b6cff6d2ccd3b0251cf918d0a`. The audit consumes source HEAD
`f1d56c010e6ba10a3b9418bd7cc5953c067250f2` read-only. Of 58 explicit source
citations, 19 changed and five cited Lean files do not exist at the historical
pin. Later source results retain their own scopes; they cannot all be
attributed to the earlier tag. The full comparison is in the
[primitive inventory](../adversarial_verification/docs/primitive_inventory.md).

The [Genesis/WDP crosswalk](../wdp_integration.md) records how these selected
families and their form contracts enter the central paper and native owners.

## Verification and formal limits

The two original Python test files execute 67 tests, with a separate pinned
Lean build test skipped when its declared toolchain is unavailable. Those
passes do not establish full extraction fidelity. New tests expose legacy
limitations and reject forged, malformed and unauthorized demo receipts.

declarations. The [fresh Lean verifier](../adversarial_verification/scripts/verify_lean.py)
compiles the education modules and five new choice lemmas in isolation,

Lean 4.33.1 and is explicitly a compatibility check; this directory's declared
4.34.0 toolchain remains unchanged and is not claimed freshly verified.
No full original Genesis proof census or twelve-gate pass is asserted.
