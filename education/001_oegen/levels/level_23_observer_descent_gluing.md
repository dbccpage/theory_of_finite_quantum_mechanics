# Level 23: Two-chart gluing

AuthorityEffect: None. Teaching milestone; no identity with an S(n) stage or MSS index.

**Carrier:** The concrete G014 three-locus carrier and overlapping two-locus observers.

**Supplied assumptions:** Each record has actual values; the two records agree on their shared locus.

**Statement:** For records (v1,v2) and (v2′,v3), compatibility v2=v2′ permits the unique glued triple (v1,v2,v3). Unequal overlap values prohibit such a triple. The source proves its concrete preservation, uniqueness and obstruction statements; the broader finite-cover prose is not the scope of every Lean declaration. Labels naming observers alone supply neither restrictions nor gluing.
