# Genesis: admission, observation and continuation

AuthorityEffect: None. Source edition: `f1d56c010e6ba10a3b9418bd7cc5953c067250f2` (September 29, 2026).

Genesis is part of the mathematical and engineering foundation of Project Anomalon–Omega. Its continuing contribution is to make the admission of structure, its observation and its permissible change explicit. The original 19L57G1 source, this educational implementation, the current Rust primitives and WDP are distinct implementations and proof populations. Their correspondences are statements to establish at their actual interfaces, not a reason to discard that lineage.

Start with the opaque inscription `<>`. Its visible glyphs do not declare a pair, ambient space or relation. In the source admission model, showing a token is a presentation event and leaves the regime unchanged. Admitting a datum, form, instance, uniform rule or separation requirement is a separate operation. The host types used to implement these operations describe the model; they are not silently admitted as structure possessed by the initial inscription.

The [preceding synthesis and historical reports](archive/README.md) remain available without rewriting their receipts. This account supersedes their present-tense status paragraphs. ## The admission calculation

An erased regime is $R=(D,F,I,U,Q)$: admitted datum indices, form arities, instances, uniform rules and persistent separation requirements. Token-aware regimes also retain their opaque tokens. An instance must name an existing form and have exactly its arity of admitted arguments. A supply event changes neither carrier. The source's optional replay can refuse; a companion's total no-op on an invalid event is a different semantics.

Contexts plug a datum into the holes of a declared form. Derivability means that the resulting instance is admitted or its form has a uniform rule. Two data collapse when every well-formed context agrees about derivability. Separation requires an actual distinguishing context. An imposed requirement records a demand for separation; recording it does not make the demand true.

The two retained readings differ on whether the initial inscription was admitted. At the comparison point, Reading L has two data and Reading F has three. With no forms and no instances, all are observationally collapsed. Under the supplied declare-then-assert response grammar, one item cannot separate the target pair; a unary form and one instance cost two items and do separate it. This lower bound belongs to that grammar.

The first cost is $\mu_1(\Delta)=|\Delta|$. The second is the lexicographic pair $\mu_2(\Delta)=(|\Delta|,\sum\text{new-form arities})$. At bounds two items and arity two, the source reports six valid responses in three isomorphism classes for L under $\mu_1$, and ten in five classes for F. Under $\mu_2$, each reading has two minimal responses in one class. The measure is an explicit choice. Higher-arity responses explain why the unrestricted $\mu_1$ minimum is not a finite list of these classes.

Search implementation has an additional naming obligation. In the historical companion's [fresh-name collision case](https://github.com/dbccpage/the_symbol_game/blob/f7230710b70d70a3fe6ba8410bb6fb51772bec2f/omega_engine_education/documentation/001_oegen/research_extension/docs/source_fidelity.md), source augmentation chooses names already present in one token spelling: four of ten candidates become invalid under deduplication, while an isomorphic renaming makes all ten valid. Equal canonical keys therefore do not by themselves make this generator equivariant. The source remains frozen; its consumers must check freshness and retain refusal outcomes.

## Carriers and theorems

The admission regime is not the later Passage state. Passage has occurrence, assertion, witness, protection, observation, budget, license, refusal and halt fields. Its traces, guards, swap cells and costs are the subject of separate theorems. Budget and license can vary on cycles even though the remaining core cannot. Token erasure, observation quotients, complex coordinate models and optional physical models each need their own preservation statement.

The native bridge proves exact path/event replay correspondences and that supplied commuting step translations lift histories. An injective commuting encoding can nevertheless hide an entire source system in the budget register. NB-16 adds a map of observations: if the passage observation of an encoded state equals the mapped source observation, and the observation map is injective, then the source observation is constant on every source cycle. A two-state toggle observed by its state cannot have such a translation. This tests a particular native observation; it is not a theorem that every encoding preserves every question. See [Level 65](levels/level_65_quantum_gravity_bridge_commuting.md).

For an integer potential $\phi$, the native edge cost is
\[
d\phi(s,e)=\phi(T_e(s))-\phi(s).
\]
For a two-step square, the difference between the two path costs is
\[
d_\square(d\phi)(s;e,f)=\phi(T_f(T_e(s)))-\phi(T_e(T_f(s))).
\]
It vanishes for all potentials exactly when the endpoints agree. A noncommuting pair has not yet supplied a closed two-cell. Closed costs are invariant under the verified swap cells; a nonzero cycle cost excludes a potential. The state-changing-step count is closed and non-exact, whereas the budget delta is exact. NS-5 has explicit swap sequences and interchange modulo `FarRel`; it is not a strict higher category by naming alone. See [Level 29](levels/level_29_native_structure_separations.md).

CL-13 says that a quantity preserved by **every** Passage event has its genesis value at every state reachable from genesis. It does not quantify over all arbitrary states, derive a physical conservation law or settle the source's native-action question. See [Level 80](levels/level_80_scoped_authorization_invariants.md).

The [source's Section 3 correction ledger](https://github.com/dbccpage/19L57G1/blob/f1d56c010e6ba10a3b9418bd7cc5953c067250f2/docs/section3_claim_corrections.md) is part of this edition: finite descriptor uniqueness is not categorical initiality; the G013 cell definitions do not establish a strict 2-category; Boolean negation is not a universal self-predicting machine; integer-matrix proofs, rational computations and separately defined contextuality values keep their own carriers. G012 proves a typed codec and a cycle obstruction to a commuting left-invertible translation into admission traces; it does not prove that its separate Python string codec refines the Lean codec. These are useful established results and specific remaining bridges, not a single blanket verdict on the research estate.

## Alpha and the executable continuation

The source reproduced and repaired substituted-member execution, unreplayable-parent acceptance and reused-license execution. The final reference binds a license to history, target, measure and bounds, replays the parent, recomputes the evaluation, and executes that computed member. Unique minimality does not grant authorization; ties need an explicit disposition. The scope is a trusted local host. Scope strings and receipts are not network authentication. Python-to-Lean driver refinement and the endogenous-demand/Driver B question remain open.

A consumer must bind the source revision and full parent history; verify replay; declare reading, measure and bounds; evaluate fresh; check candidate membership; preserve ties and resource outcomes; bind authorization to that evaluation and authorized holder; prevent license reuse; and compare the executed result with the authorized member. Each is an implementation obligation. A flag named `licensed` is insufficient.

At that revision, the [research extension](https://github.com/dbccpage/the_symbol_game/blob/f7230710b70d70a3fe6ba8410bb6fb51772bec2f/omega_engine_education/documentation/001_oegen/research_extension/README.md) ran the actual pinned search/certification/export adapters, a companion admission proof, general signed transport and supplied domain packets through mathematical Rust owners. The [WDP Genesis construction](../../../omega_engine_atlas/coda_atlas/F004_papers/001_finite_obstruction_calculus/000_witnessed_difference_programme/docs/derivations/genesis_construction.md) starts a literal-root construction from the canonical Rust primitives, checks each admitted prefix against WDP, and constructs a finite simplicial complex. A triangle filling changes its homology; preservation of the old question is not inferred.

The engineering direction is to retain Genesis as the admission and continuation owner and extract established mathematical correspondences into the relevant mathematical and quantum crates. An exact encoding needs a round trip and operational correspondence. A quotient or invariant projection needs its declared protected questions and retained continuation data. Those different contracts let the project extend its foundation without pretending that all later structures were present in the original inscription.
