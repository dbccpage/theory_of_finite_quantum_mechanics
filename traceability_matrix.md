The Fermion Sign Problem (FSP) program provides an exact, discrete structural resolution of the sign problem on declared finite lattice models. It separates complexity barriers (Troyer-Wiese NP-hardness for generic, frustrated, or unconstrained models) from non-negative partition function reconstruction on bipartite, particle-hole symmetric, or meron-cluster lattices.

1. **Bounded Quotient Normal Form (BQNF):** A 5-fold canonical terminal routing theorem partition (`ClosedResidual`, `AdmittedZero`, `BoundarySyndrome`, `ExactLift`, `RepresentationInvalid`).
