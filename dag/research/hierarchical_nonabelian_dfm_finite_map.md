# QM-213: Substantial-vs-Artificial and Hierarchical Non-Abelian DFM Finite Map

## Main Finite Invariants (Computed, Exact over $\mathbb{Q}$)

### 1. Substantial vs. Artificial Classification via `LocalGreen` / `GlobalRed` Patch Cohomology

On the 4-vertex, 5-edge punctured diamond cell complex $X_{\mathrm{punct}}$ ($V = \{v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3\}$, edges $e_0=(0,1), e_1=(1,2), e_2=(0,2), e_3=(2,3), e_4=(1,3)$, one triangular face $F_0 = (v_0, v_1, v_2)$ covering the left cycle while the right cycle $(v_1, v_2, v_3)$ is punctured) and on the 5-vertex extended diamond poset $P_5 = \{0 < \{1, 2\} < 3 < 4\}$ with non-Abelian Borel structure group $B_2(\mathbb{Q}) \subset \mathrm{GL}(2, \mathbb{Q})$:

- Artificial symmetry (`LocalGreen` + `GlobalGreen`, `HolonomyVerdict::GaugeTrivial`):
  - For a connection $U^{\mathrm{art}}_e = h(t) h(s)^{-1}$ generated by non-commuting Borel vertex potentials $h : V \to B_2(\mathbb{Q})$, both the face holonomy on $F_0$ and all fundamental cycle holonomies equal $I_2$ (`PureGaugeVerdict::PureGauge`).
  - The local vertex dressing field $u_{\mathrm{loc}}(v) = T(v)^{-1}$ neutralizes all 5 edges (both tree edges and cotree chords) to $I_2$.
  - On $P_5$, `OperatorCarrier::flat_cover_extension` returns `Ok(A)` and the extended 1-cochain satisfies $K(A) = b A + A \smile A = 0 \in C^2(P_5)$.
- Substantial flat-patch symmetry (`LocalGreen` on $F_0$, `GlobalRed` on the punctured cycle, `HolonomyVerdict::FlatNontrivial`):
  - When $U^{\mathrm{sub}}$ has trivial face holonomy around $F_0$ ($H(F_0) = I_2$, `LocalGreen`) but non-trivial Borel holonomy $M = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1/2 \end{pmatrix} \neq I_2$ around the punctured cycle (`PureGaugeVerdict::NotPureGauge`), no single-valued local vertex dressing can neutralize all 5 edges.
  - Any dressing $u_T(v) = T(v)^{-1}$ is forced to be non-local (supported on a spanning tree $T$), neutralizing the 3 tree edges to $I_2$ while concentrating the non-Abelian holonomy onto the cotree chord $e_4$.
  - On $P_5$, `OperatorCarrier::flat_cover_extension` refuses the mismatched saturated paths with `RestrictionError::PathDependent { lower: 0, upper: 3, first_path: [0, 1, 3], second_path: [0, 2, 3] }`.
  - Residual ambiguity of the second kind ($T_1 \to T_2$): switching spanning trees from $T_1 = \{e_0, e_2, e_3\}$ to $T_2 = \{e_0, e_1, e_4\}$ transforms the dressed connection by the vertex intertwiner $C(v) = u_{T_2}(v) u_{T_1}(v)^{-1}$ while preserving the trace ($\operatorname{tr} = 5/2$) and determinant ($\det = 1$) of the fundamental cycle holonomy.

### 2. Two-Stage Hierarchical Non-Abelian Subgroup Dressing $N \triangleleft B_2(\mathbb{Q})$

Every invertible upper-triangular matrix $M \in B_2(\mathbb{Q}) = T \ltimes N$ factors uniquely into a diagonal quotient matrix $D = \pi_{B/N}(M) = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, d_2) \in T \cong B_2(\mathbb{Q})/N$ and a unit upper-triangular unipotent matrix $n(M) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & b/d_1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \in N \triangleleft B_2(\mathbb{Q})$. Along a spanning tree with root-to-vertex transport $T_B(v) = D(v) N_{\mathrm{tree}}(v)$:

- Stage 1 ($N$-dressing $u_N : V \to N$): defining $u_N(v) = D(v) N_{\mathrm{tree}}(v)^{-1} D(v)^{-1} \in N$ (which stays in $N$ because $N \triangleleft B_2(\mathbb{Q})$ is normal) gives $u_N(v) T_B(v) = D(v) \in T$.
  - On every tree edge $e = (s \to t)$, the Stage-1 dressed connection $U^{(1)}_e = u_N(t) U_e u_N(s)^{-1} = D(t) D(s)^{-1} \in T$ is diagonal (its unipotent off-diagonal entry is neutralized to $0$).
  - On all 5 edges (tree and cotree), the quotient projection is invariant: $\pi_{B/N}(U^{(1)}_e) = \pi_{B/N}(U_e)$.
  - Residual symmetry of the first kind: under any residual diagonal torus transformation $t : V \to T = B/N$, all tree edges of $(U^{(1)})^t$ remain diagonal.
- Stage 2 ($T = B/N$ quotient dressing $u_T : V \to T$): defining $u_T(v) = D(v)^{-1} \in T$ satisfies the exact factorization $u_T(v) u_N(v) = N_{\mathrm{tree}}(v)^{-1} D(v)^{-1} = T_B(v)^{-1}$, and dressing $U^{(1)}$ by $u_T$ reproduces `tree_gauge_indexed.residual()` on every edge.

### 3. Incidence DGA Curvature Covariance and Discrete Bianchi Identity on $P_5$

On the 5-vertex extended diamond poset $P_5 = \{0 < \{1, 2\} < 3 < 4\}$ with 7 strict 2-chains in $C^2(P_5)$ and 2 strict 3-chains ($[0, 1, 3, 4]$ and $[0, 2, 3, 4]$) in $C^3(P_5)$:

- Because the internal differential $b : C^p(P_5) \to C^{p+1}(P_5)$ deletes only internal vertices $i \in \{1, \dots, p\}$, every endpoint-indexed vertex dressing $(f^u)_{[v_0, \dots, v_p]} = u_{v_0} f_{[v_0, \dots, v_p]} u_{v_p}^{-1}$ commutes with $b$ and with the cup product $\smile$.
- Consequently, for any Borel 1-cochain $A \in C^1(P_5; \mathfrak{b}_2(\mathbb{Q}))$, Stage-1 unipotent dressing $u_N : V \to N$, and Stage-2 quotient transformation $t : V \to T = B/N$:
  - $b^2 A = 0 \in C^3(P_5)$,
  - $K(A^{u_N}) = b A^{u_N} + A^{u_N} \smile A^{u_N} = u_N K(A) u_N^{-1}$,
  - the diagonal $(B/N)$ entries of $K(A^{u_N})$ equal those of $K(A)$ on every 2-chain in $C^2(P_5)$,
  - $K((A^{u_N})^t) = t K(A^{u_N}) t^{-1} = (t u_N) K(A) (t u_N)^{-1}$, and
  - the discrete Bianchi identity $D_{A^u} K(A^u) = b K^u + A^u \smile K^u - K^u \smile A^u = 0 \in C^3(P_5)$ holds identically on both 3-chains before dressing, after Stage 1, and after Stage 2.
