## 1. Executive Summary

This note records the finite algebraic formulation of the Standard Model electroweak sector ($SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y \to U(1)_{\mathrm{EM}}$) over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}[i]$:

1. **Part A (`FQM-EW-DFM-DRESSING`): Finite $SU(2)_L$ Scalar-Doublet Dressing Field $u(\Phi) \in SU(2, \mathbb{Q}[i])$:**
   Rather than invoking spontaneous breaking of a local gauge symmetry (which Elitzur's theorem forbids on a lattice) or introducing Faddeev-Popov gauge-fixing ghosts, any nonvanishing scalar doublet $\Phi = (\phi_+, \phi_0)^T \in \mathbb{Q}[i]^2$ with radial norm $r \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ satisfying $|\phi_+|^2 + |\phi_0|^2 = r^2$ determines an explicit $SU(2)_L$-valued dressing matrix
   $$u(\Phi) = \frac{1}{r} \begin{pmatrix} \phi_0^* & \phi_+ \\ -\phi_+^* & \phi_0 \end{pmatrix} \in SU(2, \mathbb{Q}[i]).$$
   This dressing matrix satisfies $\det u(\Phi) = 1$, $u(\Phi)^\dagger u(\Phi) = I_2$, left $SU(2)_L$ equivariance $u(g\Phi) = g \, u(\Phi)$ for all $g \in SU(2, \mathbb{Q}[i])$, and eliminates the three Goldstone components identically via
   $$\Phi^u = u(\Phi)^\dagger \Phi = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ r \end{pmatrix}$$
   without gauge fixing. Dressed left-handed fermion doublets $\Psi_L^u(x) = u(\Phi(x))^\dagger \Psi_L(x)$ and dressed lattice links $U_{xy}^u = u(\Phi(x))^\dagger U_{xy} u(\Phi(y))$ are strictly invariant under local $SU(2)_L$ transformations and transform only under the residual $U(1)_{\mathrm{EM}}$ gauge group generated by $Q_{\mathrm{EM}} = T_3 + Y$.

2. **Part B (`FQM-EW-WEINBERG-CUSTODIAL`): Rational Weinberg Spectral Projectors & Custodial $\rho_{\mathrm{EW}} = 1$ Identity:**
   Evaluating the dressed scalar kinetic term at radial scale $v \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$ yields $m_W^2 = \frac{1}{4} g^2 v^2$ and the $2 \times 2$ neutral gauge mass-squared matrix
   $$M_{\text{neutral}}^2(g, g', v) = \frac{v^2}{4} \begin{pmatrix} g^2 & -g g' \\ -g g' & g'^2 \end{pmatrix}$$
   over $\mathbb{Q}$, which satisfies $\det(M_{\text{neutral}}^2) = 0$ (massless photon $\gamma$) and $\operatorname{Tr}(M_{\text{neutral}}^2) = \frac{v^2}{4}(g^2 + g'^2) = m_Z^2$. The squared Weinberg mixing fractions and the orthogonal spectral projectors onto the photon and $Z^0$ eigenspaces are rational functions of $(g, g')$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ (requiring no square roots):
   $$\sin^2 \theta_W = \frac{g'^2}{g^2 + g'^2}, \qquad \cos^2 \theta_W = \frac{g^2}{g^2 + g'^2}, \qquad \Pi_\gamma = \frac{1}{g^2 + g'^2} \begin{pmatrix} g'^2 & g g' \\ g g' & g^2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \Pi_Z = I_2 - \Pi_\gamma.$$
   Across all nonvanishing rational couplings $(g, g')$, the tree-level custodial parameter satisfies
   $$\rho_{\mathrm{EW}} \equiv \frac{m_W^2}{m_Z^2 \cos^2 \theta_W} = 1,$$
   in contrast to a $Y = 1$ scalar triplet which yields $\rho_{\text{triplet}} = 1/2 \neq 1$.

3. **Part C (`FQM-EW-GELL-MANN-NISHIJIMA`): Gell-Mann–Nishijima Charge Operator $Q_{\mathrm{EM}} = T_3 + Y$:**
   With Standard Model hypercharges $Y_{\mathrm{SM}} = (Y_Q, Y_u, Y_d, Y_L, Y_e) = (1/6, 2/3, -1/3, -1/2, -1)$, the dressed fermion electric charges are $(Q_u, Q_d, Q_\nu, Q_e) = (2/3, -1/3, 0, -1)$ with vector-like electromagnetic current matching $Q(f_L) = Q(f_R)$ for $u, d, e$ and composite neutrality $Q(p) = 2 Q_u + Q_d = +1$, $Q(n) = Q_u + 2 Q_d = 0$, $Q(p) + Q(e) = 0$.

4. **Part D (`FQM-EW-ANOMALY-CANCELLATION`): Chiral Gauge & Gravitational Anomaly Cancellation over $\mathbb{Q}$:**
   At $N_c = 3$ quark colors, the four 1-loop triangle anomaly polynomials vanish simultaneously over $\mathbb{Q}$:
   $$A_{SU(3)^2 U(1)_Y} = 0, \quad A_{SU(2)^2 U(1)_Y} = 0, \quad A_{\mathrm{grav}^2 U(1)_Y} = 0, \quad A_{U(1)_Y^3} = -\frac{3}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 0,$$
   whereas colorless quarks ($N_c = 1$) fail with $A_{SU(2)^2 U(1)_Y} = -1/3 \neq 0$ and $A_{U(1)_Y^3} = +1/2 \neq 0$. Under the linear anomaly constraints with $Y_L = -1/2, Y_e = -1, Y_Q = \frac{1}{2 N_c}, Y_u = Y_Q + \delta, Y_d = Y_Q - \delta$, the cubic equation $A_{U(1)_Y^3} = 0$ simplifies identically to $-3 \delta^2 + 3/4 = 0 \iff \delta^2 = 1/4$.
