## 1. Executive Summary

This research dossier establishes the finite-graph topological and algebraic mechanism underlying identical-particle exchange statistics, Pauli exclusion, the Jordan–Wigner Canonical Anticommutation Relations (CAR), the Hanbury Brown–Twiss bosonic bunching factor of 2, and the periodic-table atomic shell capacities $2n^2 = (2, 8, 18, 32)$ and Madelung noble-gas closures $Z \in \{2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86, 118\}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$:

1. **Discrete 2-Particle Configuration Square 2-Complex $\operatorname{Conf}_2^{(2)}(G)$:**
   For two non-colliding identical particles on a finite simple graph $G = (V, E)$, the unordered configuration complex $\operatorname{Conf}_2^{(2)}(G)$ has $0$-cells $\{\{u, v\} \subset V : u \neq v\}$ of cardinality $\binom{|V|}{2}$, $1$-cells corresponding to single-particle hops $(u, v) \leftrightarrow (u, w)$ along edges $(v, w) \in E$ into unoccupied vertices, and commuting $2$-cells (disjoint-move squares) for every vertex-disjoint edge pair $e_1 \cap e_2 = \emptyset$:
   - **1D Path Obstruction ($P_3$) & Simple Tree Y-Junction ($Y = K_{1,3}$):** For $P_3$, $(V_2, E_2, F_2) = (3, 2, 0)$ with $b_1^{(1)} = b_1^{(2)} = 0$ (no exchange loop). For the simple star $Y = K_{1,3}$, $(V_2, E_2, F_2) = (6, 6, 0)$ with $b_1^{(1)} = b_1^{(2)} = 1$ and $\operatorname{tors}_{\mathbb{Z}_2} = 0$, showing that a degree-$3$ vertex without disjoint-edge squares generates free abelian $\mathbb{Z}$ exchange holonomy (`ABELIAN_BRAID_Z`) rather than $\mathbb{Z}_2$.
   - **Planar Braid Regimes ($C_4$ and $K_4$):** For $C_4$, $(V_2, E_2, F_2) = (6, 8, 2)$, $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\partial_2) = \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(\partial_2) = 2$, $b_1^{(1)} = 3$, $b_1^{(2)} = 1$, and $\operatorname{tors}_{\mathbb{Z}_2} = 0$ (`ABELIAN_BRAID_Z`). For planar $K_4$, $(V_2, E_2, F_2) = (6, 12, 3)$, $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\partial_2) = \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(\partial_2) = 3$, $b_1^{(1)} = 7$, $b_1^{(2)} = 4$, and $\operatorname{tors}_{\mathbb{Z}_2} = 0$ (`PLANAR_FREE_BRAID`).
   - **Non-Planar Symmetric-Group Involution Regime ($K_5$):** For $K_5$, $(V_2, E_2, F_2) = (10, 30, 15)$, $b_1^{(1)} = 21$, $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\partial_2) = 7$, $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(\partial_2) = 6$, $b_1^{(2)} = 21 - 7 = 14$, and $\operatorname{tors}_{\mathbb{Z}_2}(H_1(\operatorname{Conf}_2^{(2)}(K_5); \mathbb{Z})) = 7 - 6 = 1 > 0$ (`SYMMETRIC_Z2_INVOLUTION`), so $H_1(\operatorname{Conf}_2^{(2)}(K_5); \mathbb{Z})$ carries an explicit $\mathbb{Z}_2$ torsion class (`2 [γ_σ] = 0` in cellular homology after attaching the 15 disjoint-edge squares) where $\chi(\sigma)^2 = 1$ factors over $\mathbb{Q}$ as $(\chi - 1)(\chi + 1) = 0$, admitting exclusively $\chi = +1$ (bosons) and $\chi = -1$ (fermions).
2. **Slater Determinant Pauli Exclusion, Fermi Hole, and Hanbury Brown–Twiss Bosonic Bunching:**
   - In the alternating sector ($\chi = -1$), the $N$-particle amplitude is the exterior wedge product (Slater determinant $\det[\phi_i(x_j)]$), which vanishes identically whenever two particles share an orbital ($\phi_a = \phi_b \implies a b - b a = 0$) or coincide at the same graph site (Fermi hole $p_{\text{coincide}} = 0$), while its Born readout square $(-1)^2 = +1$ remains exchange-invariant.
   - In the symmetric sector ($\chi = +1$), the $N$-particle amplitude is the bosonic permanent $\operatorname{perm}[\phi_i(x_j)]$, whose coincidence weight $(a b + b a)^2 = 4 a^2 b^2$ equals exactly twice the distinguishable sum $(a b)^2 + (b a)^2 = 2 a^2 b^2$ (Hanbury Brown–Twiss factor of 2).

   - On the finite Fock space $\bigwedge^*(\mathbb{Q}^d)$, the Jordan–Wigner creation and annihilation matrices $c_i^\dagger, c_i$ satisfy exact nilpotency $(c_i^\dagger)^2 = c_i^2 = 0$ and $\{c_i, c_j^\dagger\} = \delta_{ij} I$.
