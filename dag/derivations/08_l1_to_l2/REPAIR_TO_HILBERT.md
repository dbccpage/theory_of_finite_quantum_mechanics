# Repair quotients, positive weights and the Hilbert boundary

AuthorityEffect: None. Consolidates the common argument of QM-002/003/005/010/011/020/062/122.

**Declared finite repair model.** For d₀:C⁰→C¹ and a norm on C¹,
Φ([x])=infᵅ‖x−d₀α‖ is the quotient seminorm on C¹/im d₀. It is a norm when
the repair image is closed. Finite dimension supplies closedness; the distance
identity itself does not need finite dimension. This is a statement about the
declared normed model, not a selection of that norm from `<>`.

**Mass is extra data.** Nonnegative weights wᵢ with Σwᵢ=1 can be placed on a
finite set. Writing diag(w) embeds that simplex in a supplied matrix algebra.
Choosing phases and square roots gives ψᵢ=√wᵢ exp(iθᵢ), hence |ψᵢ|²=wᵢ.
That identity verifies a chosen lift. It does not establish that all physical
readouts must be quadratic or that a field contains every required square root.

**A population report can lose dynamical information.** Let
ψ±=(3,±4)/5 and U=((3,4),(−4,3))/5. Both states have population report
(9/25,16/25). Yet Uψ+=(1,0) and Uψ−=(−7,−24)/25, whose first populations
are 1 and 49/625. Therefore this U has no update rule on that report alone.
The conclusion uses the supplied real vector carrier, U and square report.
It is not a uniqueness theorem for complex QM, and the L1 norm itself does
not erase signs; the selected report does.

**Complex structure is a conditional construction.** An admitted operator J
with J²=−I defines (a+ib)x=ax+bJx over Q(i). A compatible symmetric pairing
with g(Jx,y)=−g(x,Jy) gives a Hermitian form after a convention is fixed.
Existence and selection of J and g remain premises. Finite rotations, all
rational phases, and their metric completion are distinct carriers.
