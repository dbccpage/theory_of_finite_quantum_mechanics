## Tomography and local reversibility do not choose a cone

For n,m>=2, the separable cone SEP and its trace-dual block-positive cone BP
are distinct valid locally tomographic composites of the same quantum local
systems. Pair SEP states with BP effects, or BP states with SEP effects, taking
order-unit intervals for normalized effects. Each has independent preparation,
product tests, trace normalization, no-signalling for product measurements, and
all local unitary conjugations. Both are closed convex cones. Neither forces
PSD(nm). These are full cones, not finite lists of test states.

An explicit two-qubit separation uses

    psi = (|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2), B=|psi><psi|,
    W = partial_transpose_A(B) = SWAP/2.

For all complex a,b, <a,b|W|a,b>=|<a,b>|^2/2>=0, whereas
<01-10|W|01-10>=-1. Thus W belongs to BP but not PSD. Every separable positive
matrix has positive partial transpose (transpose preserves local positivity,
as do sums), while B does not. Thus B belongs to PSD but not SEP. Embed this
two-level construction into any larger pair of local dimensions.

The ordinary controlled shift sends the separable |+><+| tensor |0><0| to B,
so SEP fails its forward closure. Since C|-,1>=|01>-|10>, with the unnormalized
minus vector, C*WC has a negative product test. Thus BP fails closure too.
These are the two directions used in TFQ-0412, without an assumption that the
state cone is self-dual.

## An unspecified reversible interaction is insufficient for a fixed pair

Let Gamma be partial transpose on A and let K_tw=Gamma(PSD). Gamma is an
invertible trace-preserving real linear map and an isometry for the trace
pairing. Thus K_tw is closed, pointed, generating, and self-dual. It contains
all product positive matrices and lies in BP. Local conjugations preserve it,
since Gamma Ad_(U tensor V) Gamma = Ad_(conj(U) tensor V).

For any entangling unitary R, the transformation Gamma Ad_R Gamma is reversible
on K_tw and can create correlations. For a continuous R(t), it also gives a
continuous reversible interaction. Nevertheless K_tw differs from PSD in fixed
local coordinates: it contains W above. It is order-isomorphic to PSD via a
local transpose convention, so this is not a claim of an inequivalent abstract
quantum theory. It shows that a bare existence-of-interaction hypothesis does
not fix the stated product-coordinate convention for one bipartite system.

For the two-qubit controlled shift, Ad_C and Gamma Ad_C Gamma agree on all
computational-basis input states. They disagree on the complete product
projector calibration table, including coherent imaginary projectors.
Dephasing after Ad_C also passes the classical truth table, but fails that
calibration and reversibility. The exact tests retain both mutations.

## Removing tomography admits hidden joint coordinates

Take V=Herm(nm) direct-sum Herm(nm), positive cone PSD direct-sum PSD and unit
Tr(X)+Tr(Y). Prepare a product as (A tensor B)/2 in each summand. Let a product
effect act as Tr[(E tensor F)(X+Y)]. These choices have factorized independent
probabilities, local quantum marginals, and a controlled shift acting on each
summand. Full tests can also detect the summand flag. The states (rho,0) and
(0,rho) are distinct but agree on every product test, so local tomography fails.
The composite dimension is twice (nm)^2. Hence the coordinate reconstruction
cannot dispense with operational separation.

## A weaker-calibration route with broader composition assumptions

There is a published alternative for systems generated by identical qubits:
local tomography, all local unitaries, continuous reversible interaction, and
closure under adding identical systems and permuting their roles force standard
quantum theory. The proof uses three-system consistency to eliminate the
partially transposed alternative above. See de la Torre, Masanes, Short and
Mueller, Theorems 1 and 2 in
[Deriving quantum theory from its local structure and reversibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5482).
This is an external theorem, not a new Lean result. Its qubit-family and
composition-closure assumptions must not be replaced by a claim about any
isolated pair of arbitrary local dimensions.

The present finite-dimension route uses a more specified gate but does not
require a continuous global dynamics postulate or a classification of all
interactions. Neither route derives the local complex quantum systems from
primitive difference. The sufficient package is explicit; no claim that all
listed premises are logically minimal has been made.
