# Admission, difference and reports

AuthorityEffect: None. Combined entry point for PREGENESIS through WDP.

The inscription `<>` and a mathematical model of distinction are different
entries in the DAG. `PG-INERT-DIAMOND` records the inscription;
`PG-ROOT-DIFF-STRUCTURE` admits structure. `TFQ-0001` records the gap between
them. Do not read the numbering as a physical history before time is supplied.

Once a carrier X and report q:X→Y are declared, a question f:X→Z can be answered
from the report precisely when it is constant on every fibre of q. Necessity:
f=g∘q gives f(x)=f(x′) whenever q(x)=q(x′). Sufficiency: define g on the
realized image by the common value in each fibre. Extending g off that image
is a separate contract. This is `PG-HISTORY-REPORT-CRITERION`; it does not
construct X, q or the underlying logic from the inscription.

Admitting a tag can separate a collapsed pair. Choosing that tag, preserving an
old answer, and proving that a family of tags separates every pair are separate
obligations. An injective lift alone does not specify a recovery map on every
new state. The Genesis and S(n) records retain these distinctions.

WDP then takes a declared chain/cochain window. The condition d₁d₀=0 makes
repair by d₀ invisible to d₁; it does not derive the window or its coefficients.
The graph retains the Genesis-to-window gap and the residual-to-weight gap.
Those gaps are prior to assigning probabilities or Hilbert coordinates.

For computation, admitting separated marks permits finite alphabets. Words,
rewriting, machine simulation and unbounded supply are further declarations.
See [the computation route](../../../atlas/computation_complexity_pvnp/README.md)
and the existing `COMP-C1` through `COMP-C8` records.
