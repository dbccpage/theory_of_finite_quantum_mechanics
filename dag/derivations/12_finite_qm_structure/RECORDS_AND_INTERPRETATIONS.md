# Records, interference and interpretations

AuthorityEffect: None. Combined scope of QM-043, QM-090/091/093/094/095 and the Everett teaching draft.

In a declared tensor model, an interaction can correlate a system basis with
orthogonal records: Σᵢaᵢ|i⟩|0⟩↦Σᵢaᵢ|i⟩|rᵢ⟩. Ignoring the record removes
the corresponding off-diagonal terms from the reduced system state. This
follows from ⟨rⱼ|rᵢ⟩=δᵢⱼ and the supplied partial trace. It is not an
additional collapse law or a derivation of the probability pairing.

Under ideal joint reversible control the correlation can be undone, recovering
interference. An unread record and an erased record are therefore different
experimental conditions. Loss of interference alone does not identify its
cause, and actual control of every relevant environmental record is a separate
physical assumption.

Relative-state language, Copenhagen descriptions and other interpretations
can agree on a declared finite packet of predictions. Agreement on that packet
does not select an ontology, establish branching worlds, or establish that every
interpretation makes identical predictions under every possible extension.
