# Missing pieces between finite quantum models and quantum gravity

AuthorityEffect: None. A research obligation register, not a completed physical theory.

The repository has finite relational, cochain, gauge, recoupling and curvature
models. Their roles differ. A kinematic configuration space, a finite identity,
a numerical experiment and a physical dynamical law cannot stand in for one
another. The relevant existing stages are FINITE_QG, CONTINUUM_INTERFACE,
CAUSAL_QFT, ADS_CFT and LOOP_QUANTUM_GRAVITY.

These obligations are route-dependent. A theory with fundamental discreteness
may target an effective smooth regime without claiming a literal continuum
limit; it still owes controlled recovery of the measured regime. AdS/CFT and
loop-gravity branches are distinct candidate routes and cannot be silently
combined into one theorem.

The exact Regge proxy in `TFQ-0100` is distinct from the floating scripts in
`TFQ-0099` and `TFQ-0101`. The finite spin-foam receipts do not discharge the
four-dimensional constraint and continuum problems. No algebraic rearrangement
of these files changes those boundaries.
