# TFQ-0011 finite QCD certificate

AuthorityEffect: None.

This note records a finite rational certificate. It does not identify that certificate with continuum confinement.

## Plaquette gauge

Let G be a group and let g, h, k, m, a, b, c, d be elements of G. The four edge values after vertex gauges a, b, c, d are a^{-1} g b, b^{-1} h c, c^{-1} k d and d^{-1} m a. Their product collapses by cancellation of b b^{-1}, c c^{-1} and d d^{-1}:

a^{-1} g h k m a.

So a vertex gauge conjugates the plaquette product. It does not build an SU(3) matrix representation.

In S3, write s01 for the transposition of 0 and 1 and s12 for the transposition of 1 and 2. These two transpositions do not commute. The product s01 s12 1 1 is not the identity, so a four-edge word can be nontrivial.

## Channel formulae and beta coefficients

Write C_F(n) = (n^2 - 1) / (2 n) and C_A(n) = n. At n = 3,

C_F = 4/3, C_A = 3,

singlet = -C_F = -4/3,

octet = C_A / 2 - C_F = 3/2 - 4/3 = 1/6,

sextet = 1/2 - 1/(2*3) = 1/3,

antitriplet = -1/2 - 1/6 = -2/3.

The singlet value is negative and the octet value is positive.

The gluon split used by the finite control is 4 C_A + (-1/3) C_A = (11/3) C_A. At C_A = 3 this is 11. One Dirac flavor, with the factor (-4/3) * (1/2), contributes -2/3. The recorded abelian coefficient -4/3 is negative. Define

beta0(n_c, n_f) = (11/3) n_c - (2/3) n_f.

Then beta0(3, n_f) = 11 - (2/3) n_f. The values are 11, 9, 23/3, 7, 1/3 and -1/3 at n_f = 0, 3, 5, 6, 16 and 17. For a rational n_f, 11 - (2/3) n_f > 0 if and only if n_f < 33/2, because 2/3 is positive. Every natural number n ≤ 16 therefore gives a positive value, and n = 17 gives -1/3.

Define

beta1(n_c, n_f) = (34/3) n_c^2 - ((10/3) n_c + (n_c^2 - 1) / n_c) n_f.

At n_c = 3 the coefficient of n_f is (10/3)*3 + 8/3 = 38/3, and (34/3)*9 = 102, so beta1(3, n_f) = 102 - (38/3) n_f. The values are 102, 116/3 and -302/3 at n_f = 0, 5 and 16. The ratio (-beta0(3, 16)) / beta1(3, 16) = (-1/3) / (-302/3) = 1/302.

The same beta1 values are recovered from (34/3) C_A^2 - ((20/3) C_A + 4 C_F) T_F n_f with T_F = 1/2 and C_F = (n_c^2 - 1) / (2 n_c).

## Creutz monomials and declared counts

For a nonzero rational b and integers r, t ≥ 1, the area monomial is b^(r t) and the perimeter monomial is b^(2(r+t)). The Creutz ratio is (W(r,t) W(r-1,t-1)) / (W(r,t-1) W(r-1,t)).

The area exponents satisfy r t + (r-1)(t-1) = r(t-1) + (r-1)t + 1, so the ratio equals b. The perimeter exponents are equal, so the ratio equals 1. At b = 1/9 the area ratios on the pairs (2, 2) and (3, 3) equal 1/9, the perimeter ratios equal 1, and 1/9 is not 1.

The declared integers 9 and 12 satisfy 9 - 1 = 8, 12 - 9 + 1 = 4 and 12 - 8 = 4. These are counts only. No 3x3 complex is constructed in the proof.
