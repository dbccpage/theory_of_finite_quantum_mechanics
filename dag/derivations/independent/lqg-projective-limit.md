# An infinite spin-label measure from compatible finite laws

AuthorityEffect: None.

This result advances `LQG-CONTINUUM-LIMIT-GAP` by constructing a genuine
infinite-configuration measure from supplied compatible finite label laws.
The full LQG continuum/semiclassical target stays OPEN. The probabilities
and their compatibility are hypotheses, not consequences of a finite
recoupling table or of complex spin-foam amplitudes.

## The supplied finite laws

Let A be a finite discrete topological alphabet with a supplied measurable
structure. For each finite set I of natural-number coordinate indices,
supply a probability measure P_I on the finite configurations I -> A.
Require compatibility under restriction:

    J subset I  ==>  P_J = (restriction I -> J)_* P_I.

The conclusion is a unique probability measure mu on all configurations
Nat -> A, equipped with the product measurable structure, whose marginal
on every finite I is P_I. The theorem permits correlations; it does not
assume that P_I is a product of independent one-coordinate distributions.
The label alphabet {0,1,2} is the `Fin 3` specialization.

## Cylinders and finite additivity

A cylinder fixes an event S on finitely many coordinates I and permits all
other coordinates. Give that cylinder mass P_I(S). The supplied consistency
makes the value independent of the chosen finite presentation. Mathlib's
`projectiveFamilyContent` packages the resulting finite additive content on
the ring of measurable cylinders. The finite measures give finite content.

This step names existing foundational measure-theory lemmas explicitly.
The new result proves the additional continuity property needed to extend
that content to a countably additive measure in this finite-alphabet model.

## Compactness gives continuity at the empty set

The infinite product of finite discrete spaces is compact. Every cylinder
is closed: its finite-coordinate base set is finite and closed, and
restriction to those coordinates is continuous. Thus a decreasing sequence
of nonempty cylinders has nonempty intersection by compactness.

Consequently, if a decreasing sequence of cylinders has empty intersection,
one of the cylinders is already empty. Every later one is empty as well.
Its content is eventually zero, so the content tends to zero. The Lean
lemmas `cylinder_closed`, `empty_stage` and `content_tendsto_zero` prove this
argument; finite sampling is not used to assert compactness.

A finite additive content on a ring with this continuity property is
sigma-additive. `content_sigma_subadditive` connects the compactness argument
to Mathlib's extension machinery. `limitMeasure` then uses the
Caratheodory construction on the sigma-algebra generated by cylinders,
which is exactly the product measurable structure.

## Marginals, normalization and uniqueness

`limit_marginals` proves that the constructed measure gives every cylinder
its prescribed finite mass, equivalently that its finite restriction is
P_I. In particular its total mass is one (`limit_probability`). Two finite
measures with these cylinder values agree on the generated sigma-algebra,
so `limit_unique` and `exists_unique_limit` establish uniqueness.

Conversely, composing coordinate restrictions shows that any infinite
measure with marginals P_I forces the family to be compatible.
`projective_of_limit` proves this necessity, and
`exists_limit_iff_projective` combines it with existence. For supplied
probability laws on a finite alphabet, compatibility is therefore exactly
the remaining condition for this measure extension. Unit total mass at
each finite stage alone is insufficient.

## Countably many local constraints

For each n, supply a finite window I_n and an allowed event S_n in that
window. If P_(I_n)(S_n) = 1, the corresponding cylinder has mu-probability
one. The intersection of countably many probability-one events also has
probability one. Thus almost every infinite configuration satisfies every
supplied local constraint simultaneously. At least one such configuration
exists. These are `countable_constraints` and
`constraints_have_configuration`.

`spin_labels_extension` instantiates the measure construction with `Fin 3`.
`spin_constraints_ae` additionally proves the local-constraint conclusion
for arbitrary allowed predicates on finite spin windows; every such
predicate is measurable. It can be applied to level-two triangle
admissibility when the supplied finite laws give those predicates full
mass. It does not infer those full-mass premises from a label census.
The conclusion is almost everywhere, not that all configurations in the
ambient product satisfy the constraints.

## Independent exact model and negative controls

`checks/independent/lqg-projective-limit.py` uses exact rational path
probabilities for a correlated Markov chain z_n in {0,1,2}. The initial law
is (1/2, 1/3, 1/6), and the transition matrix is

    [ 1/2  1/3  1/6 ]
    [ 1/4  1/2  1/4 ]
    [ 1/6  1/3  1/2 ].

Each supplied local triangle has labels (z_n,z_n,0), which satisfy the
level-two triangle inequalities, even-sum condition and sum-at-most-four
condition. These are explicit triangle blocks; a manifold incidence
structure is not asserted. Direct path enumeration produces probability
laws through four blocks. The positive support sizes are 3, 9, 27 and 81.
The script recomputes those counts, normalization, all earlier-level full
marginals, every marginal on subsets of at most three coordinates, and the
probability-one constraints. A separate transfer-matrix calculation checks
nonlocal pair marginals. The example is explicitly verified to be
correlated, so an unintended independence assumption would be exposed.

Negative controls check a normalized but incompatible family, signed
weights with unit total, a violated local constraint without its full-mass
premise, and two laws with the same finite support but different weights.
The script imports no repository module. These are finite controls; the
infinite extension and uniqueness are proved in Lean, not inferred by
extrapolation from the four-block enumeration.

## What is proved

Existence and uniqueness of an infinite probability measure with the
supplied finite marginals; necessity of compatibility; preservation of
countably many probability-one local constraints and existence of a
satisfying infinite configuration; and the three-spin-label specializations.

## What is assumed

The finite alphabet, discrete topology, product measurable structure,
normalized nonnegative finite laws, their restriction compatibility, and
the full-mass local constraints. The construction uses the usual
compactness and measure-extension foundations already in Mathlib.

## What is open

Obtaining such laws from physical LQG or spin-foam amplitudes; identifying
coordinates with a triangulation/refinement system; changing the cutoff
alphabet; signed or complex amplitudes; a manifold, semiclassical geometry,
Einstein dynamics or a physical cosmological constant. No route from the
existing finite recoupling data to those conclusions is promoted.
