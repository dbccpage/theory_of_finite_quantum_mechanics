# DS-QM062-U1-RESIDUES-IMPORT

The imported claim asks for continuity of reversible dynamics, global field uniqueness, local tomography, and a U(1) phase fiber. This note records one integer matrix of order 4 and the remainder identity on the natural numbers.

## Matrix and remainder

The identity matrix is the tuple (1, 0, 0, 1). The matrix J is the tuple (0, -1, 1, 0). Matrix product is the usual row-column product of these four integers. The square of J is (-1, 0, 0, -1). The cube is (0, 1, -1, 0). The fourth product is the identity. J, the square, and the cube differ from the identity.

The scalar steps 0 * 0 - 1 * 1 = -1 and (-1) * (-1) = 1 are the diagonal entries of that square and of the fourth product.

For every natural number k, the remainder of k + 4 on division by 4 equals the remainder of k. The k-th right product by J equals the representative of that remainder: identity, J, the square, or the cube. The product equals the identity if and only if the remainder is 0. The product at k + 4 equals the product at k. The remainders of 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 are 0, 1, 2, 3 and 0.

## What is proved

The order-4 multiplication table of this integer matrix, the remainder identity (k + 4) % 4 = k % 4 for every natural number k, and the equality of the k-th product with the representative of k modulo 4.

## What is assumed

Arithmetic of the integers and the natural numbers, including division with remainder by 4. No circle, no complex field, and no continuity axiom are assumed.

## What is open

Continuity of reversible dynamics, global field uniqueness across dimension n, local tomography, a U(1) phase fiber, a complex involution, and a separation of that fiber from Q/Z torsion. The matrix identities name none of those carriers.
