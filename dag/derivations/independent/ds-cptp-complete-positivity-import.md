# DS-CPTP-COMPLETE-POSITIVITY-IMPORT

The imported claim asks for complete positivity of a tensor extension, Kraus, Choi and Stinespring forms, and coherence transport above a classical kernel. This note records one nonnegative integer product and one strict sign.

## Scalar forms

For every integer n, n * n is at least 0. If t, x and w are integers and t is at least 0, then t * ((x + w) * (x + w)) is at least 0.

The sum 1 * (-1) + (-1) * 1 equals -2. For every integer t, t times that sum equals (-2) * t, and t * (-2) equals 2 * (-t). If t is greater than 0, then t * (-2) is less than 0 and -t is less than 0. If t is 0, then t * (-2) is 0. The sum of squares of the four integers 0, 1, -1 and 0 equals 2.

## What is proved

Nonnegativity of an integer square and of a product of two nonnegative integers, the family t * ((x + w) * (x + w)) for t at least 0, the pairing -2, the strict sign of t * (-2) for t greater than 0, the zero product at t = 0, and the sum of squares 2.

## What is assumed

Arithmetic of the integers. No tensor product, no channel, and no environment extension are assumed.

## What is open

Complete positivity of a tensor extension, Kraus, Choi and Stinespring forms, and coherence transport above a classical kernel. The scalar sign does not select those forms.
