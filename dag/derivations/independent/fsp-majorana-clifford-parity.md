## Declared algebra

Let A be a complex unital ring with an involutive star compatible with complex
scalar multiplication. For a declared index type K, supply operators c_j and
the canonical anticommutation relations (CAR):

    c_j c_k + c_k c_j = 0,
    c_j c_k* + c_k* c_j = δ_jk 1.

These are the fields of `CARFamily`; they are hypotheses. The star-ring and
complex-algebra laws are also imports. No norm, representation, vacuum, state,
or Hilbert-space completion is assumed in the generic identities. Complex
scalars are symbolic; the concrete certificates below use Q(i) only.

Index gamma by K × Bool. The false entry is c_j + c_j*, and the true entry is
−i(c_j − c_j*). If K is finite, `gamma_index_count` computes the index count
as twice the cardinality of K. This does not assert linear independence of
the operators merely because they have different indices.

## Algebraic calculation

Taking stars of the first CAR identity gives the creation-creation identity.
Reversing indices in the mixed identity gives
{c_j*, c_k} = δ_jk 1. Expand each gamma anticommutator bilinearly:

    {γ_j,false, γ_k,false} = 2δ_jk 1,
    {γ_j,false, γ_k,true} = 0,
    {γ_j,true, γ_k,true} = 2δ_jk 1.

Here −i squared is −1, with no approximation. Star reverses the sign of i
and of c_j − c_j*, so both kinds of gamma are self-adjoint. Lean declarations
`gamma_star` and `gamma_ac` state these identities for arbitrary K and A.

The inverse formulas are

    c_j  = (γ_j,false + i γ_j,true)/2,
    c_j* = (γ_j,false − i γ_j,true)/2.

The declarations `recover_c` and `recover_star_c` establish them. Each set
therefore lies in the complex subalgebra generated by the other set.
`generated_algebra` proves equality of the concrete generated subalgebras.
It is not an identification with a real or complex abstract Clifford algebra,
a universal property, faithfulness, or an all-mode dimension theorem.

## Occupation and parity

Set n_j = c_j* c_j. The diagonal CAR equation gives c_j² = 0, since the
scalar 2 is invertible. Also c_j c_j* = 1 − n_j. Thus

    n_j² = c_j*(1 − c_j*c_j)c_j = n_j,
    n_j* = n_j,
    −i γ_j,false γ_j,true = 1 − 2n_j.

The corresponding declarations are `annihilate_sq`, `occupation_sq`,
`occupation_star`, and `localParity_pair`. In particular the minus sign in
front of i is essential. For q_j = 1 − 2n_j, the declarations
`localParity_star` and `localParity_sq` give q_j* = q_j and q_j² = 1.

For any ordered list of modes, `parity_product` rewrites the product of the
Majorana pairs into the same ordered product of q_j. No unordered product or
permutation independence is silently introduced by this statement.

There is a further conditional, inductive statement. On any A-module, supply
a vector v and bits b_j with n_j v = b_j v. `localParity_action` gives
q_j v = (−1)^b_j v. `parity_action` then gives

    (product over the declared list of q_j) v
      = (−1)^(number of true bits in that list) v.

The list can be empty and may contain repetitions. To interpret the exponent
as total particle number, the list must enumerate the chosen modes once.
Existence and completeness of a common occupation eigenbasis, construction
of exterior Fock space for arbitrary mode count, and the operator notation
(−1)^N_op are not supplied by this theorem.

## Exact independent route

`checks/independent/fsp-majorana-clifford-parity.py` imports only the standard
library and SymPy. It constructs Jordan-Wigner operators independently:

    c_j = Z^tensor(j) tensor [[0,1],[0,0]] tensor I^tensor(N−j−1),
    Z = diag(1,−1).

For N from 0 through 4, it checks CAR, self-adjoint gammas, the Clifford
relations, the inverse formulas, occupation projections, the signed local
parity identity, commuting local parities, global parity adjoint and square,
and the global parity/odd-generator anticommutators. It computes the number
operator and parity diagonal in the occupation basis. Matrix dimensions are
1, 2, 4, 8, 16; gamma counts are 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. Every entry is admitted by
SymPy's Gaussian-rational domain Q(i).

Only for N from 0 through 3 does it additionally enumerate every ordered
Clifford monomial and compute the rank of their flattened matrices: 1, 4,
16, 64. These equal the dimensions of the corresponding full complex matrix
algebras. Rank for N = 4 is explicitly omitted, and no all-mode span or
real-Clifford identification is inferred from these finite ranks.

The checker rejects wrong parity sign, missing Majorana phase, and missing
Jordan-Wigner string mutations. It refuses five invalid mode bounds using
runtime checks, including under Python optimization. Normal and optimized
outputs are recorded in the independent replay receipt.

## Superselection countermodel

`oneCAR` constructs the one-mode CAR matrices in Lean. `one_parity` computes
P = diag(1,−1). Let rho have every entry equal to 1/2. Lean checks its star,
trace, idempotence, rational Gram factorization, and positive semidefiniteness.
It is a legitimate density matrix, yet

    P rho − rho P = [[0,1],[−1,0]].

The noncommutation is the conclusion of `parity_superselection_not_forced`;
the full displayed commutator is independently computed in SymPy. This
countermodel concerns unrestricted CAR states. It does not refute a physical
theory that separately restricts observables or states by parity.
