# CT-INFORMATION-MEDIA-INTEROPERABILITY

For arbitrary types, cloners on admitted blank slices compose to a cloner on their Cartesian product. Product-local reversible maps alone do not implement all permutations of two bits: controlled flip is an involution but cannot be written (f(a),g(b)). Product cloning therefore does not supply the full permutation-task clause of interoperability.

## Derivation

Apply the first cloner to the first coordinates and the second cloner to the second coordinates, then regroup. On the product blank, each component is duplicated. However, a map (a,b)↦(f(a),g(b)) has a first output independent of b. Controlled flip sends (false,false) and (false,true) to outputs with different first bits. It cannot be such a product map, even though applying it twice is the identity. Local cloning and local permutations alone are consequently insufficient to obtain every composite permutation.
