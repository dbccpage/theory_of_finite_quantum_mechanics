# FQ-GAP-CONTINUUM-HILBERT-LIFT

Node status stays CONDITIONAL. The standing text is an integer slice of the narrowest arithmetic claim. Scalar-field selection and the completion stay outside that slice.

## The equation 2 * a = 1

Let a be an integer. The product 2 * a is even. The integer 1 is odd. An even integer and an odd integer are distinct, so 2 * a is not 1.

## The discriminant identity

Let t be an integer. Expand (2 * t - 1) * (2 * t - 1). First distribute on the left factor, then on each remaining product. The square (2 * t) * (2 * t) is 4 * (t * t), and the cross terms are 2 * t and 2 * t. The expansion is 4 * (t * t) - 4 * t + 1.

The same value is obtained from the left side. The product 4 * (t * t - t - 1) expands to 4 * (t * t) - 4 * t - 4. Adding 5 cancels the constant -4 against +5 and leaves 4 * (t * t) - 4 * t + 1.

## Integer squares

The square of 0 is 0, the square of 1 is 1, and the square of 2 is 4. None of these is 5. If an integer has absolute value at least 3, its square is at least 9, and 9 is greater than 5. A negative integer has the same square as its absolute value. So no integer square equals 5.

## No integer root

Suppose t * t - t - 1 = 0. Substitute that value into the identity. The left side becomes 4 * 0 + 5, so (2 * t - 1) * (2 * t - 1) = 5. No integer square equals 5, and 2 * t - 1 is an integer. The supposition is false. Therefore t * t - t - 1 is not 0.

## What is proved

Four arithmetic statements: no integer a satisfies 2 * a = 1; for every integer t, 4 * (t * t - t - 1) + 5 = (2 * t - 1) * (2 * t - 1); no integer square equals 5; and t * t - t - 1 is not 0.
