## 1. Narrowed Mathematical Statement and Scope Fences

`TFQ-0118` separates the checked finite evidence in Package `007` (injective cyclic inclusion and point-separating character sums inside a declared budget) from the five open continuum obligations (`cdx2.haar_continuous_density`, metric completion, semantic normal forms, general chain maps, and `cdx2.interacting_qft`):

1. **Injective Dyadic Cyclic Inclusion (`dyadicInclude : Fin N → Fin (2 * N)`)**:
   - Sends $x \mapsto 2x$, satisfying strict injectivity (`dyadicInclude_injective`), modular-addition homomorphism $2((a+b)\bmod N) = (2a+2b)\bmod (2N)$ (`dyadicInclude_add_mod_hom`), and non-surjectivity onto `Fin (2 * N)` since odd residue $1$ is missed at every finite level (`dyadicInclude_misses_odd_residue`).

2. **Point-Separating Character Sums Inside a Declared Finite Budget ($\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z}$)**:
   - Over Gaussian rationals $\mathbb{Q}[i]$, the dual character sum $\sum_{k=0}^3 \chi_k(x)\overline{\chi_k(y)}$ equals $(4,0)$ when $x=y$ and $(0,0)$ when $x \neq y$ (`z4_character_point_projector_exact`), so every pair of distinct points $x \neq y$ in `Fin 4` is separated by at least one character (`z4_characters_separate_distinct_points`).

3. **Five Open Continuum Obligations Fences (`cdx2.haar_continuous_density` & `cdx2.interacting_qft`)**:
   - **Discretization Defect Fence (`discretization_defect_closed_form`, `discretization_defect_strictly_positive_for_all_finite_N`, `discretization_defect_dyadic_budget_table`)**: On an $N$-point uniform grid ($N \ge 1$), the left Riemann sum of $f(t)=t^2$ is $\frac{(N-1)(2N-1)}{6N^2}$, leaving a strictly positive rational discretization defect $\frac{1}{3} - S_N(t^2) = \frac{3N-1}{6N^2} > 0$ at every finite budget $N \ge 1$.
   - **Interacting Quartic Non-Superposition Fence (`quartic_superposition_defect_identity`, `quartic_superposition_at_unit_iff_free`)**: For the scalar equation-of-motion operator $E_g(\phi) = m^2\phi + g\phi^3$, the superposition residual $E_g(a+b) - (E_g(a)+E_g(b)) = 3gab(a+b)$ vanishes at $a=b=1$ if and only if $g=0$, showing that free linear character identities do not discharge an interacting QFT (`cdx2.interacting_qft`).
