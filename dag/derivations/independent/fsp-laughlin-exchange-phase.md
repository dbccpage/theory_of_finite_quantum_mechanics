# FSP-LAUGHLIN-EXCHANGE-PHASE

In the formal angle-residue model modulo 2m, for every m>1 the distinct residues 1 and m+1 have the same doubled residue 2. Thus full-braid monodromy alone does not select an exchange residue. At m=3 the two residues are 1 and 4. This is an algebraic ambiguity, not a Berry-phase derivation.

## Derivation

Represent an exchange angle by k*pi/m modulo 2*pi. Doubling takes k to 2k modulo 2m. The residues 1 and m+1 are distinct and in range when m>1, while their doubles differ by 2m. Their angles differ by pi. No algebraic information in this doubled residue distinguishes them; a physical exchange law requires additional input.

## What is open

The statistical exchange phase of Laughlin quasiholes requires the wavefunction, adiabatic transport and Berry connection, plus a branch/continuity convention. The algebraic non-uniqueness does not refute the Arovas-Schrieffer-Wilczek result.
