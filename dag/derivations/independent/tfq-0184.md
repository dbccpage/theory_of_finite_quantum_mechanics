## Cubic reflexivity

For every natural number n, the product n * n * n is at most n * n * n. That comparison is reflexivity of the order on Nat.

## Elimination split

Let n be a natural number with n at least 1. Write the elimination product (n - 1) * n * (2 * n - 1) and the gap n * (3 * n - 1). Their sum equals twice the cube:

2 * (n * n * n) = (n - 1) * n * (2 * n - 1) + n * (3 * n - 1).

The gap is at least 2, so the elimination product is strictly below twice the cube.

Set n = k + 1 with k a natural number. Then n - 1 = k, 2 * n - 1 = 2 * k + 1, and 3 * n - 1 = 3 * k + 2. Expand both sides as polynomials in k. Each side equals 2 * (k * k * k) + 6 * (k * k) + 6 * k + 2.

## The count at n = 4

The cube 4 * 4 * 4 equals 64. The sum 1 * 1 + 2 * 2 + 3 * 3 equals 14. The quotient (3 * 4 * (2 * 4 - 1)) / 6 equals 14. And 14 is strictly below 64.
