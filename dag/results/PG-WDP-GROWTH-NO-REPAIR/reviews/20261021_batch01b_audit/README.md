# PG-WDP-GROWTH-NO-REPAIR review

The source claims are PROVED_LEAN, but wdp_growth_subcomplex_monotone is the monotonicity of a List Nat sum under appending, and wdp_two_simplex_attachment_preserves_phi1 is rfl because attachTwoSimplex updates only the triangle list; neither involves the minimum over potentials that defines Phi1. The Rust witness uses hard-coded constants. The real content is proved here: restriction monotonicity of the attained weighted-L1 minimum under hypotheses the source does not state (d0 compatibility, h restricted, weights not raised, minima attained), with kernel counterexamples when a hypothesis is dropped, the definitional invariance of Phi1 under attachment, the additive rise of the representative norm and gamma2_repair (equal to the source Gamma2 at the minimal residual when d1 d0 = 0). The necessity wording of the source (a decrease requires deleted cells, changed weights or changed values) is the contrapositive of phi_growth_not_lower for the modelled structure. Source external hashes match the pins (Obstruction.lean, derivation_dag_witnesses.rs, DerivationDagQMClosure.lean); the paper pin tfq_0346 is a CRLF-era hash.

Supported: Kernel-checked over finite index types, for rational windows whose attained minima Phi1 exist (existence of minimisers is a hypothesis, IsPhi, not proved): if the sub-window d0 is the restriction of the big d0 (hcompat), its cochain is the restriction and its weights are at most the restrictions of the big weights, then Phi1(sub) <= Phi1(big) (phi_restrict_le); hence growth with an extending cochain and weights not lowered never lowers Phi1 (phi_growth_not_lower, which is the contrapositive of the source necessity wording: a decrease needs deleted cells, lowered weights, an incompatible d0 or changed cochain values); Phi1 <= the unrepaired residual sum; attaching a 2-cell leaves (D0, w, h) and hence Phi1 untouched (definitional) and adds |row . h| to the representative norm ||d1 h||_1; this is the source Gamma2 (the norm at the minimal residual) only when d1 d0 = 0, which gamma2_repair proves and which every valid row preserves (closed_attach, triangle_row_closed). Boundaries as kernel instances: incompatible d0, weights raised 2 -> 3 (Phi1 4 -> 6), and norm arithmetic only for J(a,b) = |a+b| + |a-b|/4 with J(1,-1) = 1/2 < J(1,0) = 5/4 (for d0 = 0 the defect of h is J(h), so deleting a coordinate raises it; this consequence is not stated as a theorem about Phi1). Native table of 82 pairs (9 named restrictions plus all 63 edge subsets of K4 for one cochain, 6 attachments incl. an invalid row, 4 edits) with a verified primal potential and dual vector for both windows of every pair and Phi1 recomputed by the dual LP in the checker; the strict decreases from deleting cells (3 -> 0), lowering weights (6 -> 2) and changing the cochain (1 -> 0) are native rows, not kernel instances.

Remaining: Monotonicity for arbitrary norms J or renormalised weights (the instances show it fails), or when the sub d0 is not the restriction of d0; existence of minimisers for weighted L1 as a Lean theorem; that each of the three failing hypotheses is by itself sufficient to lower Phi1 in general (kernel instances exist for raised weights and a changed d0; the native rows cover deletion, a changed cochain and lowered weights); equal-weight subdivision and filtration limit pages (clauses of WDP-CH04-P09 absent from the source Lean); the source Gamma2 at a minimal residual of a non-closed window (non-unique, withheld); complex coefficients; any physical reading.

Next test: Prove the equal-weight subdivision clause of WDP-CH04-P09 (subdividing an edge through a new degree-two vertex with equal weights leaves Phi1 unchanged) as a Lean theorem over IsPhi and check it by exact enumeration; separately discharge the existence of minimisers (IsPhi attainment) for weighted L1 so that phi_restrict_le has no attained-minimum hypothesis.
