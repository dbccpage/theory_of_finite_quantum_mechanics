# Written review: channel-class CC-21 through CC-25

AuthorityEffect: None. Original user attachment is unchanged. The channel IDs here are a separate namespace from the Born/tomography requests numbered 21-30. No completion of those unrelated requests is implied.

## Main results accepted on written mathematical review

- CC-21: rational-to-complex extension in finite dimensions, rational Hermitian PSD density, transpose/swap negative witness, the Choi block extraction, and d-admissibility implying PSD Choi are correct. Approximation must explicitly choose Hermitian X. The maximally entangled witness Omega Omega*/d is a rational normalized state. The unnormalized swap test gives -2; the normalized test gives -1 on the same unnormalized antisymmetric vector.
- CC-22: Gram-to-Kraus reshaping with input-first vec(K)=sum_j e_j tensor K e_j is correct. PSD Choi iff rational Kraus iff complete positivity, the 4 rank(C) bound, trace-preservation equivalence and Heisenberg duality are correct. Rank(C) is the minimal complex Kraus number; rational presentations can need more operators. No eigendecomposition is intrinsically required for every minimal factorization. With this vec convention, Tr_output C=(sum_a K_a* K_a)^T, so the stated equivalence to I is correct.
- CC-23: rational isometry dilation, partial trace and Heisenberg compression formulas are correct, as is the projective-measurement instrument Q_i W. Its rows/codomain retain the stated outcome dimensions. Unitary completion is unnecessary for these proofs. The ancillary comment that producing a rational completion necessarily means solving norm equations is overstated; an explicit rank-one update works, as sketched in corrected_notes.md.
- CC-24: the stochastic kernel is the Choi diagonal, identity versus dephasing share a kernel, the rational Choi mixture supplies a nontrivial family, and every rational stochastic kernel has a rational Kraus lift. Correct rectangular shapes, convexity scope and composition claims as below.
- CC-25: sequential/tensor closure, partial trace, state preparation, isometric embedding and state/effect duality hold for the assumed normalized Kraus maps. These are conditional algebraic closure results. Presentation categories must be distinguished from equality of CP maps.

## Required corrections

1. CP is positivity preservation under ancillas, not normalized-state preservation. The zero map and 2 id are CP but not TP and do not send density matrices to density matrices. To derive deterministic CPTP channels, add output normalization for every normalized input, or trace preservation. CP and TP are separately necessary. State-preparation assumptions and local K-linearity/tensor action are also imported physical assumptions.
2. The full composite PSD cone is sufficient and stronger than needed for the Choi test. For fixed input d, positivity on the one allowed maximally entangled rational state at ancilla dimension d already forces C>=0; CC-22 then forces all ancilla positivity. Merely permitting some entangled states is not enough. The swap example separates positivity from complete positivity; it does not prove that every part of the full-cone assumption is logically indispensable.
3. In CC-24, a stochastic kernel with e output rows and d input columns has Kraus lift c E_{j'j} in K^{e by d}, not generally K^{d by d}.
4. Off-diagonal Choi entries include both off-diagonals of Phi(E_jj) (creation of output coherence from classical inputs) and blocks Phi(E_jk), j!=k (input-coherence response). Trace preservation also imposes linear off-diagonal block trace constraints; PSD alone is not the remaining constraint.
5. Kernel multiplication is not restricted exactly to dephasing-covariant maps. Its necessary and sufficient condition is Delta_g Phi (Id-Delta_e) Psi Delta_d=0 on diagonal inputs, with spaces stated. No coherence detection by Phi or no coherence creation by Psi is sufficient. Both maps may fail dephasing covariance and still have multiplicative kernels. Explicit exact controls are saved separately.
6. d=1 with e>1 has an e-component probability column giving diag(rho); it is not a trivial kernel. Only d=e=1 has the unique scalar trace-preserving channel/kernel. A square permutation kernel example must explicitly have d=e.
7. Rational-channel fibers are closed under rational convex combinations. Their ambient full-complex Hermitian spectrahedra are real-convex; restricting entries to Q(i) generally destroys closure under irrational weights.
8. Normalized Kraus families modulo branch-index permutations form a category of presentations, with canonical index identifications. They are not the operational channel category: {I} and {3I/5,4I/5} define the same identity channel but different such presentations. For channels, quotient by equality of linear maps, equivalently equality of Choi matrices. The morphism sets of CPTP maps are not K-vector spaces; 'defined using K entries' does not imply K-linear enrichment.
9. r=1 is a one-dimensional trivial ancilla (zero ancilla qubits), not a zero-dimensional vector space. r_1 r_2 is the composed presentation's Kraus count, not a universal formula for a minimal count.
