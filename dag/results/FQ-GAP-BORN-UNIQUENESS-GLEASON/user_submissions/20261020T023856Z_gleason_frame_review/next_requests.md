# Five small proof requests GF-1 through GF-5

Separate from pending BR and RS requests. State fields, domains, zero cases and imports. Regularity remains an external theorem here.

GF-1. Prove the elementary dictionary: A1-A2 give a weight-one frame function, A3 supplies nonnegativity, the shared-complement argument gives ray invariance, and the resulting frame functions correspond to nonnegative normalized finitely additive projection measures. Include zero and identity projectors.

GF-2. Given an imported regularity theorem, prove PSD, trace one and uniqueness of rho for a GIVEN assignment over R and C. Exhibit two different admissible assignments on the same H. Explain why real rays need not identify a complex Hermitian completion.

GF-3. Verify the rational bases v=(20/29,21/29,0), u=(21/29,-20/29,0), z=(0,0,1), B'=(v,u,z), B=(v,(3u+4z)/5,(-4u+3z)/5). Show the largest-overlap rule changes the shared v's value and normalized absolute amplitudes give 20/41 versus 100/247. Give the separate 3-4-5 rotated quartic total 337/625.

GF-4. Treat POVMs as finite labeled families including repeats and zeros. Derive partial additivity, monotonicity and rational homogeneity. For R/C prove real homogeneity by rational squeezing and well-defined cone-scaling/difference extensions. Separate real trace duality from optional complex-linear extension.

GF-5. Prove that glued sections s_B together with a fixed local evaluation h_v, g(v,B)=h_v(s_B restricted to v), imply A2. Give contextual evaluations on identical glued sections when the rest of B may affect g. Identify the remaining D1/D2 premises instead of inferring them from a finite list of tests.
