# Five small next proof targets

HL-R1. Prove the norm/form compatibility distinction: the complex parallelogram law defines a unique polarized form, but it equals a previously supplied h only when the quadratic norms agree. Check the antilinear-first sign and the scaled-norm counterexample.

HL-R2. Prove the forward-versus-adjoint graph-growth distinction for general subcomplex inclusions with diagonal weights. State exactly when zero extension commutes with incidence and divergence. Derive the global-energy Galerkin operator including boundary edges.

HL-R3. Prove the atomless refinement criterion: the Boolean colimit is atomless iff every stage cell eventually has at least two descendants. Under nonemptiness and countability deduce Cantor space. Exhibit the tail-only subdivision counterexample.

HL-R4. Prove projected strong resolvent convergence using the form-inner-product orthogonal approximation argument in part (f). Track the complementary projection explicitly; do not assume the stage subspaces lie in the operator domain.

HL-R5. Prove the mixed-pair spectral-pollution example for all n, then show why additional adjoint compatibility forbids that example. Separate strong convergence on a fixed vector from norm behavior on a moving stage vector.

These are proof/formalization targets. They do not select the physical Laplacian, geometry or continuous-time dynamics.
