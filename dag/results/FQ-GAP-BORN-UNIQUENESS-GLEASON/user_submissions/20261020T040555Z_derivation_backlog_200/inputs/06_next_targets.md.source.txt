# Five small hand-proof or formalization targets

MUB-41. Prove the sharper entrywise unitarity bridge |E(U)-E(U')|<=2||U-U'||op for matrices with unit columns. Include U nonunitary and the max-entry norm convention.

MUB-42. Prove the corrected strict-threshold equivalence in MUB-39. State separately why excluding a closed outer candidate set is sufficient and can be stronger.

MUB-43. Prove the finite open-cover argument in MUB-38, including the empty-complement case, rational-turn boxes, seams, and a positive bridge remainder. No value of delta* may be assumed known beyond the conditional hypothesis delta*>0.

MUB-44. Prove the coupled transpose/rebase transformation on an exact MUB tuple, tracking every basis including the standard one. State why it does not justify naive simultaneous transpose of arbitrary centers or boxes.

MUB-45. Specify and prove a sound enclosure-search invariant: every feasible twelve-vector assignment survives all necessary-condition pruning unless a rigorous contradiction is certified. Allow multiple vectors in one box. Keep full coverage separate from local rejection soundness.

These support the open exclusion target. Solving them does not by itself produce a global atlas or solve mu(6)=3.
