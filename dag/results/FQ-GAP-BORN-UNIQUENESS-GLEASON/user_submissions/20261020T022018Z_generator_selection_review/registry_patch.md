# Candidate generator-selection cell

Status: REVIEWED CANDIDATE, NOT AN APPLIED FROZEN-REGISTRY EDIT.

> Declare the complex Hilbert space, real time parameter, and time-homogeneous unitary group law. On a separable Hilbert space, weak measurability of all matrix coefficients suffices for strong continuity; Stone then uniquely determines the self-adjoint generator of that specified group. A sampled U(tau) alone does not select the group: logarithm branches and spectral multiplicities remain ambiguous. A fixed finite-dimensional energy branch is a selection rule. An infinite compatible refinement family determines at most one continuous extension, but existence additionally requires continuity of the induced dense-time representation. Locality, symmetry and energy restrictions need separate existence/uniqueness proofs. Preserve the coefficient-category and finite/infinite-dimensional assumptions explicitly.

Use Stone for the self-adjoint unitary generator. Record a Hille-Yosida/Markov semigroup import separately if that is the chosen dynamical class. Do not promote a refinement, locality or minimality slogan into an established unique selection.
