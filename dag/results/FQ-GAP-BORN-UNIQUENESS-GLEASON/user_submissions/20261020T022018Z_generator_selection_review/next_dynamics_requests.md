# Five small dynamics requests DT-1 through DT-5

Requested proof units, not kernel certificates. Some have written sketches in countermodels.md and need a complete formal statement. Preserve Hilbert/real-time/field assumptions and list operations, identities, zero cases and admitted compositions.

DT-1. Finite-dimensional logarithms. For tau>0 and a complex unitary U, classify all self-adjoint H with exp(-i tau H)=U using refinements of each U-eigenspace and integer energy branches. Prove uniqueness in a fixed half-open band of width 2pi/tau. Give the uncountable projection family for U=I_2 and explain why simple spectrum differs from degenerate spectrum.

DT-2. Coherent roots need not be continuous. Prove the a_n,z_n dyadic construction in countermodels.md is compatible and defines a homomorphism on tau Z[1/2], but fails strong continuity at zero and admits no continuous real-time extension. Use the explicit even-subsequence bound; do not rely on numerical phase plots.

DT-3. Correct dyadic extension theorem. Prove a strongly continuous-at-zero homomorphism on tau Z[1/2] extends uniquely to a strongly continuous unitary group on R. Prove the limiting operators are surjective, using inverse limits along -q_n rather than assuming every strong limit of unitaries is unitary. Then explain Stone's conclusion and show finite-depth grids retain aliases.

DT-4. Two sample times. Prove two exact incommensurate times determine at most one strongly continuous group, conditional on existence. Give a scalar pair of commuting samples with no such interpolation. State precisely why exact timing/operator data and the continuity hypothesis are imports rather than finite experimental conclusions.

DT-5. Markov versus unitary embedding. Prove the 2x2 flip is not a finite-time exponential of a real rate matrix, but is a complex unitary exponential. Prove the explicit Q_+,Q_- cyclic 3x3 example in countermodels.md has the same stochastic exponential. Keep the real analytic coefficients and column convention explicit; distinguish rate positivity from a branch choice alone.
