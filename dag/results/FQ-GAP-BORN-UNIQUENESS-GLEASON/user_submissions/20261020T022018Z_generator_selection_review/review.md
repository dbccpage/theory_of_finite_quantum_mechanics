## Accepted central distinction

Given an exact real-parameter unitary group on a separable complex Hilbert space, weak measurability of every matrix coefficient implies strong continuity. Stone then gives a unique densely defined self-adjoint H with U(t)=exp(-itH). The Hilbert space, real time, group law and measurability/topology are inputs; they are not finite witnesses. The sign convention fixes the corresponding skew generator to -iH. Kuznetsova's paper explicitly records the separable automatic-continuity statement and distinguishes stronger operator measurability outside the separable case.

A specified complete complex-space unitary U at tau>0 has a bounded self-adjoint logarithm by bounded Borel functional calculus, but the sampled operator does not uniquely select a real-parameter group. H -> H+(2pi k/tau)I already gives infinitely many self-adjoint generators with the same sampled U. The supplied Z example is correct. The stochastic comparison is also correct: not every Markov matrix has a rate-matrix logarithm, and embeddings can be nonunique.

## Corrections and precise scope

1. Branch shifts are not an additive 'kernel of exp' on all self-adjoint matrices: matrix exp is not a homomorphism for noncommuting sums. The displayed H+(2pi/tau)N(H) family is valid but does not classify all logarithms with degeneracy. In finite dimension, choose eigenvalues exp(-i theta_lambda) of U with fixed representatives theta_lambda. Every self-adjoint log refines the U-eigenspaces into mutually orthogonal subprojections P_lambda,k and has H=sum_(lambda,k) (theta_lambda+2pi k) P_lambda,k/tau. Simple U spectrum gives integer choices; degenerate eigenspaces also permit continuous subspace choices.
2. Consequently group interpolations need not be countable. For U=I_2 every rank-one projection P yields H_P=2pi P/tau; distinct P give distinct strongly continuous groups. For example P_x=(1+x^2)^(-1)[[1,x],[x,x^2]], x real, is an uncountable family. A fixed countable eigenvalue-branch list is not the whole degeneracy freedom.
3. Compatible dyadic roots alone do not imply a continuous extension and do not produce a generator merely as an 'inverse limit'. The exact countermodel below defines a homomorphism on tau Z[1/2] that is discontinuous at zero. An induced dyadic representation that IS strongly continuous at zero has a unique strongly continuous extension to R; then Stone uniquely determines H. Both the entire infinite family and the continuity condition are extra data.
4. No finite dyadic depth removes all aliases: H+(2pi 2^N/tau)I agrees at every time k tau/2^N. Refinement is not the sole mathematical sampling option either: two exact incommensurate sample times determine at most one strongly continuous group, conditional on existence, because their generated subgroup is dense. Such timing assumptions are themselves declared real-time data, not automatically finite experimental certification.
5. Locality, symmetry and lower bounds are restrictions, not general uniqueness theorems. H=0 and H=(2pi/tau)P on one site are both local, nonnegative and give the identity sample. Periodic identity shifts preserve every symmetry. A 'minimal energy' rule must specify the admissible set, reference and objective; different choices need separate uniqueness proofs. All finite self-adjoint matrices are bounded below, so that condition alone is vacuous there.
6. A fixed half-open energy interval of width 2pi/tau gives a unique logarithm in FINITE dimension. Infinite-dimensional principal Borel functional calculus requires an endpoint qualification: spectrum is closed, and a principal log with values in a half-open interval can have both endpoints in its spectral closure. Do not assert every unitary has spectrum of its log contained in that literal half-open interval; use a stated Borel branch or a spectral gap/endpoint spectral-projection condition.
7. Norm continuity means bounded generator. This restricts an infinite-dimensional class allowing unbounded energies. In finite dimension all the relevant operator topologies agree and continuous unitary groups are norm-continuous automatically. State which dimensional regime the row concerns.
8. Strong continuity of a full unitary group is the hypothesis for Stone, not a choice of interpolation. Hille-Yosida is a semigroup generator theorem and does not by itself make a generator self-adjoint; it should not replace Stone in the unitary claim. The group law expresses time-homogeneous composition/invertibility; equating it unqualifiedly with physical 'memorylessness' needs an additional model definition.
9. Unitary embeddability here uses the complete complex operator category. It need not preserve the rational coefficient category: at tau=1 the scalar sample U=-1 has no rational scalar H, since h=(2k+1)pi. If only the CHANNEL Ad_U is sampled, global phase is absent and H+cI is indistinguishable for every real c, before imposing any energy-reference convention.
10. The generator-selection issue is one open bridge, not demonstrated here to be the only underdetermined rung. Prior phase/carrier, composite and measurement imports remain explicitly open in the other bundles.
