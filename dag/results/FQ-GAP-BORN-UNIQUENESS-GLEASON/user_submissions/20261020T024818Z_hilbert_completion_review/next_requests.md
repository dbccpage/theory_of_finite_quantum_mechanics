# Small HL proof requests

HL-1. Correct place lemma. Distinguish embeddings from places modulo conjugation and from a choice of i. For t=zeta_7+zeta_7^-1 prove A=2+t, B=t^2, A-B=(2-t)(t+1). Prove that embeddings k=1,2 preserve A>B and k=3 reverses it. Explain why finite algebraic comparisons see only a finite conductor. Keep exact probabilities distinct from unnormalized squared magnitudes.

HL-2. Refinement isometry. For function pullback prove parent weight=sum child weights iff weighted l2 isometry. Translate to cell mass a=w f, derive its different refinement map, and prove the inverse-weight norm identity. Give a two-cell l1 parallelogram failure and explain why completion preserves it.

HL-3. Stone realization. For a countable refining sequence of finite partitions with compatible finite weights, construct its countable Boolean algebra and inverse-limit Stone space. Prove the clopen charge is a premeasure, extends uniquely to Borel, and finite-stage functions are dense in L2 modulo null sets. State countability and total-mass hypotheses explicitly.

HL-4. Closability admission. Prove Tx=sum n x_n on finite sequences is not closable using e_n/n, and show why its formal T*T is not defined on the entire colimit. Then prove closability of the actual graph differential under explicitly stated local finiteness or weighted-degree hypotheses. Do not transfer a generic finite-matrix compatibility check into this conclusion.

HL-5. Galerkin convergence. Starting from a closed nonnegative form q and nested finite spaces with form-core union, prove generalized resolvent convergence by form-norm orthogonal projection. Explain the zero extension on complements, invoke the self-adjoint group-convergence theorem precisely, and distinguish strong compact-time convergence from operator-norm convergence. Cover form-domain vectors outside the operator domain.
