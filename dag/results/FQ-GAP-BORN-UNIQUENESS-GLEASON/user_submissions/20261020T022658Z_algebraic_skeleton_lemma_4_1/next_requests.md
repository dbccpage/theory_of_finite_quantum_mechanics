# Small follow-up proof units AS-4.1

1. Coherent roots. Prove that the order-n phase subgroup equals all roots of X^n-1, and explicitly justify a compatible family of primitive generators before identifying the embedded groups with the standard Z/n direct system. State the result as noncanonical isomorphism.
2. Fixed weight witnesses. From g^2=epsilon ell and g*=epsilon g for odd-prime quadratic Gauss sums, construct s*=s, s^2=ell. Include ell=2, rational denominators and zero. Explain why D3 is redundant.
3. Minimality versus ceiling. Prove X^3=2 has no root in Q^ab, while K0, Qbar and C each satisfy D1-D3. Prove the exact phase (3+4i)/5 is nontorsion using rational algebraic integers. Record the two failed transfer targets K0 and mu_infinity.
4. Semialgebraic transfer. Write the coordinate translation z=x+iy for polynomial/conjugation equations, inequations and real weight inequalities with algebraic parameters. Invoke a precisely cited RCF quantifier-elimination theorem; prove existential transfer from R to R_alg. Do not enlarge the witness language silently.
5. Completion bridge. Given an embedding of the cyclotomic field in C and its inherited standard metric, prove its completion is C. Prove the analogous statement for the torsion circle subgroup. List the metric and completeness as imports rather than consequences of D1-D3.
