**Lemma 4.1 (Algebraic skeleton of finite interference data).**

*Declared data.*

- (D1) A coefficient field K with a ring involution a ↦ a*. A phase is an element u with u u* = 1; the phases form a group Φ(K) ⊂ K^×.
- (D2) Phase witnesses: for every n ≥ 1 an n-fold cyclic interference is realizable, i.e. Φ(K) contains a subgroup of order n. Refinement: for m | n, an m-fold splitting is a coarsening of an n-fold splitting.
- (D3) Weight witnesses: for every rational p ∈ [0,1] some a ∈ K has a a* = p.
- No topology, limits, or infinite configurations are declared.

*Statement.* Under (D1)–(D3):

(a) char K = 0.

(b) The finite-order phases form Φ_fin(K) = Tor(K^×) ≅ colim_{m|n} ℤ/nℤ ≅ ℚ/ℤ. The subfield they generate, K₀ := ℚ(Φ_fin) ≅ ℚ(μ∞), is the union of all cyclotomic fields; * restricts to ζ ↦ ζ⁻¹. The pair (K₀, *) is unique up to isomorphism.

(c) K₀ already satisfies (D3): √p ∈ K₀ for every rational p ≥ 0. By Kronecker–Weber, K₀ = ℚ^ab.

(d) Under any embedding K₀ ↪ ℂ, Φ_fin ↦ μ∞, a dense proper subgroup of U(1), and K₀ ↦ a dense proper subfield of ℂ that is countable (hence not complete) and not algebraically closed.

(e) (Ceiling.) Let W be any finite witness — a finite system of polynomial equations, inequations, and weight comparisons with coefficients in ℚ̄ in finitely many amplitude variables. W is realizable over ℂ iff it is realizable over ℚ̄. No finite witness distinguishes ℂ from ℚ̄, or U(1) from the countable group U(1)(ℚ̄) = {u ∈ ℚ̄ : u u* = 1}.

*Proof sketch.*

(a) In characteristic p, xᵖ − 1 = (x − 1)ᵖ, so Φ(K) has no element of order p, contradicting (D2) at n = p.

(b) *Colimit.* Every finite subgroup of the multiplicative group of a field is cyclic (xⁿ − 1 has at most n roots), so the order-n witness of (D2) is μ_n(K) = ⟨ζ_n⟩ and is unique. For m | n, μ_m ⊂ μ_n, and the refinement map is the inclusion ζ_m ↦ ζ_n^{n/m}; abstractly ℤ/m → ℤ/n, k ↦ (n/m)k. The index set (ℕ≥1, |) is directed under lcm, so the colimit of this system of injections is the union ⋃ₙ μ_n(K) = Tor(K^×). Define ℤ/n → ℚ/ℤ by k ↦ k/n mod ℤ. The transition maps commute ((n/m)k / n = k/m), each stage map is injective (k/m ∈ ℤ ⟹ m | k), and every k/n ∈ ℚ/ℤ is hit; hence colim ≅ ℚ/ℤ.\
&#x20;*Field.* ℚ ⊂ K by (a); K₀ = ℚ(⋃ μ_n) is the splitting field over ℚ of {xⁿ − 1 : n ≥ 1}, unique up to isomorphism.\
&#x20;*Involution.* u u* = 1 forces u* = u⁻¹ on every phase; K₀ is generated by phases, so * is determined on K₀ and is the automorphism inverting every root of unity (the element −1 ∈ Gal(ℚ(μ∞)/ℚ) ≅ Ẑ^×).

(c) i = ζ₄; √2 = ζ₈ + ζ₈⁻¹; for an odd prime p the Gauss sum g_p = Σ_{k=1}^{p−1} (k/p) ζ_pᵏ satisfies g_p² = (−1)^{(p−1)/2} p, so √p ∈ ℚ(μ_{4p}). Multiplicativity gives √d ∈ K₀ for all d ∈ ℤ, and √(a/b) = √(ab)/b. Kronecker–Weber: every abelian extension of ℚ lies in some ℚ(μ_n), so K₀ = ℚ^ab.

(d) μ∞ = {e^{2πik/n}} is dense in U(1) because ℚ/ℤ is dense in ℝ/ℤ; e^{iθ} with θ/π irrational is not torsion, so the inclusion is proper. K₀ ⊇ ℚ(i) is dense in ℂ. K₀ is a countable union of finite extensions, hence countable, hence not complete. If ∛2 ∈ ℚ^ab then ℚ(∛2)/ℚ would be a subextension of an abelian extension and so Galois; it is not normal (one real root).

(e) ℝ_alg ≼ ℝ as real closed fields (Tarski: RCF is model complete). (ℂ, +, ·, *) and (ℝ, +, ·, ≤) are mutually interpretable (x is real iff x = x*; x ≥ 0 iff x = y y* for some real y), and ℚ̄ = ℝ_alg(i); hence (ℚ̄, *) ≼ (ℂ, *). A finite witness is an existential sentence with parameters in ℚ̄, so it holds in ℂ iff it holds in ℚ̄. □

*Remark (named competitors).* ℤ[i] fails (D3) (no division). ℚ(i) fails (D2) at n = 3. Both sit inside the ceiling of (e). Note that (3+4i)/5 ∈ ℚ(i) is a phase of infinite order, so "finite phase group" and "phase group of a finite-data field" are different objects: μ∞ ⊊ U(1)(ℚ̄) ⊊ U(1), all three distinct, the first two countable.

*Residual.* The ceiling of finitely witnessed structure is K₀ = ℚ^ab under (D2)–(D3), or ℚ̄ if arbitrary algebraic constraints are admitted; both countable and dense. The step K₀ → ℂ, Φ_fin → U(1) follows from no admitted witness and must be entered as a declared completion axiom (continuity of reversible transformations, or closure of the state space), after which ℂ against ℝ and ℍ is a separate selection by local tomography.
