# Five small next proof units

RC-A. **Effect representation.** Given an affine functional e:D(C^n)->[0,1], prove existence and uniqueness of Hermitian E with e(rho)=Tr(E rho), and prove 0<=E<=I using pure states. Handle n=1. Separate mathematical representation from physical availability.

RC-B. **Antiunitary exclusion.** Prove that the transpose on M_2 is positive and trace preserving but not CP, using the Bell partial transpose. Extend the conclusion to conjugation by any fixed unitary followed by transpose. Keep global antiunitary state symmetries distinct from local processes.

RC-C. **Object-completeness repair.** Prove that a quantum theory containing all tensor powers of a qubit, and closed under realizing every exposed face as a system, contains a system D(C^n) for every n>=1. Exhibit rank-n projection faces. Without face closure, prove the powers-of-two countertheory.

RC-D. **Composite checkpoint.** Starting with the precisely stated Jordan-model product assumptions, prove trace-form factorization and the two Hanche-Olsen unit-leg identities. State every assumption used; dimension multiplication is insufficient. If importing Barnum--Wilce, pin the exact lemma and definition instead of replacing this task by a named axiom presented as a proof.

RC-E. **Process completeness.** Given coherent standard complex composites, universal unitary control, pure ancillary preparation and discarding, prove every CPTP map between available systems has an allowed dilation whenever the needed ancillary systems exist. Treat necessity and availability separately. This does not determine a unique instrument for a POVM.

Each result advances one explicit obligation. None alone proves that the finite packet theory satisfies A0--A4 or reconstructs the entire target.
