# Five small residual proof units RS-36 through RS-40

Separate from pending BR-31 through BR-40. State the scalar domain, zero cases and admitted operations. These are follow-up proof requests, not completion certificates.

RS-36. Common-sign lemma. In a finite real affine subspace A and with w_e>0, prove that any two weighted l1 minimizers r,s have r_e s_e>=0 for every e. Use midpoint feasibility and equality of each nonnegative coordinate triangle gap. Construct sigma_e and prove that |r| is linear on the entire optimal set. Include identically zero coordinates.

RS-37. Coherent point theorem. Using RS-36, prove L(r)_e=w_e|r_e|/Phi is an affine injection on the minimizing polytope. Prove normalization, partition reindexing and coarse-graining plus finest residual-faithfulness correspond to a unique optimal repair. Show every finite repair mixture has the same outputs as its barycenter; give two different ensemble measures with the same family. Separate rational from real coefficients.

RS-38. Certified interval theorem. Prove rational and real optima agree for rational data, M_R is a rational polytope, and both extrema of each sector mass are computed on the common optimal face using s>=+-r and sum ws<=Phi. Give rational primal/dual endpoint certificates for one example, and explain why marginal intervals do not specify the whole joint polytope.

RS-39. Naturality boundary. Prove global equivariance of the vertex mean, positive invariant quadratic minimizer and edge-entropy maximizer. Verify the three distinct answers on omega=(0,0,1,2) with four parallel edges. For omega=(0,0,0,1,2,3), prove the entropy maximizer is the unique root in (4/5,5/6) of 2t^3-6t^2+11t-6 and is irrational. Explain how this differs from inability to replay an algebraic number exactly.

RS-40. Density-completion fiber. For a distribution mu, prove the commuting-sector observations identify exactly the PSD Hermitian matrices with diagonal mu. Show zero diagonal entries force a zero row/column, and prove uniqueness iff mu is a vertex of the probability simplex. Give rational qubit completions with the same diagonal but different coherences, and a rejected Hermitian trace-one completion violating PSD.
