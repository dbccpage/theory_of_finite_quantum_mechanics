# Corrected residual-sector lemmas RS-31 through RS-35

Written statements, not kernel certificates. Work first in real vector spaces with finite rational matrices d0,d1, d1d0=0, rational omega and strictly positive rational weights. Let M_R be the minimizing residual polytope of omega+im_R(d0), and M_Q=M_R intersect Q^E. The rational and real optima agree. Assume Phi>0 wherever masses are normalized.

RS-31. The cochain d1 omega and normalized weighted face masses (if nonzero) are invariant under coboundary repair. M and normalized mass sets depend on the full quotient class, weights and sectors; the normalized mass sets are scale invariant. The scalar pair (Phi,||d1 omega||) generally does not determine the mass point. Phi=0 gives M={0} and no normalized edge masses.

RS-32. M_R is a nonempty rational polytope. Any two members have coordinatewise consistent signs. Thus choose sigma_e in {+1,-1} with |r_e|=sigma_e r_e on M_R. The finest mass map L(r)_e=w_e sigma_e r_e/Phi is an affine rational isomorphism from M_R to its image P_edges. All partition images are rational convex polytopes and both endpoints of each sector mass interval are rational LP optima. These endpoints coincide for all sectors iff that partition's image is a singleton. Whole-component sectors suffice but are not necessary. Rational repairs give the corresponding rational mass points.

RS-33. A normalized, reindexing-compatible family over all partitions satisfying coarse-graining and finest residual-faithfulness is exactly the pushforward family of one mu in P_edges. It is represented by a unique r*=L^-1(mu). Ensembles of repairs represent the same family exactly when their barycenters agree; every ensemble is observationally equivalent to a single barycentric repair. Probability measures are optional, with their scalar/measurability structure declared. Constant coarse masses do not imply a unique repair or a unique measure.

RS-34. Naturality alone does not select an admissible point: the submitted four-parallel-edge example has three globally equivariant rules yielding distinct sector values (1/3,2/3), (1/2,1/2) and (4/9,5/9). All depend only on declared data once the rule is specified; none is required by normalization/coarse-graining/naturality alone. Rational quadratic costs and vertex means supply exact rational examples. Entropy can require real-algebraic extension; irrationality by itself does not preclude exact symbolic replay. Transitive vertex symmetry fixes an invariant point, not an ensemble measure.

RS-35. Sector masses are the probabilities of the declared diagonal density diag(mu) under commuting sector projections. The set of ALL complex density completions is {rho=rho*: rho>=0, diag(rho)=mu}; trace one is automatic. If a rational matrix is required, additionally impose entries in Q(i), including the diagonal. Off-diagonals remain constrained by PSD, and sector probabilities do not choose them. A vertex distribution has a unique completion; other distributions have nonunique completions. This is a classical embedding, not a reconstruction of general quantum coherence.
