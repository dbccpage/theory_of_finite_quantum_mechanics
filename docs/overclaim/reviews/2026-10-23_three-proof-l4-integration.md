## Exact scoped statements and remaining bottlenecks

### DFM-BPHZ-CONTINUUM-FENCE (OPEN)

Statement: On the declared overlapping edge sets L = {0,1,2}, R = {2,3,4} and G = {0,1,2,3,4}, a Zimmermann forest is a subset whose members are pairwise nested or disjoint, and the forests are exactly the six subsets in which L and R do not both occur. For the order-0 evaluations T_L I(x,y) = I(0,y), T_R I(x,y) = I(x,0) and T_G I(x,y) = I(0,0), the signed six-forest sum equals I(x,y) - I(0,y) - I(x,0) + I(0,0). For the declared numerator N(x,y) = 2 + 3x + 5y + 7xy that sum is 7xy, so the quotient by xy equals 7 wherever xy is nonzero. The three-forest sum that omits G equals 7xy - 2, and its quotient is 7 - 2/(xy). At (x,y) = (1/2, 1/3) the two quotients are 7 and -5. Algebraic cancellation for actual Feynman-parametric integrands, and any use of this identity before continuum integration, is not established.

Bottleneck: The six-forest count, the order-0 collapse, and the quotient 7 are recorded only for these three edge sets and this numerator. A forest formula on actual Feynman-parametric integrands, a subtraction operator of positive degree, and a convergence statement before continuum integration remain open. The nested sector kernel at z = 3/5 gives 9/50, which is a different identity.

### DFM-ELECTROWEAK-CONTINUUM-FENCE (OPEN)

Statement: For every z in Q(i) and every doublet Phi in Q(i)^2, the un-normalized dressing U(Phi) = [[conj phi2, phi1], [-conj phi1, phi2]] satisfies U(z Phi) = U(Phi) diag(conj z, z). When the squared modulus of z is 1 and a rational s with s*s equal to the squared norm of Phi is supplied, dividing every entry by that same s preserves the identity and s*s equals the squared norm of z Phi. On Phi = (1, 0), the unit z = 3/5 + (4/5)i with s = 1 has normalized (1, 2) entry 3/5 + (4/5)i. On the same Phi, z = 2 has squared norms 1 and 4. The (1, 2) entry of U(2 Phi)/2 is 1, whereas the (1, 2) entry of (U(Phi)/1) diag(2, 2) is 2; these two sides of the proposed normalized covariance law differ. Continuum phases, the dressed derivative term, and an electroweak reading without a declared rational square root stay unproved.

Bottleneck: The normalized residual law is recorded when z lies in Q(i), the squared modulus of z is 1, and a rational s with s*s equal to the doublet squared norm is supplied. Continuum gauge fields W_mu, derivatives, phases outside Q(i), and the Elitzur or symmetry-breaking reading stay open.

### DFM-CKM-PHYSICS-FENCE (OPEN)

Statement: For every rational n, (n - 1)^2 - n(n - 1)/2 = (n - 1)(n - 2)/2 and (n - 1)(n - 2)/2 + (n - 1) = n(n - 1)/2. At n = 1, 2, 3, 4 the polynomial (n - 1)^2 has values 0, 1, 4, 9 and (n - 1)(n - 2)/2 has values 0, 0, 1, 3; they agree at n = 1 and differ at n = 2, 3, 4. At n = 3 the three values are 4, 3, and 1. The quartet of four ones in Q(i) has imaginary part 0, and the quartet with second factor i has imaginary part 1, so the CP value 1 matches only the second. Derivation of a mixing matrix from Yukawa couplings, a unitarity identification of these counts, and measured CP values stay unproved.

Bottleneck: Yukawa couplings, mass-basis diagonalization, a unitarity identification of the counting polynomials, and measured CKM values stay open. The general nonzero-label table classification uses supplied commutative-group rescalings; its restriction to physical unitary mixing matrices is not established.
