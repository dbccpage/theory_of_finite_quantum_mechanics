The new module re-proves these in a second namespace. What is new: `InSameOrbit` and `diag_const_on_orbit` (l.87, 163; valid for every `S`, no transitivity), `distinguished_labels_break_uniqueness_3` (l.424, `diag(1,1,2)` on three sectors with `S = {swap 0 1}`), and `diag12` with the empty set (l.357).
- Bottleneck: the PR replaced "No finite-theory primitive forces readout invariance under sector relabelling; ... Deriving the law ... is item 1" by "Reduced: ...". That deleted the statement that the law is not derived. Restored, with one added sentence stating what the module does and does not do. The generated prompt/audit record had also picked up status `CONDITIONAL` from the "Reduced" wording while the node is IMPORTED_ASSUMPTION; regenerated to IMPORTED_ASSUMPTION.
- PR body table: "Readout invariance under sector permutation sigma | `Invariant` | Proved | FULL" marks a *definition* as proved. The node statement (invariance holds for the readout) is the hypothesis and is **not proved**; the corrected table below says so. "Transitive generated group forces uniform diagonal" and the selection rows are conditional on symmetry of `G` and sector additivity; the rows now name those hypotheses.
- Lean header (`## Outcome: Reduced`), receipt `proof_boundary`, derivation note ("Reduction Theorem", Outcome Classification) and `dag/pfp/campaign/LOG.md` repeated the "Reduced" label. All corrected.
- Derivation note, "What is proved" item 1: now says the selection theorem is a consequence of the supplied laws and is also in `SectorFormUniqueness.eq_smul_one_generated`; a "What is not proved" block was added.
