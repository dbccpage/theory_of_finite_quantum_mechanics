## Why the promotion does not stand

The module is sound, it compiles with standard axioms only, and the receipt, the sha256 and the PR body's axiom lines are accurate. What it proves is a property of its own definitions:

1. `emptyDistinctions (_ : Inscription) : Distinctions := ∅` (`lean4/CampaignPgwPresentationLift.lean:112`) is the constant empty map, and `q := emptyDistinctions` (line 134). The hypothesis of the node, that O is empty, is therefore built into the definition of the distinction map. `O0` and `O1` (lines 115, 118) are that constant map evaluated at `nil` and `markA`, not the observation families O_0 and O_1 of two stages. `O0_empty`, `O1_empty`, `O1_eq_O0` and `no_new_internal_distinction` are `rfl` or `∅ \ ∅ = ∅`. There is no observation family, no operation "supply <a>", and q is not derived from anything.
2. `ConvEquiv` (line 177) defines the opaque convention as equality of profiles and the exact-inscription convention as equality of inscriptions. `statement_holds_under_opaque_only` (line 242) is then the unfolding of those two definitions on a two-element type with a constant profile. That is a definition that restates the conclusion, in a single closed instance. The node itself lists the opaque convention under `imported_structure` as an assumption, and the PR left that line in place while claiming "Derived", which the campaign defines as proved "with no new imports".
3. P1 is wrong against the statement. The statement says supplying <a> changes P from {<>} to {<a>}. The module has `P1 = {nil, markA}` (line 79) and proves growth `P0 ⊂ P1` (line 97), not `P1 = {<a>}`.
4. Section 8 of the module (`Regime`, `UnarySep`, `Collapsed`, lines 283 to 315) is a separate structure. No declaration connects q to `UnarySep`, so `two_marks_in_unformed_regime_collapsed` does not support any claim of the node beyond illustrating the idea.
5. The "independent" check `checks/independent/pgw-presentation-not-expressive-lift.py` has no repository imports and exits 0, but it re-encodes the same two-inscription model with q hard-coded as the constant empty map. It is the same model in a second language, not a second route to the claim.

The existential and non-implication parts of the statement (x ≠ y with q(x) = q(y); neither direction of injectivity follows) are legitimate "can hold" statements and a witness proves them, but the witness lives in the model where q is constant by definition.
