## Statement versus theorem

The 24 nodes with newly local Lean references retain their exact statement,
status and bottleneck. Each of the 20 distinct archive declarations is a
supplement to a same-named local declaration already cited by that node. Their
actual types were read; theorem names and source comments were not accepted as
proofs of the larger physics claims. The archive module and its 20 cited symbols
replay under Lean 4.35.0. `FiniteQM.lean` also replays. Printed axiom reports contain
only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The source-bound raw replay
and per-node reference inventory are retained in `l4_core_lean_replay.json` and
`l4_reference_review.json` in the importer research directory.

These supplements are **NARROWED relative to the full node statements**, not new
full-node certificates. The weighted-sum lemma assumes additivity/homogeneity;
the gauge lemma assumes norming, compatibility and orbit invariance. The bridge
lemma checks a finite rational-square search and Boolean bookkeeping, not the
unbounded irrationality theorem or bridge completion. The residual-sector lemma
uses two explicit four-coordinate tuples. The QM122 lemma includes a bounded
Gaussian search. The continuum lemma only rewrites a finite algebraic equality
and two Boolean flags. Entropy/transcendence, registry minimality, tomography,
Bell, and physics names do not upgrade the finite scalar/Boolean statements.
The retained current local proofs and existing scope fences remain authoritative
for the larger node statements. No original scientific status is raised.

The other six archived Lean files are historical source references, not newly
installed resolving Lean citations. They were byte-verified but **not rebuilt**:
the shared Mathlib cache is missing and free disk space is below the repository's
cache-work minimum. Their archival contracts do not claim proof validity.
