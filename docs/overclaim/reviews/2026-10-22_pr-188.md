The Lean (`BornGaussianRationalFrameObstruction.lean:70-72`, `145-148`) states:
  - the hypothesis `ParityDetermined` holds only on Gaussian-integer vectors that are not (1+i)-divisible, that is not all of `(a+b)`, `(c+d)`, `(e+g)` even, and the class is `((a+b)%2, (c+d)%2, (e+g)%2)`, not the parity of the coordinates;

  - "does not transfer" is wider than the theorem. A construction that is class-valued in residues modulo 2 of Z[i] (four residues per coordinate), or that is parity-valued on real vectors only and free on non-real ones, is not excluded. The theorem excludes only the (1+i)-parity-class ansatz on all Gaussian-integer vectors. The same wider wording is in the PR body (and in the docstrings of the Lean header).
  - Corrected in the node bottleneck, the receipt `limits`, the Lean docstrings and the PR body.
