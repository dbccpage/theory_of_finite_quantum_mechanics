## Corrections to the PR's own text

- PR body table, rows 1 and 3 ("Attractive spin-balanced pairing identity d_up = d_down ... FULL"): the Lean theorems `attractive_pairing_sq_nonneg` / `attractive_weight_nonneg` (lean4/CampaignFspHubbardProtected.lean) state `0 <= d * d` and `0 <= C * (d * d)` for `0 <= C`. They do not state `d_up = d_down`, and nothing in Lean relates the attractive transfer matrices to the square form. Corrected: FULL for the arithmetic statement, NOT PROVED in Lean for the pairing identity.
- PR body table, row 3 (half-filled "particle-hole pairing d_down = d_up / det P_up ... FULL", `half_filled_bipartite_weight_eq`): this theorem has the pairing relation as its hypothesis `h_pair`. The relation `eta T eta = T^-1` and the multi-slice product are not formalized. Corrected: NOT PROVED in Lean (prose derivation in the note; exact check on 256 fields at one parameter set).
- PR body table, rows 7 and 8 (witnesses "FULL"): `witness_doped_ring_weight_neg` and `witness_triangle_weight_neg` state that two **stored** rational pairs have a negative product. That the pairs are `det(I + P_sigma)` of the ring and triangle matrices is not a Lean statement; it is established by exact recomputation outside Lean. Corrected: FULL for the stored pairs, computationally checked (not Lean) for the identification.
- PR body table, row 9 ("Refutation of unconditional determinant positivity ... FULL", `not_unconditional_positivity`): the statement is `not (forall p : DeterminantPair Q, 0 <= p.weight)` over arbitrary pairs of rationals. Any opposite-sign pair refutes it, so it carries no Hubbard content beyond the witnesses. Corrected: arithmetic only.
- Node bottleneck as pushed ("... negative witnesses 15721 and 1281 refute unconditional determinant positivity"): it did not say what is unproved, and "refute" overstated `not_unconditional_positivity`. Replaced (see below).
- Derivation note Section 3 ("Both negative witnesses are verified in Lean 4") and Section 4 items 1 to 4 ("exact rational evaluation shows ..."; "Refutation ... is refuted in Lean 4"): corrected to the arithmetic statements; added what is prose-only and what is computed.
- Derivation note Section 3 lists the ring bonds as (0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(0,3). The witness values are obtained with the bonds applied in lexicographic order (0,1),(0,3),(1,2),(2,3), then reversed; the listed order gives different determinants (checked). Added to the note and to the check's header.
- The Lean module header ("Proved in Lean 4: Algebraic determinant pairing identities ...") and docstrings claimed more than the declarations state. Comments rewritten; no declaration changed. The receipt was re-sealed (new sha256 69e8f526...).
