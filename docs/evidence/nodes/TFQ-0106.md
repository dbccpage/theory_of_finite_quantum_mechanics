## Statement

> derive_cosmological_constant_residual builds a float mean of hinge terms that mix a deficit angle with a sine and a supplied Immirzi number, then divides by a cell-volume parameter. The function's Track B note says a continuum magnitude near the quoted dark-energy scale still needs an external lattice-volume calibration. What is finite is that floating residual on a default valency list. What is not discharged is the physical cosmological constant, which stays GAP, and the Einstein equation. A few hinges are not a measured Lambda and not quantum gravity. Narrowed in Lean 4 and an independent Python verifier to three exact rational algebraic components and scope fences over Q: (1) exact rational replay of CosmologicalCoupling::effective_scalar_curvature and evaluate_action in code/rust/src/general_relativity/cosmological_constant.rs (rust_cosmological_action_unit_test_replay, cosmological_action_zero_iff_curvature_balance, cosmological_action_scaling_and_invariance); (2) the external cell-volume calibration underdetermination fence showing that H / V_cell scales as s^-4 under 4D length rescaling and can match any nonzero target Lambda_target by choosing V_cell = H / Lambda_target (cell_volume_4d_length_rescaling, any_target_lambda_achievable_by_cell_volume_calibration, cell_volume_calibration_injective, immirzi_and_cell_volume_scale_degeneracy, default_valency_scaffold_calibration_spread); and (3) the 1-scalar vs. 10-component 4D symmetric Einstein tensor fence showing that anisotropic profiles (Lambda + delta, Lambda - delta, Lambda, Lambda) have vanishing scalar mean residual while violating the componentwise Einstein equation with squared residual 2 * delta^2 \> 0 (four_dim_symmetric_tensor_component_counts, anisotropic_curvature_vanishing_scalar_mean_fence, anisotropic_profile_violates_tensorial_einstein, diagonal_einstein_iff_sq_residual_zero).
