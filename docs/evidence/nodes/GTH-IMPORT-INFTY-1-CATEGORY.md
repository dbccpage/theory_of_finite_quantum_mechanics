## Statement

> Assumed, and not derived: an (infinity,1)-category in the sense of a quasicategory, a simplicial set in which every inner horn has a filler. Joyal, Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 175 (2002) 207-222, isolates that inner-horn condition and its relation to Kan complexes. Boardman and Vogt, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 347 (1973), introduced the weak Kan complexes of which this is the later name. Lurie, Higher Topos Theory, arXiv:math/0608040, develops the theory. An (infinity,1)-category is not a finite semi-simplicial set. A finite inner-horn condition on a finite complex, were one stated, would be only an approximation of this assumption.
