## Statement

> The meson-side output of QM-221 is the pair of channel coefficients -4/3 and +1/6 on a quark-antiquark color decomposition. The Rust fields singlet_channel_casimir and octet_channel_potential_factor store those rationals. There is no finite Hilbert space of spatial meson orbitals, no relative wavefunction, and no mass operator in qcd_beta0_wilson_confinement_control.rs. Calling the attractive sign a meson composite would add structure the control does not build. The obligation is a separate finite composite, with a declared carrier and a color contraction, before a meson label is used as more than a channel name. Narrowed in Lean 4 and an independent Python verifier to three exact finite/algebraic components and non-equivalence fences: (1) exact rational SU(N)/SU(3) quark-antiquark channel Casimir factors (-4/3 singlet and +1/6 octet), 3 x 3 = 1 + 8 dimension decomposition, weighted trace cancellation 1*(-4/3) + 8*(1/6) = 0, and SU(2) vs. SU(3) channel contrast (suN_qqbar_casimir_channel_formulas, su3_qqbar_dimension_and_weighted_trace_sum_zero, singlet_attractive_vs_octet_repulsive_sign_dichotomy, su2_vs_su3_qqbar_casimir_channel_contrast); (2) explicit 3x3 color-trace contraction qqbarColorTrace and singlet/octet projectors singletProj, octetProj with idempotence, tracelessness, and orthogonality (color_contraction_projector_idempotence_and_orthogonality); and (3) declared finite 36 * L^d meson composite carrier accounting and fences showing that channel Casimir rationals do not supply a spatial orbital wavefunction, radial mass operator, or spin-hyperfine splitting (finite_meson_composite_carrier_dimension, meson_mass_operator_requires_spatial_kinetic_and_potential_terms_fence, channel_casimir_ratio_not_spatial_wavefunction_fence, pseudoscalar_vs_vector_hyperfine_splitting_requires_spin_operator_fence, tfq0032_meson_channel_scope_ledger_fence).
