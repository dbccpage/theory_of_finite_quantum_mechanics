## Statement

> finite_groupoid_algebra.rs linearizes a ValidatedFiniteGroupoid as convolution over Q(i). The module states that the associated complex C*-algebra is a mathematical extension, not a native finite computation. gauge_cochain_complex.rs is a different object: an exact complex on packets in M_2(Q(i)), with d0 sending anti-Hermitian generators to commutators on effects and states. Neither file is a Hopf gauge theory, and neither file stores SU(3) link variables. The obligation is to keep the groupoid convolution and the qubit packet complex from being cited as the color connection used later in the QCD control. Narrowed in Lean 4 and an independent Python verifier to three exact finite/algebraic components and non-equivalence fences: (1) 2-object pair groupoid convolution associativity, two-sided unit e00 + e11, orthogonal object idempotents e00 * e11 = 0, and the (2p+1)^2 != 2q^2 parity obstruction for the C*-norm sqrt(2) completion (pair_groupoid_convolution_associative, pair_groupoid_unit_and_orthogonal_object_idempotents, groupoid_cstar_norm_requires_sqrt_extension_fence); (2) M_2(Z\[i\]) qubit packet commutator coboundary d0(Omega)(X) = \[Omega, X\] tracelessness, scalar kernel, Leibniz derivation rule, and Jacobi identity (packet_d0_commutator_trace_zero, packet_d0_scalar_kernel_and_leibniz_derivation, packet_d0_jacobi_identity); and (3) non-equivalence fences separating groupoid convolution and the M_2 packet complex from each other (I2 * I2 = I2 != 0 = \[I2, I2\]), from Hopf gauge modules, and from SU(3) color links (matrix entries 4 != 9, Lie dimension 3 != 8, fundamental Casimir 3/4 != 4/3, and Lie derivation vs. multiplicative link law) (groupoid_convolution_vs_packet_d0_distinct_objects_fence, m2_packet_vs_su3_color_dimension_and_casimir_fence, neither_module_is_hopf_or_su3_scope_ledger_fence, commutator_derivation_breaks_grouplike_multiplicativity_fence).
