## Statement

> Let a finite shadow be a finite semi-simplicial set or a finite globular set as in GTH-SHADOW. Its set of path components, pi_0, is the quotient of the 0-cells by the equivalence relation generated by finite 1-zigzags. Its finite fundamental groupoid has the 0-cells as objects and, as morphisms, equivalence classes of finite 1-paths, the equivalence being the one generated by the 2-cells when 2-cells are part of the shadow. A finite n-type presentation keeps cells and face data through dimension n and stops there. None of these quotients or presentations is constructed, because no shadow is constructed from the admission gate.
