# Geometric Measure Deficit of the 22,379 Phase Ball Atlas on Continuous Hadamard Submanifolds

Type: observation
Confidence: high
Source: Falser reports strategy_L02_N01, strategy_L02_N02, strategy_L02_N03, strategy_L02_N07 (Level 2); Candidate strategy_L04_N00 and Falser report strategy_L04_N00 (Level 4)
Relevant to: Second-basis moduli space covering, Gap G1, and continuous Hadamard families in MUB(6).

## Statement
The finite atlas of 22,379 numerical phase balls of radius $r = 0.016$ is geometrically and measure-theoretically insufficient to cover the known continuous submanifolds of order-6 complex Hadamard matrices $\mathcal{H}_6$:
1. **2D Affine Fourier-Diță Family $F_6(a, b)$**:
   The parameter space is the 2-torus $\mathbb{T}^2 \cong [0, 2\pi)^2$ with Riemannian area $(2\pi)^2 \approx 39.4784$. The Euclidean area of 22,379 disks of radius $r = 0.016$ is at most $22,379 \times \pi (0.016)^2 \approx 17.9983$, covering at most $45.59\%$ of the 2D torus even with zero overlap. Covering $\mathbb{T}^2$ requires at least $49,088$ balls (and $\ge 59,356$ under optimal hexagonal circle packing).
2. **3D Non-Affine Karlsson Family $K_6^{(3)}$**:
   The parameter space is a 3-dimensional manifold with volume $(2\pi)^3 \approx 248.0502$. The volume of 22,379 Euclidean 3-balls of radius $r = 0.016$ is $22,379 \times \frac{4\pi}{3} (0.016)^3 \approx 0.3840$, covering only $0.1548\%$ ($< 0.16\%$) of the manifold. Covering a 3D manifold with balls of radius $0.016$ requires at least $14,457,429$ balls ($> 14.4$ million).

## Evidence
1. **Analytical Volume Ratios**:
   - $\text{Vol}(\mathbb{T}^2) / \text{Vol}(B_2(0.016)) = \frac{39.4784}{0.0008042} \approx 49,087.4$.
   - $\text{Vol}(\mathbb{T}^3) / \text{Vol}(B_3(0.016)) = \frac{248.0502}{1.7157 \times 10^{-5}} \approx 14,457,428.3$.
2. **Hexagonal Packing Density**:
   Optimal circle packing density in 2D is $\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{3}} \approx 0.9069$, giving covering density $\frac{2\pi}{3\sqrt{3}} \approx 1.2092$, requiring $\ge 59,356$ balls for $\mathbb{T}^2$.
3. **Local Basin Generation**:
   The 22,379 phase balls were generated by local Newton-Kantorovich root-refinement from unrefuted cells in Stage B, capturing only the attraction basins of numerically discovered roots rather than establishing a global partition of unity.

## Implications
1. Claims that the 22,379 phase ball atlas provides an "unrestricted covering of $\mathcal{H}_6$ without classification" are geometrically impossible. Over $54.4\%$ of a 2D family and over $99.8\%$ of a 3D family are omitted by the atlas.
2. In the absence of an exact algebraic global root-isolation certificate (e.g. cylindrical algebraic decomposition or a Positivstellensatz refutation of the complement variety on $\mathbb{T}^9$), isolated complex Hadamard matrices or exotic connected components outside numerical attraction basins are left uninspected.
3. Target A cannot be certified as an unconditional proof of $N(6) = 3$ without resolving this global moduli covering debt (Gap G1).
