## 1. Executive Summary & Slogan

A single unresolved primitive difference (non-exact 1-cochain $\omega = d_0 s$) on a finite cell complex $K$ generates persistent quotient residuals $\Phi([\omega]) = \inf_\alpha \|\omega - d_0 \alpha\|_1 > 0$. As the complex undergoes cell extensions $K_0 \hookrightarrow K_1 \hookrightarrow \dots \hookrightarrow K_n$, the sequence of complexes forms a filtration.

The spectral sequence $E_r^{p,q}$ associated with this complex growth filtration formalizes the global cochain repair dynamics:

> **Spectral Slogan:**  
> A cochain class killed by a differential $d_r$ represents an **obstruction removed**.  
> A class reaching the limit page $E_\infty$ represents a **permanent residual $\iff$ structure built**.

## 2. Two Natural Filtrations of the Growing Complex

On a finite cell complex $K$ expanding via admissible extensions:

1. **Skeleton Filtration**:
   $$
   F_p K = K^{(p)} \quad \text{(the } p \text{-skeleton of } K \text{)}.
   $$
   Computes standard cellular cohomology.

2. **Stage Growth Filtration**:
   $$
   K_0 \hookrightarrow K_1 \hookrightarrow K_2 \hookrightarrow \dots \hookrightarrow K_n = K,
   $$
   where each stage $K_{m+1}$ is an admissible cell extension (edge subdivision or 2-simplex attachment) satisfying $\Phi_{K_{m+1}}([\iota^* \omega]) < \Phi_{K_m}([\omega])$.

## 3. Associated Spectral Sequence $E_r^{p,q}$

From the stage filtration $G_p C^*(K) = C^*(K, K_p)$, we construct an exact couple:
$$
D_1^{p,q} = H^{p+q}(K_p), \qquad E_1^{p,q} = H^{p+q}(K_p, K_{p-1}).
$$
The associated spectral sequence $E_r^{p,q}$ has differentials $d_r: E_r^{p,q} \to E_r^{p+r, q-r+1}$.

### Physical & Algebraic Meaning of Pages

| Page / Term | Algebraic Role | Physical & Structural Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$E_1^{p,q}$** | $H^{p+q}(K_p, K_{p-1})$ | Raw defect insertion at growth stage $p$ (e.g. attached 2-simplex $\sigma_2$). |
| **$d_r$ Differential** | $d_r: E_r^{p,q} \to E_r^{p+r, q-r+1}$ | Cell boundary repair / coboundary integration map across $r$ expansion stages. |
| **$\ker(d_r) / \operatorname{im}(d_r)$** | Page transition $E_{r+1}$ | Filtering out exact repairs ($\Phi = 0$). |
| **Class killed by $d_r$** | $[\omega] \in \operatorname{im}(d_r)$ | **Obstruction Removed** — defect is completely healed by higher-dimensional cell filling. |
| **$E_\infty^{p,q}$ Limit Page** | Permanent cohomology $\mathrm{Gr}_p H^*(K)$ | **Structure Built** — persistent, non-killable residual topological class ($\Phi > 0$). |

## 4. Elementary Engine: Long Exact Sequence of the Pair $(K_p, K_{p-1})$

The spectral sequence is simply the global bookkeeper of the sequence of long exact couples generated by pairs $(K_p, K_{p-1})$:
$$
\dots \to H^k(K_p, K_{p-1}) \xrightarrow{j^*} H^k(K_p) \xrightarrow{i^*} H^k(K_{p-1}) \xrightarrow{\delta} H^{k+1}(K_p, K_{p-1}) \to \dots
$$
- Attaching a 2-simplex $\sigma_2$ to a 3-cycle $\partial \sigma_2$ introduces relative cochain $C^2(\sigma_2, \partial \sigma_2)$.
- The coboundary map $\delta$ maps the 1-cycle defect to 0 in $H^1(K_p)$ if and only if $\partial \sigma_2 = d_0 s$ (exact filling).

## 6. Spectral Sequence Convergence (Bounded Filtrations)

Because every complex that appears in the PrimDiff / Constraint-Closure toy model is finite, both natural filtrations (skeleton filtration and stage growth filtration) are **bounded and exhaustive**. Consequently the associated spectral sequence **converges strongly** after finitely many pages. There are no $\lim^1$ issues, no conditional-convergence hypotheses, and no infinite-page phenomena of the kind that appear for infinite or continuous filtrations.

### Precise Statement

**Lemma (Strong Convergence for Bounded Filtrations).**  
Let $C^\bullet$ be a cochain complex of finite-dimensional vector spaces (or free modules of finite rank). Let $F_p C^\bullet$ be a filtration that is bounded:
$$
F_p = 0 \quad (p \ll 0), \qquad F_p = C \quad (p \gg 0)
$$
and exhaustive. Then the associated spectral sequence converges strongly: there exists $r_0$ such that for all $r \ge r_0$,
$$
E_r^{p,q} \;\simeq\; E_\infty^{p,q} \;\simeq\; \operatorname{gr}_p H^{p+q}(C).
$$
Every class is therefore either killed by some differential $d_r$ ($r < r_0$) or survives permanently as a graded piece of the cohomology of the final complex.

*Proof / Source:* Elementary linear algebra of filtered complexes (Stacks Project Tag 012W, Weibel §5.4–5.5). No continuum analysis is required.

### Consequences for the Growth Process

| Filtration | Length | Stabilizes by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skeleton filtration** of a complex of dimension $\le d$ | $\le d+1$ | $r \le d+2$ |
| **Stage growth filtration** with $N$ licensed extensions | $N$ | $r \le N+2$ |

- A residual class that can be driven to zero by a finite sequence of subdivisions or cell attachments is killed on some finite page $\implies$ **obstruction removed**.
- A residual that survives every licensed extension appears as a non-zero permanent cycle on $E_\infty$ $\implies$ **structure built**.

The practical computational rule already used by the growth iterator ("stop when $\Phi$ no longer decreases under any licensed extension") is exactly the computation of the $E_\infty$ page.

### Elementary Illustration

Start with the boundary cycle $\gamma$ of a triangle ($H^1 \simeq \mathbb{R}$). Attach the 2-cell. The relative group $H^2(K, \partial K)$ is $\mathbb{R}$ and the connecting homomorphism is an isomorphism, so $[\gamma]$ is killed on the $E_2$ (or $E_3$) page. The spectral sequence of this two-stage filtration therefore has $E_\infty^{*,1} = 0$. Any residual $\Phi$ that remains after attachment lives on the 2-cochains (higher-degree permanent class) or vanishes if the section becomes exact. Because the filtration has only two or three stages, strong convergence is immediate.

## 7. Upgrade: General Finite Complexes

All load-bearing definitions and theorems in this theory are stated for an **arbitrary finite simplicial complex** $K$ (or finite abstract cell complex, or finite poset):

- A **primitive difference** on $K$ is a 1-cochain $\omega \notin \operatorname{im} d_0$.
- $\Phi([\omega]) = \inf_\alpha \|\omega - d_0 \alpha\|_1 > 0$ is the obstruction certificate.
- **Licensed growth** = any finite sequence of elementary extensions (cell attachments or subdivisions) that decrease $\Phi$ in the lowest residual degree or push the residual into higher degree.
- The skeletal filtration or the stage growth filtration of $K$ is bounded; the associated spectral sequence **converges strongly**.
- Permanent $E_\infty$ classes are residual structure that cannot be removed by further licensed growth.

The single-edge defect and the 2-simplex attachment remain only the *minimal non-contractible worked examples* and pedagogical illustrations. Nothing in the theory depends on starting from a 3-cycle. This removes any circular $N=3$ motif and establishes a general finite-complex residual-obstruction theory fully aligned with PO-000's finite-carrier language.
