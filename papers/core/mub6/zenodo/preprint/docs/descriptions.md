# Exact reductions and quantitative bounds for mutually unbiased bases in dimension six (MUB6)

Subtitle: Exact reductions, conditional interfaces and open exclusion gaps

## Abstract

This technical checkpoint studies mutually unbiased bases in dimension six through exact matrix reductions, quantitative minor bounds, polynomial encodings and conditional exclusion interfaces. It reports a minor with squared determinant at least 54/5 and inverse-entry squared moduli at most 10/27, a bounded block reconstruction chart, and a balanced system of 648 rational polynomial equations of degree at most two in 320 bounded real coordinates.

## Why This Matters

The reductions separate matrix normalization and bounded polynomial encodings
from the remaining infeasibility problem. Finite checks, source-level
implications and dimension-wide exclusion statements require different evidence.

## Core Theorems

Reported source results concern bounded minors, block reconstruction, balanced
polynomial equivalences and positive flat moment extraction under explicit
hypotheses. The preprint does not supply a fresh complete formal replay.

## Scope Boundaries and Non-Claims

Whether the relevant systems are infeasible remains open. The Fourier route retains its landing premise; the full 56-cell and cusp calculation is incomplete. The moment approach retains its compatibility, flatness and empty-variety obligations. No unconditional dimension-wide MUB bound follows. In particular, this work does not establish that the maximum number of mutually unbiased bases in dimension six is three.
