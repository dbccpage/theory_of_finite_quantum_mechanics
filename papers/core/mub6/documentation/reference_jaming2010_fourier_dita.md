# Fourier-Dita Family MUB Quartet Exclusion (Jaming et al. 2010)

Type: reference
Confidence: high
Source: Candidate strategy_L00_N01, strategy_L00_N07 (Level 0)
Relevant to: Excluding the continuous Fourier-Dita family $F(x,y)$ from participating in MUB quartets in dimension 6.

## Bibliographic Info
- Authors: Philippe Jaming, Maté Matolcsi, Péter Mora, Ferenc Szöllősi, Mihály Weiner
- Title: A generalized Pauca-Marcus type theorem and mutually unbiased bases in dimension 6
- Year: 2010
- ArXiv/DOI: arXiv:0902.0722 / J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 43 (2010) 474005
- Theorem/Lemma Number: Theorem 1.1 / Section 3
- Verified via web search: yes

## Key Result
No member of the two-parameter continuous affine Fourier-Dita family of complex Hadamard matrices $F_6(x, y)$ can be extended to four mutually unbiased bases in $\mathbb{C}^6$. Specifically, any set of mutually unbiased bases containing the standard basis $I_6$ and a member of $F_6(x, y)$ contains at most three bases.

## Hypotheses / Conditions
1. Dimension is $d = 6$.
2. The initial complex Hadamard matrix belongs to the Fourier-Dita family $F_6(x, y)$.

## How It Applies
This result provides one of the two foundational branches in reducing $N(6) = 3$ to candidate families: since $F(x, y)$ cannot extend to 4 MUBs, any quartet must come from another component of the complex Hadamard variety (such as the Szöllősi family $H_6^{(2)}(a,b)$ or isolated matrices).
- What it supports: Supports nonexistence of MUB quartets containing a Fourier-Dita matrix.
- What it does not support: Does not by itself rule out quartets containing members of the Szöllősi family or isolated matrices like the Tao matrix.
- Narrowest defensible claim: No quartet of MUBs in $\mathbb{C}^6$ contains a member of the Fourier-Dita family.
- Next test needed: Integrate with certified exclusions of the Szöllősi family.

## Caveats
Established in the published literature and widely accepted in the field. Does not address the Szöllősi family or isolated sporadic matrices.
