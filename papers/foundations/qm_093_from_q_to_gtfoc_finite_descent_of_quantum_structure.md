**Result in one line.** Starting from the continuum frontier $\Omega_Q$ (the Q

1. Why a Researcher Should Care


Three deep questions sit behind this paper:

**Q1: Where does quantum structure come from?** Axiomatic reconstructions
(Hardy 2001; Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti 2011; Masanes–Müller 2011) derive
complex Hilbert QM by *assuming* operational axioms and proving the structure
follows. This paper instead begins with an imported quantum interface and checks it
against the tower's declared constraints. The result is a compatibility and
survival audit, not a reconstruction theorem and not a proof that every
admissible interface must be quantum.

**Q2: What is checked vs. imported?** The numbered papers in this series
separate bounded tower checks from structures that must be imported. This paper
synthesizes that audit into a single descent chain, so a reader can see which
facts are proved under declared carriers, which are registry-relative, and
which structures remain imported. The five imports — complex carrier, Born
pairing, CPTP dynamics, trace-distance geometry, tensor composition — are
explicitly marked at each layer where they enter.

**Q3: Why does the tower work?** The five primitives
$$\text{Difference} \to \text{Repair} \to \text{Quotient} \to \text{Closure} \to \text{Checked evidence}$$
look abstract. This paper makes them concrete by testing an imported QM
interface at each layer. The finite-shadow step checks an ℓ¹ contraction under
a declared stochastic carrier; the quotient step constructs a declared
obstruction complex; the closure/transport step audits an imported CPTP
interface. None of these checks derives that interface.

4. The Q Layer: Where Complex Hilbert Lives


**What Q carries.** The Q layer ($\Omega_Q$) is the continuum frontier of the
tower. It is not a tower layer proper — it is the *source* from which the tower
descends. Q carries:

- **Complex Hilbert spaces** $\mathcal H$ (possibly infinite-dimensional)
- **Continuous unitaries** $U(t) = e^{-iHt}$ (the Stone–von Neumann group)
- **Bounded operators** $\mathcal B(\mathcal H)$ with operator norm
- **Density operators** $\rho \in \mathcal B(\mathcal H)_+^1$ (positive, trace 1)
- **Observables** as self-adjoint operators or POVMs
- **The Born rule** $p(E|\rho) = \operatorname{Tr}(E\rho)$ as the pairing.

**What Q does NOT carry.** Q does not carry authority. A claim that lives in Q
— "this state has energy $E$", "this measurement outcome has probability $p$"
— is a formal proposal, not an authorized physical claim. Any bounded authority
decision lies outside this paper and may consume checked descent evidence.

**The import declaration.** Every structure listed above is an *import* when it
enters the tower. Complex Hilbert space, Born pairing, CPTP maps, and trace
distance enter at Q as declared interfaces, not as tower-derived objects. The
bounded checks below show that these *specific* imports pass. Weaker alternatives
are excluded only where a stated theorem or frozen-registry audit establishes the exclusion.

5. CODA-CS: Finite-Shadow ℓ¹ Audit


**The bounded CODA-CS theorem.** The CODA-CS layer ($\Omega_\kappa$) forms a pro-system
$\{K_\lambda\}$ of finite-dimensional shadows of Q with replayable refinement
maps. For any quantum representation that descends through CODA-CS, the shadow
weights must be in $\ell^1$.

**Machine-checked result (LT-M).** The Lean 4 file
`QM.StochasticL1` proves:

$$
\boxed{
\text{STOCH-L1-001: Column-stochastic maps contract the } \ell^1 \text{ norm.}
}
$$

Formally (over $\mathbb Q$): for any column-stochastic kernel $S$ (nonneg
entries, each column summing to $1$) and vector $x \in \mathbb Q^n$,
$\|Sx\|_1 \leq \|x\|_1$, where $\|\cdot\|_1$ is the finite $\ell^1$ norm
$\sum_i |x_i|$. The theorem is `QM.stochastic_l1_contraction`; it is `sorry`-free.
This is the division-algebra-free *precursor* to trace-norm contractivity — it
selects no metric and mints no authority (it is the classical $\ell^1$ shadow,
not the Born rule). It uses standard Mathlib `Finset`/`abs` foundations
(expected axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; the exact
`#print axioms` reading is confirmed at proof sign-off). LT-M is secured +
MECHANIZED as **ENC-1055** (Column-Stochastic $\ell^1$ Contraction; aliases
`paper:005:classical-l1-contraction` / `paper:O025:C5`).

**Why this matters.** The finite shadow of a quantum state is a probability
distribution over a finite outcome set. Probability distributions have $\ell^1$
norm equal to 1. The contraction theorem proves that quantum evolution (modeled
as a column-stochastic map on the shadow) preserves this norm — it does not
increase probability. This is the $\ell^1$ shadow of Born's rule.

**What this checks.** Every interface admitted to this CODA-CS packet must carry declared $\ell^1$
weights. Classical probability distributions carry $\ell^1$ weights — they pass
CODA-CS. Real-Hilbert rebits carry $\ell^1$ weights after taking the shadow. What
fails at CODA-CS is any interface that tries to use a divergent or non-normalizable
weight — such proposals are refused.

**What CODA-CS does not establish.** It does not derive the complex carrier or the Born pairing. Later layers audit those declared imports. CODA-CS establishes only the bounded $\ell^1$ contraction under its stated carrier hypotheses.

11. CODA-FOC: The Obstruction Kernel Classifies a Declared Interface


**The CODA-FOC classification theorem.** The CODA-FOC layer ($\Omega_\partial$) is the
finite obstruction kernel. For a quantum interface to be evaluated here, its
finite cochain carrier and comparison data must already be declared. The result
classifies that data; it does not select the complex Hilbert interface from all
possible interfaces.

**The obstruction complex.** CODA-FOC equips every quantum packet with a
three-term cochain complex:

$$
C_Q^0 \xrightarrow{d_Q^0} C_Q^1 \xrightarrow{d_Q^1} C_Q^2,
\qquad d_Q^1 d_Q^0 = 0.
$$

The two diagnostics:

$$
\Phi_Q([x]) = \inf_{\alpha \in C_Q^0} \|x - d_Q^0 \alpha\|_Q
\quad \text{(quotient obstruction magnitude)},
$$
$$
\Gamma_Q([x]) = \|\bar d_Q^1([x])\|_Q
\quad \text{(closure defect)}.
$$

classify every residual into an **obstruction diagnostic class**:

$$
\operatorname{Tri}_Q([x]) =
\begin{cases}
\mathsf{ExactClass}, & \Phi_Q([x]) = 0, \\
\mathsf{ClosedResidual}, & \Phi_Q([x]) > 0 \wedge \Gamma_Q([x]) = 0, \\
\mathsf{ActiveClosureFailure}, & \Gamma_Q([x]) > 0.
\end{cases}
$$

These classes do not directly construct `ExactRepair`,
`StabilizedSurvivor`, an authority status, or any action permission.

**Why classical gluing fails.** A classical interface (simplex probability)
has $\Phi_Q = 0$ everywhere — every defect can be repaired by a classical
gauge. A quantum interface has $\Phi_Q > 0$ for contextual observables: some
residuals cannot be classically repaired. This is the finite-tower
version of Abramsky–Brandenburger contextuality — the failure of a global
section in the presheaf of local distributions.

12. The Survival Theorem


The full descent program is captured by the Survival Theorem.

**Honest provable form.** The theorem is a *conditional assembly*, not an origin
derivation. The two registered hypotheses $\mathsf{Imported}$ and
$\mathsf{VerifiedFiniteReplay}$ discharge conditions 1–5 (definitionally for 1–2,
and from the already-mechanized backing kernels for 3–5), but they do **not** by
themselves entail condition 6 (checked-route eligibility): that the packet's
historical/local tag lands in $\mathbb E_{\mathsf{exact}} \cup
\mathbb E_{\mathsf{scoped}}$ requires the
*external* sealed CODA-FDA-Q $\Pi^\sharp$ replay (PL-QM005-2). We therefore expose
that sealed replay as an explicit third antecedent
$\mathsf{SealedReplayTerminalizes}(P_Q)$, yielding the assembled implication that
is now mechanized. The identifier is retained for theorem compatibility; it is a
non-authoritative checked-evidence predicate, not a current terminal constructor.

The condition-6 antecedent is **genuinely non-vacuous**. In the mechanization
$\mathsf{SealedReplayTerminalizes}(P_Q)$ reads the packet's own sealed tag
$\Pi^\sharp(P_Q) = P_Q.\mathsf{sealedTag}$ and asserts it lands in the admissible
union; it is therefore *packet content* and **can fail** — when the sealed readout
is $\mathsf{refusal}$ the antecedent is false (Lean lemma `sealed_can_fail`
exhibits such a packet). Correspondingly there is **no** hypothesis-free discharge
of condition 6: $\neg(\forall \mathcal F_Q\, P_Q.\ \mathsf{Terminalizes}(\mathcal
F_Q, P_Q))$, so the assembled theorem truly *consumes* $\mathsf{hSealed}$ and the
implication is a real conditional, not a tautology carrying no packet content.

**Theorem (QM Survival Under Finite Descent).** *[Assembled implication
MECHANIZED (`QM.SurvivalTheorem`, axiom-free); conditional on the
external sealed CODA-FDA-Q $\Pi^\sharp$ replay (PL-QM005-2). Not an origin
derivation.]*

For the declared finite $M_2$ quantum interface $\mathcal F_Q$ and a
well-formed quantum packet $P_Q$ (Paper 09):

$$
\mathsf{Imported}(\mathcal F_Q)
\wedge
\mathsf{VerifiedFiniteReplay}(P_Q)
\wedge
\mathsf{SealedReplayTerminalizes}(P_Q)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{SurvivesDescent}(\mathcal F_Q \mid P_Q),
$$

where $\mathsf{SurvivesDescent}$ requires:

1. $\mathsf{DeclaredInterface}(\mathcal F_Q, P_Q)$ — the interface is declared, not derived
2. $\mathsf{ReplayFiniteEvidence}(\mathcal F_Q, P_Q)$ — replay evidence is finite and exact
3. $\mathsf{RespectsQuotientRepair}(\mathcal F_Q, P_Q)$ — repair does not escape the quotient $Q^1 = C^1/\operatorname{im}(d_0)$
4. $\mathsf{CertifiedContextTransport}(\mathcal F_Q, P_Q)$ — context transport is $\chi$-certified
5. $\mathsf{MetricSubordinate}(\mathcal F_Q, P_Q; \Phi_1, \Gamma_2)$ — diagnostics are metric-subordinate
6. $\exists t \in \mathbb E_{\mathsf{exact}} \cup \mathbb E_{\mathsf{scoped}}: \Pi_{\mathcal F}^\sharp(P_Q) = t$ — the legacy/local tag passes the checked evidence-route predicate.

**Proof (assembly; condition-by-condition discharge).**

- **Condition 1 (declared interface)** follows definitionally from
  $\mathsf{Imported}(\mathcal F_Q)$: the interface enters the system by
  declaration (its complex-Hilbert carrier, Born pairing, CPTP maps, and
  trace-distance geometry are *imports*), so $\mathsf{DeclaredInterface}$ is the
  import flag itself (`cond1_declared`).

- **Condition 2 (finite, exact replay evidence)** follows definitionally from
  $\mathsf{VerifiedFiniteReplay}(P_Q)$: the replay evidence is a finite list of
  residuals reproduced bit-exactly (`cond2_finite_evidence`).

- **Condition 3 (repair stays in the quotient).** A repair step retargets the
  defect predicate to a *weaker* one ($B \Rightarrow B'$) and changes the class
  only on coboundaries (inside $\operatorname{im}(d_0)$). The condition asserts
  **two** facts, and condition 3 *genuinely consumes* the confined-change
  hypothesis: (i) **quotient-confinement** — every defect the repair actually
  clears (surviving under $B$, i.e. $\neg B(x)$, but cleared under $B'$, i.e.
  $B'(x)$) lies in $\operatorname{im}(d_0)$, because clearing it changed its class
  ($B(x) \neq B'(x)$) and the confinement hypothesis then forces $x \in
  \operatorname{im}(d_0)$; so nothing *outside* $Q^1 = C^1/\operatorname{im}(d_0)$
  is ever cleared. And (ii) **repair monotonicity** — the surviving-defect count
  cannot increase. Part (i) is the firewall content the predicate is named for and
  is *not* derivable without the confinement hypothesis; part (ii) bundles the
  mechanized `ProjectionObstructionQuotient` (ENC-1065) and
  `RepairMonotonicity` (ENC-1066), with the needed monotonicity fact reproved
  locally as `repair_monotone`. Together they discharge `cond3_quotient_repair`.

- **Condition 4 ($\chi$-certified context transport).** The verified replay
  carries the $\chi$-certificate token, and the interface is a least-cost row of
  its *declared* registry — registry-relative minimality, **not** universal
  minimality (`RegistryRelativeMinimality`, ENC-1075). Note the
  CODA-MUR-Q KCS transport pentagon is proved-prose; its dedicated Lean is an open
  hardening target (PL-QM019-1), so condition 4's $\chi$-certificate is
  proved-prose and the fine pentagon Lean is cited as open, **not** a blocker
  (`cond4_certified_transport`).

- **Condition 5 (metric-subordinate diagnostics).** The scalar quotient
  diagnostic satisfies $\Phi_1 = \lVert R_\Lambda \rVert_{\text{Banach-shadow}}$
  at the scalar level (`CofiberScalarSeparation`, ENC-1077, axiom-free), and
  $\Gamma_2$ is bounded by its declared trace-norm subordination bound on the
  finite evidence (ENC-0247). Both are carried by the verified replay
  (`cond5_metric_subordinate`).

- **Condition 6 (checked-route eligibility)** is the only condition not
  derivable from import + replay. It is discharged from the external sealed
  $\Pi^\sharp$ replay hypothesis $\mathsf{SealedReplayTerminalizes}(P_Q)$,
  which asserts that *this packet's* historical/local readout
  $\Pi^\sharp(P_Q) = P_Q.\mathsf{sealedTag}$ lands in the evidence union
  $\mathbb E_{\mathsf{exact}} \cup \mathbb E_{\mathsf{scoped}}$.
  Because the assertion is read off the packet's own tag, it is genuine packet
  content that **can be false** — a packet with $\Pi^\sharp$ readout
  $\mathsf{refusal}$ refutes it (`sealed_can_fail`) — so condition 6 is truly
  conditional on the sealed certificate and is not provable from the empty
  context. The legacy tags are pairwise distinct, and refusal is not in the
  admitted evidence union (`TerminalDisjointness`, ENC-1064; firewall
  ENC-1063). The historical theorem name `cond6_terminalizes` therefore proves
  an exact non-refusal evidence-route check only; it constructs no current
  terminal or authority result.

The conjunction of the six discharges is the assembled implication
`survival_under_finite_descent`, mechanized axiom-free in
`QM.SurvivalTheorem`. $\qquad\blacksquare$

**The import is load-bearing.** The theorem does not prove $\mathsf{OriginDerived}(\mathcal F_Q)$. The complex Hilbert carrier, Born pairing, CPTP maps, and trace-distance geometry are imported. What is proved is that these specific imports pass the encoded checks; weaker alternatives are excluded only where a stated finite theorem or frozen registry audit does so. The survival is the content; the import is the source. The mechanization makes this firewall *checkable as a real separation between two genuinely inhabitable predicates*. The origin claim is modelled as an **independent** interface marker $\mathsf{OriginDerived}(\mathcal F_Q) \equiv (\mathcal F_Q.\mathsf{originFlag} = \mathsf{true})$ — a predicate that *holds* of some interfaces (`origin_is_inhabitable` exhibits one) and *fails* of others — rather than as the empty type. The canonical interface/packet then satisfies the full $\mathsf{SurvivesDescent}$ conclusion while having $\mathsf{originFlag} = \mathsf{false}$, so $\mathsf{OriginDerived}$ genuinely fails of it: there is no implication $\mathsf{SurvivesDescent} \Rightarrow \mathsf{OriginDerived}$ (`survival_does_not_mint_origin`, `no_survival_to_origin`), and because the target predicate is non-empty this is a real separation, not a vacuous one.
