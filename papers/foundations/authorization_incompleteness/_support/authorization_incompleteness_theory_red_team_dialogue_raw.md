## Round 1: Turing scope

**Red R1.** Calling the direct candidate a Turing-machine theorem would
overclaim. It contains a finite rule list and a mathematical total tape,
but no byte encoding, parser, universal machine, or simulation theorem.

**Blue.** Conceded. The source is a direct finite-rule, Turing-style
operational seed only.

**Green.** Carry forward. The title, paper prose, and ledger must say
finite-rule syntax and fuel-bounded execution; no claim may call it
Turing complete.

## Round 2: Halting and terminality

**Red R2.** A designated state provides a syntactic terminal predicate,
not an answer to whether a computation is useful, true, authorized, or
globally final.

**Blue.** Conceded. The candidate defines DeclaredHalt solely by the
machine state and gives distinct supplied terminal policies the same
halt result.

**Green.** Carry forward. The implementation must state that fuel
exhaustion is not non-halting, and a halt is not authorization.

## Round 3: Lean correspondence

**Red R3.** A green Lean build alone is too weak. The candidate must be
rooted explicitly, its exact declarations must be cited, its axiom
report must be recorded, and its computation fixture must not be
inflated into a universal result.

**Blue.** Conceded. The current direct compile has no sorry/admit
marker, but several declarations report propext. That dependency must
be visible rather than described as axiom-free.

**Green.** Carry forward. The next implementation must record the
exact current hash, Lake build, source scan, and declaration-level
axiom ceilings.

## Round 4: Carrier admission

**Red R4.** A direct syntax module does not close the global
computability lift. The admission gate still lacks program and input
bytes, an extensional semantic property, nontrivial witnesses, a
universality theorem, and an effective reduction.

**Blue.** Conceded. The module closes only a small part of the syntax
and bounded-execution field for a candidate carrier.

**Green.** Carry forward. Create a machine-readable candidate record
that prohibits an admitted status while any required field remains
open or partial.

## Round 5: Existing research and provenance

**Red R5.** PO-022 and PO-023 cannot supply the missing result because
their Lean companions are explicitly True scaffolds. A no-literature
prompt run also cannot show absence of training influence, novelty, or
validity.

**Blue.** Conceded. They remain design and provenance sources only.

**Green.** Carry forward. The direct candidate record must cite their
current ceilings and must not import their Lean scaffolds as evidence.
