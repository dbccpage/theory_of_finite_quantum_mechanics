# Source map and evidence boundary

**Recorded:** 2026-08-20

**AuthorityEffect:** None.

This map records what was inspected before starting the new theorem
program. It prevents three bad inheritances: treating a green build as a
semantic proof, treating an old paper's open claim as closed, or copying
the sealed derivation into the live repository.

## Sealed derivation: 19L57G1

**Citation:** `dbccpage/19L57G1@sealed-derivation-v1`, annotated tag
object `e147f01cfed1d17410c55a1e78367fdadc63a5ae`, commit
`3100c4ed5ce70d2b6cff6d2ccd3b0251cf918d0a`.

The sealed verification record reports eight mechanical gate families:
prompt identity, citation resolution, tests and coverage, type checking,
artifact regeneration, Lean build and axiom reports, no-authority skills,
and paper cross-references. It also explicitly says that these gates do
not determine whether a paper's theorem claims correspond to Lean
statements, whether all notions have source classifications, whether
tied minima were retained, or whether the terminal prose conclusion is
the strongest defensible one.

**Use in this paper:** evidence that mechanical replay and semantic
authorization are distinct layers.

**Not used:** no code, prompts, paper text, derivation files, artifacts,
or Lean modules are copied. The sealed tag is not cited as proof of the
Authorization Incompleteness Theorem.

## PO-000 authorization boundary

**Source:**
`coda_university/coda_research/002_projection_obstruction/000_projection_obstruction/lean4/po000_authorization_boundary.lean`

The source proves the report/fiber obstruction in Bool-valued form:
if `p x = p y` and independently supplied `valid x != valid y`, then no
`decide : M -> Bool` factors validity through `p`. It also proves a
positive counterpart under fiber constancy and a section, and a toy
negative/positive model.

**Use in this paper:** fixed formal anchor. Every successor map from a
proposal carrier to a report carrier must either prove fiber constancy or
retain a split-fiber residual. The new Lean module is a self-contained
statement mirror, not a separate replacement theorem.

**Not used:** PO-000's geometric, quantum, cochain, or short-time
claims. Paper 0 is not modified.

## PO-022 and PO-023 computation tracks

**Sources:**

- `coda_university/coda_research/002_projection_obstruction/022_finite_presentation_calculus_for_computation/finite_presentation_calculus_for_computation.md`
- `coda_university/coda_research/002_projection_obstruction/023_halting_as_a_non_faithful_quotient/halting_as_a_non_faithful_quotient.md`
- `lean4/coda_research/002_projection_obstruction/022_finite_presentation_calculus_for_computation/finite_presentation_calculus_for_computation.lean`
- `lean4/coda_research/002_projection_obstruction/023_halting_as_a_non_faithful_quotient/halting_as_a_non_faithful_quotient.lean`

PO-022 frames finite presentations, typed transition dispositions,
replay, and a bounded Turing-simulation target. PO-023 applies the P0
fiber criterion to a binary stopping observation: relative to a richer
operational target, stop/non-stop can collapse completed/refused or
productive/cyclic behavior. Both papers explicitly preserve Turing's
undecidability theorem and queue, rather than assert, their transport
claims.

Both current Lean companions say in their headers that every declaration
has statement `True` and proves nothing about the named paper claim.
They are scaffolds, not mechanized PO-022/PO-023 evidence.

**Use in this paper:** provenance for the controlled Turing series and
its scope fences. The AIT tape-control model is a small distinct finite
countermodel of report inadequacy; it does not close any PO-022 or PO-023
formal theorem queue.

**Not used:** bounded simulation, universal simulation, diagonal
transport, FPC implementation, silicon realization, novelty, or any
claim that a richer terminal defeats undecidability.

## FOC-028 authority-and-computation scope fence

**Source:**
`coda_university/coda_research/001_finite_obstruction_calculus/028_finite_authority_and_non_terminal_computation/finite_authority_and_non_terminal_computation.md`

FOC-028 explicitly audits its own `halting_does_not_imply_authority`
Lean name: the cited result is an inhabitation fact over a Boolean record
whose field `halted` is a named `Bool`, not a theorem about Turing
machines. Its useful surviving distinction is terminological and
architectural: a syntactic terminal is not automatically an evidence
terminal.

**Use in this paper:** an audit precedent and a scope fence against
promoting a suggestive theorem name or a halt-shaped Boolean to a result
about computability or authority.

**Not used:** the Boolean-record witnesses do not prove AIT-G5, AIT-G6,
or the LLM corollary.

## Primitive Difference / J011 starting spine

**Sources:**

- `coda_university/coda_research/_support/projection_obstruction_primitive_difference_walk/J011_mini_spine.md`
- `lean4/genx/_work_review_queue/grok_pd_night_20260814/J011_minimal_publication.lean`

J011 begins with two unequal marks and a Primitive Difference. It records
that a look, a supplied report, a split section, dynamics, cohomology,
and physical hosts are additions. Its derived quotient map recovers the
declared zero relation, but it does not select an arbitrary report or a
unique section.

**Use in this paper:** the starting interface for AIT-PD0 and AIT-PD1.
The rooted AIT-PD1 Lean bridge imports J011's actual `pdProj P` and
specializes the P0 factorization shape to a supplied validity predicate.
It supplies the discipline that each new authorization structure must be
declared and priced.

**Not used:** J011 is a candidate support unit, not an estate-close
derivation of an authorization theorem. AIT-PD0 must make its own
explicit model extensions.

## Authority firewall

**Source:**
`coda_university/coda_research/001_finite_obstruction_calculus/027_the_authority_firewall_a_machine_checked_separation_of_evidence_from_authority_in_finite_systems/`

The paper gives a type-design separation between residual evidence and
authority terminals. Its own scope ledger leaves verifier soundness as
an irreducible external trust-root issue and states that a type-level
firewall does not make the verifier inputs semantically true.

**Use in this paper:** architecture vocabulary and a concrete example of
why a type boundary is useful but insufficient for global semantic
authorization.

**Not used:** its pointwise type separation is not promoted to the
global AIT-G5 or AIT-G6 claims.
