QM_090 — Interpretations as Packet-Indistinguishable Authority Shadows
======================================================================

**A finite-descent criterion for when quantum interpretation claims become testable**

**Status:** Daily Blackboard Program (Track C)
**Version:** v0.1.0 Pre-Print
**Rule:** $\boxed{\text{Derive only what the tower forces. Import nothing silently. Refuse where it cannot speak.}}$

**Lead.** The competing interpretations of quantum mechanics — Copenhagen, many-worlds, Bohmian
mechanics, QBism, objective collapse, relational QM — are read here as *authority shadows*: distinct
narratives laid over the *same* finite observer-residual structure, none of which earns ontological
authority from that structure alone. The single load-bearing discipline is the authority firewall
$\mathrm{StructureDerived}(x)\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{IsAuth}(x)$ (ENC-1063, MECHANIZED): a structural
or formal reading never promotes itself to an authority terminal. Every interpretive reading in this
paper is fenced `[INTERPRETIVE]`; none is typed proven, and none mints authority.

**Why a researcher should care.** Interpretation debates in quantum foundations stall because rival
readings agree on every observable prediction yet each claims to say what is "really" the case. This
paper supplies a sharp, checkable criterion for when such a debate is *substantive* versus merely
narrative: an interpretation is wrong only when it makes a **packet-distinguishable** false claim —
a difference that survives the finite descent audit. Everything else is a shadow over the same
calculus, and labeling it as such (rather than as derived ontology) is a falsifiability discipline,
not a metaphysical stance.

**Notation pointer.** $\Phi_Q,\Gamma_Q,Q^1,H^1,\chi_F,\Lambda_Q,\mathcal F_Q,K_{\mathrm{comm}}$ and the
packet vocabulary follow the canonical definitions in the QM-series obstruction-calculus papers
(QM_081 finite descent, QM_006 tower walk, QM_001 cofiber bridge); the authority firewall
$\mathrm{StructureDerived}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{IsAuth}$ is ENC-1063.

Level Q boundary

This paper terminalizes only the finite operational claims declared in its packet. Such claims are routed to **Level Q** ($\Omega_Q$; `9999_Q`) as quarantined frontier probes. A Q receipt may record representation, obstruction, residual uncertainty, or a next admissible probe, but it is **not** an authority terminal for the quantum ontology claim.

3. Worked Example — when an interpretation *is* and *is not* a shadow


This minimal example replays the classifier's only sharp criterion on concrete numbers
**[WORKED EXAMPLE]**.

Take one finite observer residual with $\Phi_Q([x])=1>0$ and $\Gamma_Q([x])=0$ (a survivor class:
locally closed, no classical global repair). Compare two readings of the *same* packet:

* **Reading A (packet-indistinguishable):** "the survivor class is one branch of a larger wavefunction"
  vs. "the survivor class is the observer's irreducible residual." Both readings leave the packet
  numbers untouched — $\Phi_Q=1$, $\Gamma_Q=0$, every predicted outcome statistic identical. The
  classifier's verdict: *narratively distinct, packet-indistinguishable*; the calculus does not
  adjudicate, and **neither reading gains authority** (ENC-1063).

* **Reading B (packet-distinguishable):** an objective-collapse reading that predicts a measurable
  deviation — say a residual decay so that the *same* observer packet now reports $\Phi_Q=0.9$ at the
  declared replay time. This claim differs on the packet, so it is adjudicable: it is *checked*
  against the standard-QM packet ($\Phi_Q=1$) and is **correct or incorrect by replay**, not by
  narrative. It still mints no authority. A sealed $\Pi^\#$ receipt would add checked terminal-policy evidence only.

The example shows the whole mechanism: an interpretation is a shadow exactly while it leaves every
packet number invariant; it becomes adjudicable (not authoritative) precisely when it perturbs one.

4. Aim and Reconstruction Conjecture


This track asks whether quantum structure can be selected from the calculus of **closed non-exact finite-observer residuals**. We ask: *What theory, if any, is forced when local observer packets are individually valid but fail to glue into a single classical global state?*

A classical theory demands:
$$ \text{all local sections glue globally} $$

One contextual obstruction signature is **[DEFINITION]**:
$$ \text{local sections close locally but leave nonzero survivor classes} $$
$$ \Phi_Q([x]) > 0, \qquad \Gamma_Q([x]) = 0 $$

**Open Working Conjecture [CONJECTURE]:** A quantum realization may provide a finite authority calculus for observer-relative residuals that cannot be classically glued away. Contextual obstruction alone does not select QM — this is the program's central *unresolved* reconstruction question (tracked as obligations PL-QM001-1 / PL-QM008-2), stated here, not resolved.

5. The Derivation Target


*A target, not a result (this section names the goal; it does not discharge it).* If a system possesses:

1. Finite local measurement contexts
2. Nontrivial overlap constraints
3. No global classical repair
4. Stable probabilistic pairing
5. No-signalling closure

then the natural terminal calculus is a contextual finite packet theory, with QM standing as **a**
most successful *known* realization — **not** a proven-unique one. If non-QM contextual packet
theories satisfy these criteria (e.g. generalized-probabilistic / Spekkens-type toy models — cf. **QM_101** pairing audit, or **QM_120** / Mermin for concrete contextuality packets), then obstruction alone does not derive QM, and additional representation transport ($\chi_F$)
is required **[INTERPRETIVE]**. Whether the five conditions *force* complex Hilbert QM is the
unresolved reconstruction question of §4; this section names the target, it does not discharge it.

  contributes the shadow taxonomy plus its falsifiability criterion.

7. Falsifiability


**[FALSIFIABILITY]** This paper's substantive claim fails if any of the following holds:

* a structural/formal derivation is exhibited that produces an authority terminal with no sealed
  $\Pi^\#$ — i.e. a counterexample to ENC-1063 ($\mathrm{StructureDerived}\Rightarrow\mathrm{IsAuth}$);
  such a failure would refute the entire program's core doctrine; **or**

* two interpretations that agree on *every* finite observer packet are shown to be nonetheless
  adjudicable by the calculus (which would refute the packet-distinguishability criterion of §2); **or**

* If the five conditions force complex Hilbert QM, the reconstruction target is upgraded;
  the interpretation-shadow classifier remains valid unless an interpretation itself
  is forced by the same packet data.

8. What This Paper Does NOT Claim


* It does **not** derive quantum mechanics from contextual obstruction (§4–§5 are an open target).
* It does **not** prove any interpretation true or false in general — only that each is a shadow until
  it adds a packet-distinguishable claim (§2, `[INTERPRETIVE]`).

* It does **not** grant authority to any interpretation, reading, or structural derivation; no sealed $\Pi^\#$ terminal constructs authority; bounded promotion remains an external authenticated human decision (ENC-1063).
* It does **not** establish universal uniqueness or quantum origin of the O022 interface lift (which is
  registry-relative, ENC-1075).

* It does **not** promote Hilbert-space representation, wavefunction state, measurement formalism, or
  observer language into ontology (Level Q boundary, §Level Q boundary).
